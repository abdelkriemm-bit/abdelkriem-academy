Grammar: Present continuous (am / is / are + -ing)

Spelling: make → making · sit → sitting · study → studying Habit vs now: I usually wear a suit, but today I'm wearing jeans.

العربية تستخدم نفس الفعل للعادة وللآن، أما الإنجليزية فتستخدم am / is / are + ing لما يحدث الآن فقط.

PositiveNegativeQuestion
I am working. She is cooking. They are playing.He isn't working.Are you listening?

Exercises

Exercise 1 — Use the present continuous.

  1. 1) She ___ (cook) dinner now.
  2. 2) I ___ (read) a book at the moment.
  3. 3) They ___ (play) football right now.
  4. 4) We ___ (not watch) TV.
  5. 5) ___ you ___ (wait) for me?
  6. 6) It ___ (rain).
Show the answers
1) is cooking · 2) am reading · 3) are playing · 4) aren't watching · 5) Are … waiting · 6) is raining

Exercise 2 — Present simple or present continuous?

  1. 1) I usually (drink) ___ tea, but today I (drink) ___ coffee.
  2. 2) Listen! The baby (cry) ___.
  3. 3) He (go) ___ to work every day.
  4. 4) Look! She (wear) ___ a red scarf.
Show the answers
1) drink / am drinking · 2) is crying · 3) goes · 4) is wearing

Exercise 3 — Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) I am work now.
  2. 2) She wearing a hat.
  3. 3) Are you understand?
  4. 4) It raining.
Show the answers
1) I am working now. · 2) She is wearing a hat. · 3) Do you understand? · 4) It is raining.

Exercise 4 — Translate into English.

  1. 1) ماذا ترتدي اليوم؟
  2. 2) أنا أطبخ الآن.
  3. 3) هم لا ينامون؛ إنهم يشاهدون التلفاز.
  4. 4) الجو بارد في الشتاء.
Show the answers
1) What are you wearing today? · 2) I'm cooking now. · 3) They aren't sleeping; they're watching TV. · 4) It's cold in winter.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak

Write & Speak — Look out of your window or around a café. Write 6 sentences about what is happening now, and what the weather is like. Example: It's sunny and warm today. A man is selling fruit in the street. Two boys are playing football. A woman is waiting for the bus. She is wearing a green scarf. I am drinking tea and writing this.
