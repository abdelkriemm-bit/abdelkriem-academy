Grammar: Reported speech (statements) · say and tell

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

When we report what someone said, the verb usually goes one step back into the past:

am / is → was · do → did · did / have done → had done · will → would · can → could.

say something · tell SOMEONE something.

في العربية نقول «قال لي» فيكتب المتعلمون ✗ He said me. لكن say لا يأتي بعده الشخص مباشرة، أما tell فلا بد أن يأتي بعده الشخص: ✓ He said (that) he was tired. ✓ He told me (that) he was tired. ✗ He told that… وعند نقل الكلام نرجع بالزمن خطوة للماضي ونغيّر الضمائر: “I am busy” → She said she was busy.

Direct speechReported speech
“I am tired.”He said (that) he was tired.
“I'm working.”She said she was working.
“I have finished.”He said he had finished.
“I saw Ali.”She said she had seen Ali.
“I will call you.”He said he would call me.
“I can help.”She said she could help.
say + (that)tell + person + (that)
He said (that) he was busy. ✗ He said me…He told me (that) he was busy. ✗ He told that…

Time and place words change: today → that day · tomorrow → the next day · yesterday → the day before · here → there.

that is optional: She said (that) she was hungry.

If something is still true, you can keep the present: He said he lives in Giza.

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Complete with said or told.

  1. 1) She ___ me she was busy.
  2. 2) They ___ us they were moving to Aswan.
  3. 3) He ___ that he liked the film.
  4. 4) My father ___ he was proud of me.
  5. 5) What did she ___ to you?
  6. 6) I ___ the doctor that I couldn't sleep.
Show the answers
1) told 2) told 3) said 4) said 5) say 6) told

Exercise 2 · Report the sentences.

  1. 1) Ahmed: “I'm tired.”
  2. 2) Mona: “I don't like coffee.”
  3. 3) Omar (to me): “I'll call you tomorrow.”
  4. 4) Sara: “I can't come to the party.”
  5. 5) My friends: “We have finished the project.”
  6. 6) Nour: “I saw Karim yesterday.”
Show the answers
1) Ahmed said (that) he was tired. 2) Mona said (that) she didn't like coffee. 3) Omar told me (that) he would call me the next day / the following day. 4) Sara said (that) she couldn't come to the party. 5) My friends said (that) they had finished the project. 6) Nour said (that) she had seen Karim the day before / the previous day.

Exercise 3 · Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) He said me that he was late.
  2. 2) She told that she was hungry.
  3. 3) Last week they said they will come, but they didn't.
  4. 4) He told to me the truth.
  5. 5) Last month she told me she will help me with my CV, but she forgot.
Show the answers
1) He told me that he was late. / He said that he was late. 2) She said that she was hungry. 3) Last week they said they would come, but they didn't. 4) He told me the truth. 5) Last month she told me she would help me with my CV, but she forgot.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) قال إنه متعب.
  2. 2) أخبرتني أنها ستتصل بي.
  3. 3) قالوا إنهم لا يستطيعون المجيء.
  4. 4) تخاصمت مع أخي، لكننا تصالحنا.
Show the answers
1) He said (that) he was tired. 2) She told me (that) she would call me. / She told me (that) she would phone me. 3) They said (that) they couldn't come. 4) I fell out with my brother, but we made up. / I had an argument with my brother, but we made up.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — Write 120–150 words: tell the story of a phone call or conversation with a friend or relative — good news, an argument or a secret. Report at least six things people said (said that…, told me that…, explained that…). Then tell the story aloud to someone at home.

Example: Last week my best friend Hana called me late at night. She told me that she had some big news. At first I thought something bad had happened, but she said that she was getting engaged! She explained that she had met Youssef at work two years earlier and that they had been friends for a long time. I told her that I was really happy for her. Then she said that the engagement party would be in Alexandria in December and that she needed my help to choose a dress. Hana admitted that she was a bit nervous because Youssef's family was very big. I told her that she didn't need to worry, because everyone loved her. Before we said goodbye, she said she couldn't wait to see me. I promised that I would always support her.
