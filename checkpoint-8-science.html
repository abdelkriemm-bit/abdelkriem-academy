  • Lesson 1Stage 8 Science: The Tests and How to PrepareHow Cambridge Lower Secondary science tests work — two 45-minute papers, question styles, mark-scheme words and results — and how to use this Stage 8 course to prepare.
  • Lesson 2Thinking and Working Scientifically: Models, Investigations and Analysing ResultsEvery Stage 8 science skill — analogies and formulae, hypotheses, planning, risk, keys, accurate measuring, tables, graphs, anomalies, conclusions and evaluation — with one investigation followed from start to finish.

Biology

  • Lesson 3Joints and Antagonistic MusclesBall-and-socket and hinge joints, and how antagonistic muscles such as the biceps and triceps move the bones at a hinge joint.
  • Lesson 4Blood and Its ComponentsRed blood cells, white blood cells and plasma: what each looks like and what it does — transporting oxygen, protecting against pathogens, and carrying cells, nutrients and carbon dioxide.
  • Lesson 5Breathing, Gas Exchange and Diffusion in the LungsHow the lungs, ribs, intercostal muscles and diaphragm move air, how oxygen and carbon dioxide diffuse in the alveoli, and why breathing is not the same as respiration — with Checkpoint-style practice.
  • Lesson 6A Balanced Diet, Energy Stores and LifestyleThe nutrients in a balanced diet and what each one does, how animals get and store energy, and how diet and smoking affect growth and health — with food-label calculations and Checkpoint-style practice.
  • Lesson 7Aerobic RespirationHow every plant and animal cell releases energy from glucose in its mitochondria, the word equation for aerobic respiration, why it is a controlled release, and the classic experiments — with Checkpoint-style practice.
  • Lesson 8Ecosystems, Bioaccumulation and Invasive SpeciesEcosystems around the Earth and the habitats inside them, how toxic substances build up along food chains, and how invasive species such as water hyacinth change whole ecosystems — with food-web practice in Checkpoint style.

Chemistry

  • Lesson 9The Structure of the AtomThe Rutherford model of the atom, the charges of protons, neutrons and electrons, the gold foil evidence for the nucleus, and the electrostatic attraction that holds every atom together.
  • Lesson 10Purity, Concentration and ChromatographyWhat purity means and how to calculate it, how concentration depends on the number of solute particles in a volume, and how to carry out and read paper chromatography.
  • Lesson 11Word Equations, Exothermic and Endothermic ReactionsHow to write word equations and name the products, the main reaction patterns, and how to use a temperature change to decide whether a reaction or process is exothermic or endothermic.
  • Lesson 12The Reactivity of MetalsThe reactivity series of nine metals, how each reacts with oxygen, water and dilute acids, the word equations and the hydrogen test, and why unreactive (inert) substances such as gold are so useful.
  • Lesson 13Impure Products and Solubility of SaltsWhy real reactions give impure mixtures, how to separate and purify a salt, and how to read and use solubility curves — with worked examples, common mistakes and a Checkpoint-style quiz.

Physics

  • Lesson 14Speed and Distance–Time GraphsHow to calculate speed, distance and time, find average speeds, and read, draw and interpret distance–time graphs — with worked examples and a Checkpoint-style quiz.
  • Lesson 15Balanced and Unbalanced Forces, and MomentsHow balanced and unbalanced forces change motion, the skydiver story, and how to calculate moments and balance seesaws and levers — with worked examples and a Checkpoint-style quiz.
  • Lesson 16Pressure, Particles and DiffusionHow to calculate pressure, how particles cause pressure in gases and liquids, and how diffusion mixes gases and liquids — with worked examples, common mistakes and a Checkpoint-style quiz.
  • Lesson 17Reflection and RefractionThe law of reflection, images in plane mirrors, and why light bends when it passes between air, water and glass — explained by the change in the speed of light.
  • Lesson 18Dispersion and ColourHow a prism shows that white light is a mixture of colours, and how colours of light are added, subtracted by filters, absorbed and reflected to give objects their colour.
  • Lesson 19Magnetic Fields and ElectromagnetsWhat a magnetic field is and how to draw it, how magnetic fields push and pull on each other, and how to make, use and investigate the strength of an electromagnet.

Earth and space

  • Lesson 20The Earth's Magnetic Field and Earth's ResourcesWhy the Earth is a giant magnet, and how humans use renewable resources (wind, tidal and solar power, bioplastics) and non-renewable fossil fuels — with worked examples and a Checkpoint-style quiz.
  • Lesson 21Climate, Weather and Climate ChangeThe difference between weather and climate, the evidence that the Earth's climate cycles between ice ages and warm periods, and how changes in the atmosphere change the climate — with graphs, worked examples and a Checkpoint-style quiz.
  • Lesson 22Galaxies and AsteroidsWhat a galaxy is made of — stars, planetary systems, dust and gas — and how asteroids formed from rocks left over when the Solar System formed, with diagrams, worked examples and a Checkpoint-style quiz.
