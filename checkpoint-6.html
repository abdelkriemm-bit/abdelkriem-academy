Reading: grammar & punctuation

Every grammar and punctuation point the reading questions test, with practice.

  • Parts of Speech (Word Classes)Every word class you need for Checkpoint 6: nouns (including abstract nouns), pronouns (including relative pronouns), verbs, modal verbs and infinitives, adjectives, adverbs, determiners, prepositions and conjunctions, how to name a word class in context, and practice with answers.
  • Phrases and ClausesNoun phrases, expanded noun phrases, prepositional phrases and fronted adverbials, main and subordinate clauses, and relative clauses: how to spot them, how Checkpoint asks about them, how to use them in your writing, with practice and answers.

  • Conjunctions and ConnectivesCoordinating and subordinating conjunctions, connectives grouped by function (time, cause, contrast, addition and more), how to link paragraphs, and how Checkpoint 6 asks about them.
  • Punctuation: Every Mark and Why Writers Use ItEvery punctuation mark you need for Checkpoint 6, from full stops to semicolons, with the uses to learn, all the possible answers to 'Why does the writer use ___?', speech punctuation, apostrophes and practice.
