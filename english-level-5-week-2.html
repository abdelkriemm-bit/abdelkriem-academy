Grammar: The impersonal passive · It is said that… · He is believed to…

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

Use the impersonal passive to report what people in general say, believe or expect, without saying who. Two patterns: It + is said / believed / thought + that + clause · Subject + is said / believed / thought + to + infinitive (to do / to be doing / to have done).

في العربية نقول «يُقال إن…» و«يُعتقد أن…»، فيترجمها المتعلم حرفيًا: It is said that he is rich ✓، لكنه يخطئ في النمط الثاني فيقول He is said that he is rich ✗. القاعدة: إذا بدأت بـ It فاستخدم that، وإذا بدأت بالشخص نفسه فاستخدم to + المصدر: He is said to be rich. وإذا كان الحدث في الماضي نستخدم to have + التصريف الثالث: He is said to have left the country (يُقال إنه غادر البلد).

Active (people say…)It + passive + that…Subject + passive + to…
People say that he is very rich.It is said that he is very rich.He is said to be very rich.
Experts believe that the city is growing fast.It is believed that the city is growing fast.The city is believed to be growing fast.
People think that she left the company.It is thought that she left the company.She is thought to have left the company.
Reports said that the minister was ill.It was reported that the minister was ill.The minister was reported to be ill.

to + infinitive = same time (to be) · to be + -ing = in progress now (to be growing) · to have + V3 = earlier (to have left).

Common verbs: say, believe, think, know, expect, report, claim, consider, estimate, understand.

This structure is typical of news reports, essays and formal writing: it sounds objective and impersonal.

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Rewrite the sentences starting with “It”.

  1. 1) People say that the new manager is very strict.
  2. 2) Experts expect that prices will rise next year.
  3. 3) People believe that the pyramids were built by skilled workers.
  4. 4) Journalists reported that the actor had left Egypt.
  5. 5) Many people think that money brings happiness.
Show the answers
1) It is said that the new manager is very strict. 2) It is expected that prices will rise next year. 3) It is believed that the pyramids were built by skilled workers. 4) It was reported that the actor had left Egypt. 5) It is thought that money brings happiness.

Exercise 2 · Rewrite the sentences starting with the words given.

  1. 1) It is said that Hany is a brilliant chef. → Hany …
  2. 2) It is believed that the company is planning a new branch in Alexandria. → The company …
  3. 3) It is thought that the thief escaped through the window. → The thief …
  4. 4) It is known that she speaks five languages. → She …
  5. 5) It is reported that two people were injured. → Two people …
Show the answers
1) Hany is said to be a brilliant chef. 2) The company is believed to be planning a new branch in Alexandria. 3) The thief is thought to have escaped through the window. 4) She is known to speak five languages. 5) Two people are reported to have been injured.

Exercise 3 · Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) He is said that he is a millionaire.
  2. 2) It is believed the singer to be very generous.
  3. 3) She is thought to leave the country last year.
  4. 4) The new bridge is expected open next month.
  5. 5) It says that hard work beats talent.
Show the answers
1) He is said to be a millionaire. / It is said that he is a millionaire. 2) The singer is believed to be very generous. 3) She is thought to have left the country last year. 4) The new bridge is expected to open next month. 5) It is said that hard work beats talent.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) يُقال إن هذا المطعم هو الأفضل في القاهرة.
  2. 2) يُعتقد أن اللاعب أُصيب في التدريب.
  3. 3) شقّ طريقه إلى القمة بالمثابرة والعزيمة.
  4. 4) من المتوقّع أن تحقّق الشركة نجاحًا كبيرًا.
Show the answers
1) It is said that this restaurant is the best in Cairo. / This restaurant is said to be the best in Cairo. 2) The player is believed to have been injured in training. / It is believed that the player was injured in training. 3) He worked his way up to the top through perseverance and determination. 4) The company is expected to be very successful. / It is expected that the company will be very successful. / The company is expected to achieve great success.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — Write an article (200–250 words) for a website called “Young Egypt” with the title “What does success really mean?”. Use at least four impersonal passive structures and words from Sets 1–3. Then give it as a 2-minute talk.

Example: What does success really mean? It is often said that success means having a big salary, an expensive car and a flat in New Cairo. Successful people are thought to be the ones who appear on television or have millions of followers. But is that really the whole story? In my opinion, success is far more personal. Take my uncle, Mahmoud. He is known to have started his career as a waiter in a small café in Mansoura. Over twenty years, he worked his way up and now runs his own restaurant. He is not famous, and he is certainly not a millionaire, but he is respected by everyone in his neighbourhood. For him, success means providing for his family and treating his staff fairly. It is widely believed that talent is the key to achievement. However, psychologists who study high achievers suggest that determination and perseverance matter just as much. People who keep going after a setback often go further than those who give up at the first obstacle. So what should young people aim for? I would argue that the best goal is not to impress others but to become the best version of yourself. Set yourself realistic goals, celebrate every milestone and learn from your failures. If you do that, you will have achieved something that no salary can measure: a life you are proud of.
