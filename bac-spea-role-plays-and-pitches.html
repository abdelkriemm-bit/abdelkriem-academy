Role-plays and Pitches

إزاي تمثل دور وتتكلم بوجهة نظر الشخصية، وإزاي تعرض فكرة مشروع في pitch قصير وترد على أسئلة الجمهور بثقة.

البكالوريا المصرية · Egyptian Baccalaureate › تانية بكالوريا English

الدرس ده عن إيه؟

In a role-play you pretend to be a character (a manager, an activist, an influencer…) and speak from their point of view. In a pitch you present a new idea or project in a short time and try to persuade an audience to support it, then answer their questions.

الدرس ده عن نوعين من الكلام فيهم «تمثيل» شوية:

  • Role-play = تمثيل دور: تبقى شخصية تانية (مدير شركة، عامل مصنع، ناشط بيئي، صاحب متشكك…) وتتكلم بعيونها هي مش بعيونك إنت.
  • Pitch = عرض فكرة جديدة بسرعة (دقيقة لـ ٣ دقايق) علشان تقنع ناس يدعموها، زي اللي بيعرض مشروع على مستثمرين، وبعدين ترد على أسئلتهم.

في الكتاب: Unit 8 فيه The Career Pitch Panel: فريق من *educational consultants* بيعرض برنامج تدريب مهني جديد على *a board of investors* (تحضير ٥ دقايق، Pitch ٣ دقايق، والفصل يسأل سؤالين عن *feasibility*). Unit 10 فيه ٣ أنشطة: The Convincing Post (influencer وصاحب متشكك)، The Fast Fashion Trial (محاكمة تمثيلية بـ ٤ أدوار)، وPitch Your Project (pitch دقيقة واحدة لمبادرة استهلاك واعي، والمجموعة التانية تسأل سؤال صعب واحد). وكمان في Unit 4 (مدير سوبر ماركت وناشط بيئي) وUnit 6 (The Wetland Meeting).

الجزء الأول: الـ Role-play

The secret of a good role-play is to think like your character: What do they want? What are they worried about? What evidence would they use?

سر الـ role-play الناجح إنك تنسى رأيك الشخصي وتفكر زي الشخصية. اسأل نفسك ٣ أسئلة قبل ما تبدأ:

السؤالEnglishمثال: عامل مصنع في The Fast Fashion Trial
الشخصية دي عايزة إيه؟What does my character want?مرتب أحسن وظروف شغل آمنة
قلقانة من إيه؟What is my character worried about?إنه يخسر شغله لو اشتكى
هتستخدم دليل إيه؟What evidence would they use?ساعات شغل طويلة، ضغط المواعيد من الماركات

خطوات الـ role-play:

  1. اقرأ الدور والموقف (position) كويس. في الكتاب كل دور مكتوب جنبه موقفه بالظبط.
  2. اكتب حجتين للشخصية بالـ JRE (رأي + سبب + دليل).
  3. اتكلم بضمير I / we كأنك الشخصية: *As a factory worker, I…*
  4. اسمع الشخصيات التانية وردّ عليهم (اعترف + رد).
  5. استخدم الجرامر المطلوب في النشاط (الكتاب دايمًا بيحدد: reported speech / stative verb / quantifier).
  6. في الآخر: اتفاق أو تصويت زي ما النشاط بيقول.

الجرامر اللي الكتاب بيطلبه في الأنشطة دي

نموذج Role-play: The Convincing Post

Model role-play (pairs, about 1½ minutes)176 words
Task: Partner A plays an influencer trying to convince a friend to buy a trending product. Partner B plays a doubtful friend who questions the real motivation behind the recommendation. Use at least one stative verb to express an opinion.

A (influencer): Hi, Karim! Have you seen my latest video? I'm showing everyone the new SmartBottle. It tells you when to drink water. I believe this product is really worth it because it keeps you healthy.

B (doubtful friend): Hmm. I don't think I actually need this. I already drink water when I'm thirsty. Isn't it just about the money the company pays you?

A: I understand why you're doubtful, but I really use it every day. It has changed my routine, and it looks great too.

B: It looks nice, I agree. But it costs eight hundred pounds! A normal bottle does the same job for fifty. And you said "everyone is using it". That's urgency language, isn't it? It makes people feel they must buy now.

A: OK, that's a fair point. The company did send it to me for free.

B: So you should tell your followers that. I prefer honest recommendations. Then people can decide for themselves.

A: You're right. In my next video, I'll say clearly that it was a gift.

ليه النموذج ده قوي؟
  • كل واحد في دوره: الـ influencer بيقنع بحماس، والصاحب بيشك ويسأل *Isn't it just about…?* (من جمل الكتاب).
  • Stative verbs صح: *I believe / I don't think / feel / I prefer* ← ولا واحد فيهم في الـ continuous.
  • الرد بأدب: *I understand why you're doubtful, but…* و*that's a fair point*.
  • ربط بالوحدة: الصاحب لاحظ urgency language (من جدول تحليل البوستات في Unit 10)، وكمان السعر كدليل.
  • نهاية واضحة: وصلوا لحل (الصراحة مع المتابعين).

الجزء التاني: الـ Pitch

A pitch is short and persuasive. In one to three minutes you explain the problem, your solution, how it works, its impact, and you finish with a clear request. Then you answer questions confidently.

الـ pitch زي الإعلان القصير بالظبط: وقت قليل، وكل جملة لازم تقنع. ده الهيكل اللي بيغطي طلبات الكتاب في Unit 8 وUnit 10:

الجزءبتقول إيه؟جمل جاهزة
١. Hook + Problem (The Gap)إيه المشكلة؟ ليه مهمة؟Every year, … / Did you know that…? / The problem is that…
٢. Solutionفكرتكم إيه؟Our idea is to… / We aim to provide…
٣. How it worksهتشتغل إزاي عمليًا؟ (مين، فين، إمتى)Students would… / Every month,… / It works like this:…
٤. Skills / What's differentإيه اللي مميز فيها؟Students will master three practical skills:… / Unlike existing options, this would…
٥. Impact (بـ quantifiers)هتأثر قد إيه؟If this ran every month, it would… / Many families could… / A large amount of waste would…
٦. Request / Closeإنتوا عايزين إيه من الجمهور؟We are asking you to… / Join us and… / This initiative will bridge the gap…

بعد الـ pitch: الأسئلة (Q&A). الكتاب بيقول الجمهور بيسأل عن feasibility (هل الفكرة ممكن تتنفذ فعلًا؟ فلوس؟ وقت؟ ناس؟). إزاي ترد:

  • اشكر على السؤال: *That's a great question.*
  • جاوب على طول وبوضوح، ويُفضل برقم أو مثال: *We would start with only one room and ten volunteers.*
  • لو مش عارف كل حاجة، قول بصراحة واقترح: *We haven't calculated the exact cost yet, but we plan to ask local businesses for support.*
  • ارجع لفكرتك الأساسية في جملة: *So the project is realistic and cheap to start.*

نموذج Pitch: Pitch Your Project (دقيقة واحدة)

Model one-minute pitch + answer to a challenging question210 words
Task: In groups of three, invent a new mindful consumption initiative for your school or neighbourhood. Prepare a one-minute pitch: what it is and what problem it solves, how it would work, and at least one sentence with quantifiers describing its potential impact. Then answer ONE challenging question.

Speaker 1: How many of you have a broken phone charger or a torn school bag at home? Most of us just throw these things away and buy new ones. That creates a lot of waste and costs our families money.

Speaker 2: Our idea is to open a Repair Café in the school library every first Thursday of the month. Students bring broken items, and volunteers, including parents who are electricians or tailors, help them fix them for free. Unlike existing repair shops, this would also teach students how to repair things themselves.

Speaker 3: If this ran every month, it would save a large amount of money and keep many items out of the rubbish. Even if only a few students came at first, every repaired item would mean one less new product. So join us and give your broken things a second life!

Question from the other group: What if nobody volunteers to repair things?

Speaker 2: That's a great question. We have already spoken to two teachers who can sew and one parent who repairs electronics, so we can start small. After the first month, we will ask older students who learnt the skills to become volunteers too. So the project can grow step by step.

ليه النموذج ده قوي؟
  • Hook + Problem: سؤال للجمهور + المشكلة (رمي الحاجات = waste + فلوس).
  • Solution + How it works: *Our idea is to…* (من جمل الكتاب) + تفاصيل عملية: فين؟ إمتى؟ مين بيصلّح؟
  • What's different: *Unlike existing repair shops, this would…* ← جملة من الـ Useful phrases في الكتاب.
  • Impact بـ quantifiers: *a lot of / a large amount of / many / a few / Most of us* ← الكتاب طالب جملة واحدة على الأقل، والنموذج فيه أكتر.
  • Close: دعوة واضحة (*So join us…*).
  • الرد على السؤال الصعب: شكر ← إجابة بحقايق (مدرسين + ولي أمر) ← خطة للمستقبل ← جملة ترجع للفكرة. ده رد على feasibility.
  • توزيع الكلام على ٣ متحدثين زي ما المجموعة في الكتاب تلاتة.
أشهر الأخطاء
  • ✗ في الـ role-play تقول رأيك إنت مش رأي الشخصية ← ✓ *As a fashion brand executive, I believe we simply supply what customers demand.*
  • ✗ *I am wanting / I am believing* ← ✓ *I want / I believe* (stative verbs)
  • ✗ *much customers / many water* ← ✓ *many customers / much water / a large amount of water*
  • ✗ pitch كله كلام عام: *Our idea is very good.* ← ✓ قول إزاي هتشتغل وقد إيه هتأثر.
  • ✗ تتلخبط من السؤال وتقول *I don't know* ← ✓ *That's a great question. We would start by…*
  • ✗ تعدي وقت الـ pitch ← ✓ دقيقة واحدة يعني حوالي ١٢٠ – ١٥٠ كلمة بس.
حيلة: «مشكلة – حل – إزاي – قد إيه – تعالوا»

احفظ الـ pitch كده: مشكلة (The problem is…) ← حل (Our idea is to…) ← إزاي (It works like this…) ← قد إيه (Many / A large amount of…) ← تعالوا (Join us / Support us). ٥ جمل بس تبقى pitch كامل لو الوقت قصير.

إزاي بييجي في الأسئلة؟

١) Role-play بجدول أدوار (Unit 10 – The Fast Fashion Trial): كل طالب ياخد دور (Fashion Brand Executive / Factory Worker / Environmental Activist / Everyday Shopper) ويدافع عن موقفه بـ quantifier واحد على الأقل، وبعدين الفصل يصوّت مين عليه أكبر مسؤولية.

مثال إجابة (Environmental Activist): *In my view, real change would require the whole industry model to change. A large amount of responsibility lies with brands, because they produce new collections every week. It isn't fair to blame only shoppers, because most of them don't know how their clothes are made.*

٢) Pitch panel (Unit 8): *The Gap ← Skills (three practical skills) ← The Future (Egypt's economic development)* + ٣ كلمات من الوحدة + سؤالين عن الـ feasibility.

٣) Role-play بجرامر محدد (Unit 6 – reported speech، Unit 10 – stative verb): لازم تستخدم القاعدة المطلوبة في كلامك، زي: *The fisherman told us that fishing was their livelihood.*

تدريب

A) Choose the correct answer from a, b, c or d.

  1. 1. Influencer: "I ______ this product is really worth it." a. am believing b. believe c. believing d. am believe

    Show answer
    b — believe فعل stative، مبيجيش في الـ continuous.

  2. 2. Activist: "______ responsibility lies with the brands." a. A large amount of b. Many c. A few d. Several

    Show answer
    a — responsibility مبتتعدش، يبقى a large amount of.

  3. 3. Pitch: "If this ran every month, it ______ hundreds of books." a. will save b. saves c. would save d. saved

    Show answer
    c — If + past ← would (زي جملة الكتاب *If this ran every month, it would…*).

  4. 4. "______ customers return clothes they have never worn." a. Much b. Many c. A little d. A large amount of

    Show answer
    b — customers بتتعد، يبقى many.

  5. 5. In a pitch panel, "feasibility" questions ask: a. if the idea is funny b. if the idea can really be done (money, time, people) c. your name d. the history of the topic

    Show answer
    b — feasibility = إمكانية التنفيذ.

  6. 6. Which sentence is best for a pitch close? a. That's all, I think. b. Join us and give your old uniforms a second life! c. I forgot the rest. d. Bye.

    Show answer
    b — دعوة واضحة ومقنعة.

B) Role-play: what would your character say?

  1. 7. You are a supermarket manager. The activist says shops must donate all unsold food.

    Show answer
    *As a supermarket manager, I understand your concern, but we have to think about food safety. A fair compromise might be donating packaged food that is still within its date.*

  2. 8. You are an environmental activist answering the manager above.

    Show answer
    *I understand that safety is important, but throwing away safe food while families are hungry is wrong. Many charities already collect food safely every day.*

  3. 9. You are a fashion brand executive in the mock trial.

    Show answer
    *Speaking as a brand executive, I believe we simply supply what customers demand. Most shoppers want new styles every week. It isn't fair to blame only brands.*

  4. 10. You are a factory worker in the mock trial.

    Show answer
    *As a factory worker, I can tell you that our wages and working hours are set by pressure from brands. Many of us work very long hours to finish orders on time.*

  5. 11. You are an everyday shopper in the mock trial.

    Show answer
    *From my point of view as a shopper, individuals have limited power. I don't know how most clothes are made, because labels give very little information.*

  6. 12. You are a local developer in the Wetland Meeting (Unit 6).

    Show answer
    *From my perspective, people need homes, and this land is flat and well-irrigated. If we build on only part of it, then birds can still use the rest.*

C) Answer the challenging question after your pitch.

  1. 13. "Who will pay for your training programme?"

    Show answer
    *That's a great question. We plan to partner with local companies that need trained workers. They would pay part of the cost because they benefit from the graduates.*

  2. 14. "Why would students come to a swap event?"

    Show answer
    *Because they save money and get useful things for free. We would also make it fun, with music and a small prize for the student who brings the most items.*

  3. 15. "What if the shared tools are lost or broken?"

    Show answer
    *Good point. Every borrower would write their name and return date in a simple record, and a small group of volunteers would check the tools every week.*

  4. 16. "How do you know there are enough jobs for your graduates?"

    Show answer
    *Renewable energy is growing fast in Egypt, for example with large solar projects, so many companies need technicians to install and maintain the systems.*

  5. 17. "Isn't one school too small to make a difference?"

    Show answer
    *It is a small start, but if a few schools copy the idea, the impact grows. Even one shelf can reduce the demand for many new purchases.*

D) Practice prompts with model ideas.

  1. 18. (Unit 8) Career Pitch Panel: pitch a training programme in Renewable Energy.

    Show answer
    Gap: not enough trained solar technicians. Skills: installing panels, maintaining systems, safety. Future: supports Egypt's energy transition and creates jobs. Persuasive: *This initiative will bridge the gap between schools and the energy sector.*

  2. 19. (Unit 8) Career Pitch Panel: Healthcare.

    Show answer
    Gap: hospitals need more nursing assistants and lab technicians. Skills: first aid, patient care, using medical equipment. Future: healthier communities and more jobs. Persuasive: *Our programme ensures graduates are ready from day one.*

  3. 20. (Unit 8) Career Pitch Panel: Information Technology.

    Show answer
    Gap: companies need cybersecurity and app developers. Skills: coding, network security, customer support. Future: a growing digital economy. Persuasive: *We aim to provide practical training with real projects.*

  4. 21. (Unit 10) Pitch Your Project: a uniform swap event.

    Show answer
    Problem: students grow fast and uniforms are expensive. How: once a term in the school hall. Impact: *Many families would save a large amount of money.* Close: *Bring what doesn't fit, take what does!*

  5. 22. (Unit 10) Pitch Your Project: a tool-sharing library for your neighbourhood.

    Show answer
    Problem: most people use a drill only a few times a year. How: tools are kept at a community centre and borrowed for a week. Impact: fewer purchases, less waste. Close: *Borrow, don't buy.*

  6. 23. (Unit 10) The Convincing Post: an influencer promoting fast-fashion clothes.

    Show answer
    Influencer: *I love this collection, it's so cheap!* Friend: *I don't think you need ten new shirts. Isn't it just about the brand paying you? A cheap shirt has hidden costs: water and factory workers' wages.*

  7. 24. (Unit 4) Role-play: a brand marketing manager and a parent. Do brands target teenagers unfairly?

    Show answer
    Manager: we create products teenagers want. Parent: adverts use influencers and urgency to pressure young people. Compromise: clear labels when a post is an advert.

  8. 25. (Unit 6) The Wetland Meeting: you are the fishing community member. Use reported speech.

    Show answer
    *The conservationist said that the wetland needed full protection, and the developer told us that people needed houses. But my family depends on the fish and clean water. A fair compromise might be protecting the water and building only on dry land nearby.*
