was / were · there was / there were

«كان / كانوا» = was / were. لا تقل I was go — was تأتي مع صفة أو مكان: I was tired / I was at home.

PositiveNegativeQuestion
I / he / she / itwaswasn'tWas he…?
you / we / theywereweren'tWere you…?

am / is → was · are → were · there is → there was · there are → there were

Exercises

Exercise 1 · Complete with was, were, wasn't or weren't.

  1. 1) I ___ tired yesterday.
  2. 2) They ___ at the beach last week.
  3. 3) The film ___ boring.
  4. 4) We ___ at a wedding last night.
  5. 5) ___ you at work yesterday?
  6. 6) She ___ happy. (–)
Show the answers
1) was · 2) were · 3) was · 4) were · 5) Were · 6) wasn't

Exercise 2 · Complete with there was / there were / was there / were there.

  1. 1) ___ a lot of cars in the street.
  2. 2) ___ a small shop in my village.
  3. 3) ___ any people in the park?
  4. 4) ___ a problem with the car.
Show the answers
1) There were · 2) There was · 3) Were there · 4) There was

Exercise 3 · Make questions.

  1. 1) You were at home.
  2. 2) The film was good.
  3. 3) They were late.
  4. 4) She was born in Cairo.
Show the answers
1) Were you at home? · 2) Was the film good? · 3) Were they late? · 4) Was she born in Cairo?

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) كنت قلقًا أمس.
  2. 2) أين كنتم الأسبوع الماضي؟
  3. 3) لم تكن الرحلة مملة.
  4. 4) وُلد أبي في قرية.
Show the answers
1) I was worried yesterday. · 2) Where were you last week? · 3) The trip wasn't boring. · 4) My father was born in a village.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak

Write & Speak — Write 6 sentences about your childhood with was / were / there was / there were.

Example: I was born in 1992 in Zagazig. My house was small but nice. There was a big tree next to it. My primary school was near my house. My teachers were kind. I was a quiet child.
