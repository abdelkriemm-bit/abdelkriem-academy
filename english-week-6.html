10 sentences to memorise · جمل تحفظها

احفظ جملتين كل يوم، وقول كل جملة بصوت عالي 5 مرات لحد ما تطلع منك لوحدها.

  1. 1I'd like a cup of tea, please.أريد كوبًا من الشاي من فضلك.
  2. 2How much is this? — It's fifty pounds.بكم هذا؟ — بخمسين جنيهًا.
  3. 3Can I have the bill, please?الحساب من فضلك.
  4. 4We need some eggs and some bread.نحتاج إلى بعض البيض وبعض الخبز.
  5. 5Is there any milk in the fridge?هل يوجد حليب في الثلاجة؟

Grammar: Countable / uncountable · some / any · How much? How many? · I'd like…

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

some in positive sentences and offers (Would you like some tea?) · any in negatives and questions (I don't have any rice. Is there any milk?) How much is it? = asking the price.

«بكم؟» = How much is it? · وفي المطعم الأدب أن تقول …I'd like أو …Can I have بدلًا من I want.

Countable (can count)Uncountable (can't count)
an apple → two apples · How many apples?water, rice, bread, milk, money (no a, no -s) · How much water?

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 — Complete with How much or How many.

  1. 1) ___ eggs do we need?
  2. 2) ___ water do you drink?
  3. 3) ___ is this shirt?
  4. 4) ___ children do you have?
  5. 5) ___ sugar do you take?
  6. 6) ___ people are there?
Show the answers
1) How many · 2) How much · 3) How much · 4) How many · 5) How much · 6) How many

Exercise 2 — Complete with some or any.

  1. 1) I want ___ bread.
  2. 2) We don't have ___ rice.
  3. 3) Is there ___ juice?
  4. 4) I'd like ___ fish, please.
  5. 5) There aren't ___ tomatoes.
  6. 6) Would you like ___ tea?
Show the answers
1) some · 2) any · 3) any · 4) some · 5) any · 6) some

Exercise 3 — Countable (C) or uncountable (U)?

  1. 1) chair
  2. 2) water
  3. 3) egg
  4. 4) rice
  5. 5) banana
  6. 6) money
Show the answers
1) C · 2) U · 3) C · 4) U · 5) C · 6) U

Exercise 4 — Translate into English.

  1. 1) أريد كيلو طماطم من فضلك.
  2. 2) بكم هذا القميص؟
  3. 3) لا يوجد أي سكر.
  4. 4) هل يمكنني الدفع نقدًا؟
Show the answers
1) I'd like / I want a kilo of tomatoes, please. · 2) How much is this shirt? · 3) There isn't any sugar. / There's no sugar. · 4) Can I pay in cash? / Can I pay cash?

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — Write a 6-line conversation in a café or shop. Read both parts aloud, then act it out with someone. Example: A: Good evening. Can I help you? — B: Yes, I'd like a cup of tea, please. — A: Anything else? — B: A piece of cake, please. How much is it? — A: It's ninety pounds. — B: Here you are. Thank you!
