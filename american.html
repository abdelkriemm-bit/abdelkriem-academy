How to use these courses

  1. Read the overview page of your test first.
  2. Work through the lessons in order and do the quiz at the end of each page.
  3. Take a full timed practice test, then review every explanation, not only the wrong ones.
  4. Go back to the lessons for the skills where you lost the most marks, then take the next test.
