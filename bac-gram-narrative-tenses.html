Narrative Tenses

الأزمنة التلاتة اللي بنحكي بيها قصة في الماضي: past simple و past continuous و past perfect، وإزاي نستخدمهم في الـ diary.

تانية بكالوريا English

الدرس ده عن إيه؟

Narrative tenses are the past tenses we use to tell a story: the past simple (main events), the past continuous (background and actions in progress) and the past perfect (an earlier past).

الدرس ده عن الأزمنة اللي بنحكي بيها قصة حصلت في الماضي. أي حكاية محتاجة 3 حاجات: الأحداث الأساسية (حصل إيه؟)، الخلفية (كان بيحصل إيه حوالينا؟)، وحاجة حصلت قبلها (كان حصل إيه قبل كده؟). لكل واحدة زمن.

في الكتاب الدرس ده في Unit 2: Leisure Time. الكتاب بيقول في أول الوحدة إنك هتستخدم the three narrative tenses، وبيشوفهم في القصة Not Exactly a Relaxing Day (رحلة العجل على النيل، صفحة 58-60)، وبعدين بتستخدمهم في كتابة diary entry (صفحة 62-63)، وبييجي سؤال عليهم في Consolidation Review A.

الفكرة ببساطة: القصة زي فيلم

Think of a story as a film. The past continuous is the scene in the background. The past simple is the action that moves the story forward. The past perfect is a flashback to something that happened earlier.

الزمنفي الفيلمسؤالهمثال
Past continuousالمشهد في الخلفية: الكاميرا بتصوّر اللي كان بيحصل.كان بيحصل إيه؟The sun was shining and birds were singing.
Past simpleالأكشن: الأحداث اللي بتحرّك القصة واحدة ورا التانية.حصل إيه؟We started early and cycled along the Nile.
Past perfectالفلاش باك: حاجة حصلت قبل الأحداث دي.كان حصل إيه قبلها؟I had never cycled so far before.

1) Past Simple: الأحداث الأساسية

Form: verb + -ed (regular) or the second form (irregular). Negative: didn't + base. Question: Did … + base? Use it for finished actions and for a sequence of events in the story.

الحالةالتكوينمثال
مثبتV2: verb + ed / شاذWe started early. I felt tired.
منفيdidn't + baseI didn't give up.
سؤالDid + subject + base?Did you enjoy the trip?

بنستخدمه للأحداث اللي خلصت، خصوصًا لما تيجي ورا بعض بالترتيب: «صحيت، لبست، ركبت العجلة». ده العمود الفقري لأي قصة.

  • We took short breaks, drank water and continued moving forward.3 أحداث ورا بعض بالترتيب ← past simple.
  • My friend insisted that I join him.حدث واحد خلص.

2) Past Continuous: الخلفية واللي كان بيحصل

Form: was / were + verb-ing. Use it for (1) the background of a scene, (2) an action in progress when another action happened, (3) two actions happening at the same time (while).

الحالةالتكوينمثال
مثبتI / he / she / it was + ing — you / we / they were + ingI was cycling. They were laughing.
منفيwasn't / weren't + ingI wasn't enjoying it any more.
سؤالWas / Were + subject + ing?Were you training at 6 o'clock?

بنستخدمه في 3 حالات:

  1. وصف المشهد في أول القصة: The wind was blowing and people were walking by the river.
  2. حدث طويل اتقطع بحدث قصير: I was cycling when my phone rang. (الطويل continuous، والقصير اللي قطعه past simple)
  3. حدثين طوال في نفس الوقت بـ while: While I was struggling, my friend was cycling happily.

الكتاب في جدول الـ diary بيقول: في الـ turning point استخدم الـ Past Continuous علشان توصف اللي كان بيحصل حواليك في اللحظة دي.

3) Past Perfect: الماضي اللي قبل الماضي

Form: had + past participle (V3) for all subjects. Use it for an action that happened before another past action or a past time. Common words: by the time, before, after, already, just, never… before, when.

الحالةالتكوينمثال
مثبتhad + V3 (لكل الضمائر)The sun had already risen.
منفيhadn't + V3I hadn't expected it to be so hard.
سؤالHad + subject + V3?Had you ever cycled that far?

لو عندك حدثين في الماضي، والحدث الأول حصل قبل التاني، الأقدم ياخد had + V3، والأحدث ياخد past simple.

  • When we finally stopped, I realised we had cycled much farther than I had expected.الأول: لفّينا مسافة كبيرة وكنت متوقع أقل. بعدين: وقفنا وأدركت. الأقدم had + V3.
  • By the time we reached the river, the sun had already risen.by the time + past simple ← الجزء التاني past perfect. الشمس طلعت قبل ما نوصل.
  • I had never felt so exhausted before.never … before = عمري ما حسيت كده قبل كده.
قارن
  • When I arrived, the match started. ← وصلت وبعدين الماتش بدأ (بدأ وأنا موجود).
  • When I arrived, the match had started. ← الماتش بدأ قبل ما أوصل (فاتني الأول).
  • When I arrived, they were playing. ← لما وصلت كانوا في نص اللعب.

الكلمات الدالة

Time words help you choose the tense, but always check the meaning too.

الكلمةمعناهاغالبًا معاهامثال

while / asفي الوقت الليpast continuousWhile we were cycling, the sun grew stronger.
by the timeعلى ماpast simple + past perfectBy the time I got home, my family had eaten.

at 6 o'clock yesterday / all morningفي وقت محدد في الماضيpast continuousAt six we were still cycling.

أمثلة من موضوعات الوحدة

الأمثلة دي عن الهوايات، الرياضة، والخروج في الطبيعة، زي قصة رحلة العجل في الكتاب.

  • I was scrolling through my phone when my coach called me.حدث طويل (كنت بقلّب) اتقطع بحدث قصير (اتصل).
  • While the crowd was cheering, Malak was running the last lap.حدثين طوال في نفس الوقت ← while + past continuous.
  • By the time the concert began, the musicians had attended five rehearsals.البروفات حصلت قبل الحفلة ← had attended.
  • Ziad had never played the oud before he joined the music club.never … before ← past perfect، وبعدها حدث أحدث past simple.
  • The air was still cool, and the road was quiet.وصف الخلفية بفعل was (حالة مش حدث). وصف مستمر ممكن كمان بالـ continuous.
  • At one point, I seriously considered giving up.حدث في القصة ← past simple.
  • My legs were aching and my breathing was uneven.وصف إحساس مستمر في اللحظة دي.
  • After we had rested in the shade, we continued cycling.after + past perfect للأقدم، وبعده past simple.
  • I didn't realise how tired I was until I sat down.past simple منفي ومثبت.
  • When we got back, my mother had already cooked dinner.الأكل اتعمل قبل ما نرجع.
ملحوظة عن used to

في أول القصة الراوي بيقول «I used to believe…». used to + base = كنت زمان (عادة أو فكرة في الماضي ومبقتش موجودة). ده مش واحد من التلات أزمنة، بس بيظهر كتير في الحكي.

إزاي نستخدمهم في كتابة الـ Diary

A good diary entry mixes the three tenses: past continuous to set the scene, past simple for the main events, and past perfect to look back at what happened earlier.

الكتاب بيطلب diary entry من 150-180 كلمة، والـ checklist بتاعته فيها: narrative tenses، كلمات الدرس، simile (تشبيه بـ like / as)، contrast، صوتك الشخصي، وreflection (اللي اتعلمته). شوف النموذج ده (الأزمنة bold):

Diary entry using narrative tenses158 words
Task: Write a diary entry about a day when you tried a new active hobby that you did not expect to enjoy. Describe what happened, how you felt and what you learned. Write about 150-180 words.

Dear Diary,

Today I went to my first pottery class at the community centre. I had never touched clay before, and I was sure I would hate it.

When I arrived, the other students were already sitting at their wheels. Soft music was playing, and the teacher was showing a girl how to shape a bowl. I sat down nervously.

At first, everything went wrong. The clay kept sliding away from my hands, and my bowl looked like a flat pancake. While everyone else was smiling at their work, I was thinking about leaving. But the teacher came over and told me to slow down.

By the time the class ended, I had made a small, slightly crooked cup. My hands were covered in clay, as grey as a rainy sky, but I felt strangely proud.

I learnt something today: leisure is not always about comfort. Sometimes the best moments come from trying again. Tomorrow I'm going back!

ليه النموذج ده قوي؟
  • Opening: Dear Diary + تحديد المشهد: رحت فين، وpast perfect (had never touched) لحاجة قبل اليوم ده.
  • الخلفية: were sitting / was playing / was showing ← past continuous بيوصف المشهد لما وصلت.
  • الأحداث: arrived, sat, went wrong, came, told ← past simple بالترتيب.
  • الـ turning point: While everyone else was smiling, I was thinking about leaving ← past continuous زي ما الكتاب بيطلب.
  • By the time … had made: ترتيب الأحداث واضح بالـ past perfect.
  • Simile: as grey as a rainy sky.
  • Contrast + Reflection: But… ثم I learnt something today… وآخر سطر ممتع ومختصر.
أشهر الأخطاء (غلط ← صح)
  • ✗ By the time we got to the station, the train has already left. ← ✓ … the train had already left. (ماضي ← had)
  • ✗ … the sun had already rose. ← ✓ … had already risen. (بعد had لازم V3)
  • ✗ When we arrived, the shop was already close. ← ✓ … the shop had already closed.
  • ✗ While I cycled, my phone was ringing. ← ✓ While I was cycling, my phone rang. (الطويل continuous والقصير simple)
  • ✗ When I arrived, the film had started so I watched it from the beginning. ← ✓ … the film hadn't started yet, so I watched it from the beginning. (المعنى مهم)
  • ✗ I was knowing the answer. ← ✓ I knew the answer. (know / like / want / believe مش بييجوا continuous)
  • ✗ She didn't went to training. ← ✓ She didn't go to training. (بعد didn't فعل مجرد)
  • ✗ They was laughing. ← ✓ They were laughing.
إزاي تجاوب بسرعة في الامتحان
  • حدث قصير وخلص؟ past simple. كان بيحصل في نص حاجة؟ was / were + ing.
  • while ← غالبًا past continuous. when + حدث قصير ← past simple.
  • by the time / already / never… before ← غالبًا had + V3.
  • لو فيه حدثين: اسأل نفسك أنهي اللي حصل الأول؟ الأقدم ياخد had + V3.
  • اتأكد من التصريف التالت للأفعال الشاذة بعد had.

إزاي بييجي في الأسئلة؟

الأسئلة في الكتاب والمراجعات بتيجي بالأشكال دي. هنحل مثال لكل شكل:

1) Only one sentence in each group is grammatically correct. Choose the correct answer.

a. By the time the guests arrived, we had already decorate the hall.

b. By the time the guests arrived, we had already decorated the hall.

c. By the time the guests arrived, we have already decorated the hall.

d. By the time the guests arrived, we were already decorated the hall.

الإجابة: b. ليه؟ by the time + past simple ← had + V3 (decorated). (a) decorate مش V3. (c) have للحاضر. (d) were decorated معناها «احنا اتزيّنا»!

2) Complete with the correct form of the verb in brackets.

While Hany (practise) ______ the piano, the lights suddenly (go) ______ out.

الإجابة: was practising / went. ليه؟ while + حدث طويل ← past continuous، والحدث القصير اللي قطعه ← past simple.

3) Read and answer: Find a sentence that shows something happened before another past action.

When Laila reached the stadium, her team had already scored two goals. She was disappointed, but she cheered loudly until the end.

الإجابة: «her team had already scored two goals» ← الأهداف حصلت قبل ما توصل، والدليل had + V3 وكلمة already.

تدريب

A. Choose the correct answer from a, b, c or d.

  1. 1. I ______ along the river when I saw an old friend. a. walked b. was walking c. had walked d. walk

    Show answer
    b. was walking — حدث طويل اتقطع بحدث قصير (saw).

  2. 2. By the time we got to the cinema, the film ______. a. already started b. has already started c. had already started d. was already start

    Show answer
    c. had already started — by the time ← past perfect.

  3. 3. While the musicians ______, the lights suddenly went out. a. played b. were playing c. had played d. play

    Show answer
    b. were playing — while + حدث طويل كان مستمر، والحدث القصير اللي قطعه (went out) past simple.

  4. 4. Yesterday, Mazen ______ his first marathon. a. was running b. had run c. ran d. runs

    Show answer
    c. ran — yesterday + حدث كامل خلص ← past simple.

  5. 5. She ______ never ______ a horse before she visited the farm. a. has / ridden b. had / ridden c. had / rode d. was / riding

    Show answer
    b. had / ridden — never … before + حدث في الماضي ← had + V3 (ride → rode → ridden).

  6. 6. When I opened the window, it ______ heavily, so I quickly closed it again. a. rained b. had rained c. was raining d. rains

    Show answer
    c. was raining — المطر كان شغال لما فتحت الشباك (خلفية).

  7. 7. After he ______ his homework, Ahmed went out to play football. a. had finished b. has finished c. was finishing d. finishes

    Show answer
    a. had finished — after + الحدث الأقدم ← had + V3.

  8. 8. We ______ to the beach because the weather was terrible. a. didn't went b. weren't go c. didn't go d. hadn't went

    Show answer
    c. didn't go — didn't + base.

B. Only one sentence in each group is grammatically correct. Choose the correct answer.

  1. 9. a. While I was painting, my little brother was watching me. b. While I painted, my little brother watching me. c. While I was paint, my little brother was watching me. d. While I painting, my little brother watched me.

    Show answer
    a — حدثين طوال في نفس الوقت، والاتنين was + ing.

  2. 10. a. When we arrived, the match has finished. b. When we arrived, the match had finish. c. When we arrived, the match had finished. d. When we arrived, the match was finish.

    Show answer
    c — الماتش خلص قبل ما نوصل ← had + V3.

  3. 11. a. They was training when the storm started. b. They were training when the storm started. c. They were training when the storm was start. d. They trained when the storm had starting.

    Show answer
    b — they ← were، والحدث القصير started (past simple).

  4. 12. a. I had never swam in the sea before that summer. b. I had never swim in the sea before that summer. c. I had never swum in the sea before that summer. d. I have never swum in the sea before that summer.

    Show answer
    c — swim → swam → swum، و before that summer (ماضي) ← had.

C. Complete the story with the correct form of the verbs in brackets.

  1. 13. Last Friday, my cousin and I (decide) ______ to climb a small hill near Fayoum.

    Show answer
    decided — حدث كامل في الماضي (Last Friday).

  2. 14. When we started, the sun (shine) ______ and a light wind (blow) ______.

    Show answer
    was shining / was blowing — وصف الخلفية لما بدأنا.

  3. 15. I (never / climb) ______ a hill before, so I was a bit nervous.

    Show answer
    had never climbed — never … before في قصة ماضية ← past perfect.

  4. 16. Halfway up, while we (rest) ______ on a rock, my cousin (drop) ______ his water bottle.

    Show answer
    were resting / dropped — حدث طويل + حدث قصير قطعه.

  5. 17. By the time we (reach) ______ the top, we (drink) ______ all our water.

    Show answer
    reached / had drunk — by the time + past simple، والحدث الأقدم had + V3 (drink → drank → drunk).

  6. 18. We (sit) ______ down, (look) ______ at the view and (laugh) ______ at how tired we were.

    Show answer
    sat / looked / laughed — أحداث ورا بعض بالترتيب ← past simple.

D. Join, correct and write as asked.

  1. 19. Join with 'when': Mariam was reading in the garden. Her phone rang.

    Show answer
    Mariam was reading in the garden when her phone rang. — الطويل continuous والقصير simple.

  2. 20. Join with 'by the time': The guests arrived. We prepared the food before that.

    Show answer
    By the time the guests arrived, we had prepared the food. — التحضير حصل الأول.

  3. 21. Find and correct the mistake: While I cycled home, I was seeing a beautiful sunset.

    Show answer
    While I was cycling home, I saw a beautiful sunset. — الطويل continuous، و see فعل مش بييجي continuous بمعنى «شاف».

  4. 22. Find and correct the mistake: I realised that I left my keys at the gym.

    Show answer
    I realised that I had left my keys at the gym. — نسيان المفاتيح حصل قبل ما أدرك.

  5. 23. Write the opening of a diary entry (2 sentences) using the past continuous to set the scene.

    Show answer
    مثال: Dear Diary, This afternoon the streets were busy and people were rushing home. I was waiting at the bus stop with my football boots in my bag. — وصف المشهد بالـ continuous.

  6. 24. Write ONE sentence about a time you finished something difficult. Use the past perfect and the past simple.

    Show answer
    مثال: When I finally crossed the finish line, I had run five kilometres for the first time in my life. — الجري حصل قبل لحظة الوصول.
ملخص الدرس في سطرين
  • Past simple = الأحداث الأساسية بالترتيب (V2 / didn't + base).
  • Past continuous = الخلفية، وحدث طويل اتقطع، وحدثين مع بعض بـ while (was / were + ing).
  • Past perfect = حاجة حصلت قبل حدث تاني في الماضي (had + V3)، مع by the time / already / never… before.
  • when + حدث قصير، while + حدث طويل. والأفعال زي know / like / believe مش بتيجي continuous.
  • في الـ diary: continuous للمشهد، simple للأحداث، perfect للفلاش باك، وفي الآخر reflection.
