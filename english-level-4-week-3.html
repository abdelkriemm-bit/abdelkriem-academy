Grammar: wish / if only — present wishes and past regrets

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

wish / if only + past simple = I want the present to be different. I wish I had a car. (I don't have one.)

wish / if only + past perfect = regret about the past. I wish I hadn't said that. (I said it.)

wish + could = ability · wish + someone + would = I want someone to change an annoying habit.

بالعربية نقول «ليتني أعرف» بالمضارع، ولذلك يخطئ كثيرون فيقولون I wish I know. في الإنجليزية نرجع بالزمن خطوة إلى الوراء: للحاضر نستخدم الماضي البسيط (I wish I knew)، وللماضي نستخدم الماضي التام (I wish I had known). ومع فعل be يمكن استخدام were مع كل الضمائر: I wish I were taller. أما wish + would فتكون للشكوى من سلوك شخص آخر، ولا نقول I wish I would.

MeaningFormExample
Present wishwish + past simpleI wish I lived near the sea.
Abilitywish + couldI wish I could speak French.
Past regretwish + past perfectI wish I had studied harder.
Annoying habitwish + someone + wouldI wish my neighbours would stop shouting.
Strong feelingIf only + the same formsIf only I hadn't lost my wallet!

Wrong: I wish I know. ✔ I wish I knew.

Wrong: I wish I didn't go yesterday. ✔ I wish I hadn't gone yesterday.

For possible future things, use hope, not wish: I hope you pass the exam. (not I wish you pass)

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Complete with the correct form of the verb.

  1. 1) I wish I ___ (have) more followers.
  2. 2) I wish I ___ (not post) that photo last night.
  3. 3) If only I ___ (can) concentrate better!
  4. 4) Mona wishes she ___ (know) how to fact-check a story.
  5. 5) I wish my brother ___ (stop) playing games all night.
  6. 6) If only we ___ (check) the source before we shared it!
Show the answers
1) had 2) hadn't posted 3) could 4) knew 5) would stop 6) had checked

Exercise 2 · Write a sentence with I wish for each situation.

  1. 1) I don't have a laptop.
  2. 2) I believed a fake story and shared it.
  3. 3) I can't stop scrolling at night.
  4. 4) My friend always sends me silly videos at 2 a.m.
  5. 5) I didn't go to bed early yesterday.
  6. 6) I live very far from my work.
Show the answers
1) I wish I had a laptop. 2) I wish I hadn't believed (shared) that fake story. / I wish I had checked the story before I shared it. 3) I wish I could stop scrolling at night. 4) I wish my friend wouldn't send me silly videos at 2 a.m. / I wish my friend would stop sending me silly videos at 2 a.m. 5) I wish I had gone to bed early yesterday. / I wish I had gone to bed earlier yesterday. 6) I wish I didn't live so far from my work. / I wish I lived near my work.

Exercise 3 · Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) I wish I know the answer.
  2. 2) I wish I didn't say that yesterday.
  3. 3) If only I have more time!
  4. 4) I wish I would be taller.
  5. 5) I wish you pass your exam tomorrow.
  6. 6) She wishes she can drive.
Show the answers
1) I wish I knew the answer. 2) I wish I hadn't said that yesterday. 3) If only I had more time! 4) I wish I were (was) taller. 5) I hope you pass your exam tomorrow. 6) She wishes she could drive.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) ليتني لم أصدّق تلك الشائعة.
  2. 2) ليت لديّ متابعين أكثر.
  3. 3) تحقق من المصدر قبل أن تشارك الخبر.
  4. 4) ليتني أستطيع إغلاق هاتفي يومًا كاملًا.
  5. 5) انتشر الخبر بسرعة، لكنه كان كاذبًا.
Show the answers
1) I wish I hadn't believed that rumour. / If only I hadn't believed that rumour. 2) I wish I had more followers. 3) Check the source before you share the news. / Check the source before sharing the news. 4) I wish I could switch off my phone for a whole day. / I wish I could turn off my phone for a whole day. 5) The news spread quickly, but it was fake. / The news spread fast, but it was false.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — “Social media: friend or enemy?”: write an opinion paragraph of 150–200 words. Give your opinion, two reasons with examples, and one wish about your own habits. Then say your opinion aloud in two minutes.

Example: In my opinion, social media is a good friend but a dangerous enemy if we don't control it. On the one hand, it keeps us in touch with people we love. My cousin lives in Canada, and thanks to WhatsApp and Facebook, I see photos of her children every week. It is also a quick way to follow breaking news and learn new skills, such as cooking or English. On the other hand, social media spreads fake news and rumours very fast. Last year, a story about schools closing in Cairo went viral, and thousands of parents panicked before the ministry said it wasn't true. I wish more people would check reliable sources before they shared posts like that. Another problem is addiction. Many young people compare themselves to influencers and feel unhappy about their own lives. Personally, I wish I didn't spend three hours a day scrolling. If only I had started limiting my screen time earlier! Now I switch off my notifications after ten o'clock, and I already feel calmer.
