Grammar: must / have to / don't have to / mustn't

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

must / have to + verb = it is necessary (You must / have to wear a seatbelt.)

mustn't = it is not allowed (You mustn't smoke here.)

don't have to = it is not necessary; you can choose (You don't have to wear a suit.)

Past: had to (I had to work late.) — must has no past form.

أكبر خطأ: الظن أن mustn't و don't have to بمعنى واحد! mustn't = ممنوع (لا تفعل)، أما don't have to = مش لازم (براحتك). ومن الأخطاء أيضًا: must to go ✗ (بعد must فعل مجرد بدون to)، و He have to ✗ (الصحيح He has to). و must غالبًا قاعدة رسمية أو رأي المتكلم نفسه، و have to التزام من الخارج (قانون، مدير، ظروف).

PositiveNegativeQuestionPast
mustYou must stop.You mustn't park here. (= not allowed)Must I …? (formal)had to
have toI have to go. / She has to go.You don't have to pay. (= not necessary)Do I have to …? / Does she have to …?had to / didn't have to

must + verb without to: You must call (NOT must to call). have to always has to.

For the past, use had to / didn't have to: I didn't have to wear a uniform at my old school.

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Complete with mustn't or don't have to.

  1. 1) You ___ use your phone while you're driving.
  2. 2) It's a free museum. You ___ pay.
  3. 3) Tomorrow is a holiday, so we ___ get up early.
  4. 4) You ___ touch that machine. It's dangerous.
  5. 5) Students ___ cheat in exams.
  6. 6) You ___ bring food. There's a restaurant there.
Show the answers
1) mustn't 2) don't have to 3) don't have to 4) mustn't 5) mustn't 6) don't have to

Exercise 2 · Complete with the correct form of have to.

  1. 1) My father ___ work on Saturdays. (positive)
  2. 2) ___ you ___ wear a uniform at your school?
  3. 3) It was a holiday yesterday, so we ___ go to work. (negative)
  4. 4) Last night I ___ stay at the office until ten. (positive)
  5. 5) Nour ___ hand in her assignment today. (negative)
  6. 6) ___ Omar ___ work overtime last week?
Show the answers
1) has to 2) Do … have to 3) didn't have to 4) had to 5) doesn't have to 6) Did … have to

Exercise 3 · Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) You must to wear a seatbelt.
  2. 2) She have to sign the contract.
  3. 3) I musted work late yesterday.
  4. 4) Do I must pay now?
  5. 5) You don't have to smoke in the hospital. It's not allowed.
Show the answers
1) You must wear a seatbelt. 2) She has to sign the contract. 3) I had to work late yesterday. 4) Do I have to pay now? (or: Must I pay now?) 5) You mustn't smoke in the hospital. It's not allowed.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) يجب أن أكون في العمل الساعة الثامنة.
  2. 2) ممنوع أن تستخدم الهاتف هنا.
  3. 3) ليس من الضروري أن ترتدي بدلة.
  4. 4) اضطررت إلى العمل يوم الجمعة الماضي.
Show the answers
1) I have to (must) be at work at eight (o'clock). 2) You mustn't (can't) use your phone here. / You aren't allowed to use your phone here. 3) You don't have to wear a suit. 4) I had to work last Friday.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — “The rules where I work or study”: write 80–100 words about the rules at your workplace or school. Say what you have to do, what you mustn't do and what you don't have to do. Then explain the rules to a new colleague or student (speak for one minute).

Example: I work in a pharmacy in Heliopolis, and we have a lot of rules. First, we must wear a white coat and a name badge. We have to be on time, because the morning shift starts at nine. We mustn't eat or drink behind the counter, and we mustn't sell some medicines without a prescription. We don't have to work every Friday, because we take turns. Last month I had to work overtime, because a colleague was ill. The rules are strict, but they are important for our customers.
