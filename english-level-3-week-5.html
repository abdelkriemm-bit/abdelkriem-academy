10 sentences to memorise · جمل تحفظها

  1. 1If I won the lottery, I would buy a flat by the sea.لو ربحت اليانصيب، لاشتريت شقة على البحر.

Grammar: Second conditional · If I had…, I would… · If I were you, I'd…

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

If + past simple, would / wouldn't + verb = an imaginary or unlikely situation now or in the future.

If I were you, I'd… = giving advice.

«لو» في العربية تجعل كثيرًا من المتعلمين يقولون ✗ If I would have money أو ✗ If I will have money. القاعدة: بعد if نستخدم الماضي البسيط (had, lived, won) — والمعنى ليس ماضيًا بل خيالي الآن — و would في الجملة الأخرى فقط. وللنصيحة نقول دائمًا If I were you (حتى مع I). قارن: الشرط الأول (If it rains, I'll stay home) = شيء ممكن فعلًا، والشرط الثاني (If I lived in London, I'd…) = شيء غير حقيقي.

if-clause (past simple)main clause (would + verb)
If I had more money,I would travel more.
If she didn't work so hard,she wouldn't be so tired.
If I were you,I'd talk to the manager.
First conditional (real, possible)Second conditional (imaginary)
If it rains tomorrow, I'll stay at home.If I lived in London, I'd visit the museums every week. (I don't live there.)

✗ If I would have money, I would buy a car. ✓ If I had money, I would buy a car.

For advice say If I were you (not If I was you).

'd = would: I'd, she'd, we'd. could = would be able to: If I had time, I could help you.

The if-clause can come first (with a comma) or second (no comma): I'd buy it if it were cheaper.

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Complete with the second conditional.

  1. 1) If I ___ (have) more time, I ___ (learn) to cook.
  2. 2) She ___ (buy) that dress if it ___ (be) cheaper.
  3. 3) If we ___ (live) in Alexandria, we ___ (go) to the beach every weekend.
  4. 4) What ___ you ___ (do) if you ___ (find) a wallet in the street?
  5. 5) If he ___ (not spend) so much, he ___ (can) save up.
  6. 6) I ___ (not borrow) money if I ___ (not need) it.
Show the answers
1) had · would learn 2) would buy · were / was 3) lived · would go 4) would … do · found 5) didn't spend · could 6) wouldn't borrow · didn't need

Exercise 2 · First or second conditional? Choose the correct answer.

  1. 1) If it (rains / rained) tomorrow, we'll stay at home.
  2. 2) If you (pay / paid) by card, you'll get a 10% discount.
  3. 3) If I (am / were) a millionaire, I'd help poor families.
  4. 4) If she (gets / got) the job, she will move to Cairo.
  5. 5) If I knew the answer, I (will / would) tell you.
  6. 6) If we had a garden, we (would grow / will grow) our own vegetables.
Show the answers
1) rains 2) pay 3) were 4) gets 5) would 6) would grow

Exercise 3 · Give advice with “If I were you, I'd…”.

  1. 1) My phone is very old. (buy a new one)
  2. 2) I always run out of money at the end of the month. (make a budget)
  3. 3) This jacket is too expensive. (wait for the sales)
  4. 4) My new shoes are too small. (take them back)
  5. 5) I owe my brother a lot of money. (pay him back soon)
Show the answers
1) If I were you, I'd buy a new one. 2) If I were you, I'd make a budget. 3) If I were you, I'd wait for the sales. 4) If I were you, I'd take them back. 5) If I were you, I'd pay him back soon.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) لو كنت مكانك، لاشتريت الأرخص.
  2. 2) لو كان معي مال كافٍ، لسافرت حول العالم.
  3. 3) لا أستطيع تحمّل تكلفة هذا الهاتف.
  4. 4) هل يمكنني استرداد المال؟
Show the answers
1) If I were you, I'd buy the cheaper one. / If I were you, I would buy the cheapest one. 2) If I had enough money, I would travel around the world. / If I had enough money, I'd travel the world. 3) I can't afford this phone. 4) Can I have a refund? / Can I get my money back? / Could I get a refund?

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — “If I won a million pounds…”: write 120–150 words. What would you buy, who would you help, and what wouldn't you change? Finish with one piece of money advice for a friend (If I were you, I'd…). Then ask three people “What would you do if you won a million pounds?”

Example: If I won a million pounds, the first thing I would do is pay back the money I owe my brother. Then I would buy a small flat for my parents in Sheikh Zayed, because they have lived in a rented flat all their lives. I wouldn't buy an expensive car, because I think it's a waste of money in Cairo traffic. If I had more money than I needed, I would help my neighbours' children with their school fees. I would also travel. I'd love to visit Japan and see Tokyo. However, I wouldn't stop working. If I didn't work, I would get bored very quickly. My friend Karim always runs out of money before the end of the month. Karim, if I were you, I'd make a budget, shop around before buying anything and cut down on eating out.
