Your 30 minutes every day

  1. 5 min · Review yesterday's words and sentences.
  2. 8 min · Learn today's 6 words and 2 sentences — say each one aloud 5 times.
  3. 10 min · Listen to today's lesson using the 4-step method.
  4. 5 min · Practise today's exercise, then check the answer key.
  5. 2 min · Speak: say 3 sentences about your day.

6 days a week, and on day 7 just enjoy a story or a video. 30 minutes every day beats 3 hours once a week.

The 4-step listening method

1 · Listen only

Don't read. Try to catch the general idea.

2 · Listen and read

Follow the script or subtitles and underline new words.

3 · Shadow

Pause after each sentence and repeat it with the same sound and speed.

4 · Listen again

No text this time — you will understand much more.

Too fast? On YouTube tap Settings → Playback speed → 0.75 and turn on English subtitles, not Arabic.

Before you start

  • Take a free online English level test today, and again at the end of Week 12 to see your progress.
  • Get a notebook: English on the left, Arabic on the right — one page per week.
  • Use a free flashcard app for the 6 words of each day, 5 minutes every morning.
  • Make English part of your day: change your phone language, talk to yourself in English, and send yourself a voice note every Friday.

The golden rule: don't try to understand every word. Understand the idea, keep listening, and the rest comes with time.
