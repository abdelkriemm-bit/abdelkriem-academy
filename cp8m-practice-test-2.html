Stage 8 Maths — Practice Test 2

A full end-of-Stage-8 maths test in Checkpoint style: two 1-hour papers of 50 marks (Paper 1 no calculator, Paper 2 calculator) set around a family holiday in Alexandria, a garden design, a school canteen survey, taxi fares, a cycling race and tiling patterns — with full worked solutions and a mark scheme.

Before you start

  • This is an end-of-Stage-8 practice test in the style of Cambridge Lower Secondary maths tests. The Cambridge Checkpoint test itself is taken at the end of Stage 9; this test checks the Stage 8 work that Checkpoint builds on.
  • It has two papers, like the Stage 8 Progression Test: Paper 1 — 1 hour, 50 marks, no calculator. Paper 2 — 1 hour, 50 marks, calculator allowed.
  • Equipment: a pen, a pencil, a ruler, a protractor and compasses (geometrical instruments). Tracing paper is optional. Use a calculator in Paper 2 only.
  • Answer all the questions. The marks for each part are shown with the question. For questions worth 2 or 3 marks, show your working — you can get a mark for a correct method even if the final answer is wrong.
  • Do one paper at a time with the timer on, in a quiet place. Write on paper first if you like — you can print the question paper (PDF) below and type your answers in at the end.
  • How marking works here: typed answers (numbers, short answers and multiple choice) are checked automatically. Written answers (explanations, constructions and working) you mark yourself with the mark scheme shown after you finish — be strict, as a real examiner would be.
  • Your score is a practice score to show you what to revise. It is not an official Cambridge result.
قبل ما تبدأ

ده امتحان تدريبي على شكل امتحانات كامبريدج لآخر Stage 8. الورقة الأولى ساعة من غير آلة حاسبة، والورقة التانية ساعة بالآلة الحاسبة، وكل ورقة عليها 50 درجة. اكتب خطوات الحل دايمًا، لأن فيه درجات على الطريقة حتى لو الناتج النهائي غلط. الإجابات اللي بتتكتب بالأرقام بتتصحح لوحدها، أما الشرح والرسم فإنت اللي بتصححهم بنموذج الإجابة. الدرجة دي للتدريب بس، مش نتيجة رسمية من كامبريدج.

After the test
  • Read the worked solution for every question, including the ones you got right — check that your method was efficient and your working was clear.
  • Make a list of the questions you lost marks on and write down why: a wrong method, a careless slip, a missing unit, or not reading the question (e.g. ‘simplest form’, ‘complete turns’, ‘1 decimal place’).
  • Fractions, decimals, percentages and ratio (Paper 1 Q4–Q10, Paper 2 Q1–Q2): revise Fractions: Recurring Decimals, Mixed Numbers and Order of Operations, Ordering and Calculating with Decimals, Percentage Increase, Decrease and Absolute Change and Multiplying and Dividing by 0.1 and 0.01, and Significant Figures.
  • Integers, powers and roots (P1 Q1, Q2, Q6; P2 Q4c, Q7a–b): revise Order of Operations and Calculating with Integers, Types of Number and Index Laws, Squares, Cubes and Roots (Positive and Negative) and Factors, Multiples, Primes, HCF and LCM.
  • Algebra (P1 Q11–Q14; P2 Q3, Q6f, Q7d): revise Algebraic Expressions: Simplifying, Expanding and Factorising, Writing Expressions and Formulae, and Changing the Subject, Equations and Inequalities, Sequences and the nth Term, Functions and Straight-line Graphs (y = mx + c) and Reading and Interpreting Real-life Graphs.
  • Geometry (P1 Q3, Q15–Q19; P2 Q4a–b, Q6, Q7c, Q8): revise Area of Parallelograms and Trapezia, Volume of Prisms and Surface Area, Circumference of a Circle, and Miles and Kilometres, Angles in Triangles and on Parallel Lines, Constructions with Ruler and Compasses, Quadrilaterals and Regular Polygons, 3D Shapes: Euler's Formula and Plans and Elevations, Bearings and Midpoints and Translation, Reflection, Rotation and Enlargement.
  • Statistics and probability (P1 Q20; P2 Q5, Q9, Q10): revise Data Collection and Sampling, Representing Data, Averages, Range and Comparing Distributions and Probability: Complementary and Combined Events.
  • Wait a week, then try the same questions again without looking at the solutions, or move on to another practice test.
