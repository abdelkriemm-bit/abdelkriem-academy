be going to · will / won't · Would you like…? Let's…

«سـ / سوف» لها في الإنجليزية شكلان: going to للخطة المقررة، و will للقرار اللحظي أو الوعد.

be going to + verbwill + verb
A plan you already made: I'm going to visit my uncle on Friday.A decision now, a promise, an offer or a guess: I'll help you. It will rain. · will not = won't

Exercises

Exercise 1 · Use be going to.

  1. 1) I ___ (visit) my uncle on Friday.
  2. 2) She ___ (buy) a new car.
  3. 3) We ___ (not travel) this summer.
  4. 4) ___ they ___ (move) to a new flat?
Show the answers
1) am going to visit · 2) is going to buy · 3) aren't going to travel · 4) Are … going to move

Exercise 2 · will ('ll) or going to?

  1. 1) The phone is ringing. — I ___ answer it!
  2. 2) I ___ study medicine next year. I have a place at university.
  3. 3) It's cold. — I ___ close the window.
  4. 4) We ___ get married in June. We booked the hall.
Show the answers
1) 'll · 2) 'm going to · 3) 'll · 4) 're going to

Exercise 3 · Match each question with the best answer

  1. 1) Would you like a coffee?
  2. 2) Let's go to the park.
  3. 3) Can you come on Friday? (you are busy)
  4. 4) Would you like to come to my wedding?
Show the answers
1) a · 2) b · 3) c · 4) d

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) سأسافر إلى دبي العام القادم (خطة).
  2. 2) سأتصل بك لاحقًا (وعد).
  3. 3) هل تود أن تأتي إلى حفلتي؟
  4. 4) لن أتأخر.
Show the answers
1) I'm going to travel to Dubai next year. · 2) I'll call you later. · 3) Would you like to come to my party? · 4) I won't be late.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak

Write & Speak — Write 6 sentences about your plans for next week, then write a short message inviting a friend somewhere.

Example: Next week I'm going to be busy. On Sunday I'm going to start a new course. On Tuesday I'm going to visit my sister. … Message: Hi Ali! Would you like to watch the match with me on Friday? Let's meet at the café at eight. See you!
