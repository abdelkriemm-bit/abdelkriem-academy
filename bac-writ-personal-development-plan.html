الدرس ده عن إيه؟

A Personal Development Plan (PDP) helps you identify the skills you want to improve and the actions you will take to achieve your goals.

الـ Personal Development Plan (اختصارها PDP) يعني «خطة تطوير نفسك». إنت بتكتب فيها: أنا عايز أحسّن مهارة إيه؟ ليه المهارة دي مهمة لشغلي في المستقبل؟ وهعمل إيه بالظبط علشان أحسّنها؟

في الكتاب الدرس ده في Unit 8: Pathways to Progress، جزء 8.4 The Skills Employers Really Want، صفحة Writing in Action (صفحة 89-90). الجزء ده بيتكلم عن الـ employability skills (المهارات اللي بتخليك تلاقي شغل وتحافظ عليه) زي communication, teamwork, problem-solving, adaptability, leadership. والمطلوب في الكتاب خطة من 150-180 كلمة.

الفكرة ببساطة: «أنا فين؟ عايز أوصل فين؟ وهوصل إزاي؟»

A PDP answers three simple questions: Which skill? Why is it important? How will I develop it?

فكّر فيها زي خطة رجيم أو خطة مذاكرة: مش كفاية تقول «أنا عايز أبقى أحسن». لازم تحدد حاجة واحدة واضحة، وخطوات صغيرة واقعية تعملها كل أسبوع، وتقول المهارة دي هتفيدك إزاي في الشغل.

الفرق بينها وبين الـ blog post عن «My Vocational Dream» إن الـ blog بيحكي عن حلمك، لكن الـ PDP بتركّز على المهارة والخطة: Goal ← Steps ← Future.

الهيكل اللي الكتاب بيقترحه (Suggested Structure)

Sectionوظيفتهاعبارات مفيدة
Introductionقول المهارة اللي عايز تحسّنها (وممكن تذكر الشغلانة اللي بتحلم بيها).I would like to improve… / One skill I need to develop is…
Goalاشرح ليه المهارة مهمة + هدف شخصي واضح.This skill is important because… / Employers value this skill because… / My goal is to…
Action Planاوصف الخطوات اللي هتعملها (خطوة خطوة، بأوقات واضحة).I plan to… / I will practise… / I intend to… / To achieve this goal, I will…
Conclusionاشرح المهارة هتدعم شغلك في المستقبل إزاي.This skill will help me… / In the future, …
اكتبها 4 فقرات

الـ rubric في الكتاب بيقول Organise your writing into clear paragraphs. فالأسهل إنك تعمل كل جزء من الهيكل في فقرة لوحده: 4 فقرات صغيرة. وممكن تحط عنوان فوق الخطة: Personal Development Plan.

عبارات مفيدة (Useful Language)

Stating goals (من الكتاب)

  • I would like to improve…
  • One skill I need to develop is…
  • My goal is to strengthen…
  • By the end of this year, I want to be able to…

Explaining reasons (من الكتاب)

  • This skill is important because…
  • It will help me…
  • Employers value this skill because…
  • Since most jobs involve…, …

Describing actions (من الكتاب)

  • I plan to…
  • I will practise…
  • I intend to…
  • To achieve this goal, I will…
  • I am currently… (حاجة بتعملها دلوقتي)

Connectors (لازم 2 على الأقل)

  • because / since
  • therefore
  • furthermore / in addition
  • first, … then, … finally, …

Future focus

  • This skill will help me succeed as a…
  • In the workplace, I will be able to…
  • In the long term, …
  • I would like to improve my communication skills.بداية بسيطة ومباشرة للـ Introduction: المهارة بالاسم.
  • Employers value teamwork because most projects are done in groups.سبب مرتبط بالشغل + connector (because).
  • My goal is to give a two-minute presentation without reading from my notes.هدف واقعي وواضح تقدر تقيسه (two minutes / without notes).
  • I plan to join the school debate club next term.خطوة عملية بوقت واضح (next term).
  • I am currently practising speaking English with my cousin every weekend.Present continuous (قاعدة الوحدة): حاجة بتعملها الفترة دي.
  • To achieve this goal, I will ask my teacher for feedback after every presentation.خطوة تانية في الـ Action Plan.
  • Therefore, I will be able to explain my ideas clearly to customers.Therefore = علشان كده (نتيجة).
  • In addition, problem-solving will help me fix faults quickly at work.In addition = بالإضافة لكده + Future focus.
Present continuous في الخطة

الوحدة بتدرّس الـ present continuous (am / is / are + ing). استخدمه علشان تقول إنت بتعمل إيه الفترة دي: I am learning to use design software. / I am taking an online course. وبعدها استخدم will / plan to / intend to للخطوات الجاية.

إزاي تكتب الخطة خطوة بخطوة

  1. اختار شغلانة نفسك تشتغلها (مثلًا: solar energy technician, nurse, graphic designer, chef, electrician).
  2. اختار مهارة واحدة أو اتنين من الـ employability skills اللي تحتاجها في الشغلانة دي (communication / teamwork / problem-solving / adaptability / time management).
  3. Introduction: قول المهارة (I would like to improve…) والشغلانة اللي بتحلم بيها.
  4. Goal: اشرح ليه المهارة مهمة (Employers value this skill because…) واكتب هدف واقعي واضح (My goal is to…).
  5. Action Plan: اكتب 3 خطوات عملية بأوقات (every week / next term / by June). استخدم I plan to / I will / I intend to.
  6. Conclusion: المهارة هتساعدك إزاي في الشغل (This skill will help me…).
  7. راجع الـ rubric: connectors اتنين على الأقل؟ فقرات واضحة؟ كلمات عن الشغل والمهارات؟ 150-180 كلمة؟
خلي الهدف واقعي
  • ✗ My goal is to become the best speaker in the world. ← هدف مش واقعي ومش واضح.
  • ✓ My goal is to give a three-minute talk in class by the end of the year. ← هدف واقعي تقدر تقيسه.

نموذج كامل (Model Answer)

Personal Development Plan: Communication and Teamwork172 words · يغطي نقاط الـ Writing Rubric التسعة في الكتاب
Task: Write a personal development plan (150-180 words) explaining which employability skills you would like to improve, why these skills are important for your future career, how you plan to develop them and how they can help you succeed in the workplace.

Personal Development Plan

I would like to become a solar energy technician, and one skill I need to develop for this career is communication. I would also like to improve my teamwork.

These skills are important because technicians work in teams and explain problems to customers every day. Furthermore, employers value workers who can cooperate with colleagues and give clear instructions. My goal is to explain a technical idea confidently in English for three minutes by the end of this school year.

To achieve this goal, I will join the school debate club next term and present at least one topic every month. I am currently watching short videos about solar panels, and I plan to explain each one to my younger brother in simple words. In addition, I intend to volunteer for group projects instead of working alone.

In the future, these skills will help me work safely with other technicians and earn the trust of customers. Therefore, I will not only fix systems but also build good relationships at work.

ليه النموذج ده قوي؟
  • Introduction: المهارة بالاسم من أول سطر (one skill I need to develop is communication) + الشغلانة (solar energy technician). ده الـ Clear Goal.
  • Goal paragraph: سبب مرتبط بالشغل (These skills are important because…) + Employers value… (عبارات الكتاب) + هدف واقعي تقدر تقيسه: three minutes / by the end of this school year.
  • Action Plan: 3 خطوات عملية بأوقات: debate club next term / explain videos to my brother / volunteer for group projects. وفيها عبارات الكتاب: To achieve this goal, I will… / I plan to… / I intend to…
  • Present continuous: I am currently watching… (قاعدة الوحدة) لحاجة بيعملها الفترة دي.
  • Connectors: because, Furthermore, In addition, Therefore (الـ rubric طالب اتنين على الأقل، هنا أربعة).
  • Conclusion = Future focus: المهارات هتساعده إزاي في الشغل (work safely / earn the trust of customers).
  • Vocabulary about careers: technician, employers, colleagues, customers, cooperate, technical (rubric point 8).
  • Paragraphs: عنوان + 4 فقرات واضحة = الأربع أجزاء في الـ Suggested Structure.

أشهر الأخطاء

غلط ← صح
  • ✗ I want to improve myself. (مش واضح أنهي مهارة) ← ✓ I would like to improve my time management.
  • ✗ I will try to be better. ← ✓ I will practise speaking English for 15 minutes every day.
  • ✗ My goal is improve my teamwork. ← ✓ My goal is to improve my teamwork.
  • ✗ I plan join a club. ← ✓ I plan to join a club.
  • ✗ I am practise every day. ← ✓ I practise every day. / I am practising every day.
  • ✗ كتابتها كلها فقرة واحدة طويلة ← ✓ فقرات واضحة (Introduction / Goal / Action Plan / Conclusion).
  • ✗ تنسى تقول المهارة هتفيدك إزاي في الشغل ← ✓ اختم بـ This skill will help me…
  • ✗ practice (فعل) في الإنجليزي البريطاني ← ✓ الفعل practise والاسم practice.

إزاي بييجي في الأسئلة؟

الأسئلة في الكتاب والمراجعات بتيجي بالشكل ده:

1) MCQ عن الـ genre:

A personal development plan mainly helps you … a) describe a holiday b) identify skills and plan actions to improve them c) advertise a product d) report the news

الإجابة: b — ده تعريف الكتاب: identify the skills you want to improve and the actions you will take.

2) سؤال من Reflection Questions (زي صفحة 91):

What employability skill would you like to improve? How do you plan to develop it?

إجابة نموذجية: I would like to improve my problem-solving skills because technicians face new faults every day. I plan to solve one logic puzzle daily and to help my uncle repair household appliances at weekends. — المهارة + السبب + خطتين عمليتين.

3) JRE (زي سؤال 8.4):

Why do employers value soft skills as much as technical skills?

J: Employers value soft skills as much as technical skills because both are needed for success. R: Technical skills help people get jobs, while communication, teamwork and adaptability help them solve problems and work effectively with others. E: For example, the unit explains that technical knowledge can be taught relatively quickly, whereas discipline and teamwork take years to develop.

قائمة المراجعة (من الـ Writing Rubric في الكتاب)

  1. حددت مهارة أو أكتر عايز تحسّنها؟
  2. شرحت ليه المهارات دي مهمة لشغلك في المستقبل؟
  3. كتبت هدف شخصي واضح (My goal is to…)؟
  4. وصفت خطوات محددة هتعملها؟
  5. شرحت المهارة هتساعدك إزاي في المستقبل؟
  6. استخدمت 2 connectors على الأقل (because, since, therefore, furthermore, in addition)؟
  7. قسمت كتابتك فقرات واضحة؟
  8. استخدمت كلمات عن المهارات والشغل (employers, colleagues, career, technician…)؟
  9. راجعت الـ spelling والـ grammar والـ punctuation، والعدد 150-180 كلمة؟

تدريب

أولًا: اختار الإجابة الصحيحة (a / b / c / d)

  1. 1. The ability to adjust to new situations is called … a) leadership b) adaptability c) employability d) discipline

    Show answer
    b — adaptability = التأقلم مع المواقف الجديدة.

  2. 2. 'My goal … my time management.' a) is strengthen b) is to strengthen c) to strengthen d) strengthening is

    Show answer
    b — My goal is to + فعل في المصدر.

  3. 3. I intend … an online course in graphic design. a) take b) taking c) to take d) took

    Show answer
    c — intend + to + المصدر.

  4. 4. At the moment, I … a first-aid course at the youth centre. a) take b) am taking c) took d) takes

    Show answer
    b — At the moment = present continuous (am taking).

  5. 5. Which sentence is the most realistic target? a) I will become a famous engineer next week. b) I will be perfect at everything. c) I will read one article about engineering every week. d) I will never make mistakes.

    Show answer
    c — هدف صغير واضح تقدر تعمله، وده معنى Realistic Targets.

  6. 6. Which section describes the steps you will take? a) Introduction b) Goal c) Action Plan d) Conclusion

    Show answer
    c — Action Plan: Describe the steps you will take to develop it.

  7. 7. Teamwork is important … most workplace projects are done in groups. a) therefore b) because c) however d) although

    Show answer
    b — because بتجيب السبب.

ثانيًا: كمّل بالشكل الصحيح للكلمة اللي بين القوسين

  1. 8. Good communication skills increase a graduate's ……… (employ).

    Show answer
    employability — بعد graduate's محتاجين اسم.

  2. 9. Employees should be ……… because conditions often change. (adapt)

    Show answer
    adaptable — بعد be محتاجين صفة.

  3. 10. Effective ……… among team members helps projects succeed. (collaborate)

    Show answer
    collaboration — بعد Effective محتاجين اسم.

  4. 11. I am ……… (current) practising my presentation skills.

    Show answer
    currently — بنوصف فعل، فمحتاجين ظرف (adverb).

  5. 12. Regular practice builds ……… and helps learners improve. (consistent)

    Show answer
    consistency — بعد builds محتاجين اسم.

ثالثًا: الجملة دي من أنهي جزء؟ (Introduction / Goal / Action Plan / Conclusion)

  1. 13. 'One skill I need to develop is problem-solving.'

    Show answer
    Introduction — بتذكر المهارة.

  2. 14. 'To achieve this goal, I will help my uncle in his workshop every Friday.'

    Show answer
    Action Plan — خطوة عملية.

  3. 15. 'This skill will help me become a reliable electrician.'

    Show answer

  4. 16. 'Employers value this skill because machines often break down unexpectedly.'

    Show answer
    Goal — بتشرح ليه المهارة مهمة.

رابعًا: صحّح الغلط

  1. 17. I plan improving my leadership skills.

    Show answer
    I plan to improve my leadership skills.

  2. 18. I would like improve my teamwork.

    Show answer
    I would like to improve my teamwork.

  3. 19. Employers values workers who are reliable.

    Show answer
    Employers value — الفاعل جمع، فمفيش s.

  4. 20. I am wanting to become a nurse.

خامسًا: اكتب جملة

  1. 21. Write ONE realistic goal for improving your time management.

    Show answer
    مثال: My goal is to finish my homework before 8 p.m. every day for the next two months. — هدف واضح بوقت محدد.

  2. 22. Write TWO action steps using 'I plan to' and 'I intend to'.

    Show answer
    مثال: I plan to use a weekly planner. I intend to switch off my phone while I am studying.

ملخص الدرس في سطرين
  • الـ PDP = خطة بتقول مهارة إيه عايز تحسّنها، ليه، وهتعمل إيه.
  • الهيكل: Introduction ← Goal ← Action Plan ← Conclusion، كل جزء في فقرة.
  • عبارات أساسية: I would like to improve… / This skill is important because… / My goal is to… / I plan to… / To achieve this goal, I will…
  • خلي الهدف واقعي وواضح بوقت، واستخدم 2 connectors على الأقل.
  • الطول 150-180 كلمة، واختم بإن المهارة هتساعدك في الشغل إزاي.
