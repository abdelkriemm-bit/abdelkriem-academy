Grammar: used to · subject and object questions

used to + verb = past habits or states that are not true now (I used to live in Tanta. = I don't live there now.)

Negative: didn't use to · Question: Did you use to …?

Subject questions: when Who or What is the subject, don't use do / does / did: Who called you? (NOT Who did call you?) · What happened?

نعبّر بالعربية عن عادات الماضي بكلمة «كان»: «كنت ألعب»، فيترجمها المتعلم I was play ✗ أو I was playing (وهذا حدث مستمر في لحظة معينة وليس عادة). للعادة التي انتهت استخدم used to + فعل مجرد. وانتبه: بعد did و didn't نكتب use to بدون d. وفي الأسئلة عن الفاعل (مَن فعل؟ ماذا حدث؟) لا نستخدم did: Who broke the window? وليس Who did break the window?

PositiveNegativeQuestion
I used to walk to school.I didn't use to walk to school.Did you use to walk to school?
She used to be shy.She didn't use to like tea.Did she use to work here?
Object question (with do / did)Subject question (no do / did)
Who did you call? → I called Omar.Who called you? → Omar called me.
What did Mona say? → She said hello.What happened? → The car broke down.
Which bus do you take? → I take the 12.Which bus goes to Giza? → The 12 goes to Giza.

used to is only for the past. For present habits, use the present simple with usually: I usually walk to work (NOT I use to walk).

Don't use used to with a number of times: I visited Luxor three times (NOT I used to visit Luxor three times).

Exercises

Exercise 1 · Complete with used to, didn't use to or Did … use to.

  1. 1) I ___ live in Mansoura, but now I live in Cairo.
  2. 2) My father ___ smoke, but he gave up ten years ago.
  3. 3) ___ you ___ have long hair?
  4. 4) We ___ have a TV when I was young. (negative)
  5. 5) There ___ be a cinema in our street.
  6. 6) She ___ like vegetables, but now she loves them.
Show the answers
1) used to 2) used to 3) Did … use to 4) didn't use to 5) used to 6) didn't use to

Exercise 2 · Rewrite the sentences with used to.

  1. 1) When I was a child, I played in the street every day.
  2. 2) My grandmother made bread at home in the past.
  3. 3) In the past, people didn't have mobile phones.
  4. 4) Was Omar shy when he was a child?
  5. 5) Life was simpler in those days.
Show the answers
1) When I was a child, I used to play in the street every day. 2) My grandmother used to make bread at home (in the past). 3) In the past, people didn't use to have mobile phones. 4) Did Omar use to be shy when he was a child? 5) Life used to be simpler in those days.

Exercise 3 · Write the questions.

  1. 1) Mona broke the cup. (Who …?)
  2. 2) Mona broke the cup. (What … Mona …?)
  3. 3) Something happened at work. (What …?)
  4. 4) Omar called me last night. (Who called …?)
  5. 5) I called Omar last night. (Who did …?)
  6. 6) The 12 bus goes to Giza. (Which bus …?)
Show the answers
1) Who broke the cup? 2) What did Mona break? 3) What happened at work? 4) Who called you last night? 5) Who did you call last night? 6) Which bus goes to Giza?

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) كنت أذهب إلى المدرسة سيرًا على الأقدام.
  2. 2) لم يكن أبي يشرب القهوة.
  3. 3) مَن أعطاك هذا الكتاب؟
  4. 4) ماذا حدث بعد ذلك؟
Show the answers
1) I used to walk to school. (or: I used to go to school on foot.) 2) My father didn't use to drink coffee. 3) Who gave you this book? 4) What happened after that?

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak

Write & Speak — “Then and now”: write 80–100 words about how your life (or your town) has changed. Use used to and didn't use to for the past, and the present simple for now. Then interview an older person in your family: ask five questions like “Did you use to …?” and “What happened when …?”

Example: When I was a child, my life was very different. My family used to live in a small flat in Shubra. I didn't use to have a phone or a computer. I used to play football in the street and watch cartoons in the afternoon. My mother used to give me five pounds pocket money every week, and I felt rich! Nowadays I live in 6th of October City and I work in a bank. I no longer play in the street; I go to the gym instead. Life is easier now, but sometimes I miss the old days.
