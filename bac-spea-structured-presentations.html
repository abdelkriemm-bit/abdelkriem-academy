Structured Presentations (3–5 minutes)

الدرس ده عن إيه؟

A structured presentation is a short talk with a clear beginning, middle and end. You state a clear position, give arguments with evidence, mention the other side, and finish with a recommendation and a strong conclusion.

الدرس ده عن إنك تقف قدام الفصل وتتكلم لوحدك من ٣ لـ ٥ دقايق عن موضوع، بشكل منظم ومقنع. متخافش: الـ presentation مش «خطبة»، هي شوية أجزاء صغيرة ورا بعض، وكل جزء ليه جمل جاهزة.

في الكتاب: Unit 4 (Summative Assessment) بيطلب *a structured (3 – 5) minute individual presentation with a clear position, two arguments with evidence, and a recommendation*. وفي وحدات تانية فيه presentations أقصر (2 – 3 minutes) زي *Heritage Day* في Unit 3، و*Environment Week* في Unit 6، و*Career and Passion Day* في Unit 7، و*Well-being Week* في Unit 9، و*Sustainability Week* في Unit 10. وفي Unit 2 فيه Peer Assessment Checklist زميلك بيقيّمك بيها. الدرس ده هيعلمك تعمل الاتنين.

الفكرة ببساطة: الساندوتش

Think of a presentation as a sandwich: the opening and the conclusion are the bread, and your arguments are the filling. Tell the audience what you will say, say it, then tell them what you said.

فكّر في الـ presentation كأنه ساندوتش: العيش من فوق = المقدمة، الحشو = النقط بتاعتك بالأدلة، العيش من تحت = الخاتمة. وفيه قاعدة قديمة سهلة:

  1. قولهم هتقول إيه (المقدمة: الموضوع + رأيك + هتتكلم عن إيه).
  2. قولهولهم (النقط واحدة واحدة بالـ JRE).
  3. قولهم قلت إيه (الخاتمة: لخّص وكرر رأيك واقترح حاجة).

هيكل الـ Presentation (٣ – ٥ دقايق)

ده الهيكل اللي بيغطي كل طلبات الكتاب في Unit 4: موقف واضح + حجتين بدليل + اعتراف بالرأي التاني + توصية. الوقت تقريبي علشان تتمرن بيه:

الجزءتعمل فيه إيه؟وقت تقريبيجمل جاهزة
١. Openingسلّم على الجمهور، ابدأ بـ hook (سؤال أو رقم مفاجئ)، قول الموضوع ورأيك، وقول هتتكلم عن إيه٣٠ – ٤٥ ثانيةGood morning, everyone. / Have you ever wondered…? / Today I'm going to talk about… / I believe that…
٢. Argument 1أول حجة بالـ JRE (رأي ← سبب ← دليل ← ربط)دقيقة تقريبًاFirstly,… / My first point is… / For example,… / This shows that…
٣. Argument 2تاني حجة بالـ JREدقيقة تقريبًاSecondly,… / Moving on to my second point,… / According to…
٤. Opposing viewاعترف بالرأي التاني ورد عليه٣٠ – ٤٥ ثانيةSome people believe that… However,… / Admittedly,…
٥. Recommendationاقترح حل أو نصيحة عملية محددة٣٠ ثانيةI would recommend that… / We should… / One practical step is…
٦. Conclusionلخّص، كرر رأيك بكلام مختلف، جملة أخيرة قوية، واشكر الجمهور٢٠ – ٣٠ ثانيةTo sum up,… / So, remember… / Thank you for listening. Are there any questions?
إزاي تبقى الـ presentation ٣ دقايق أو ٥؟

الفرق بين ٣ و٥ دقايق مش إنك تتكلم أسرع، الفرق في عدد الأمثلة. للـ ٣ دقايق: دليل واحد لكل حجة. للـ ٥ دقايق: دليلين لكل حجة + رقم أو قصة قصيرة في المقدمة. والطالب العادي بيقول حوالي ١٢٠ – ١٣٠ كلمة في الدقيقة، يعني الـ ٣ دقايق حوالي ٤٠٠ كلمة.

الشكل التاني: Describe – Explain – Analyse – Reflect

Many presentations in the book ("guest speaker" tasks of 2–3 minutes) follow four verbs: Describe what it is, Explain why it matters, Analyse a deeper idea, Reflect / Evaluate (including a different view).

في Units 3, 6, 7, 9, 10 الكتاب بيدّيك المهمة على شكل ٤ أفعال، وتحتها Build your ideas (جمل جاهزة). كل فعل = جزء من كلامك:

الفعل في السؤاليعني إيه؟جملة من نوع جمل الكتاب
Describeاوصف الحاجة: إيه، فين، إمتىThe site is located in… / This hobby started in…
Explainاشرح ليه مهمة أو مين بيعتمد عليهاThis place represents… / This resource is shared by…
Analyseحلل: إيه اللي بيحصل لو…؟ إيه الفكرة الأعمق؟If it disappears, then… / One important turning point was…
Reflect / Evaluateرأيك + الرأي التانيFor me, this shows… / Although some people believe…, I think…

كلمات الربط والانتقال (Signposting)

Signposting language tells the audience where you are in your talk. It makes you easy to follow, like signs on a road.

الجمهور مش شايف ورقتك، فلازم تقولهم إنت فين: «دي أول نقطة… ننتقل للتانية… وفي الآخر…». دي اسمها signposting (زي اليافطات على الطريق). الـ Peer Checklist في Unit 2 بيسأل: *Did the speaker use linking words (firstly, however, in conclusion…)?*

المقدمة (Opening)

  • Good morning, everyone. My name is …
  • Have you ever wondered why …?
  • Did you know that …?
  • Today, I'm going to talk about …
  • In this presentation, I will argue that …
  • I'll start with …, then I'll move on to …, and finally …

الانتقال بين النقط

  • Firstly, … / My first point is …
  • Moving on to my second point, …
  • Another important reason is …
  • Let's now look at …
  • Finally, …

الدليل

  • For example, … / For instance, …
  • According to the text, …
  • Research has shown that …
  • A good example in Egypt is …

الرأي التاني

  • Some people believe that … However, …
  • Admittedly, … Nevertheless, …
  • Although some people think …, I believe …

التوصية (Recommendation)

  • I would recommend that …
  • My recommendation is to …
  • We should all …
  • One practical step we can take is …

الخاتمة

  • To sum up, … / In conclusion, …
  • So, the main message is …
  • Remember: …
  • Thank you for listening.
  • Are there any questions?

الإلقاء نفسه (Delivery)

How you speak matters as much as what you say. Use notes, not a script. Look at your audience, speak slowly and clearly, and pause after important points.

الكتاب في Unit 4 بيقول: *You may use brief notes (maximum 5). Do not read from a full script. Speak naturally.* وفي Unit 9: *I speak clearly and confidently.* يعني:

Presentation نموذجية كاملة (من ٣ لـ ٤ دقايق)

المهمة من نوع Unit 4 Summative. اقرأها بصوت عالي وشوف هي بتاخد وقت قد إيه. الكلام بسيط وجمله قصيرة علشان يتقال بسهولة:

Model presentation: clear position, two arguments, opposing view, recommendation418 words
Task: Which topic from Unit 4 (brand culture, food waste, sale events or digital consumption) is the biggest challenge for young people today? Deliver a structured 3–5 minute presentation with a clear position, two arguments with evidence and a recommendation.

Good morning, everyone. Have you ever bought something on sale, taken it home and then never used it? I think most of us have. Today, I'm going to argue that sale events, like Black Friday, are the biggest consumption challenge for young people today. I'll start with two arguments, then I'll look at the other side, and finally I'll give you a recommendation.

Firstly, sale events encourage impulse buying. This means buying something quickly, without thinking, only because it looks cheap. Shops use words like "Only today!" and "Limited stock!" to make us feel that we must act now. For example, last November my cousin bought three pairs of trainers in one night because they were half price. Two pairs are still in their boxes. This shows that sales do not always save money. Sometimes they make us spend money we did not plan to spend.

Moving on to my second point, sale events increase waste. When people buy more than they need, many products end up thrown away or forgotten. According to the unit, unnecessary purchases lead to more waste, and every product we throw away also wastes the water, energy and materials used to make it. In addition, young people see these offers on social media all day, so the pressure on them is even stronger than on adults. This proves that the problem is not only about money. It is also about our planet.

Admittedly, sale events have some benefits. Some people believe they help families buy things they really need, such as a winter coat or a laptop for school, at a lower price. I understand this point, and it is true for careful shoppers. However, most sale advertising does not target careful shoppers. It targets our emotions. So even though sales can be useful, the harm they cause is still greater.

So, what can we do? My recommendation is simple: before any sale, write a list of what you really need and set a budget. If an item is not on your list, wait twenty-four hours before buying it. If you still need it the next day, it is probably a good decision.

To sum up, sale events push young people to buy without thinking, and they create waste that harms our planet. That is why I believe they are the biggest consumption challenge for our generation. So, the next time you see "50% off", remember: the best deal is sometimes not buying at all. Thank you for listening. Are there any questions?

ليه النموذج ده قوي؟
  • Opening: سلام + hook (سؤال للجمهور) + موقف واضح (*I'm going to argue that…*) + خريطة الكلام (*I'll start with…, then…, and finally…*).
  • Argument 1 بالـ JRE: *Firstly* ← شرح كلمة صعبة (*impulse buying. This means…*) ← مثال حقيقي (ابن العم والجزم) ← ربط (*This shows that…*).
  • Argument 2 بالـ JRE: *Moving on to my second point* ← دليل من الوحدة (*According to the unit…*) ← فكرة زيادة (*In addition*) ← ربط (*This proves that…*).
  • Opposing view: *Admittedly* + *Some people believe* + رد بـ *However* + *even though* ← نفس اللي الكتاب طالبه في Black Friday: اعترف بفايدة وبعدين وضح إن الضرر أكبر.
  • Recommendation: نصيحة عملية ومحددة (قايمة + ميزانية + استنى ٢٤ ساعة)، مش كلام عام زي *We should be careful*.
  • Conclusion: *To sum up* + تلخيص الحجتين + تكرار الرأي + جملة أخيرة الناس تفتكرها + شكر وأسئلة.
  • الطول حوالي ٤٢٠ كلمة = من ٣ لـ ٤ دقايق تقريبًا بسرعة كلام عادية، ولو زودت مثال لكل حجة توصل لـ ٥ دقايق.
أشهر الأخطاء
  • ✗ تبدأ بـ *My topic is food waste.* وبس ← ✓ ابدأ بـ hook وقول رأيك وخريطة الكلام.
  • ✗ ٥ نقط ضعيفة من غير أدلة ← ✓ حجتين قويتين كل واحدة بالـ JRE.
  • ✗ تنسى الرأي التاني ← ✓ جزء صغير: *Admittedly… However…*
  • ✗ توصية عامة: *We must save the planet.* ← ✓ توصية محددة: *Write a shopping list before every sale.*
  • ✗ تخلص فجأة: *That's it.* ← ✓ *To sum up,… Thank you for listening.*
  • ✗ تقرأ من الورقة ← ✓ ٥ كلمات مفتاحية بالكتير على كارت.
خطة تحضير في ١٠ دقايق
  • دقيقتين: اختار موقفك واكتبه في جملة واحدة.
  • ٣ دقايق: اكتب حجتين، وجنب كل واحدة دليل واحد (من النص أو من مصر أو من حياتك).
  • دقيقة: اكتب الرأي التاني في كلمتين + ردك عليه.
  • دقيقة: اكتب توصية عملية واحدة.
  • ٣ دقايق: اتمرن بصوت عالي مرة كاملة بالساعة.

إزاي بييجي في الأسئلة؟

الأسئلة في الكتاب بتيجي بالأشكال دي:

١) Presentation Task بـ Describe / Explain / Analyse / Reflect (Unit 3 مثلًا):

As a young guide at your school's Heritage Day, prepare a 2–3 minute presentation about a heritage site and explain why it is important.

الحل باختصار: *Describe:* The Karnak Temple is located in Luxor. It was built over many centuries. ← *Explain:* It represents the power and beliefs of ancient Egypt. ← *Analyse:* One important idea it preserves is how Egyptians worked together on huge projects. ← *Reflect:* Although some people believe old sites are only for tourists, I think they teach us who we are.

٢) Presentation فيها نقط جاهزة وإنت تختار (Unit 4 – Black Friday): *Choose your two strongest points… Acknowledge the other side… End with a strong conclusion.* ← نفس هيكل النموذج فوق بالظبط.

٣) Peer Assessment / Self-Reflection: زميلك بيسأل: هل قدّم الموضوع بوضوح؟ هل فيه دليل؟ هل فيه رأي بسبب؟ هل استخدم linking words؟ هل صوته واضح؟ ← خلي الأسئلة دي checklist تراجع بيها نفسك قبل ما تقدّم.

تدريب

A) Which part of a presentation is each sentence from? (Opening / Argument / Opposing view / Recommendation / Conclusion)

  1. 1. "Did you know that teenagers check their phones dozens of times a day?"

    Show answer
    Opening — hook برقم أو سؤال.

  2. 2. "Admittedly, screens can help students learn new skills."

    Show answer
    Opposing view — اعتراف بالرأي التاني.

  3. 3. "My recommendation is to switch off notifications during homework."

    Show answer
    Recommendation — نصيحة محددة.

  4. 4. "Moving on to my second point, screens affect our sleep."

    Show answer
    Argument — انتقال لنقطة تانية.

  5. 5. "So, remember: you control your phone, not the other way round. Thank you for listening."

    Show answer
    Conclusion — جملة أخيرة + شكر.

  6. 6. "I'll start with the effects on sleep, then I'll talk about concentration, and finally I'll suggest a solution."

    Show answer
    Opening — خريطة الكلام (preview).

B) Choose the correct answer from a, b, c or d.

  1. 7. The book says you may use brief notes. What is the best type of notes? a. the whole talk written out b. five key words or short phrases c. no notes and no plan d. a copy of the textbook page

    Show answer
    b — الكتاب بيقول *maximum 5* ومن غير full script.

  2. 8. Which is the best hook? a. My topic is fast fashion. b. I will talk now. c. How many times have you worn the clothes you bought last month? d. Fast fashion.

    Show answer
    c — سؤال بيخلي الجمهور يفكر.

  3. 9. A 3–5 minute presentation in Unit 4 must include: a. a clear position, two arguments with evidence and a recommendation b. only a story c. a list of ten facts d. a poem

    Show answer
    a — ده بالظبط اللي الكتاب كاتبه.

  4. 10. "______, I'd like to look at the other side of the argument." a. Thank you for listening b. Now c. Good morning d. To sum up

    Show answer
    b — Now بتنقلك لجزء جديد. (To sum up للخاتمة بس)

  5. 11. Which recommendation is the most effective? a. We should do something. b. Things must change. c. Each class could run a monthly swap event for books and uniforms. d. People are bad.

    Show answer
    c — محددة وعملية (مين؟ إيه؟ إمتى؟).

  6. 12. You forget your next point in the middle of your talk. What should you do? a. say "I forgot" and sit down b. look at your note card, pause, and continue c. start again from the beginning d. laugh and stop

    Show answer
    b — ده سبب وجود الكارت.

C) Improve the weak parts.

  1. 13. Weak opening: "Hi. Climate. I will talk."

    Show answer
    Good morning, everyone. Have you noticed how much hotter our summers have become? Today, I'm going to talk about climate change and argue that young people can lead real change.

  2. 14. Weak argument: "Sport is good. Very good."

    Show answer
    Firstly, sport improves mental health. This is because exercise reduces stress. For example, after joining a football team, many students feel calmer before exams. This shows that sport supports well-being.

  3. 15. Weak conclusion: "That's all."

    Show answer
    To sum up, small daily habits protect our health. So, remember: ten minutes of exercise is better than none. Thank you for listening.

  4. 16. Weak recommendation: "We must save water."

    Show answer
    My recommendation is that every family checks its taps once a week and fixes any leak immediately.

D) Presentation practice: اعمل خطة سريعة واتكلم ٣ دقايق. (أفكار نموذجية للخطة)

  1. 17. (Unit 1) What is the best way for teenagers to look after their well-being?

    Show answer
    Position: balance is the key. Arg 1: enough sleep (screens off before bed). Arg 2: physical activity (a short daily walk). Other side: some say well-being is only mental, but body and mind are connected. Recommendation: a weekly balance plan.

  2. 18. (Unit 2) What is the best way for Egyptian teenagers to balance screen time with offline activities?

    Show answer
    Position: set clear limits. Arg 1: family time (meals without phones). Arg 2: clubs and sports at youth centres. Other side: screens are needed for study, but study apps are different from scrolling. Recommendation: a "no phone after 10 p.m." rule.

  3. 19. (Unit 3) A heritage site in Egypt you think everyone should visit.

    Show answer
    Describe: the Grand Egyptian Museum near the Pyramids. Explain: it preserves our collective memory. Analyse: technology helps visitors understand history. Reflect: some prefer digital tours, but the real visit is unforgettable.

  4. 20. (Unit 5) How can your school reduce its carbon footprint?

    Show answer
    Position: small school actions matter. Arg 1: recycling and less paper. Arg 2: saving electricity (lights off, fans instead of air conditioning when possible). Other side: some say only governments can act, but individual actions add up. Recommendation: a student eco-team.

  5. 21. (Unit 6) A resource shared by different countries or species.

    Show answer
    Describe: the River Nile. Explain: many countries, farmers and wildlife depend on it. Analyse: if one part is polluted, everyone downstream suffers. Reflect: although each country has its own needs, cooperation is the only fair solution.

  6. 22. (Unit 7) How a hobby can grow into something more.

    Show answer
    Describe: a hobby like photography started on a phone. Explain: practice and small decisions helped it grow. Analyse: a turning point, like a first paid job. Reflect: although some believe money kills passion, balance can keep the enjoyment.

  7. 23. (Unit 8) A vocational field that is important for Egypt.

    Show answer
    Position: tourism and hospitality. Arg 1: it is one of the most important sectors of the economy. Arg 2: it creates many jobs for young people. Other side: some think university is the only path, but practical skills are in demand. Recommendation: more training in hotels during school.

  8. 24. (Unit 10) The hidden cost of a product you use every day.

    Show answer
    Describe: a cheap T-shirt or a fast delivery. Explain: what happens behind the scenes (water, factories, transport). Analyse: why the cost stays invisible (no information on the label). Recommendation: buy fewer, better things and repair them.
ملخص الدرس في سطرين
  • الهيكل: Opening (hook + رأي + خريطة) ← حجتين بالـ JRE ← الرأي التاني ← توصية محددة ← خاتمة وشكر.
  • في مهام الـ guest speaker امشي ورا الأفعال: Describe ← Explain ← Analyse ← Reflect.
  • استخدم signposting: *Firstly / Moving on to / Finally / To sum up*.
  • ملاحظات قصيرة (٥ بالكتير) مش ورقة تقرأها، وبُص للجمهور واتكلم ببطء.
  • ٣ دقايق ≈ حوالي ٤٠٠ كلمة: اتمرن بصوت عالي بالساعة.
