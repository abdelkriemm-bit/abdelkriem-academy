تانية بكالوريا · Language · Unit 1

Hedging and Neutral Language

إزاي تكتب كلام دقيق وهادي من غير مبالغة باستخدام may / might / some / suggest، وتحوّل الجملة المنحازة لجملة محايدة.

تانية بكالوريا English

الدرس ده عن إيه؟

Hedging means using careful words (such as may, might, some, suggest) to show that a claim is not 100% certain. Neutral language is balanced, objective and free from bias.

الدرس ده عن إزاي تكتب كلام عاقل ومحترم ودقيق، من غير مبالغة. بدل ما تقول «السوشيال ميديا بتدمّر كل المراهقين»، تقول «الاستخدام الزيادة ممكن يأثر على بعض المراهقين». الجملة التانية أهدى، وأصدق، وأصعب حد يعترض عليها.

في الكتاب ده في Unit 1: هدف الكتابة في الوحدة بيقول «use neutral and hedging language in evidence-based sentences». هتلاقي جدول Original (Biased) → Revised (Neutral + Hedging) وتمرين Rewrite the Bias (صفحة 17)، والجدول اللي فيه Hedging words وNeutral language (صفحة 19)، وكمان Conditional Structures (Hedging) (صفحة 34).

الفكرة ببساطة: «ممكن… غالبًا… بعض»

Academic writing needs precision, caution and clarity. A good writer does not claim more than the evidence shows.

تخيل دكتور بيقولك: «الدوا ده هيشفيك 100%». وتاني بيقولك: «الدوا ده غالبًا هيساعدك، والدراسات بتقول إنه نفع مع أغلب الحالات». تصدّق مين أكتر؟ التاني، لأنه دقيق ومش بيبالغ.

ده بالظبط الـ hedging: إنك «تحوّط» كلامك. الكلمة جاية من hedge يعني «سور شجر» بيحمي الجنينة. إنت كمان بتحمي جملتك من إن حد يقول «لأ، ده مش صحيح دايمًا».

وليه ده مهم في الـ JRE؟ لأن الـ claim بتاعك مبني على evidence (دراسة، أرقام، مثال). والدراسة عادة بتقول «بعض الطلبة» أو «ممكن»، فمينفعش إنت تكتب «كل الطلبة» و«أكيد». لازم كلامك يكون على قد الدليل.

أدوات الـ hedging (مع القواعد بتاعتها)

There are six main ways to hedge a claim. Notice the grammar after each one.

ToolWordsGrammar (القاعدة)Example
1. Modal verbsmay, might, could, canmodal + base verb (من غير s ولا ing)Screens may affect sleep.
2. Hedging verbsseem, appear, tend+ to + verbTeenagers tend to compare themselves to others.
3. Reporting verbssuggest, indicate, implyResearch + verb + that + clauseResearch indicates that exercise reduces stress.
4. Adverbspossibly, probably, perhaps, often, usually, generallyقبل الفعل الأساسي أو بعد is / are (و perhaps غالبًا في أول الجملة)Walking is probably the easiest exercise.
5. Quantifierssome, many, most, certain, a number of, several+ اسم جمعSome teenagers might accept online information without checking it.
6. PhrasesIt is possible that… / There is evidence that… / … is likely to… / In some cases,…+ clause (و is likely to + base verb)It is possible that filters lower self-esteem.

وفيه أداة سابعة من الكتاب نفسه: الـ conditionals. الكتاب بيقول إن الـ conditionals بتساعد الكاتب يعبّر عن التأكد أو الاحتمال أو الحذر:

  • If schools offer counselling, stress levels may fall.First conditional + may = احتمال حقيقي بس مش مؤكد. (الشكل ده من الكتاب صفحة 34)
  • If more schools offered counselling, students would most likely feel better supported.Second conditional + most likely = افتراض وفيه حذر.
  • If the study had included more schools, the findings would have been more conclusive.Third conditional = بنقول إن الدراسة كان فيها نقص، وده نوع من الحذر في الحكم على الدليل.

سلّم التأكد: من «أكيد» لـ «يمكن»

Hedging words show different levels of certainty. Choose the level that matches your evidence.

مستوى التأكدWordsExample
100% (absolute — منحاز غالبًا)always, will definitely, all, neverSocial media always harms teenagers.
عاليusually, most, probably, is likely toSocial media is likely to affect most heavy users.
متوسطoften, many, can, tends toSocial media can affect many teenagers.
قليلmay, might, could, some, possiblySocial media might affect some teenagers.

يعني الـ hedging مش معناه إنك تبقى ضعيف أو مش عارف. معناه إنك تختار الكلمة المظبوطة على قد الدليل. لو الدراسة قالت 80% يبقى most، لو قالت 20% يبقى some.

يعني إيه Neutral language بالظبط؟

Neutral language is balanced, objective and free from bias. It reports what the evidence shows, without emotion or exaggeration.

Neutral language بيعمل إيه؟BiasedNeutral
بيشيل الكلمات المطلقةAll teenagers are addicted.Many teenagers spend several hours a day online.
بيشيل الصفات العاطفيةa horrible, toxic appa popular app that some users find stressful
بيبدّل الأفعال القوية بأفعال هاديةPhones destroy concentration.Phones may reduce concentration.
بيذكر المصدرEveryone knows that…A 2024 school survey found that…
بيعرض الناحيتينGaming is a waste of time.Gaming can be relaxing, but too much of it may affect sleep.
بيشيل الـ CAPITALS و !!!There are NO excuses!!!There are several reasons to …
أفعال قوية ← أفعال هادية (احفظ الجدول ده)
  • destroy / ruin ← affect / harm / reduce
  • control ← influence
  • prove ← suggest / indicate
  • everyone knows ← many people believe / research suggests
  • is the worst thing ← presents certain risks
  • should be banned ← may need guidelines / limits

إزاي تحوّل جملة biased لجملة neutral؟ (4 خطوات)

Follow these steps to rewrite a biased sentence.

  1. دوّر على الكلمة المطلقة (all, every, always, never) وبدّلها بـ quantifier: some / many / certain.
  2. دوّر على الفعل القوي أو الصفة العاطفية (destroys, horrible) وبدّلها بكلمة هادية: affects / may harm.
  3. ضيف modal أو hedging verb: may / might / could / tends to.
  4. ضيف مصدر لو ينفع: Research indicates that… / Some studies suggest that…

تعالى نطبّق على جملة واحدة خطوة بخطوة:

  • Video games make all children violent.الجملة الأصلية: all = مطلق، make violent = حكم قوي.
  • Video games make some children violent.خطوة 1: all ← some.
  • Video games could influence some children's behaviour.خطوة 2 و 3: make violent ← could influence behaviour.
  • Some research suggests that certain types of video games could influence some children's behaviour.خطوة 4: ضفنا المصدر. دي جملة neutral + hedging كاملة.

أمثلة من مواضيع الوحدة (Biased ← Neutral)

  • ✗ Junk food destroys teenagers' health. → ✓ Eating junk food regularly may harm some teenagers' health.destroys ← may harm، teenagers ← some teenagers.
  • ✗ Everyone sleeps badly after using a phone at night. → ✓ Research indicates that using a phone late at night can reduce sleep quality.everyone ← research indicates + can.
  • ✗ Influencers always lie. → ✓ Some influencers appear to show only the best moments of their lives.always lie ← some + appear to + معلومة أدق (highlight reel).
  • ✗ Mindfulness solves every student's stress. → ✓ Mindfulness seems to help many students manage stress.solves every ← seems to help many.
  • ✗ Teenagers never read books now. → ✓ Teenagers tend to read fewer printed books than in the past.never ← tend to + fewer.
  • ✗ Exercise is the only answer to anxiety. → ✓ Regular exercise is likely to be one useful way to reduce anxiety.the only ← one useful way + likely to.
  • ✗ Online friends are fake. → ✓ Online friendships may be less deep than face-to-face ones in some cases.fake ← may be less deep + in some cases.
  • ✗ This study proves screens cause depression. → ✓ This study suggests a link between heavy screen use and low mood.proves cause ← suggests a link. خلي بالك: «link» أهدى من «cause».

استخدمه في الكتابة: فقرة JRE فيها hedging

Model — evidence-based paragraph (JRE + hedging)133 words
Task: Write a short analytical paragraph answering this question: Does daily screen time affect teenagers' sleep? Use neutral and hedging language.

Daily screen time appears to have a negative effect on many teenagers' sleep. One reason is that bright screens may delay the body's natural signal to rest, so students who scroll late at night tend to fall asleep later. Research indicates that teenagers who use their phones in bed often report shorter and lighter sleep. For example, in a survey at our school, most students who kept their phones next to their beds said they felt tired in the morning. However, not all screen use is harmful, since some students use calm apps that might help them relax. Therefore, it is possible that the time and type of screen use matter more than the screen itself. If schools taught students to switch off an hour before bed, sleep quality would most likely improve.

ليه النموذج ده قوي؟
  • الجملة الأولى (Justify) = الـ claim، وفيها hedging: appears to + many. مش «all teenagers».
  • Reason: may delay + tend to = سبب منطقي من غير مبالغة.
  • Evidence: Research indicates that + مثال من survey + most (مش «everyone»).
  • التوازن (neutral): However, not all screen use is harmful… = عرضنا الناحية التانية.
  • الخاتمة: second conditional + would most likely = حذر في التوقع (زي مثال الكتاب صفحة 34).
  • مفيش ولا كلمة absolute ولا intensifier ولا علامة تعجب: ده الـ neutral tone.
أشهر الأخطاء (غلط ← صح)
  • ✗ Screens may affects sleep. ← ✓ Screens may affect sleep. (بعد الـ modal فعل من غير s)
  • ✗ Teenagers tend comparing themselves. ← ✓ Teenagers tend to compare themselves. (tend / seem / appear + to)
  • ✗ Research suggests to exercise helps. ← ✓ Research suggests that exercise helps. (suggest + that + clause)
  • ✗ It may possibly perhaps help. ← ✓ It may help. (كلمة hedging واحدة كفاية، متكترش)
  • ✗ Water may boil at 100°C. ← ✓ Water boils at 100°C. (الحقيقة العلمية الثابتة مش محتاجة hedging)
  • ✗ «Some research implies» ومعاها «always» في نفس الجملة. ← ✓ متخلطش hedging مع absolute: Some research implies that social media could influence behaviour.
إزاي تجاوب بسرعة في الامتحان
  • سؤال «Rewrite using neutral and hedging language»: طبّق الـ 4 خطوات (مطلق ← some، قوي ← هادي، ضيف may، ضيف المصدر).
  • سؤال «Which sentence is the most neutral?»: دوّر على الجملة اللي فيها some / may / suggest ومفيهاش all / never / !!.
  • سؤال «What is the effect of hedging?»: اكتب It shows caution / uncertainty and makes the claim more accurate and fair.
  • متحذفش الفكرة الأصلية: الجملة الجديدة لازم يبقى ليها نفس المعنى بس أهدى.

إزاي بييجي في الأسئلة؟

الأسئلة في الكتاب والمراجعات بتيجي بالشكل ده:

1) Rewrite the biased sentence using neutral and hedging language.

  • "Smartphones completely ruin students' ability to concentrate."الجملة الأصلية: completely = intensifier بيخلي الكلام مطلق، ruin = فعل قوي، students = كل الطلبة.
  • Smartphones may reduce some students' ability to concentrate.إجابة كاملة: ruin ← may reduce، students ← some students، وشلنا completely.

2) MCQ: choose the hedging / neutral option.

  1. Which sentence uses hedging? a. Exercise always improves mood. b. Exercise might improve mood for many people. c. Exercise is extremely good for everyone. d. Do you really want to feel sad?

    Show answer
    b — might + many = hedging. (a) absolute، (c) intensifier + absolute، (d) rhetorical question.

3) Explain the difference between two sentences.

  • A: Fast food destroys children's health. / B: Some studies suggest that eating fast food frequently may harm some children's health.الإجابة: Sentence A is biased because it uses a strong verb (destroys) and generalises about all children. Sentence B is neutral because it gives a source (some studies suggest), uses hedging (may) and limits the claim (frequently, some children).

تدريب

A. Choose the correct answer from a, b, c or d.

  1. 1. Hedging words are used to a. make ideas stronger b. show uncertainty or caution c. make the reader angry d. state facts with full confidence

    Show answer
    b — ده تعريف الكتاب للـ hedging words.

  2. 2. Which word is a hedging word? a. never b. extremely c. could d. every

    Show answer
    c. could — modal بيبيّن الاحتمال.

  3. 3. Teenagers ______ to spend more time online at weekends. a. tend b. may c. are tending d. tends

    Show answer
    a. tend — tend + to. (teenagers جمع، فمن غير s)

  4. 4. Research ______ that regular breakfast improves concentration. a. suggests b. suggest to c. is suggest d. suggesting

    Show answer
    a. suggests — research مفرد + suggests that.

  5. 5. Late-night gaming may ______ students' marks. a. affects b. affecting c. affect d. to affect

    Show answer
    c. affect — modal + base verb.

  6. 6. Which sentence is the most neutral? a. Fast food is poison. b. Nobody should ever eat fast food. c. Eating fast food often may increase health risks. d. Isn't fast food terrible?

    Show answer
    c — often + may + increase risks = neutral. الباقي مبالغة أو مطلق أو سؤال بلاغي.

  7. 7. If students slept eight hours, they ______ concentrate better. a. will most likely b. would most likely c. would have d. had

    Show answer
    b — second conditional (slept) + would most likely = حذر في التوقع.

  8. 8. Neutral language is a. emotional and exciting b. balanced, objective and free from bias c. full of absolute words d. written in capital letters

    Show answer
    b — تعريف الكتاب.

B. Rewrite these biased sentences using neutral and hedging language.

  1. 9. Students waste all their free time playing video games.

    Show answer
    Some students may spend a large part of their free time playing video games. — all ← a large part of، waste ← may spend، students ← some students.

  2. 10. Diet apps are completely useless.

    Show answer
    Diet apps may not be useful for everyone. / Some diet apps appear to have limited benefits. — شلنا completely و useless.

  3. 11. Every student feels stressed before exams.

    Show answer
    Many students tend to feel stressed before exams. — every ← many + tend to.

  4. 12. This study proves that music makes people happy.

    Show answer
    This study suggests that music can improve some people's mood. — proves ← suggests، makes ← can improve.

  5. 13. Online shopping ruins young people's money habits.

    Show answer
    Online shopping could encourage some young people to spend more than they plan. — ruins ← could encourage.

  6. 14. Online learning is the worst thing that has ever happened to schools.

    Show answer
    Online learning presents certain challenges for schools, although it can also have benefits. — شلنا the worst … ever، وعرضنا الناحيتين.

C. Complete each sentence with a suitable hedging word or phrase.

  1. 15. ______ students find it easier to study in the morning.

    Show answer
    Some / Many / Most — quantifier.

  2. 16. Drinking enough water ______ help you concentrate in class.

    Show answer
    may / might / can / could — modal + base verb (help).

  3. 17. Students who exercise ______ to sleep more deeply.

    Show answer
    tend / seem / appear — + to sleep.

  4. 18. ______ that reading before bed improves sleep quality.

    Show answer
    Research indicates / There is evidence / It is possible — + that + clause.

D. Read the text and answer.

A recent survey of 300 students at a Cairo school found that 62% of those who used their phones after midnight felt tired during the first lesson. However, students who used their phones for studying reported fewer problems than those who used them for games.

  1. 19. Which claim matches the evidence better? (a) Phones make all students tired. (b) Using phones after midnight may make many students feel tired. Explain.

    Show answer
    (b) — الدليل بيقول 62% مش 100%، فـ many و may على قد الدليل. (a) فيها all = مبالغة.

  2. 20. Write ONE neutral sentence about the difference between studying and gaming on phones.

    Show answer
    مثال: The survey suggests that the type of phone use may matter, as students who studied appeared to have fewer problems than those who played games.

  3. 21. Why is it NOT correct to write "Studying on phones is never harmful"?

    Show answer
    Because the text only says these students reported fewer problems, not no problems. «never» is an absolute word and goes beyond the evidence. — fewer ≠ never.

  4. 22. Write a JRE answer (3 sentences) to: Should students stop using phones after midnight? Use at least TWO hedging words.

    Show answer
    مثال: J: Students should probably avoid using phones after midnight. R: Late phone use may reduce the amount of sleep they get. E: A school survey found that 62% of students who used their phones after midnight felt tired in the first lesson. — probably + may، والدليل رقم من النص.
ملخص الدرس في سطرين
  • Hedging = كلام فيه حذر: may, might, could / tend to, seem to / suggest that / some, many / possibly, likely.
  • Neutral language = متوازن وموضوعي: من غير كلمات مطلقة، ولا صفات عاطفية، ولا !!!، ومع ذكر المصدر.
  • القاعدة: modal + base verb / tend-seem-appear + to / suggest-indicate + that.
  • تحويل biased ← neutral: مطلق ← some، فعل قوي ← هادي، ضيف may، ضيف المصدر.
  • في الـ JRE خلي الـ claim على قد الدليل، وممكن تستخدم الـ conditionals للحذر (would most likely).
