Grammar: can / can't · Wh- questions (where, when, why, how…)

Question order: Wh-word + do/does + person + verb → Where do you work? What does she do? With be: Wh-word + is/are + person → Where is your office?

لا تقل Where you work? — السؤال في الإنجليزية يحتاج do أو does: Where do you work? · وبعد can يأتي الفعل بدون s: He can speak.

PositiveNegativeQuestion
I / He / They can swim.She can't drive.Can you cook? — Yes, I can.

Exercises

Exercise 1 — Make sentences with can / can't / Can…? (✓ = can, ✗ = can't, ? = question).

  1. 1) she / speak French ✓
  2. 2) I / drive ✗
  3. 3) he / fix cars ✓
  4. 4) they / swim ✗
  5. 5) you / use a computer ?
Show the answers
1) She can speak French. · 2) I can't drive. · 3) He can fix cars. · 4) They can't swim. · 5) Can you use a computer?

Exercise 2 — Choose the question word.

  1. 1) ___ do you live? — In Giza.
  2. 2) ___ is your boss? — Mr Samir.
  3. 3) ___ do you start work? — At 8.
  4. 4) ___ do you study English? — Because I need it for my job.
  5. 5) ___ is the bus ticket? — Ten pounds.
  6. 6) ___ does the meeting take? — One hour.
Show the answers
1) Where · 2) Who · 3) What time / When · 4) Why · 5) How much · 6) How long

Exercise 3 — Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) Where you work?
  2. 2) He can speaks English.
  3. 3) What does she does?
  4. 4) I am work in a bank.
Show the answers
1) Where do you work? · 2) He can speak English. · 3) What does she do? · 4) I work in a bank.

Exercise 4 — Translate into English.

  1. 1) أخي مهندس في شركة كبيرة.
  2. 2) هل تستطيع أن تصلح هذا؟
  3. 3) متى يبدأ الاجتماع؟
  4. 4) لا أستطيع قيادة السيارة.
Show the answers
1) My brother is an engineer in a big company. · 2) Can you fix this? · 3) When / What time does the meeting start? · 4) I can't drive.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak

Write & Speak — Write 6 sentences about your job (or your dream job): what you do, where, and what you can / can't do. Example: I am a sales manager. I work for a phone company in Maadi. I start work at nine. I can use a computer and I can speak to customers. I can't speak English very well yet. My colleagues are friendly and helpful.
