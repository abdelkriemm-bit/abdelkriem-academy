Grammar: Present simple: he / she + s · does / doesn't · always, usually, never…

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

Add -es after ch, sh, s, x, o (watches, washes, goes, does). Consonant + y → ies (study → studies), but vowel + y → s (play → plays). Frequency words go before the main verb (I usually walk) but after am / is / are (He is never late).

بعد doesn't أو Does يرجع الفعل لأصله بدون s: He doesn't work (وليس doesn't works).

he / she / itNegativeQuestion
He works. She watches. He goes. She studies.He doesn't work.Does he work?

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 — Write the correct form of the verb.

  1. 1) She (work) ___ in a hospital.
  2. 2) My father (watch) ___ the news every night.
  3. 3) He (go) ___ to the gym twice a week.
  4. 4) Ali (study) ___ English every day.
  5. 5) She (not like) ___ coffee.
  6. 6) ___ your brother (drive) ___ ?
Show the answers
1) works · 2) watches · 3) goes · 4) studies · 5) doesn't like · 6) Does … drive

Exercise 2 — Put the word in brackets in the right place.

  1. 1) I walk in the morning. (usually)
  2. 2) He is late. (never)
  3. 3) We visit our grandmother. (often)
  4. 4) She drinks tea. (always)
Show the answers
1) I usually walk in the morning. · 2) He is never late. · 3) We often visit our grandmother. · 4) She always drinks tea.

Exercise 3 — Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) He work in an office.
  2. 2) She don't like football.
  3. 3) Does he plays tennis?
  4. 4) My mother cook every day.
Show the answers
1) He works in an office. · 2) She doesn't like football. · 3) Does he play tennis? · 4) My mother cooks every day.

Exercise 4 — Translate into English.

  1. 1) أختي تذهب إلى العمل بالمترو.
  2. 2) هو لا يدخّن أبدًا.
  3. 3) هل يطبخ زوجك؟
  4. 4) أحب السباحة في الصيف.
Show the answers
1) My sister goes to work by metro. · 2) He never smokes. · 3) Does your husband cook? · 4) I like swimming in the summer.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — Write 6 sentences about the day of a friend or family member (use he / she). Example: My friend Tamer is an engineer. He gets up at six. He always drinks coffee in the morning. He drives to work. He doesn't work on Friday. He plays football twice a week.
