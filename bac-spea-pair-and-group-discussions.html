Pair and Group Discussions

إزاي تشارك في مناقشة مع زميلك أو مجموعة: تقول رأيك، توافق وتعترض بأدب، تسأل غيرك، وتوصلوا لاتفاق.

تانية بكالوريا English

الدرس ده عن إيه؟

In a pair or group discussion you share ideas with other students. It is not a speech: you listen, respond, agree or disagree politely, ask others for their views and, often, reach an agreement together.

الدرس ده عن الكلام مع زمايلك في مجموعة صغيرة (اتنين، أو ٤). هنا مش مهم بس إنك تقول رأيك، المهم كمان إنك تسمع وترد على اللي اتقال، توافق أو تعترض بأدب، تسأل زميلك الساكت عن رأيه، وفي الآخر توصلوا لـ قرار مع بعض.

في الكتاب ده موجود في كل الوحدات تقريبًا تحت *Discussion in groups of four* أو *Small-Group Discussion*، والأسئلة مكتوب جنبها أفعال زي Explain / Analyse / Evaluate. وفيه أنشطة زي ترتيب الأسباب والوصول لاتفاق (Unit 4 – Food waste: *Can you reach an agreement?*)، واجتماع لجنة تخطيط (Unit 5 – Eco-Leaders Programme: *Discuss as a group and agree on a final two-day plan*)، وGlobal Skills Roundtable في Unit 8.

أضعف طالب في المناقشة هو اللي بيقول رأيه كأنه لوحده ومش سامع حد. أقوى طالب بيبدأ كلامه بإنه يربطه باللي اتقال: *As Omar said,… / I agree with Mariam, and I'd add that…* ده بيبين إنك سامع وفاهم.

أفعال الأسئلة: Explain / Analyse / Evaluate

The book labels discussion questions with thinking verbs. Each verb asks for a different kind of answer.

الكتاب بيكتب جنب كل سؤال مناقشة فعل. الفعل ده بيقولك نوع الإجابة المطلوبة:

الفعليعني إيه؟مثال سؤالتبدأ إزاي
Explainاشرح إيه اللي بيحصل أو إزايHow are young people taking action to protect the environment?Young people are… For example,…
Analyseحلل: ليه؟ إيه الأسباب؟ إيه العلاقة؟Why are young people becoming more active in the fight against climate change?One reason is that… This is connected to…
Evaluateقيّم: احكم ورأيك بدليل (لأي درجة؟ مفيد ولا لأ؟)Do you think young people can make a real difference? Why or why not?I believe that, to some extent,… because…

المهارات اللي محتاجها في المناقشة + جملها

Good group speakers use functions: they give opinions, agree, disagree politely, add ideas, ask others, check understanding, interrupt politely, keep the group on topic and summarise.

تقول رأيك

  • I think … / In my opinion, …
  • From my perspective, …
  • I'd say that …
  • I think we should include …

توافق

  • I agree with you because …
  • That's a good point.
  • Exactly! And I'd add that …
  • I completely agree with Omar.

تعترض بأدب

  • I see your point, but …
  • I'm not sure I agree. I think …
  • I understand what you mean. However, …
  • I agree up to a point, but …
  • I disagree because …

تضيف على كلام غيرك

  • As Mariam said, …
  • Building on what Ali said, …
  • Also, … / In addition, …
  • Another thing is that …

تسأل زميلك

  • What do you think, Sara?
  • Do you agree, Youssef?
  • Why do you think that?
  • Can you give an example?

تتأكد إنك فاهم

  • Do you mean that …?
  • So, are you saying that …?
  • Could you explain that again, please?

تقاطع بأدب

  • Sorry to interrupt, but …
  • Can I just add something?
  • Excuse me, may I say something here?

ترجّع المجموعة للموضوع

  • Let's get back to the question.
  • That's interesting, but we need to focus on …
  • We have two minutes left.

تقترح وتوصل لاتفاق

  • What if we started with …?
  • How about …?
  • Shall we agree that …?
  • So, we all agree that …
  • A fair compromise might be …

تلخّص (للتقديم للفصل)

  • To sum up, our group thinks that …
  • We decided that … because …
  • Most of us agreed that …, but one member felt …

أدوار المجموعة

في المجموعة اللي من ٤، نظموا نفسكم بأدوار. ده بيخلي المناقشة منظمة وكل واحد يتكلم:

الدورEnglishبيعمل إيه؟جملة مفيدة
القائدleaderيقرأ السؤال ويتأكد إن كل واحد اتكلمLet's start. What do you think, Ali?
ضابط الوقتtimekeeperيراقب الساعةWe have one minute left.
الكاتبnote-takerيكتب الأفكار المهمة باختصارCan I write that down as our first idea?
المتحدثreporter / spokespersonيقدّم رأي المجموعة للفصل في الآخرOur group decided that…

أمثلة من موضوعات الوحدات

  • I see your point, Nour, but family support might be more effective than doing activities alone.بقول لزميلتي إني فاهم كلامها وبعدين باعترض بأدب (Unit 1 – stress).
  • Building on what Omar said, notifications also damage our concentration when we study.بضيف على كلام زميلي (Unit 2 – screen time).
  • What do you think, Youssef? Have you ever trusted a viral post about history?بسأل زميل ساكت عن رأيه (Unit 3 – social media and history).
  • I'd rank overbuying first because it happens every week, not only at festivals.بشارك في ترتيب الأسباب (Unit 4 – food waste).
  • What if we started the workshop with a beach clean-up and then moved to the debate?باقترح فكرة في اجتماع اللجنة (Unit 5 – Eco-Leaders).
  • So, are you saying that fishermen should be part of the decision?بتأكد إني فاهم (Unit 6 – shared resources).
  • Sorry to interrupt, but I think we're moving away from the question.بقاطع بأدب وبرجع المجموعة للموضوع.
  • So, we all agree that vocational schools need more links with real companies.بلخّص الاتفاق في الآخر (Unit 8 – vocational education).

مناقشة نموذجية (Model Discussion)

دي مناقشة لمجموعة من ٤ على سؤال Evaluate من Unit 5. اقرأها وشوف كل واحد بيعمل إيه. الأسماء ملهاش علاقة بأي حد حقيقي:

Model group discussion (4 students, about 3 minutes)268 words
Task: Discussion in groups of four. Evaluate: Do you think young people can make a real difference in solving the climate crisis? Why or why not?

Ali (leader): OK, let's start. The question is whether young people can make a real difference in the climate crisis. Mariam, what do you think?

Mariam: I think they can, to a large extent. Young people are very active online, so they can spread awareness quickly. For example, in the text, a girl from Luxor started a Nile clean-up with five friends, and it grew to over two hundred volunteers in a year.

Omar: That's a good point, and I'd add that young people are also inventing solutions. The engineering student built a low-cost solar water heater. That's real change, not just talking.

Nour: I see your point, Omar, but I'm not sure I agree completely. A clean-up or one heater is great, but the climate crisis is huge. Big changes, like solar parks, need governments and companies.

Ali: So, are you saying that young people can't really make a difference, Nour?

Nour: Not exactly. I mean they can make a difference, but they can't solve the problem alone.

Mariam: I agree with Nour up to a point. However, young people can also push governments to act. When thousands of students speak up, leaders listen.

Ali: We have one minute left. Shall we agree on a final answer?

Omar: How about this: young people can make a real difference by raising awareness and starting local projects, but they need support from governments to make large-scale change.

Nour: Yes, I agree with that.

Ali: Great. So, to sum up, our group thinks young people can make a real difference, especially locally, but the biggest changes need everyone working together.

ليه النموذج ده قوي؟
  • Ali (القائد): بدأ بإعادة السؤال، وسأل زميلة بالاسم، وسأل سؤال توضيح (*So, are you saying…?*)، وانتبه للوقت، ولخّص في الآخر.
  • Mariam: رأي بدرجة (*to a large extent*) + سبب + دليل من نص الوحدة = JRE صغيرة جوه المناقشة.
  • Omar: وافق وضاف فكرة جديدة (*That's a good point, and I'd add that…*) بدل ما يكرر نفس الكلام.
  • Nour: اعترضت بأدب (*I see your point, but I'm not sure I agree completely*)، ولما اتسألت وضّحت قصدها (*Not exactly. I mean…*).
  • Mariam تاني: *I agree with Nour up to a point. However,…* = اعتراف + رد.
  • الوصول لاتفاق: *Shall we agree…? / How about this:…* ← ده المطلوب في أنشطة الكتاب (*agree on a final plan / reach an agreement*).
  • كل دور قصير (جملة لـ ٣ جمل)، وكل واحد بيرد على اللي قبله: ده سر المناقشة الناجحة.
أشهر الأخطاء
  • ✗ تقول رأيك وتسكت ومتسمعش حد ← ✓ ابدأ بربط كلامك باللي اتقال: *As Ali said,…*
  • ✗ *You are wrong.* / *No, this is wrong.* ← ✓ *I see your point, but…*
  • ✗ واحد بيتكلم طول الوقت ← ✓ القائد يسأل الساكت: *What do you think, Sara?*
  • ✗ تتكلم عربي مع المجموعة وتترجم في الآخر ← ✓ المناقشة كلها بالإنجليزي حتى لو جملك بسيطة.
  • ✗ تتكلموا كلكم في وقت واحد ← ✓ *Sorry to interrupt, but…* واستنى دورك.
  • ✗ تخلصوا من غير قرار ← ✓ *So, we all agree that…*
لو إنت خجول أو الإنجليزي بتاعك ضعيف
  • احفظ ٣ جمل بس: *I agree with … because…* / *I see your point, but…* / *What do you think?*
  • اكتب فكرة واحدة ومثال واحد قبل المناقشة.
  • اتكلم بدري في المناقشة: كل ما تستنى أكتر كل ما التوتر يزيد.
  • سؤال زميلك (*Can you give an example?*) مشاركة كاملة ومحسوبة لك.

إزاي بييجي في الأسئلة؟

الأنشطة في الكتاب بتيجي بالأشكال دي:

١) أسئلة Explain / Analyse / Evaluate لمجموعة من ٤ ← كل واحد يجاوب سؤال وبعدين الباقي يعلقوا.

٢) Rank and agree (Unit 4):

Rank the causes of food waste from most serious to least serious: overbuying, poor storage, festivals, misunderstanding labels. Compare your ranking with another group. Can you reach an agreement?

الحل: *I'd put overbuying first because it happens every week. — I see your point, but I think misunderstanding labels is more serious because people throw away food that is still safe. — How about putting overbuying first and labels second? — OK, we agree.* ← لاحظ: رأي + سبب + اعتراض مؤدب + اقتراح + اتفاق.

٣) Planning meeting (Unit 5): *Each person presents their ideas… Discuss as a group and agree on a final plan… Present your group's programme using claim + reason + evidence.* ← استخدم جمل الكتاب: *I think we should include… / What if we started with…? / I agree / disagree because…*

تدريب

A) Choose the correct answer from a, b, c or d.

  1. 1. You want to disagree politely. You say: a. That's stupid. b. I see your point, but I think the opposite is true. c. No! d. You don't understand anything.

    Show answer
    b — اعتراف إنك سامع + اعتراض مؤدب.

  2. 2. A group member is quiet. The leader says: a. Be quiet. b. Let's finish. c. What do you think, Hana? d. I'm the best speaker.

    Show answer
    c — بنسأل الساكت عن رأيه.

  3. 3. You didn't understand your partner. You say: a. Do you mean that schools should ban phones completely? b. Whatever. c. I agree. d. Next question.

    Show answer
    a — سؤال للتأكد (checking understanding).

  4. 4. The group is talking about football instead of the question. You say: a. I love football too! b. Let's get back to the question. c. Goodbye. d. Football is boring.

    Show answer
    b — بترجع المجموعة للموضوع.

  5. 5. "Evaluate" in a discussion question means: a. describe what happened b. give your judgement with reasons and evidence c. copy the text d. count the words

    Show answer
    b — Evaluate = احكم وقول رأيك بدليل.

  6. 6. Which sentence adds to a partner's idea? a. I disagree. b. Building on what Omar said, recycling also saves energy. c. Can you repeat? d. Time is up.

    Show answer
    b — Building on what … said = بضيف على كلامه.

B) Complete the discussion with a suitable phrase.

  1. 7. A: I think brands influence teenagers more than parents do. B: ______, because teenagers see adverts all day on social media.

    Show answer
    I agree with you / That's a good point — موافقة + سبب.

  2. 8. A: Sport is the only way to reduce stress. B: ______, but reading and art can help too.

    Show answer
    I see your point / I agree up to a point — اعتراض جزئي.

  3. 9. A: We should start the eco-workshop with a lecture. B: ______ we started with a practical activity, like planting trees?

    Show answer
    What if we started with… — اقتراح (من جمل الكتاب في Unit 5).

  4. 10. A: Hobbies are a waste of time for students. B: ______? Can you give an example?

    Show answer
    Why do you think that? — بتطلب سبب ودليل.

  5. 11. Leader: We have one minute left. ______ on a final rule?

    Show answer
    Shall we agree on a final rule? — بداية الوصول لاتفاق.

  6. 12. Reporter: ______, our group thinks that free returns should be limited to faulty items.

    Show answer
    To sum up / We decided that — تلخيص للفصل.

C) What would you say in each situation?

  1. 13. You want to say something while your partner is still talking.

    Show answer
    *Sorry to interrupt, but can I just add something?*

  2. 14. Your partner gives an opinion with no evidence.

    Show answer
    *That's interesting. Can you give an example?*

  3. 15. The group has two different rankings for the causes of food waste.

    Show answer
    *How about putting overbuying first, because we all mentioned it, and then discussing the others?*

  4. 16. You agree with one part of your partner's idea but not all of it.

    Show answer
    *I agree with you up to a point. It's true that…, but…*

  5. 17. You are the reporter and the group did not fully agree.

    Show answer
    *Most of us agreed that…, but one member felt that… because…*

D) Discussion practice: ناقش مع زمايلك في ٣ دقايق. (أفكار نموذجية تبدأ بيها)

  1. 18. (Unit 1 – Analyse) How does the writer's purpose affect the language choices they make?

    Show answer
    A writer who wants to sell uses emotional words and absolute language. A writer who wants to inform uses facts, numbers and neutral, careful language like *may* and *suggests*.

  2. 19. (Unit 1 – Evaluate) To what extent is it fair to hold individuals responsible for their own poor health choices?

    Show answer
    Only to some extent: people choose what they eat, but access to safe parks, money and education also matters. Example: gym fees can be too expensive for some families.

  3. 20. (Unit 2) Which leisure activity is best for teenagers' well-being: sport, creative arts or gaming?

    Show answer
    Sport for the body and stress. Arts for expressing feelings and flexibility of thinking. Gaming only with time limits. Possible agreement: a mix is best.

  4. 21. (Unit 1 – Analyse) Can the same information be presented differently depending on the audience? Give an example.

    Show answer
    Yes: a museum guide for children uses stories and pictures, while a history documentary uses dates and experts. The facts should stay the same, but the language changes.

  5. 22. (Unit 4) Rank: overbuying, poor storage, festivals, misunderstanding labels. Agree on the most serious.

    Show answer
    Overbuying happens weekly. Labels: people throw away safe food because of the date. Possible agreement: overbuying first, labels second, because both happen at home every week.

  6. 23. (Unit 6) Have you ever seen someone take action to protect an animal, place or cause? What did they do?

    Show answer
    A neighbour feeding street cats and taking them to a vet. A school group cleaning the beach in Alexandria. Explain what they did and why it mattered.

  7. 24. (Unit 8) Global Skills Roundtable: choose a vocational field and explain its importance.

    Show answer
    Cybersecurity: students are learning to protect networks. It is important because more services are moving online. Countries should cooperate because cyberattacks cross borders. (The book asks for two present continuous sentences and one idiom, e.g. *better safe than sorry*.)

  8. 25. (Unit 9) What can schools do to help new students feel that they belong?

    Show answer
    A buddy system, welcome activities, clubs. Evidence: a new student who joins a club makes friends faster. Possible agreement: every new student gets a buddy for the first month.
ملخص الدرس في سطرين
  • المناقشة = تسمع وترد: اربط كلامك باللي قبلك (*As … said / Building on …*).
  • اعترض بأدب: *I see your point, but… / I agree up to a point, but…*
  • اسأل غيرك (*What do you think?*)، واتأكد إنك فاهم (*Do you mean…?*).
  • اعرف الفعل: Explain اشرح، Analyse حلل الأسباب، Evaluate احكم بدليل.
  • اقفلوا بـ اتفاق وتلخيص: *Shall we agree…? / To sum up, our group thinks…*
