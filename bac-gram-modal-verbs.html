Modal Verbs (could, should, may, must)

إزاي تستخدم can / could / should / may / must علشان تقدّم الحقائق بحذر وتقيّم الأدلة وتدّي نصايح وتوصيات.

البكالوريا المصرية · Egyptian Baccalaureate › تانية بكالوريا English

الدرس ده عن إيه؟

Modal verbs (can, could, should, ought to, may, might, must) are special helping verbs. We use them to present facts carefully, to evaluate evidence and to make recommendations.

الدرس ده عن أفعال صغيرة بتيجي قبل الفعل الأساسي وبتغيّر «طعم» الجملة: هل الحاجة ممكنة؟ ولا لازم؟ ولا أنا بنصحك بيها؟ ولا أنا مش متأكد؟

في الكتاب الدرس ده في Unit 5: The Greenhouse Effect، جزء Grammar Focus: Modal verbs to present facts carefully and evaluate evidence (صفحة 154-155)، ومرتبط بالـ podcast عن الطاقة المتجددة، وبعدها بتستخدمها في كتابة الـ Informational Leaflet. الكتاب بيقول: استخدم الـ modals علشان متكتبش كل حاجة كأنها حقيقة مطلقة (Avoid stating everything as absolute fact).

الفكرة ببساطة: «درجة التأكيد»

Modals show how sure we are, or how strong our advice is. Compare: *Solar energy* solves *the problem* (100% fact) and *Solar energy* may solve *the problem* (possible, not certain).

تخيّل إن عندك «عدّاد ثقة». لما تقول «الطاقة الشمسية هتحل المشكلة» إنت متأكد 100%. لكن العلماء والكتّاب المحترمين بيقولوا «الطاقة الشمسية ممكن تحل المشكلة» لأن المستقبل مش مؤكد. ده اسمه hedging (يعني: إنك تخفف كلامك وتتكلم بحذر علشان تبقى دقيق).

  • Battery storage solves the problem of intermittency.كأنها حقيقة مؤكدة 100%. ده كلام قوي زيادة لو لسه فيه بحث.
  • Battery storage may solve the problem of intermittency.ممكن تحلها، بس مش أكيد. ده كلام حذر ودقيق (hedge). intermittency = إن الطاقة مش بتيجي طول الوقت، زي الشمس بالليل.

القواعد الذهبية للـ Modals

Modals never change their form: no -s, no -ed, no -ing. After a modal we use the base verb (infinitive without *to*). To make the negative add not. To make a question move the modal before the subject.

القاعدةصح ✓غلط ✗
بعد الـ modal الفعل مصدر من غير toSolar energy could power cities.could to power / could powers / could powering
الـ modal مبياخدش s مع he / she / itThe government must act.The government musts act.
النفي = not بعد الـ modalWe should not (shouldn't) waste energy.We don't should waste energy.
السؤال = الـ modal قبل الفاعلCan wind farms power a whole region?Do wind farms can power…?
ought to هي الوحيدة اللي فيها toSchools ought to recycle.Schools ought recycle.

كل Modal ومعناه (جدول الكتاب + شرح زيادة)

The book groups modals by function: possibility or potential, recommendations, uncertainty, and strong necessity.

الـ Modalالوظيفةمعناها بالعربيمثال (طاقة ومناخ)
canقدرة أو إمكانية مؤكدة (ability / capacity)يقدر / يستطيعWind farms can generate electricity for entire regions.
couldإمكانية في المستقبل (potential)، أو قدرة في الماضيممكن / ممكن يقدرSolar energy could power entire cities by 2050.
should / ought toنصيحة أو توصية (recommendation)المفروض / ينبغيHouseholds should switch to renewable energy.
mayاحتمال، كلام حذر (hedge)يمكن / من الجايزBattery technology may solve intermittency.
mightاحتمال أضعف من mayممكن (بس احتمال ضعيف)Some projects might fail without investment.
mustضرورة قوية أو حاجة مستعجلة (necessity / urgency)لازم / يجبGovernments must invest in clean energy now.

رتّبهم كده من الأضعف للأقوى في الاحتمال: might ← may / could ← can ← (فعل عادي = حقيقة). وفي الإلزام: should / ought to (نصيحة) ← must (لازم).

أمثلة من موضوعات الوحدة

  • Egypt can produce huge amounts of solar power because it is sunny most of the year.مصر تقدر تنتج طاقة شمسية كتير. can = قدرة مؤكدة.
  • Electric buses could reduce air pollution in Cairo.الأتوبيسات الكهربا ممكن تقلل تلوث الهوا في القاهرة. إمكانية في المستقبل.
  • Experts believe electric cars may become the global standard within a decade.الخبراء شايفين إن العربيات الكهربا يمكن تبقى هي الأساس. مش أكيد.
  • Rising seas might affect some villages in the Delta.ارتفاع البحر ممكن يأثر على قرى في الدلتا. احتمال أضعف.
  • Schools should consider installing solar panels on their roofs.المدارس المفروض تفكر تركّب ألواح شمسية. نصيحة.
  • Young people ought to turn off lights when they leave a room.الشباب ينبغي يطفوا النور. نفس معنى should.
  • We must act now to protect future generations.لازم نتحرك دلوقتي. ضرورة ومستعجل.
  • Factories must not dump waste into the Nile.ممنوع المصانع ترمي مخلفات في النيل. must not = ممنوع.
  • Solar panels cannot produce electricity at night, so we need batteries to store energy.الألواح الشمسية مش بتقدر تنتج كهربا بالليل، علشان كده محتاجين بطاريات تخزّن الطاقة.
  • Should governments ban plastic bags?سؤال: هل المفروض الحكومات تمنع الأكياس البلاستيك؟ should قبل الفاعل.

فروق مهمة لازم تعرفها

Some pairs look similar but mean different things: must not vs do not have to, can vs could, may vs must (for deduction).

الفرقالمعنىمثال
must not (mustn't)ممنوعYou mustn't burn rubbish in the street.
don't have toمش لازم (براحتك)You don't have to buy new solar panels. You can rent them.
must (التزام من المتكلم نفسه)لازم (أنا شايف كده)We must save water.
have toلازم (قانون أو ظرف من برّه)Factories have to follow environmental laws.
must (استنتاج)أكيد (متأكد من دليل)The air is grey. There must be a lot of pollution.
can't (استنتاج)مستحيل يكونThat can't be true. Solar power is getting cheaper, not more expensive.

ولو عايز تتكلم عن الماضي بنفس الأفكار، بنستخدم modal + have + التصريف التالت:

  • We should have planted more trees years ago.كان المفروض نزرع شجر أكتر من سنين (ومعملناش). ندم على الماضي.
  • The floods could have been worse.الفيضانات كان ممكن تبقى أسوأ (بس محصلش).
  • The ice may have melted because of the heat wave.الجليد يمكن يكون داب بسبب موجة الحر. احتمال عن الماضي.

إزاي تستخدمها في الـ Leaflet والـ Argument

In a leaflet, use should / can for friendly advice and must for urgent calls to action. In an argument, use may / could to hedge claims and evaluate evidence carefully.

الكتاب عايزك تستخدم الـ modals في حاجتين:

  • الـ Leaflet: نصيحة ودودة (You should… / You can start small…) وفي الـ call to action ممكن must للتأكيد.
  • تقييم الأدلة والـ JRE: بدل ما تقول «solar is the best» قول «solar may be the most important source at the moment» علشان تبقى دقيق ومتوازن.

The sun shines on Egypt almost every day. Why not use it? Solar panels can turn sunlight into electricity for your home. Extra energy may even be sold back to the grid. You should talk to your parents or your school about rooftop panels. If you are not ready for that, you could start small with a solar charger. The climate crisis is your generation's challenge, so we must act today!

لاحظ: can = قدرة مؤكدة، may = احتمال، should = نصيحة، could = اقتراح خيار، must = حاجة مستعجلة.

أشهر الأخطاء (غلط ← صح)
  • ✗ Solar energy could to power cities. ← ✓ Solar energy could power cities.
  • ✗ The government musts act. ← ✓ The government must act.
  • ✗ Wind farms can generates electricity. ← ✓ Wind farms can generate electricity.
  • ✗ We don't should waste water. ← ✓ We shouldn't waste water.
  • ✗ Do we can stop climate change? ← ✓ Can we stop climate change?
  • ✗ You mustn't buy new panels (وإنت قصدك «مش لازم»). ← ✓ You don't have to buy new panels.
  • ✗ Schools ought install panels. ← ✓ Schools ought to install panels.
إزاي تجاوب بسرعة في الامتحان
  • الكتاب بيحط المعنى بين قوسين: (ability) = can، (possibility) = may / could / might، (advice/recommendation) = should، (necessity) = must.
  • لو الجملة فيها «although this is not certain» أو «but more research is needed» يبقى احتمال: may / might / could.
  • لو فيها now / urgently / immediately غالبًا must.
  • بعد أي modal: فعل مصدر. شوف الاختيار اللي فيه s أو ing أو to وشيله.

إزاي بييجي في الأسئلة؟

الأسئلة في الكتاب والمراجعات بتيجي بالأشكال دي:

1) Complete with the correct modal verb. (زي تمرين الكتاب، والمعنى بين قوسين)

Planting trees in cities ______ (possibility) lower summer temperatures, although results differ from place to place.

الإجابة: may / could. ليه؟ المعنى احتمال، وكمان الجملة قالت «although results differ» يعني مش أكيد.

2) Only one sentence in each group is grammatically correct. Choose the correct answer.

a. Governments must investing in clean energy.

b. Governments must to invest in clean energy.

c. Governments must invest in clean energy.

d. Governments musts invest in clean energy.

الإجابة: c. بعد must مصدر من غير to ومن غير ing، وmust مبتاخدش s.

3) Rewrite the sentence more carefully using a modal verb. (hedging)

Solar energy is the answer to all of Egypt's energy problems.

الإجابة: Solar energy could / may be an important answer to many of Egypt's energy problems. ليه؟ الجملة الأصلية مبالغة (كل المشاكل). الـ modal خلاها حذرة ودقيقة زي ما الكتاب بيطلب.

تدريب

A. Complete with the correct modal verb (the meaning is given in brackets).

  1. 1. Solar panels ______ (ability) produce electricity even on cloudy days, but less of it.

    Show answer
    can — قدرة مؤكدة.

  2. 2. Every school ______ (advice) have a recycling bin in each classroom.

    Show answer
    should / ought to — نصيحة.

  3. 3. Rising temperatures ______ (possibility) cause more water shortages in the future, although scientists are still studying this.

    Show answer
    may / might / could — احتمال ومش مؤكد (still studying).

  4. 4. The world ______ (strong necessity) cut carbon emissions now to avoid disaster.

    Show answer
    must — ضرورة ومستعجلة (now).

  5. 5. Visitors ______ (prohibition) throw plastic into the sea.

    Show answer
    must not / mustn't — ممنوع.

  6. 6. You ______ (no necessity) buy an electric car to help the planet. Walking and cycling help too.

    Show answer
    don't have to — مش لازم.

B. Choose the correct answer from a, b, c or d.

  1. 7. Wind turbines ______ enough electricity for thousands of homes. a. can generates b. can generate c. can to generate d. can generating

    Show answer
    b — بعد can مصدر.

  2. 8. Experts say electric cars ______ become common in Egypt within ten years, but this is not certain. a. must b. may c. mustn't d. have to

    Show answer
    b. may — مش مؤكد = احتمال.

  3. 9. ______ we use solar energy at night? a. Do we can b. Can c. Are d. Does

    Show answer
    b. Can — السؤال: modal قبل الفاعل.

  4. 10. People ______ waste water, especially in hot summers. a. shouldn't b. don't should c. not should d. shouldn't to

    Show answer
    a. shouldn't — النفي = should + not.

  5. 11. Look at the black smoke from that factory. It ______ be very harmful to people nearby. a. must b. mustn't c. ought d. don't have to

    Show answer
    a. must — استنتاج من دليل (black smoke) = أكيد.

  6. 12. Ten years ago, we ______ started recycling, but we didn't. a. should b. should have c. must d. can

    Show answer
    b. should have — ندم على الماضي: should have + started.

C. Only one sentence in each group is grammatically correct. Choose the correct answer.

  1. 13. a. The government musts protect the coast. b. The government must protects the coast. c. The government must protect the coast. d. The government must to protect the coast.

    Show answer
    c — must + مصدر، ومن غير s.

  2. 14. a. Schools ought install solar panels. b. Schools ought to install solar panels. c. Schools ought to installing solar panels. d. Schools oughts to install solar panels.

    Show answer
    b — ought to + مصدر.

  3. 15. a. Battery storage may solves the problem. b. Battery storage may solve the problem. c. Battery storage mays solve the problem. d. Battery storage may to solve the problem.

    Show answer
    b — may + مصدر.

  4. 16. a. Do households should switch to solar? b. Should households switch to solar? c. Should households to switch to solar? d. Households should switch to solar?

    Show answer
    b — سؤال: Should + فاعل + مصدر. (d) جملة خبرية مش سؤال صح.

D. Rewrite, correct or write as asked.

  1. 17. Rewrite more carefully (hedge): Plastic bans will end ocean pollution.

    Show answer
    Plastic bans could / may reduce ocean pollution. — بدّلنا will بـ could/may، وend بـ reduce علشان منبالغش.

  2. 18. Rewrite using should: It is a good idea for families to unplug devices at night.

    Show answer
    Families should unplug devices at night. — a good idea = نصيحة = should.

  3. 19. Rewrite using must: It is necessary for factories to reduce their emissions.

    Show answer
    Factories must reduce their emissions. — necessary = must.

  4. 20. Find and correct the mistake: Young people can to make a real difference.

    Show answer
    شيل to: Young people can make a real difference.

  5. 21. Find and correct the mistake: You mustn't recycle at home, but it is very helpful.

    Show answer
    mustn't ← don't have to: You don't have to recycle at home, but it is very helpful. — المعنى «مش لازم» مش «ممنوع».

  6. 22. Write a call to action for a leaflet about saving electricity. Use TWO modal verbs.

    Show answer
    مثال: You can save energy by switching off lights. You should start today and tell your friends! — can = قدرة، should = نصيحة.

  7. 23. JRE: Is solar energy the most useful renewable source for Egypt today? Use at least TWO modals.

    Show answer
    مثال: Justify: Solar energy may be the most important renewable source at the moment. Reason: It is the fastest growing, and its cost has fallen sharply. Evidence: In Egypt, the Benban Solar Park already supplies electricity to many homes. However, without batteries it cannot supply power at night, so we should also invest in wind energy. — may = حذر، cannot = حدود، should = توصية.
ملخص الدرس في سطرين
  • can قدرة — could / may / might احتمال (حذر) — should / ought to نصيحة — must ضرورة قوية — must not ممنوع — don't have to مش لازم.
  • بعد أي modal فعل مصدر من غير to (ما عدا ought to)، ومفيش s / ed / ing على الـ modal.
  • النفي: modal + not. السؤال: modal قبل الفاعل.
  • في الكتابة استخدمها علشان متقولش كل حاجة كأنها حقيقة مطلقة (hedging)، وللنصيحة في الـ leaflet.
