تانية بكالوريا · Writing · Unit 1

Reflective Writing

إزاي تكتب رد تأملي (150-180 كلمة): كنت فاكر إيه، اتعلمت إيه بالدليل، اتغيرت إزاي، وهتعمل إيه بعد كده.

تانية بكالوريا English

الدرس ده عن إيه؟

Reflective writing is personal writing in which you look back at what you have learned or experienced, explain how your ideas have changed and why, and decide what you will do next.

الـ Reflective writing يعني كتابة «تأملية»: إنك تبص ورا على حاجة اتعلمتها أو عشتها، وتقول كنت فاكر إيه، واتعلمت إيه، ورأيك اتغير إزاي وليه، وهتعمل إيه بعد كده. هي كتابة شخصية بضمير I، بس مش حكاية وخلاص: فيها تفكير وأسباب.

في الكتاب: Unit 1 : Living Well in a Complex World بيقول في أهداف الكتابة «write reflective responses». وفي آخر كل جزء فيه Self-Reflection (صفحة 20)، وفي الـ Summative Assessment (صفحة 36) فيه Reflection Questions زي: «How have your views about lifestyle and well-being changed throughout this unit?». ونفس الأسئلة بتتكرر في آخر كل وحدة (مثلًا صفحة 64 في Unit 2).

الكتاب مش بيدي جدول هيكل خاص بالـ reflective writing في Unit 1، لكنه بيديك أسئلة التأمل وخطوة Reflect and Act في طريقة JRE+. فإحنا هنبني الهيكل من الأسئلة دي نفسها، وبطول (150-180) words زي باقي مهام الكتابة في الكتاب.

الفكرة ببساطة: قبل ← دلوقتي ← بعد كده

A good reflection moves through time: Before (what I used to think) → Now (what I have learned and why my view changed) → Next (what I will do).

تخيل إنك بتتكلم مع نفسك بعد ما خلصت الوحدة: «أنا كنت فاكر إن الصحة يعني أكل قليل وجيم. بس لما قريت عن النوم والسوشيال ميديا فهمت إن الموضوع أكبر. علشان كده هقفل الموبايل بدري». ده بالظبط الـ reflection: قبل – دلوقتي – بعد كده.

حيلة للحفظ: المراية

الـ reflection زي المراية: بتبص فيها على نفسك. مش بتحكي عن الناس، ومش بتلخص النص. كل فقرة لازم يبان فيها إنت اتغيرت إزاي.

يعني الـ reflective writing مش معناها إنك تسيب الـ JRE. لسه محتاج سبب ودليل لكل تغيير في رأيك. الفرق إنك بتتكلم عن نفسك.

الهيكل (مبني على أسئلة التأمل في الكتاب)

Use the book's reflection questions as your plan: 1. Starting point – 2. What I learned (with evidence) – 3. How and why my view changed – 4. Honest doubts or new questions – 5. Action (what I will do).

الجزءبيرد على أنهي سؤال من الكتاب؟تعمل إيه؟بداية مقترحة
1. Starting pointHow have your views… changed?قول كنت فاكر إيه قبل الوحدةBefore this unit, I used to believe that…
2. What I learnedWhich claims were based on solid evidence?حاجة أو اتنين اتعلمتهم + دليل من النصThe text showed that… / I learned that…
3. How my view changedHow have your views changed?رأيك دلوقتي وليه اتغيرAs a result, I now realise that…
4. Doubts / new questions / cultureWhat new questions do you now have? How did your cultural background influence you?اعترف بحاجة لسه مش متأكد منها، أو أثر ثقافتكI am still not sure whether… / As an Egyptian, …
5. Action (Reflect and Act)How can you apply what you've learned to your daily life?خطوة عملية هتعملهاFrom now on, I intend to…
خلي بالك

مش لازم تجاوب على كل الأسئلة الخمسة بتاعة الكتاب في نفس القطعة. لو المهمة سؤال واحد (زي How have your views changed?) خلي الأجزاء كلها تخدم السؤال ده.

الأزمنة اللي بتعمل الـ Reflection

Reflective writing uses tenses to show change over time: used to / past simple for the past, present perfect for change up to now, present simple for what you believe now, and will / intend to / be going to for the future.

الزمنبنستخدمه علشانمثال
used to + verbعادة أو فكرة قديمة خلاص اتغيرتI used to think that screens were harmless.
Past simpleحاجة حصلت خلاص (قريت، سمعت)The listening made me question perfect photos.
Present perfectتغيير حصل ولسه أثره موجود دلوقتيMy views have changed a lot.
Present simpleرأيك أو فهمك دلوقتيNow I understand that well-being is also mental.
will / intend to / be going toخطتك للمستقبلI intend to switch off my phone at 10 p.m.
  • I used to believe that a healthy life only meant dieting.used to = «كنت زمان…» ودلوقتي لأ.
  • This unit has made me more careful about what I watch online.has made = present perfect: التغيير حصل وأثره لسه معايا.
  • I didn't realise how much sleep teenagers need until I read the text.past simple + until = «ماكنتش واخد بالي لحد ما…».
  • I now realise that comparison online may affect self-esteem.present simple + hedging (may).
  • I am still not sure whether apps should limit screen time.اعتراف بشك: ده جزء مهم في التأمل.
  • From now on, I am going to walk to school twice a week.خطة مستقبل محددة.

عبارات مفيدة

Looking back (قبل)

  • Before this unit, I used to believe that …
  • At first, I thought that …
  • I had never really thought about …

What I learned (اتعلمت إيه)

  • The text showed me that …
  • One thing that surprised me was …
  • I was struck by the evidence that …
  • I learned that …

How I changed (اتغيرت إزاي)

  • My views have changed in … ways.
  • As a result, I now realise that …
  • This made me question …
  • I have started to …

Honest doubts (شك وأسئلة)

  • I am still not sure whether …
  • I wonder if …
  • One question I still have is …
  • Honestly, my background made me …

Evidence or opinion? (دليل ولا رأي؟)

  • The claim about … was based on solid research.
  • The idea that … seemed to be mostly opinion.

Action (هعمل إيه)

  • From now on, I intend to …
  • In the future, I will try to …
  • I hope to …
  • This experience has taught me that …

إزاي تكتب خطوة بخطوة

  1. اقرا سؤال التأمل وحدد هو بيسأل عن إيه: تغيير رأي؟ أسئلة جديدة؟ مهارة اتعلمتها؟ أثر ثقافتك؟
  2. اكتب في الهامش 3 كلمات: كنت فاكر إيه ← اتعلمت إيه ← هعمل إيه.
  3. افتكر دليلين من الوحدة (معلومة من نص أو تسجيل). التأمل القوي بيقول «ليه» اتغيرت، مش بس «إني» اتغيرت.
  4. ابدأ بالـ starting point: Before this unit, I used to believe that…
  5. اكتب اللي اتعلمته بـ First / Second، ومع كل نقطة دليل بسيط من النص.
  6. اعترف بحاجة لسه مش متأكد منها أو بأثر خلفيتك المصرية. ده بيبين نضج في التفكير.
  7. اختم بخطوة عملية (Reflect and Act): From now on, I intend to…
  8. راجع: ضمير I؟ أزمنة متنوعة (used to / have + p.p. / will)؟ عدد الكلمات 150-180؟

موديل كامل

Model reflective response176 words
Task: Reflection Question: How have your views about lifestyle and well-being changed throughout this unit? Write a reflective response of (150-180) words.

Before this unit, I used to believe that a healthy life simply meant eating less sugar and going to the gym. I rarely thought about sleep, and I certainly did not connect my phone with my mood. However, my views have changed in two important ways.

First, I have realised that well-being is both physical and mental. The text explained that exercise releases endorphins, which reduce stress, so a short walk may help my mind as much as my body. Second, the broadcast about the 'highlight-reel effect' made me question the perfect photos which I see online every day. I now understand that many of them are edited and may create unrealistic expectations.

I am still not sure how much responsibility belongs to social media companies and how much belongs to users like me. Honestly, my Egyptian background, in which family gatherings matter a lot, made me value social connection more than some writers did.

From now on, I intend to switch off my phone an hour before bed and spend more evenings with my family.

ليه النموذج ده قوي؟
  • Starting point: «Before this unit, I used to believe…» بيوضح الفكرة القديمة بـ used to.
  • الانتقال: «However, my views have changed in two important ways.» جملة بتجهز القارئ، وفيها present perfect.
  • What I learned + evidence: كل نقطة (First / Second) معاها دليل من الوحدة (endorphins – highlight-reel effect). يعني التأمل فيه JRE: التغيير له سبب ودليل.
  • Relative clauses: «endorphins, which reduce stress» و «the perfect photos which I see online». قواعد Unit 1 جوه الكتابة.
  • Hedging: «may help», «may create». الطالب حذر في الحكم.
  • Honest doubt + culture: «I am still not sure…» و «my Egyptian background…» بيردوا على أسئلة التأمل في الكتاب (new questions / cultural background).
  • Inverted commas: 'highlight-reel effect' بين علامتي تنصيص لأنه اسم مصطلح مش معنى حرفي.
  • Reflect and Act: آخر جملة خطوة عملية محددة (an hour before bed). مش «I will be healthy» وخلاص.
  • عدد الكلمات: 176 كلمة (المطلوب 150-180).

خلي بالك إزاي الموديل اتقسم 4 فقرات قصيرة. ده عادي في الـ reflective response. لو المطلوب paragraph واحدة اكتب نفس الأفكار بالترتيب ده من غير ما تنزل سطر.

أشهر الأخطاء (غلط ← صح)
  • ✗ تلخص النص بس: The text says exercise releases endorphins. Sleep is important. ✓ The text explained that…, so I now realise that… (اربطه بنفسك).
  • ✗ كلام عام مالوش معنى: This unit was very nice and useful. ✓ قول إيه بالظبط اللي اتغير: I used to…, but now I…
  • ✗ زمن واحد طول القطعة. ✓ نوّع: used to – have realised – now understand – intend to.
  • ✗ I use to think. ✓ I used to think. (في الكتابة دايمًا used).
  • ✗ خطة مستقبل مبهمة: I will be better. ✓ I intend to switch off my phone an hour before bed.
  • ✗ كلام مطلق: Social media is completely bad. ✓ Social media may affect some teenagers' self-esteem.
إزاي تجاوب في الامتحان
  • أول كلمة في إجابتك تكون من السؤال نفسه: views / lifestyle / well-being.
  • استخدم First / Second علشان المصحح يشوف نقاطك بسهولة.
  • اذكر دليل واحد على الأقل من نصوص الوحدة. ده اللي بيفرق التأمل الأكاديمي عن الكلام العادي.
  • جملة شك واحدة (I am still not sure…) بتبين تفكير ناقد.
  • اختم بخطوة Act: الكتاب بيختم كل دورة JRE+ بـ Reflect and Act.

إزاي بييجي في الأسئلة؟

الأسئلة في الكتاب بتيجي بالأشكال دي:

1) Self-Reflection box (أسئلة قصيرة بعد كل جزء) زي: «Which skill do you need more practice with?» والإجابة 2-3 جمل.

Answer: I need more practice with evaluating arguments. Sometimes I accept a claim because it sounds confident, even when it has no evidence. From now on, I will look for a source before I believe a post.

الشرح: المهارة (J) + السبب من تجربتي (R) + خطوة عملية (Act).

2) Reflection Questions في الـ Summative Assessment (زي صفحة 36 و64): ممكن تكتب إجابة قصيرة لكل سؤال، أو reflective response (150-180) words على سؤال واحد زي الموديل.

3) سؤال «evidence or opinion?»: «Which claims in the unit were based on solid evidence, and which were mostly opinion?»

Answer: The claim that teenagers who sleep fewer than seven hours are more likely to report anxiety was based on solid evidence, because it came from published research. In contrast, the post saying that social media 'destroys' confidence seemed to be mostly opinion, since it used absolute language and gave no source.

الشرح: قارن بين claim عنده مصدر و claim فيه كلام مطلق ومن غير مصدر. ده بيربط التأمل بمهارة الـ bias اللي في Unit 1.

تدريب

A. Is each sentence reflective (R) or not reflective (N)? Why?

  1. 1. Teenagers need eight to ten hours of sleep per night.

    Show answer
    N — معلومة من النص بس، مفيش I ولا تغيير.

  2. 2. Reading about sleep made me realise that my late nights may explain my tiredness in class.

    Show answer
    R — ربط المعلومة بنفسه وبيّن فهم جديد.

  3. 3. This unit was very good and I liked it.

    Show answer
    N — رأي عام من غير تغيير أو سبب.

  4. 4. I used to follow accounts that made me feel bad about myself, but now I unfollow them.

    Show answer
    R — قبل ← بعد، وفيه تصرف.

  5. 5. Social media platforms have changed over the past decade.

    Show answer
    N — معلومة عامة مش عنّي.

  6. 6. I am still not sure whether schools or parents should limit students' screen time.

    Show answer
    R — شك صادق وسؤال جديد.

B. Rewrite each sentence as a reflective sentence using the words in brackets.

  1. 7. I thought healthy food was boring. (used to … but now …)

    Show answer
    I used to think that healthy food was boring, but now I enjoy cooking simple balanced meals.

  2. 8. My opinion about influencers changed. (This unit has …)

    Show answer
    This unit has changed my opinion about influencers. / This unit has made me more critical of influencers.

  3. 9. I didn't know about the 'highlight-reel effect'. (until)

    Show answer
    I didn't know about the 'highlight-reel effect' until I listened to the broadcast.

  4. 10. I will exercise more. (From now on, I intend to … + a specific detail)

    Show answer
    From now on, I intend to cycle for thirty minutes three times a week.

  5. 11. I don't know if mindfulness works for everyone. (I am still not sure whether …)

    Show answer
    I am still not sure whether mindfulness works for everyone.

C. Choose the correct answer.

  1. 12. Before this unit, I ______ that screens had no effect on my sleep. a. use to think b. used to think c. am thinking d. have thought

    Show answer
    b. used to think — فكرة قديمة خلاص اتغيرت.

  2. 13. Since I started walking to school, I ______ calmer. a. feel b. felt c. have felt d. will feel

    Show answer
    c. have felt — since + present perfect: بدأ في الماضي ولسه مستمر.

  3. 14. Which sentence is the best ending for a reflective response? a. In conclusion, sleep is important. b. Everyone should sleep more. c. From now on, I intend to stop using my phone after ten o'clock. d. The text was about sleep.

    Show answer
    c — خطوة عملية شخصية ومحددة (Reflect and Act).

  4. 15. I ______ how much my mood depends on sleep until I read the text. a. have never realised before b. had never realised c. never realise d. am never realising

    Show answer
    b. had never realised — قبل وقت معين في الماضي (قبل ما أقرا النص).

  5. 16. Which sentence uses hedging? a. Comparison online destroys everyone. b. Comparison online may affect my self-esteem. c. Comparison online is always bad. d. Nobody is happy online.

    Show answer
    b — may = حذر.

D. Answer the reflection questions in 2-3 sentences.

  1. 17. What new vocabulary did you learn in this section, and how will you use it?

    Show answer
    مثال: I learned the word 'sedentary', which describes a lifestyle with little physical activity. I will use it when I write about screen time and health.

  2. 18. How can you apply what you've learned about media literacy to your daily life?

    Show answer
    مثال: Before I share a post, I will check whether it gives a source. If it uses absolute words such as 'always' or 'everyone', I will be more careful about believing it.

  3. 19. How did your own cultural background influence the way you interpreted the texts?

    Show answer
    مثال: As an Egyptian, I grew up with big family meals, so I agreed strongly with the text about social connection. However, this may have made me underestimate individual habits such as sleep.

  4. 20. What new questions do you now have about mental health or social media?

    Show answer
    مثال: I now wonder whether apps should show a warning when a photo has been edited. I would also like to know how much screen time is safe for teenagers.

E. Writing tasks: write a reflective response of (150-180) words.

  1. 21. What new analytical or rhetorical skills have you developed in this unit?

    Show answer
    خطة مقترحة: Before: I used to accept any confident post. Learned 1: identifying bias (absolute words vs hedging) + مثال من النص. Learned 2: building JRE arguments (claim – reason – evidence). Doubt: I still find it hard to spot a rhetorical question's effect. Act: I intend to rewrite one biased sentence every week.

  2. 22. How have your views about leisure and well-being changed during Unit 2?

    Show answer
    خطة مقترحة: Before: leisure = staying on the sofa. Learned: creative arts reduce stress / 'flow' + effort can make leisure meaningful (the cycling extract). Changed: now I see active leisure as more rewarding. Culture: family outings by the Nile. Act: join the school drama club.

Checklist قبل ما تسلّم

  • كتبت بضمير I / my / me وبصوت شخصي صادق.
  • بدأت بـ starting point (كنت فاكر إيه).
  • قلت اتعلمت إيه ومعاه دليل من نصوص الوحدة.
  • وضحت رأيي اتغير إزاي وليه.
  • اعترفت بـ شك أو سؤال جديد أو أثر ثقافتي.
  • ختمت بـ خطوة عملية محددة (Reflect and Act).
  • نوّعت الأزمنة: used to – past simple – present perfect – present – future.
  • استخدمت hedging في الأحكام، وعدد الكلمات 150-180.
