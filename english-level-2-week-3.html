Grammar: Comparatives and superlatives · (not) as … as

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

Short adjectives: + -er than / the + -est (cheap → cheaper than → the cheapest)

Long adjectives: more … than / the most … (modern → more modern than → the most modern)

as + adjective + as = the same · not as … as = less

في العربية نقول «أكبر من» بكلمة واحدة، فيقع المتعلم في خطأين: more bigger (الجمع بين الطريقتين)، أو استخدام from بدل than (bigger from Cairo ✗). القاعدة: الصفة القصيرة تأخذ er-، والطويلة تأخذ more، ولا نجمع بينهما أبدًا، وبعد المقارنة نستخدم than. ومع صيغة التفضيل العليا لا تنسَ the: the biggest city.

AdjectiveComparativeSuperlative
cheapcheaper thanthe cheapest
bigbigger thanthe biggest
noisynoisier thanthe noisiest
modernmore modern thanthe most modern
pollutedmore polluted thanthe most polluted
goodbetter thanthe best
badworse thanthe worst
farfurther thanthe furthest

One syllable ending in consonant + vowel + consonant: double the last letter (big → bigger, hot → hottest). Adjectives ending in -y: -ier / -iest (busy → busier → the busiest).

Use much, a lot or a bit before a comparative: much cheaper, a bit quieter.

as … as: Aswan is as hot as Luxor in summer. · Tanta isn't as big as Cairo.

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Write the comparative and the superlative.

  1. 1) cheap
  2. 2) hot
  3. 3) busy
  4. 4) modern
  5. 5) good
  6. 6) bad
  7. 7) peaceful
  8. 8) crowded
Show the answers
1) cheaper · the cheapest 2) hotter · the hottest 3) busier · the busiest 4) more modern · the most modern 5) better · the best 6) worse · the worst 7) more peaceful · the most peaceful 8) more crowded · the most crowded

Exercise 2 · Complete with the correct form of the adjective.

  1. 1) The metro is ___ (fast) than the bus.
  2. 2) Cairo is the ___ (big) city in Egypt.
  3. 3) Is the rent in Zamalek ___ (expensive) than in Shubra?
  4. 4) Today the traffic is ___ (bad) than yesterday.
  5. 5) This is the ___ (interesting) museum in the city.
  6. 6) My new flat is much ___ (quiet) than my old one.
Show the answers
1) faster 2) biggest 3) more expensive 4) worse 5) most interesting 6) quieter

Exercise 3 · Rewrite the sentences with (not) as … as.

  1. 1) Luxor is hot. Aswan is hot too. (as … as)
  2. 2) Alexandria is smaller than Cairo. (not as big as)
  3. 3) The bus is slower than the metro. (not as fast as)
  4. 4) My street and your street are both noisy. (as … as)
  5. 5) Taxis are more expensive than buses. (Buses … not as … as)
Show the answers
1) Luxor is as hot as Aswan. (or: Aswan is as hot as Luxor.) 2) Alexandria isn't (is not) as big as Cairo. 3) The bus isn't as fast as the metro. 4) My street is as noisy as your street. (or: Your street is as noisy as my street / mine.) 5) Buses aren't as expensive as taxis.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) الحياة في القرية أهدأ من الحياة في المدينة.
  2. 2) هذا أفضل مطعم في الحيّ.
  3. 3) المواصلات العامة أرخص بكثير من التاكسي.
  4. 4) الإسكندرية ليست كبيرة مثل القاهرة.
Show the answers
1) Life in the village is quieter (more peaceful) than life in the city. 2) This is the best restaurant in the neighbourhood. 3) Public transport is much cheaper than a taxi (taxis / taking a taxi). 4) Alexandria isn't (is not) as big as Cairo.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — “Two places”: write 80–100 words comparing your city or town with another place you know. Use comparatives, superlatives and (not) as … as. Then say it aloud as if you are talking to a tourist.

Example: I live in Cairo, but my grandparents live in Mansoura. Cairo is much bigger and more exciting than Mansoura. It has the best shopping centres and some of the most historic places in Egypt. However, it is also noisier and more polluted, and the traffic in the rush hour is terrible. Mansoura isn't as crowded as Cairo, and the cost of living there is lower. The rent is cheaper and, in my opinion, the people are friendlier. Cairo is better for work, but Mansoura is more peaceful. One day I want to live in a quiet place near the sea.
