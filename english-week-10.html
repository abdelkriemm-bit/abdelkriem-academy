Past simple: regular (-ed) and irregular verbs · didn't · Did…?

بعد didn't و Did يرجع الفعل لأصله: I didn't go (وليس didn't went) · Did you see? (وليس Did you saw?).

PositiveNegativeQuestion
I worked. She went.I didn't work. She didn't go.Did you work? Did she go?

Regular: + ed (worked, stayed) · study → studied · stop → stopped. Irregular verbs: learn them by heart (see the word sets).

Say -ed as /t/ (walked), /d/ (stayed) or /ɪd/ after t or d (visited, wanted).

Exercises

Exercise 1 · Write the past form.

  1. 1) go
  2. 2) eat
  3. 3) buy
  4. 4) watch
  5. 5) see
  6. 6) study
  7. 7) take
  8. 8) meet
Show the answers
1) went · 2) ate · 3) bought · 4) watched · 5) saw · 6) studied · 7) took · 8) met

Exercise 2 · Complete with the past simple.

  1. 1) Yesterday I ___ (visit) my uncle.
  2. 2) We ___ (eat) fish for lunch.
  3. 3) She ___ (not go) to work on Monday.
  4. 4) ___ you ___ (see) the match?
  5. 5) He ___ (buy) a car last year.
  6. 6) They ___ (leave) at ten.
Show the answers
1) visited · 2) ate · 3) didn't go · 4) Did … see · 5) bought · 6) left

Exercise 3 · Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) I didn't went to work.
  2. 2) Did you saw him?
  3. 3) She buyed a dress.
  4. 4) We goed to the park.
Show the answers
1) I didn't go to work. · 2) Did you see him? · 3) She bought a dress. · 4) We went to the park.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) ذهبت إلى السوق واشتريت بعض الفاكهة.
  2. 2) لم أشاهد التلفاز أمس.
  3. 3) في أي ساعة عدت إلى البيت؟
  4. 4) قابلت زميلي في المحطة.
Show the answers
1) I went to the market and bought some fruit. · 2) I didn't watch TV yesterday. · 3) What time did you come back home? · 4) I met my colleague at the station.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak

Write & Speak — “My last weekend”: write 8 sentences using first, then, after that and finally. Record yourself reading it.

Example: Last Friday was a great day. First, I got up late and had a big breakfast. Then I went to the mosque. After that, I visited my parents and we had lunch together. In the afternoon I met my friends at a café. We drank tea and talked. Finally, I came back home at ten and slept early.
