كلمات بتيجي مع بعض (Collocations)

A collocation is two or more words that are often used together, like take responsibility or raise awareness. Using them makes your English sound natural.

الـ collocation يعني «كلمات متجوزة»: بتمشي مع بعض دايمًا. مثلًا بنقول take responsibility مش do responsibility. لو حفظتهم كده كتلة واحدة، كتابتك هتبقى أقوى وهتعرف تكمّل الفراغات بسرعة.

Prefixes و Suffixes في الوحدة

A prefix comes at the start of a word and changes its meaning (un- + realistic = unrealistic). A suffix comes at the end and often changes the part of speech (aware + -ness = awareness).

الـ prefix حتة بتتحط في أول الكلمة وبتغيّر معناها (غالبًا بتقلبها للعكس). الـ suffix حتة بتتحط في آخر الكلمة وبتغيّر نوعها (من صفة لاسم، أو من اسم لصفة…). دي أسهل طريقة تعرف بيها نوع الكلمة من غير ما تكون حافظها.

الكلمات في فقرة واحدة

اقرا الفقرة دي وطلّع الكلمات الجديدة اللي عليها bold. حاول تفهم كل كلمة من الجملة اللي حواليها (meaning in context).

Since the Industrial Revolution, humans have burned fossil fuels and released huge amounts of carbon dioxide. As a result, the greenhouse effect has grown stronger, and the planet is warming at an alarming rate. Droughts, hurricanes and floods now strike with greater intensity, fertile land is becoming arid, and biodiversity is under serious threat. The scientific consensus is clear: if we do not take action, some damage may be irreversible.

Fortunately, renewable energy offers hope. In Aswan, Benban Solar Park produces clean electricity, and better battery storage is solving the problem of intermittency. National initiatives encourage recycling and energy efficiency, while young Egyptians show real determination. From low-cost solar heaters to Nile clean-up campaigns run by volunteers, their innovation proves that individual and collective actions can build a sustainable future.

المعنى ببساطة: من أيام الثورة الصناعية والإنسان بيحرق وقود أحفوري، وده قوّى الاحتباس الحراري فالأرض بتسخن بسرعة مقلقة، والكوارث زادت والتنوع البيولوجي في خطر. بس الطاقة المتجددة زي بنبان، والمبادرات، وحماس الشباب المصري، بيدّونا أمل في مستقبل مستدام. لاحظ كلمات السبب والنتيجة: As a result.

أشهر الأخطاء (غلط ← صح)
  • Solar energy is a renewal source. ← a renewable source.
  • Benban reduces pollute. ← Benban reduces pollution. (بعد reduces اسم)
  • The weather of Egypt is changing over 100 years. ← The climate of Egypt…
  • Hurricanes are becoming more frequently. ← more frequent. (بعد become صفة)
  • Many species are extinction. ← Many species are extinct. (بعد are صفة)
  • We must do action now. ← We must take action now.
  • The desert is very fertile. ← The desert is very arid. (fertile = بتطلّع زرع كتير، وعكسها arid)

النوع 1: Guess the meaning from context

"Because of changing rainfall patterns, regions that were once fertile are becoming increasingly arid." What does arid mean? a. very dry b. very cold c. crowded d. green

الإجابة: a. السبب «تغيّر المطر»، والكلمة عكس fertile. يبقى الأرض بقت ناشفة.

النوع 2: Complete with the correct form

Solar panels convert sunlight into electricity very ______. (efficient)

الإجابة: efficiently. بيوصف الفعل convert، وبعد very فيه ظرف ← نزود -ly.

النوع 3: Find a word similar in meaning

"The volunteers showed great determination to finish the clean-up." Which word is similar in meaning to determination? a. communication b. persistence c. pollution d. innovation

الإجابة: b. persistence. determination = عزيمة وإصرار. الكتاب نفسه بيعرّفها «a strong will to finish a task».
