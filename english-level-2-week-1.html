Grammar: Present simple vs present continuous · state verbs

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

Present simple = habits, routines and facts (I work in a bank. I usually take the metro.)

Present continuous = now, around now and temporary situations (I'm working from home this week.)

State verbs (know, understand, believe, remember, need, want, like, love, belong, mean) are not usually continuous: I know (NOT I'm knowing).

في العربية نقول «أشتغل» للعادة اليومية وللفترة الحالية، لذلك يخلط المتعلم العربي بين الزمنين. استخدم المضارع البسيط للعادة والحقيقة (I work in a bank)، والمضارع المستمر لما يحدث الآن أو في فترة مؤقتة (I'm working from home this week). والخطأ الأشهر: I am knowing و I am understanding — أفعال الحالة (التفكير والمشاعر والملكية) لا تأتي غالبًا بـ ing، فقل: I know و I understand.

Present simplePresent continuous
Usehabits, routines, factsnow, these days, temporary situations
ExampleI work in Giza.I'm working in Alexandria this month.
Time wordsusually, every day, on Fridaysnow, at the moment, these days, this week
State verbs (no -ing)Examples
Thinkingknow, understand, believe, remember, mean
Feelingslike, love, hate, want, prefer
Havinghave (= own), belong to, need

have can be continuous when it is an action: I'm having lunch (= eating). But: I have a car (NOT I'm having a car).

think: I think it's a good idea (opinion → simple) · I'm thinking about my future (action → continuous).

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Choose the correct form.

  1. 1) My father (is working / works) in a bank.
  2. 2) Be quiet! The baby (is sleeping / sleeps).
  3. 3) We usually (go / are going) to Alexandria in the summer.
  4. 4) This week Mona (stays / is staying) with her aunt in Luxor.
  5. 5) Ahmed (isn't eating / doesn't eat) meat. He's a vegetarian.
  6. 6) Look! Those men (are fixing / fix) the road.
Show the answers
1) works 2) is sleeping 3) go 4) is staying 5) doesn't eat 6) are fixing

Exercise 2 · Complete with the present simple or the present continuous.

  1. 1) I ___ (not understand) this word.
  2. 2) ___ you ___ (know) Omar's phone number?
  3. 3) Nour ___ (take) a cooking course these days.
  4. 4) This phone ___ (belong) to my brother.
  5. 5) What ___ you usually ___ (do) on Fridays?
  6. 6) I ___ (look) for my keys. Can you help me?
Show the answers
1) don't (do not) understand 2) Do … know 3) is ('s) taking 4) belongs 5) do … do 6) am ('m) looking

Exercise 3 · Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) I am knowing the answer.
  2. 2) She is needing a new laptop.
  3. 3) He go to the gym every day.
  4. 4) Listen! Somebody sings.
  5. 5) I'm not believing him.
  6. 6) We are having a big flat in Nasr City.
Show the answers
1) I know the answer. 2) She needs a new laptop. 3) He goes to the gym every day. 4) Listen! Somebody is singing. 5) I don't believe him. 6) We have a big flat in Nasr City.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) أنا مهتم بالرياضة، لكني لست ماهرًا في كرة القدم.
  2. 2) أختي تعمل من البيت هذا الأسبوع.
  3. 3) لا أتذكر اسمه.
  4. 4) علاقتي جيدة بجيراني.
  5. 5) هل تحتاج إلى مساعدة؟
Show the answers
1) I'm interested in sport (sports), but I'm not good at football. 2) My sister is working from home this week. 3) I don't remember his name. 4) I get on well with my neighbours. 5) Do you need (any) help?

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — “Me these days”: write 80–100 words about yourself. Say what you are like, what you usually do and what you are doing these days. Use both present tenses. Then talk about yourself for one minute without reading.

Example: My name is Ahmed and I'm 28. I grew up in Giza, but now I live in New Cairo with my wife. I'm an accountant and I work for a big company. I'm quite shy, but I'm friendly and hard-working. I usually get up at six and take the metro to work. In my spare time, I play football or read. These days I'm very busy, because I'm taking an online English course in the evenings. I'm also looking for a bigger flat, because we need more space. In my opinion, English is very important for my future.
