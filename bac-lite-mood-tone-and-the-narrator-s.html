تانية بكالوريا · Literature · Unit 2

Mood, Tone and the Narrator's Shifting Feelings

الفرق بين mood وtone، رحلة مشاعر الراوي في «Not Exactly a Relaxing Day»، بنوك كلمات، وإزاي تجاوب بالدليل.

تانية بكالوريا English

الدرس ده عن إيه؟

Mood is the feeling a text creates in the reader. Tone is the writer's or narrator's attitude. In "Not Exactly a Relaxing Day", the narrator's feelings shift from confidence to doubt to satisfaction.

الدرس ده عن تلات حاجات مرتبطين ببعض: الـ mood (الجو أو الإحساس اللي القطعة بتديهولك)، الـ tone (نبرة الراوي وموقفه)، ومشاعر الراوي وهي بتتغير (shifting feelings).

في الكتاب ده في Unit 2، Section B، مع القطعة «Not Exactly a Relaxing Day». الكتاب بيقولك: As you read, note the narrator's changing mood and tone. Underline the words or phrases that show how his feelings shift. وفي الـ Self-Assessment: «identify how the narrator's mood shifts across the text».

الفرق بين Mood و Tone

Mood = how the reader feels (the atmosphere). Tone = how the writer / narrator sounds (his attitude to the subject).

الاتنين قريبين من بعض، بس فيه فرق بسيط:

MoodTone
يعني إيه؟الجو العام والإحساس اللي إنت كقارئ بتحس بيهنبرة الكاتب أو الراوي وموقفه من الحاجة
بنسألHow does the passage make me feel?How does the narrator sound?
زيموسيقى الفيلم اللي بتخليك متوتر أو مرتاحطريقة كلام حد معاك: بيهزر؟ زعلان؟ جاد؟
أمثلةcalm, peaceful, tense, gloomy, cheerfulhumorous, honest, reflective, proud, frustrated
من القطعةcalm in the first hour (the quiet road, the shimmering Nile)honest and humorous ("laughed at my complaints")
حيلة للحفظ
  • Mood = Me (أنا القارئ حاسس بإيه).
  • Tone = Talker (اللي بيتكلم صوته عامل إزاي).

إزاي الكاتب بيعمل الـ mood والـ tone؟

الكاتب مش بيقولك «الجو هادي» على طول. بيستخدم أدوات، وإنت بتدور عليها:

  • اختيار الكلمات (word choice): «shimmered softly» = هدوء، «struggled» و«ached» = تعب.
  • الوصف والصور (description / imagery): الصوت، النور، الحر.
  • طول الجمل (sentence length): جمل طويلة هادية = راحة. جمل قصيرة = توتر أو تعب ("My legs ached.").
  • التشبيه (simile) والتضاد (contrast).
  • كلمات التحول (turning words): Then, But, However, Meanwhile, Finally.
  • الطقس والمكان (setting): الصبح الساقع ↔ الشمس القوية.

الكتاب عامل جدول بالمراحل. إحنا هنضيف عليه الكلمات اللي تحط تحتها خط والـ mood والـ tone في كل مرحلة:

الشكل العام للمشاعر: فوق ← تحت ← فوق تاني. بدأ مرتاح، نزل لـ أوحش نقطة، وطلع في الآخر أحسن من الأول. ده اسمه emotional journey. ولاحظ إن النهاية مش رجوع لنفس الإحساس: الراحة في الأول كانت كسل، والراحة في الآخر رضا بعد مجهود.

إزاي تجاوب سؤال Mood / Tone / Feelings

أي سؤال من دول جاوبه بـ 3 خطوات (اسم + دليل + شرح):

الخطوةبتكتب إيهمثال
1. Name itسمّي الـ mood / tone / feeling بكلمة دقيقةIn the first hour, the mood is calm and peaceful.
2. Quoteاقتباس قصير (2-4 كلمات)The writer describes how the Nile "shimmered softly".
3. Explainإزاي الكلمات دي بتعمل الإحساس دهThe soft light and quiet sounds make the reader feel relaxed, like the narrator.

ولو السؤال عن التغيير (shift)، اكتب قبل + كلمة التحول + بعد:

Model answer: the narrator's shifting feelings140 words
Task: How do the narrator's feelings change during the cycling trip? Use evidence from the text.

At the start, the narrator feels confident and relaxed. He believes his friend's promise that the trip "will be relaxing", and during the first hour everything feels "exactly as promised". The quiet road and the Nile that "shimmered softly" create a calm, peaceful mood.

However, his feelings shift when "things changed". As the sun grows stronger, he becomes uncertain, and soon he reaches his low point. His legs ache, his breathing is uneven and he seriously thinks about calling a taxi. The short sentences make the mood tense and show his frustration.

The turning point comes when pride and curiosity stop him from giving up. He becomes determined and keeps going "step by step". By the end, he is exhausted but "strangely satisfied", and his tone becomes reflective. He has changed: he now believes that effort can make leisure meaningful.

ليه النموذج ده قوي؟
  • رتبنا المشاعر بالترتيب: confident/relaxed ← uncertain ← low point ← determined ← satisfied.
  • كل شعور معاه اقتباس قصير كدليل.
  • كلمات التحول: However, As, The turning point comes when, By the end.
  • اتكلمنا عن الـ mood (calm, tense) والـ tone (reflective) والـ feelings مع بعض.
أشهر الأخطاء
  • ✗ تخلط بين mood وtone ← mood = القارئ حاسس بإيه، tone = الراوي صوته عامل إزاي.
  • ✗ تستخدم كلمة عامة زي «good» أو «bad» ← ✓ كلمة دقيقة: calm, tense, uplifting, reflective.
  • ✗ تقول الشعور من غير دليل ← ✓ لازم اقتباس قصير.
  • ✗ تقول إن الراوي كان مبسوط طول الرحلة ← ✓ مشاعره اتغيرت أكتر من مرة.
  • ✗ تنسى نقطة التحول ← ✓ «But something stopped me» دي أهم جملة في التغيير.
إزاي تجاوب بسرعة
  • دوّر على كلمات التحول: Then, But, However, Meanwhile, When we finally…
  • بص على الطقس: الشمس الهادية = هدوء، الشمس القوية = تعب وتوتر.
  • بص على طول الجمل: قصيرة = توتر، طويلة وهادية = راحة.
  • اختار كلمة من بنك الكلمات بدل very happy / very sad.

تدريب

أولًا: Mood ولا Tone ولا Feeling؟

  1. 1. "The passage makes the reader feel calm." — Is this about mood or tone?

    Show answer
    Mood — بيتكلم عن إحساس القارئ.

  2. 2. "The narrator sounds honest and a little humorous." — mood or tone?

    Show answer
    Tone — صوت الراوي.

  3. 3. "He felt tempted to quit." — mood, tone or the narrator's feeling?

    Show answer
    The narrator's feeling.

تانيًا: اختار الإجابة الصح

  1. 4. The mood of the first hour is best described as ______. a. tense b. calm c. gloomy d. angry

    Show answer
    b. calm — quiet road, cool air, shimmering Nile.

  2. 5. Which sentence marks the first shift in mood? a. "I believed him." b. "Then, things changed." c. "We started early." d. "I was exhausted."

    Show answer
    b

  3. 6. At the low point, the narrator feels ______. a. proud b. tempted to give up c. bored d. relieved

    Show answer
    b

  4. 7. The tone of the final paragraph is ______. a. angry b. reflective c. frightened d. sarcastic

    Show answer
    b. reflective — بيتأمل في اللي اتعلمه.

  5. 8. "My legs ached. My breathing was uneven." These short sentences create a ______ mood. a. tense b. cheerful c. peaceful d. romantic

    Show answer
    a. tense

  6. 9. The overall shape of the narrator's emotional journey is ______. a. happy all the time b. sad all the time c. high → low → high d. low → high → low

    Show answer
    c

تالتًا: طلّع الدليل (Find words that show…)

  1. 10. Find TWO words/phrases that show the narrator's calm feeling at the start.

    Show answer
    "quiet", "shimmered softly", "enjoying the view" (أي اتنين).

  2. 11. Find a phrase that shows doubt is starting.

    Show answer
    "Then, things changed." / "My energy began to disappear."

  3. 12. Find a phrase that shows the narrator's determination.

    Show answer
    "Step by step, I kept going." / "one more pedal stroke"

  4. 13. Find a phrase that shows a mixed feeling at the end.

    Show answer
    "exhausted … But I was also strangely satisfied"

  5. 14. Find a phrase that shows the narrator's honest tone.

    Show answer
    "I seriously considered giving up" / "something less comfortable to admit"

رابعًا: جاوب الأسئلة

  1. 15. How does the writer create a calm mood in the first hour?

    Show answer
    Through peaceful description: "cool" air, a "quiet" road, the Nile that "shimmered softly" and the sound of birds. Long, smooth sentences make the reader relax.

  2. 16. How does the mood change in the middle of the extract? Give evidence.

    Show answer
    It becomes tense and uncomfortable. The sun "grew stronger", the road had less shade and the narrator "struggled to keep up".

  3. 17. What is the tone when the narrator says the friend cycled "as if the heat were simply decoration"?

    Show answer
    Humorous and slightly frustrated — بيهزر بس كمان متضايق إن صاحبه مرتاح وهو بيتعب.

  4. 18. Why is "But something stopped me" important?

    Show answer
    It is the turning point: the narrator's feelings shift from wanting to quit to being determined.

  5. 19. Describe the narrator's feelings at the end in three adjectives, with evidence.

    Show answer
    exhausted ("a deep, physical tiredness"), satisfied ("strangely satisfied"), proud ("a sense of achievement").

  6. 20. Is the narrator's relaxation at the start the same as his calm feeling at the end? Explain.

    Show answer
    No. At the start, it is lazy comfort from doing nothing. At the end, it is a deep calm that comes after effort and achievement.

  7. 21. Write 3-4 sentences describing a shift in your own feelings during a sports activity. Use shift words.

    Show answer
    مثال: At first, I felt nervous before the match. Then, after the first goal, I became confident. However, I was exhausted by half-time. By the end, I felt proud of my team.
ملخص الدرس في سطرين
  • Mood = إحساس القارئ (calm, tense, uplifting). Tone = صوت الراوي (honest, humorous, reflective).
  • مشاعر الراوي: confident ← relaxed ← uncertain ← tempted to quit ← determined ← satisfied & changed.
  • أهم جمل التحول: "Then, things changed." و "But something stopped me."
  • الإجابة = اسم الإحساس بكلمة دقيقة + اقتباس قصير + شرح، وفي سؤال التغيير: قبل + كلمة تحول + بعد.
