Grammar: so / such · too / enough · linking words (although, however, because of, so)

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

so + adjective (so hot) · such + a / an + adjective + noun (such a hot day) · so / such … that = the result.

too + adjective + to (too hot to walk) · adjective + enough + to (old enough to drive).

although + a clause · however, + a new sentence · because + a clause · because of + a noun.

في العربية نقول «على الرغم من أن… إلا أن…» فيستخدم المتعلمون كلمتين للربط: ✗ Although it was expensive, but I bought it. في الإنجليزية كلمة واحدة فقط تكفي: ✓ Although it was expensive, I bought it. وبعد because of يأتي اسم فقط (because of the rain)، وبعد because جملة كاملة (because it was raining). و such تأتي مع اسم (such a nice day)، و so مع صفة وحدها (so nice).

sosuch
so + adjective: The exam was so difficult.such + a / an + adjective + noun: It was such a difficult exam.
so … that: He spoke so fast that I didn't understand.such … that: It was such a hot day that we stayed inside.
so + adverb: She drives so carefully.such + plural / uncountable noun: such kind people · such good weather
LinkerFollowed byExample
althougha clause (subject + verb)Although it was late, we went out.
howevera new sentence + commaIt was late. However, we went out.
becausea clauseWe stayed in because it was raining.
because ofa nounWe stayed in because of the rain.
sothe resultIt was raining, so we stayed in.

✗ Although it was expensive, but I bought it. ✓ Although it was expensive, I bought it.

✗ because of it was raining ✓ because of the rain / because it was raining

✗ It was so a nice day. ✓ It was such a nice day. / The day was so nice.

✗ He isn't enough old to drive. ✓ He isn't old enough to drive. · It's too hot to walk (= we can't walk).

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Complete with so or such.

  1. 1) The weather was ___ hot that we stayed at home.
  2. 2) She's ___ a kind person.
  3. 3) It was ___ an interesting lecture.
  4. 4) The exam was ___ easy!
  5. 5) They are ___ good friends.
  6. 6) He drives ___ fast that I'm scared.
Show the answers
1) so 2) such 3) such 4) so 5) such 6) so

Exercise 2 · Choose the correct answer.

  1. 1) (Although / However) it was raining, we went to the beach.
  2. 2) The flat is small. (Although / However), it's in a great area.
  3. 3) The match was cancelled (because / because of) the bad weather.
  4. 4) I was tired, (so / because) I went to bed early.
  5. 5) She got the job (because / because of) she was well prepared.
  6. 6) (Although / Because) he's rich, he isn't happy.
Show the answers
1) Although 2) However 3) because of 4) so 5) because 6) Although

Exercise 3 · Rewrite the sentences with the word(s) in brackets.

  1. 1) The coffee is very hot. I can't drink it. (too … to)
  2. 2) He's seventeen. He can't vote. (old enough)
  3. 3) It was a very boring film. I fell asleep. (such … that)
  4. 4) The road was closed because there was an accident. (because of)
  5. 5) It was expensive, but I bought it. (although)
Show the answers
1) The coffee is too hot to drink. 2) He isn't old enough to vote. 3) It was such a boring film that I fell asleep. 4) The road was closed because of an accident. / The road was closed because of the accident. 5) Although it was expensive, I bought it. / I bought it although it was expensive.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) كان يومًا جميلًا لدرجة أننا ذهبنا إلى البحر.
  2. 2) على الرغم من أنه كان متعبًا، ذهب إلى العمل.
  3. 3) تأخرنا بسبب الزحمة.
  4. 4) الماء بارد جدًا ولا يمكن السباحة فيه.
Show the answers
1) It was such a beautiful day that we went to the beach. / It was such a nice day that we went to the sea. 2) Although he was tired, he went to work. / He went to work although he was tired. 3) We were late because of the traffic. / We were late because of the traffic jam. 4) The water is too cold to swim in.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — Write 120–150 words: an opinion paragraph — “Life in 2050 will be better than life today.” Do you agree? Give reasons for and against, and use although, however, because of, so … that and such … that. Then give your opinion aloud in two minutes, as if you were speaking in a debate.

Example: In my opinion, life in 2050 will be better than life today, although there will also be some serious problems. On the one hand, technology is developing so quickly that robots will probably do many difficult and dangerous jobs. Driverless cars might make our roads safer, and doctors will be able to cure diseases which kill millions of people today. Online courses will make education cheaper, so more young people in villages will be able to get a degree. On the other hand, the population is increasing, and because of climate change, summers in Egypt may become too hot to bear. However, I'm convinced that people will find solutions. Thirty years ago, nobody could imagine a smartphone. Today it is such a normal part of life that we can't live without it. To be honest, I'm really looking forward to the future.
