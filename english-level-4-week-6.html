Grammar: Future continuous and future perfect

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

Future continuous: will be + -ing = an action in progress at a time in the future. At nine tomorrow, I'll be flying to Aswan.

Future perfect: will have + past participle = an action finished before a time in the future. By Friday, I'll have finished the project.

العربية تستخدم «سـ / سوف» لكل المستقبل تقريبًا، أما الإنجليزية فتفرّق: «سأكون أعمل» (حدث مستمر في لحظة معينة في المستقبل) = will be working، و«سأكون قد أنهيت» (حدث سينتهي قبل لحظة معينة في المستقبل) = will have finished. الخطأ الشائع: By next year I will finish my degree، والأصح مع by أن نقول will have finished. وبعد by the time و when نستخدم المضارع البسيط وليس will: By the time you arrive (وليس will arrive).

FormUseExample
will be + -ingin progress at a future momentThis time tomorrow, I'll be sitting my exam.
will be + -ingpolite question about someone's plansWill you be coming to the meeting?
will have + p.p.finished before a future time (by …)By 2030, they will have built the new city.
won't have + p.p.not finished by thenI won't have finished by six.

Time words: future continuous → at this time tomorrow, this time next week, at 8 p.m. · future perfect → by, by the time, by the end of, before.

Wrong: By the time you will arrive … ✔ By the time you arrive …

Stative verbs (know, believe, own) don't usually take -ing: By June, I'll have known her for ten years.

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Future continuous or future perfect? Choose.

  1. 1) Don't phone at seven. We (will be having / will have had) dinner.
  2. 2) This time next week, Omar (will be lying / will have lain) on the beach in Hurghada.
  3. 3) By the end of the year, I (will be saving / will have saved) 20,000 pounds.
  4. 4) By 2035, scientists (will be sending / will have sent) people to Mars.
  5. 5) By the time the film starts, we (will be finishing / will have finished) our popcorn.
  6. 6) (Will you be using / Will you have used) the car tomorrow morning?
Show the answers
1) will be having 2) will be lying 3) will have saved 4) will have sent 5) will have finished 6) Will you be using

Exercise 2 · Complete with the future continuous or the future perfect.

  1. 1) By Monday, the technician ___ (install) the new software.
  2. 2) By the time you read this message, I ___ (leave) Cairo.
  3. 3) At ten tomorrow, I ___ (take) my driving test.
  4. 4) She ___ (not finish) the report by Thursday — it's too long.
  5. 5) In twenty years' time, robots ___ (do) most of the housework.
  6. 6) ___ you ___ (work) late tonight?
Show the answers
1) will have installed 2) will have left 3) will be taking 4) won't have finished 5) will be doing 6) Will … be working

Exercise 3 · Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) By next June, I will finish my degree.
  2. 2) By the time she will arrive, we'll have eaten.
  3. 3) This time tomorrow I'll be fly to London.
  4. 4) By 2030, they will have build a new airport.
  5. 5) Call me later — at nine I will have watched the match.
  6. 6) In five years, I'll have living in Alexandria.
Show the answers
1) By next June, I will have finished my degree. 2) By the time she arrives, we'll have eaten. 3) This time tomorrow I'll be flying to London. 4) By 2030, they will have built a new airport. 5) …at nine I will be watching the match. 6) In five years, I'll be living in Alexandria.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) في مثل هذا الوقت غدًا، سأكون مسافرًا إلى الأقصر.
  2. 2) بحلول عام ٢٠٣٠، سيكونون قد بنوا محطة طاقة شمسية جديدة.
  3. 3) هل ستستخدم الحاسوب الليلة؟
  4. 4) لن أكون قد أنهيت المشروع بحلول يوم الخميس.
  5. 5) حقق العلماء اكتشافًا مهمًا.
Show the answers
1) This time tomorrow, I'll be travelling to Luxor. / This time tomorrow, I'll be flying (going) to Luxor. 2) By 2030, they will have built a new solar power station. / By 2030, they will have built a new solar power plant. 3) Will you be using the computer tonight? 4) I won't have finished the project by Thursday. 5) Scientists have made an important discovery. / Scientists made an important discovery.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — “Life in 2040”: write 150–200 words about how science and technology will change your life and the world. Use the future continuous and the future perfect at least three times each. Then give a two-minute talk about your predictions.

Example: I think that by 2040 technology will have changed our lives completely. First of all, I believe that most people won't be driving their own cars. Driverless taxis will be taking us to work, and we will be using that time to read, rest or study. In Cairo, the traffic problem might finally be solved! Secondly, I am sure that scientists will have made huge breakthroughs in medicine. They will have found cures for some kinds of cancer, and doctors will be checking our health through small devices that we wear on our wrists. Energy will also be different. By 2040, Egypt will have built many more solar parks like the one in Benban near Aswan, and our homes will be using clean, renewable energy. As for me, I will be fifty years old. I hope I will be working from home three days a week and spending more time with my family. By then, I will have visited at least ten countries, and I will have learnt a third language — maybe Chinese!
