تانية بكالوريا · Reading · Unit 1

Rhetorical Devices and Their Effects

أدوات الإقناع اللي الكاتب بيستخدمها (rhetorical questions, intensifiers, hedging, absolute words وغيرها) وإزاي تكتب تأثير كل واحدة على القارئ.

تانية بكالوريا English

الدرس ده عن إيه؟

Rhetorical devices are language techniques that writers and speakers use to persuade or affect their audience. In an exam you must identify the device and explain its effect on the reader.

الدرس ده عن «أدوات الإقناع» اللي الكاتب بيستخدمها علشان يأثر عليك: سؤال مش مستني إجابة، كلمة بتكبّر الكلام، كلمة بتخليه حذر… وإزاي تقول الأداة دي اسمها إيه وعملت إيه في القارئ (effect).

ده من أهداف Unit 1 (Reading: «recognize rhetorical devices such as rhetorical questions and intensifiers, and understand their effects»). الكتاب بيعرض في جزء Writing in Action جدول اسمه «Types of persuasive writing techniques & their effects» فيه 4 أدوات: rhetorical questions, intensifiers, hedging words, absolute words + الـ neutral language. وبتيجي أسئلة عليهم في Consolidation Review A.

الفكرة ببساطة: صوت الكاتب

The same idea can sound strong, careful or certain depending on the device the writer chooses.

تخيل 4 أشخاص بيتكلموا عن الموبايل قبل النوم:

  • Do you really need your phone in bed?Rhetorical question: مش مستني إجابة. عايزك تفكر وتجاوب في دماغك «لأ».
  • Using your phone late at night is extremely harmful.Intensifier: extremely بتكبّر الكلام وتخليه أقوى.
  • Using your phone late at night may affect your sleep.Hedging: may بتخلي الكلام حذر ومش مبالغ.
  • You should never, ever use your phone in bed. No excuses.Absolute words: never / no excuses = ثقة كاملة من غير أي استثناء.

الفكرة واحدة، بس الصوت مختلف. وده اللي بيسألك عليه الامتحان: الأداة إيه، وليه الكاتب اختارها.

الأدوات الأربعة في الكتاب

الأداة (Device)يعني إيه؟كلمات أو شكلالتأثير (Effect) زي الكتاب
Rhetorical questionسؤال مش محتاج إجابةDo I really need this? / Who wants to live in a polluted world?makes the reader think — يخلي القارئ يفكر ويوافق في سره
Intensifiersكلمات بتقوّي الصفة أو الفعلvery, extremely, significantly, really, totally, incrediblymake ideas stronger — تخلي الفكرة أقوى وأهم
Hedging wordsكلمات بتخلي الكلام حذرmay, might, could, some, often, research suggestsshow uncertainty or caution — تبيّن حذر وعدل، وتخلي الكاتب يبان موثوق
Absolute wordsكلمات من غير أي استثناءalways, every, never, all, no excuses, everyonestate ideas with full confidence — بتخلي الكلام يبان أكيد 100%

حيلة للحفظ: RIHA
  • Rhetorical question = Reader thinks (القارئ يفكر).
  • Intensifier = Increase (يزوّد القوة).
  • Hedging = Hesitate (يتردد / يحذر).
  • Absolute = All or nothing (يا كله يا مفيش).

أدوات زيادة هتقابلها في القطع (أعمق من الكتاب)

Writers also use direct address, inclusive language, repetition, the rule of three, emotive language, statistics and expert opinion, and contrast.

الكتاب بيركز على الأربعة اللي فوق، بس في القطع والمقالات الإقناعية هتلاقي أدوات تانية كتير. خليك عارفها علشان تحلل أي نص:

«Why does the writer use…?» — كل الإجابات الممكنة

When you are asked "Why does the writer use ___ in line X?", choose the effect that fits the sentence. Here are the possible answers for each device.

لو الأداة…الإجابات الممكنة (Possible effects)
Rhetorical questionto make the reader think / to involve the reader / to make the reader agree with the writer / to challenge the reader's habits / to introduce the topic in an interesting way
Intensifierto make the idea stronger / to emphasise how serious or important the problem is / to show the writer's strong feelings / to make the reader take the issue seriously
Hedgingto show caution / to avoid exaggerating / to sound fair and balanced / to make the writer seem more reliable / to show that the evidence is not complete
Absolute wordsto sound completely certain / to make the claim seem like a fact / to leave no room for disagreement / to pressure the reader (and it can make the text sound biased)
Direct address / inclusive languageto make the reader feel personally involved / to create a sense of shared responsibility / to build a connection with the reader
Repetition / rule of threeto emphasise the main point / to make the message memorable / to create rhythm and make the argument sound strong
Emotive languageto make the reader feel sympathy, fear or anger / to persuade through feelings
Statistics / expert opinionto make the argument more convincing / to give evidence / to make the writer sound reliable

قالب الإجابة (اكتب الـ 3 أجزاء دول):

The writer uses [device], "[quotation]", to [effect]. This makes the reader [feel / think / do]…

  • The writer uses a rhetorical question, "Do we really want our children to swim in a sea full of plastic?", to make the reader think. This makes the reader realise that the answer is clearly no.الأداة + الاقتباس + التأثير + أثرها على القارئ.
  • The writer uses the intensifier "extremely" to emphasise how serious the problem is. This makes readers take the issue more seriously.نفس القالب مع intensifier.

قطعة للتطبيق: كلمة في طابور الصباح

اقرا الكلمة دي (نص أصلي) اللي طالب ألقاها في طابور المدرسة عن موضوع Unit 1. السطور مترقمة.

Look Up!

(1) Good morning, everyone. Have you ever counted how many times you check your phone in a single day? (2) I tried it last week, and the number was extremely embarrassing: eighty-three times. (3) We all know the feeling. (4) A message arrives, and we reply. (5) A video ends, and we watch another. (6) A friend posts a perfect photo, and we feel a little worse about ourselves.

(7) I am not saying that phones are evil. (8) Research suggests that social media may help some teenagers stay connected with friends who live far away. (9) However, a school counsellor told me that students who spend many hours online often find it harder to sleep and concentrate. (10) That is a really serious price to pay.

(11) So, what can we do? (12) We can switch off notifications. (13) We can leave our phones outside the bedroom. (14) We can spend breaks talking, laughing and playing together. (15) Your phone will always be there tomorrow. (16) Your school days will not. (17) Look up, my friends. Look up!

إزاي بييجي في الأسئلة؟ (أمثلة محلولة)

سؤال 1 (MCQ زي المراجعة): Which persuasive technique is used in line (1)?

a. Absolute words b. Rhetorical questions c. Intensifiers d. Hedging words

الإجابة: b. «Have you ever counted…?» سؤال الكاتب مش مستني إجابة عليه، هدفه يخلي السامع يفكر في عادته هو.

سؤال 2: Why does the speaker use "may" and "some" in line (8)?

Answer: He uses hedging ("may", "some") to show caution, to avoid exaggerating, to sound fair and balanced and to make himself seem more reliable. This makes the audience trust him more because he does not exaggerate.

لاحظ: الكاتب هنا بيعترف بالجانب التاني (فايدة السوشيال ميديا) بطريقة حذرة، وده بيخليه balanced.

سؤال 3: Find an example of repetition in paragraph 3 and explain its effect.

Answer: "We can… We can… We can…" (lines 12-14). The repetition emphasises that there are many simple solutions and makes the message memorable and positive.

أشهر الأخطاء
  • تفتكر إن أي سؤال = rhetorical question ← لو الكاتب هيجاوب عليه على طول (زي line 11 «So, what can we do?») ده سؤال بيقدّم الإجابة، ممكن نسميه rhetorical بس تأثيره introduce the solution.
  • تخلط never (absolute) بـ extremely (intensifier) ← absolute = مفيش استثناء، intensifier = بيقوّي الصفة.
  • تكتب اسم الأداة بس ← لازم الاقتباس + التأثير.
  • تكتب تأثير عام زي «to make it nice» ← استخدم تأثيرات من الجدول: make the reader think / emphasise / show caution.
  • تفتكر إن hedging ضعف ← في الكتابة الأكاديمية الـ hedging قوة، لأنه بيخليك عادل وموثوق.
إزاي تجاوب في الامتحان
  • دوّر على علامة الاستفهام ← rhetorical question.
  • دوّر على very / extremely / really / totally ← intensifier.
  • دوّر على may / might / could / some / suggests ← hedging.
  • دوّر على always / never / every / all / no excuses ← absolute.
  • وبعدين اكتب: device + quotation + effect.

تدريب

A. Choose the correct answer.

  1. 1. A writer asks, "Who wants to breathe dirty air every morning?" What is the main effect of this question? a. To give the reader a statistic b. To make the reader think and agree with the writer c. To show that the writer is unsure d. To weaken the argument

    Show answer
    b — السؤال مش مستني إجابة. هدفه يخلي القارئ يفكر ويقول في سره «محدش طبعًا»، فيوافق الكاتب.

  2. 2. Which of the following is a hedging word? a. always b. extremely c. might d. every

    Show answer
    c — might بتبيّن حذر.

  3. 3. Which of the following is an absolute word? a. could b. never c. very d. perhaps

    Show answer
    b — never من غير أي استثناء.

  4. 4. "This is a significantly bigger problem than we thought." The word "significantly" is… a. a hedging word b. an intensifier c. a rhetorical question d. neutral language

    Show answer
    b — بيقوّي bigger.

  5. 5. Which sentence uses neutral language? a. Fast fashion is a disaster! b. Some studies suggest that fast fashion increases textile waste. c. Everyone hates fast fashion. d. Isn't fast fashion terrible?

    Show answer
    b — some + studies suggest = متوازن ومن غير مشاعر.

  6. 6. The main effect of hedging is to… a. make ideas stronger b. show caution c. make the reader angry d. ask a question

    Show answer
    b — show uncertainty or caution.

B. Read the speech "Look Up!" again. Answer the questions.

  1. 7. Find an intensifier in line (2). What is its effect?

    Show answer
    "extremely embarrassing" — possible effects: it makes the feeling stronger, it emphasises how serious his phone habit is, it shows his strong and honest feelings (he was surprised by his own habit), and it makes the audience take the problem seriously.

  2. 8. Find an example of inclusive language in paragraph 1. Why does the speaker use it?

    Show answer
    "We all know the feeling" (line 3) — possible effects: to make the audience feel that everyone shares the problem, to build a connection with them, to create a sense of shared responsibility, and so that they do not feel attacked.

  3. 9. What device is used in lines (4)-(6)? What is its effect?

    Show answer
    Repetition of the same pattern ("…, and we…"). You can also call it the rule of three (three examples in a row). Possible effects: it shows how automatic our phone habits are, it emphasises the point, it makes the message memorable and it creates a strong rhythm.

  4. 10. Line (9) mentions "a school counsellor". Which device is this, and why is it used?

    Show answer
    Expert opinion — possible effects: to give the argument credibility, to give evidence, to make the argument more convincing and to make the speaker sound reliable.

  5. 11. Find an example of the rule of three in line (14).

    Show answer
    "talking, laughing and playing" — 3 activities that make the solution sound fun and memorable.

  6. 12. Lines (15)-(16) use contrast. Explain the effect.

    Show answer
    "Your phone will always be there tomorrow. Your school days will not." — possible effects: the contrast shows that school days are precious and limited, it makes the right choice clear, and it persuades the audience to value their school days more than the phone.

  7. 13. "Your phone will always be there tomorrow." Is "always" biased here? Explain.

    Show answer
    Not really biased — it is an absolute word, but it is used for emphasis about something almost certainly true, not to attack anyone. Absolute words are a problem when they make unfair claims.

  8. 14. Why does the speaker repeat "Look up!" at the end?

    Show answer
    To emphasise his main message, to make it memorable, and to end the speech in a strong and motivating way.

C. Name the device in each sentence.

  1. 15. Who wants to breathe polluted air every day?

    Show answer
    Rhetorical question

  2. 16. Recycling might reduce the amount of waste in our streets.

    Show answer
    Hedging

  3. 17. Every single shopper is fooled by Black Friday.

    Show answer
    Absolute words (وكمان biased claim)

  4. 18. The ocean is incredibly important for life on Earth.

    Show answer
    Intensifier

  5. 19. You can make a difference today.

    Show answer
    Direct address

  6. 20. Museums protect our past, teach our present and inspire our future.

    Show answer
    Rule of three (+ inclusive language «our»)

D. Write your own sentences (theme: food waste).

  1. 21. Write a rhetorical question.

    Show answer
    مثال: How can we throw away food when so many people are hungry?

  2. 22. Write a sentence with an intensifier.

    Show answer
    مثال: Food waste is a really serious problem in many homes.

  3. 23. Rewrite this absolute sentence with hedging: "Leftovers always go in the bin."

    Show answer
    مثال: Leftovers often end up in the bin in some homes.

  4. 24. Why does a writer use hedging in an argument? Give TWO possible effects.

    Show answer
    Any two: to show caution / to avoid exaggerating / to sound fair and balanced / to seem more reliable.
Model answer: analysing devices in a paragraph94 words
Task: How does the speaker of "Look Up!" use rhetorical devices to persuade his audience? Refer to TWO devices.

The speaker uses rhetorical devices to make his audience think about their phone habits. Firstly, he opens with a rhetorical question, "Have you ever counted how many times you check your phone in a single day?" This makes listeners reflect on their own behaviour from the very first line. Secondly, he uses repetition in "We can switch off notifications. We can leave our phones outside the bedroom." This emphasises that the solutions are simple and possible, so the audience feels hopeful rather than guilty. Overall, these devices make the speech persuasive without sounding aggressive.

ليه النموذج ده قوي؟
  • جملة البداية بتجاوب السؤال على طول (topic sentence).
  • كل أداة: اسم الأداة + اقتباس + التأثير (This makes…).
  • استخدم linking words: Firstly / Secondly / Overall.
  • الخاتمة بتربط الأدوات بالهدف العام: persuasive without sounding aggressive.
ملخص الدرس في سطرين
  • Rhetorical question = make the reader think. Intensifier (very / extremely) = make ideas stronger.
  • Hedging (may / might / could) = show caution. Absolute (always / never / every / no excuses) = full confidence.
  • Neutral language = balanced, objective, free from bias.
  • في الإجابة دايمًا: device + quotation + effect (This makes the reader…).
