10 sentences to memorise · جمل تحفظها

احفظ جملتين كل يوم، وقول كل جملة بصوت عالي 5 مرات لحد ما تطلع منك لوحدها.

  1. 1It is often claimed that social media brings people together.كثيرًا ما يُزعَم أن وسائل التواصل الاجتماعي تقرّب بين الناس.
  2. 2I see your point, but I'm afraid I can't agree.أفهم وجهة نظرك، لكن يؤسفني أنني لا أستطيع الموافقة.
  3. 3Admittedly, school uniforms are not cheap; even so, they reduce pressure on students.لا بدّ من الاعتراف بأن الزيّ المدرسي ليس رخيصًا؛ ومع ذلك فهو يقلّل الضغط على الطلاب.
  4. 4While it is true that cars are convenient, they are also a major cause of pollution in Cairo.في حين أنه من الصحيح أن السيارات مريحة، فهي أيضًا سبب رئيسي للتلوّث في القاهرة.
  5. 5Opponents argue that homework puts too much pressure on young children.يرى المعارضون أن الواجبات المنزلية تضع ضغطًا كبيرًا على الأطفال الصغار.
  6. 6With all due respect, there is no evidence to support that claim.مع كامل احترامي، لا يوجد دليل يدعم هذا الادعاء.
  7. 7On balance, I believe the advantages outweigh the disadvantages.إجمالًا، أعتقد أن المزايا تفوق العيوب.
  8. 8This brings me to my final point.وهذا يقودني إلى نقطتي الأخيرة.
  9. 9Not only is the plan expensive, but it is also unlikely to work.ليست الخطة مكلفة فحسب، بل من غير المحتمل أيضًا أن تنجح.
  10. 10What really matters is not who wins the debate, but what we learn from it.ما يهمّ حقًا ليس من يفوز بالمناظرة، بل ما نتعلّمه منها.

Grammar: Essay and presentation language · structuring an argument (Level 5 review)

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

A strong argument has a clear structure: introduction (background + your position) → body paragraphs (one main idea each: topic sentence, explanation, example or evidence) → counter-argument + rebuttal → conclusion (summary + final thought). This week you use the grammar of the whole level inside this structure.

كثير من المتعلمين يكتبون المقال كفقرة واحدة طويلة، أو يكرّرون رأيهم دون دليل، أو يتجاهلون الرأي الآخر. المقال والعرض الجيّدان لهما هيكل واضح: مقدمة تعرض الموضوع ورأيك، ثم فقرة لكل فكرة تبدأ بجملة رئيسية (topic sentence) ثم شرح ثم مثال أو دليل، ثم تعرض الرأي المخالف وتردّ عليه (Admittedly… Even so…)، ثم خاتمة تلخّص ولا تضيف أفكارًا جديدة. واستخدم قواعد هذا المستوى لتقوية أسلوبك: المبني للمجهول غير الشخصي، والجمل المؤكِّدة (cleft)، والقلب (inversion)، وأدوات التخفيف (hedging).

PartUseful languageLevel 5 grammar to use
IntroductionIt is often claimed that… · This essay will argue that… · In this talk I'd like to…impersonal passive: It is widely believed that…
Main pointsFirst and foremost… · Furthermore… · A case in point is… · Let me illustrate this with…cleft: What matters most is… · participle clause: Having considered…
Counter-argumentOpponents argue that… · Admittedly… · While it is true that…hedging: It could be argued that… · may · tend to
RebuttalEven so… · However, this ignores… · There is little evidence that…inversion: Not only… but also… · Rarely do…
ConclusionOn balance… · All things considered… · To sum up… · I'd like to finish by…probability: … is bound to / likely to…

One paragraph = one main idea. Start each body paragraph with a clear topic sentence.

Support every claim with a reason AND an example or a fact.

In a presentation, use signposts (Moving on to… · This brings me to…) so the audience always knows where you are.

Never introduce a new argument in the conclusion.

Exercise 1 · Which part of an essay is each sentence from? Write Introduction, Main point, Counter-argument, Rebuttal or Conclusion.

  1. 1) Opponents argue that uniforms limit students' creativity.
  2. 2) All things considered, I am convinced that uniforms do more good than harm.
  3. 3) It is often claimed that school uniforms are old-fashioned, but this essay will argue that they are still useful.
  4. 4) First and foremost, uniforms reduce the pressure to wear expensive clothes.

Exercise 3 · Rewrite the sentences with a Level 5 structure, using the words in brackets.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — Write a for-and-against essay (220–250 words): “Should university education be completely free?” Present both sides, answer the counter-argument and give your conclusion. Use grammar from at least five different weeks of this level. Then turn it into a 3-minute debate speech and record it.

Example: Should university education be completely free? It is often claimed that education is a right, not a privilege. Many people therefore believe that university should be completely free for every student. This essay will examine both sides before reaching a conclusion. First and foremost, free university education gives talented students from poor families the same opportunities as wealthy ones. A case in point is my cousin from a village near Minya, who became a doctor only because she did not have to pay tuition fees. Not only did free education change her life, but it also benefited her whole community, where she now works. Furthermore, a country with more graduates is likely to have a stronger economy. Educated workers tend to earn more, pay more tax and start more businesses. Opponents argue, however, that free education is extremely expensive for the state. Admittedly, the money has to come from somewhere, and overcrowded lecture halls with three hundred students can lower the quality of teaching. It could also be argued that students value their studies less when they pay nothing. Even so, these problems are not a reason to charge everyone. What is needed is better investment and smarter spending, such as linking free places to good grades and offering loans to those who can afford to repay them later. On balance, I believe that university education should remain free, provided that the government invests enough to keep standards high. After all, a nation's future is bound to depend on the quality of its graduates.
