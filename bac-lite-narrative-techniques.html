Narrative Techniques: simile, contrast and personal voice

يعني إيه simile وcontrast وpersonal voice، أمثلة من القطعة وأمثلة جديدة، وإزاي تكتب كل التأثيرات في سؤال Why does the author use…?

البكالوريا المصرية · Egyptian Baccalaureate › تانية بكالوريا English

الدرس ده عن إيه؟

Narrative techniques are the tools a writer uses to make a story vivid and personal. In Unit 2 the book asks you to use three of them in your diary entry: a simile, contrast and personal voice.

الدرس ده عن أدوات الكاتب (narrative techniques)، يعني الحاجات اللي الكاتب بيستخدمها علشان القصة تبقى حيّة ونحس بيها. في Unit 2 (بعد القطعة الأدبية «Not Exactly a Relaxing Day») الكتاب بيطلب منك في Diary Entry Checklist تستخدم: a simile, contrast, personal voice ومعاهم narrative tenses وكلمات الدرس وreflection.

هنشرح كل أداة: يعني إيه، إزاي تعرفها، أمثلة من القطعة، أمثلة جديدة، وتأثيرها (effect). وأهم حاجة: إزاي تجاوب سؤال «Why does the author use ___?».

1) التشبيه (Simile)

A simile compares two different things using like, as … as or as if / as though. It helps the reader imagine something clearly.

الـ simile يعني تشبيه: بنقارن حاجتين مختلفين باستخدام كلمة like (زي) أو as … as (… زي …) أو as if / as though (كأن). زي ما بنقول بالعربي: «عينيه زي البحر» أو «جري كأنه صاروخ».

الشكلمعناهمثال
like + nounزيtiredness like warm sand
as + adjective + as… زي …The road was as hot as an oven.
as if / as though + clauseكأنHe cycled as if nothing had happened.

أمثلة من القطعة:

  • "a deep, physical tiredness that sat in my legs and shoulders like warm sand"شبّه التعب بـ رملة دافية: تقيلة، قاعدة في جسمه، بس دافية ومريحة شوية. يعني تعب بس مش وحش، فيه إحساس بالرضا.
  • "continued cycling as if the heat were simply decoration"صاحبه سايق كأن الحر ده مجرد ديكور، يعني مش فارق معاه خالص. ده بيبين الفرق بين خبرة الصاحب وضعف الراوي.
  • "as if … the distance were a minor detail"كأن المسافة حاجة صغيرة مش مهمة. بيأكد إن الصاحب شايفها سهلة.

أمثلة جديدة (من موضوعات الوحدة):

  • After the match, my legs felt like jelly.رجليا بقت زي الجيلي = ضعيفة ومش ثابتة من التعب.
  • The pool was as calm as glass in the early morning.حمام السباحة كان هادي زي الإزاز = ساكن تمامًا.
  • I stared at my phone as if it were a magnet.كنت باصص للموبايل كأنه مغناطيس = مش قادر أبعد عنه (screen time).
  • The final kilometre stretched in front of me like a never-ending ribbon.آخر كيلو كان زي شريطة ملهاش آخر = حاسس إنه طويل جدًا.
  • Her paintbrush moved across the canvas like a dancer.الفرشة بتتحرك زي راقصة = بخفة وجمال (creative arts).
مش كل like تبقى simile!
  • ✗ I like cycling. ← ده فعل «بحب»، مش تشبيه.
  • ✗ He looks like his brother. ← مقارنة بين حاجتين من نفس النوع (شخص وشخص)، ده مش simile أدبي.
  • ✓ He ran like the wind. ← حاجتين مختلفين (إنسان وريح) = simile.
  • الـ metaphor (استعارة) من غير like/as: «My legs were lead.» ← ده مش simile.

الـ effects بتاعة الـ simile (اكتب منها اللي ينفع في السؤال):

  • helps the reader picture / imagine the scene or feeling clearly — يخلي القارئ يتخيل.
  • makes the description vivid and more interesting — يخلي الوصف حي.
  • explains an abstract feeling (like tiredness) through something concrete we know — يوضح إحساس صعب بحاجة نعرفها.
  • emphasises a quality (how heavy, how easy, how hot) — يأكد صفة.
  • shows the narrator's feelings or attitude — يبين مشاعر الراوي.
  • creates a mood (calm, tense, humorous) — يعمل جو معين.
  • can add humour — ممكن يضحّك (زي «the heat were simply decoration»).

2) التضاد / المقارنة (Contrast)

Contrast means putting two opposite things next to each other: before and after, easy and hard, calm and tense. It makes the difference stand out and shows change.

الـ contrast يعني إنك تحط حاجتين عكس بعض جنب بعض: قبل وبعد، سهل وصعب، هادي ومتوتر، أنا وصاحبي. الهدف إن الفرق يبان وإننا نحس بالتغيير. كلمات بتدل عليه: but, however, while, whereas, then, instead, although, at first … by the end.

أمثلة من القطعة:

  • Home vs the Nile trip: "complete stillness" on the sofa ↔ cycling for hours in the sun.الراحة في البيت قصاد المجهود في الرحلة. ده تضاد في فكرة الـ leisure.
  • The sun: "gentle and golden at dawn" ↔ "stronger and more direct".الشمس الحلوة الصبح قصاد الشمس القوية بعدين = بيبين إن اليوم اتقلب.
  • The friend vs the narrator: the friend cycles easily ↔ "I struggled to keep up."صاحبه مرتاح وهو بيعاني = بيأكد تعبه.
  • The ending: "exhausted" ↔ "strangely satisfied".تعبان وراضي في نفس الوقت = الإحساس معقد ومختلط.
  • Before vs after: leisure = doing nothing ↔ leisure can come "from effort".فكرته في الأول قصاد فكرته في الآخر = اتغير واتعلم.

أمثلة جديدة:

  • At first, I spent my evenings scrolling. By the end of the month, I was painting every night.قبل وبعد = تغيير في العادات.
  • While my brother found chess boring, I found it exciting.شخصين وكل واحد له رأي = while.
  • The gym was noisy and crowded, whereas the park was silent and empty.مكانين عكس بعض = whereas.
  • I expected a lazy afternoon. Instead, I climbed a mountain.المتوقع قصاد اللي حصل = instead.

الـ effects بتاعة الـ contrast:

  • highlights the difference between two things — يبرز الفرق.
  • shows a change in the narrator's feelings or ideas — يبين التغيير.
  • creates surprise or tension — يعمل مفاجأة أو توتر.
  • makes the message clearer (comfort vs challenge) — يوضح الرسالة.
  • makes the reader compare and think — يخلي القارئ يقارن ويفكر.
  • can show irony (what we expect vs what happens) — يبين المفارقة.

3) الصوت الشخصي (Personal Voice)

Personal voice is the writer's own way of speaking to the reader. It uses the first person (I, me, my), honest feelings, private thoughts, questions to oneself and a natural, sometimes humorous style.

الـ personal voice يعني الكاتب بيكلمك كأنه صاحبك: بيستخدم I / me / my، بيقول مشاعره بصراحة، بيعترف بضعفه، بيسأل نفسه، وأسلوبه طبيعي وأحيانًا فيه خفة دم. ده عكس الكتابة الرسمية الباردة. الكتاب في Unit 8 كمان بيقول: «Use the first person (I, my, me) to share your thoughts and experiences.»

علامة الـ personal voiceبالعربيمثال من القطعة
first personضمير المتكلم"My idea of leisure was simple"
honest confessionاعتراف بصراحة"I seriously considered giving up"
thinking aloud / possibilitiesبيفكر بصوت عالي"Perhaps it was pride … Or perhaps …"
humour about himselfبيضحك على نفسه"laughed at my complaints"
private feelingsمشاعر داخلية"I was genuinely curious whether I could finish"
direct reflectionتأمل ودرس"That day changed my understanding of leisure."
short emotional sentencesجمل قصيرة فيها إحساس"My legs ached. My breathing was uneven."

أمثلة جديدة:

  • I'll be honest: I hated the first swimming lesson.اعتراف صريح + I = personal voice.
  • Was I scared? Of course I was.بيسأل نفسه ويجاوب = قريب من القارئ.
  • My phone, my faithful friend, stayed in my bag all day, and I survived!خفة دم + مشاعر = صوت شخصي.
  • I still don't know why I agreed, but I'm glad I did.تفكير داخلي وتأمل.

الـ effects بتاعة الـ personal voice:

  • makes the story feel real and believable — القصة تبان حقيقية.
  • builds a close relationship with the reader — يقرّب القارئ من الراوي.
  • helps the reader share / understand the narrator's feelings — نحس باللي هو حاسه.
  • shows the narrator's personality (honest, funny, lazy, proud) — يبين شخصيته.
  • makes the narrator likeable because he admits weakness — نحبه لأنه صريح.
  • makes the lesson / reflection more convincing — الدرس في الآخر يقنعنا أكتر.

قاعدة المدرس: إزاي تجاوب «Why does the author use ___?»

When the question asks why the writer uses a technique, name the technique, quote it briefly, then give all the possible effects that fit the example.

لما يسألك «Why does the author use a simile / contrast / personal voice in line X?» اكتب كل الـ effects الممكنة اللي تنفع مع المثال، مش effect واحد. الخطوات:

  1. سمّي الأداة: The writer uses a simile …
  2. اقتبس كلمتين: … "like warm sand" …
  3. اشرح المعنى في جملة: … to compare his tiredness to warm, heavy sand.
  4. اكتب كل التأثيرات: to help the reader imagine…, to make the description vivid…, to emphasise…, to show his feelings…, to create a mood…
Model answer: "Why does the author use a simile in 'like warm sand'?"99 words
Task: Why does the author use a simile in the phrase "like warm sand"?

The author uses a simile, "like warm sand", to compare the narrator's tiredness to heavy, warm sand sitting in his legs and shoulders. The simile helps the reader to imagine exactly how his body felt. It makes the description vivid and turns an abstract feeling, tiredness, into something concrete that we can almost touch. It emphasises how heavy and deep his exhaustion was. At the same time, the word "warm" suggests a pleasant, comforting feeling, so the simile shows that his tiredness was mixed with satisfaction. Finally, it creates a calm, peaceful mood at the end of the story.

ليه النموذج ده قوي؟
  • سمّينا الأداة واقتبسنا كلمتين بس.
  • شرحنا المقارنة (tiredness ↔ sand).
  • كتبنا كل التأثيرات: imagine، vivid، abstract → concrete، emphasise، feelings (warm = satisfaction)، mood.

الأدوات التلاتة مع بعض

الأداةإزاي تعرفهاأشهر effectمثال من القطعة

استخدمهم في الـ Diary Entry
  • Simile في النهاية علشان توصف تعبك أو فرحتك (الكتاب بيقول كده في The ending).
  • Contrast بين أول اليوم وآخره، أو بينك وبين صاحبك.
  • Personal voice: اكتب بـ I، اعترف بخوفك أو كسلك، اسأل نفسك سؤال.
  • متحطش 10 similes! واحد أو اتنين حلوين أحسن.
أشهر الأخطاء
  • ✗ تكتب effect واحد عام: «to make it interesting» وبس ← ✓ اكتب كل الـ effects اللي تنفع.
  • ✗ تقول simile لـ «I like cycling» ← ✓ like هنا فعل.
  • ✗ تسمي contrast «comparison» وخلاص ← ✓ قول contrast ووضّح الحاجتين العكس.
  • ✗ تفتكر personal voice يعني «بيستخدم I» بس ← ✓ كمان المشاعر، الصراحة، الأسئلة، خفة الدم.
  • ✗ تنقل الجملة كلها ← ✓ اقتباس كلمتين أو تلاتة.

تدريب

أولًا: سمّي الأداة (Simile / Contrast / Personal voice)

  1. 1. "He cycled as if the heat were simply decoration."

    Show answer
    Simile (as if).

  2. 2. "The sun, which had been gentle and golden at dawn, grew stronger."

    Show answer
    Contrast (الشمس قبل وبعد).

  3. 3. "Perhaps it was pride … Or perhaps it was something less comfortable to admit."

    Show answer
    Personal voice (بيفكر بصوت عالي ويعترف).

  4. 4. "I was exhausted … But I was also strangely satisfied."

    Show answer
    Contrast (تعبان ↔ راضي) + personal voice.

  5. 5. My heart was beating like a drum before the race.

    Show answer
    Simile (like).

  6. 6. I'll admit it: I was jealous of my friend's energy.

    Show answer
    Personal voice (اعتراف).

  7. 7. Last year I hated sport, whereas now I can't live without it.

    Show answer
    Contrast (whereas، قبل/بعد).

تانيًا: اختار الإجابة الصح

  1. 8. Which sentence contains a simile? a. I like painting. b. She looks like her mother. c. The lake was as still as a mirror. d. I feel tired.

    Show answer
    c — as … as + حاجتين مختلفين.

  2. 9. The main effect of contrasting the narrator with his friend is to ______. a. show the friend is lazy b. emphasise how much the narrator struggled c. describe the Nile d. end the story

    Show answer
    b

  3. 10. Personal voice makes the extract ______. a. formal and distant b. real and close to the reader c. scientific d. shorter

    Show answer
    b

  4. 11. Which linking word does NOT usually show contrast? a. however b. whereas c. because d. instead

    Show answer
    c. because — دي سبب.

تالتًا: Why does the author use ___? (اكتب كل التأثيرات)

  1. 12. Why does the author use the simile "as if the heat were simply decoration"?

    Show answer
    To compare the heat to harmless decoration and show that the friend did not notice it. It emphasises how experienced and strong the friend was, contrasts him with the suffering narrator, adds humour, and helps the reader imagine the scene. It may also show the narrator's feeling of frustration that his friend finds it so easy.

  2. 13. Why does the author contrast the sun "at dawn" with the sun later in the morning?

    Show answer
    To show the change from a pleasant start to a difficult ride, to create tension, to highlight how the conditions became harder, and to mirror the change in the narrator's mood from relaxed to uncertain.

  3. 14. Why does the author use short sentences such as "My legs ached. My breathing was uneven."?

    Show answer
    To show the narrator's personal, physical suffering, to make the reader feel his tiredness, to slow the pace and create tension, to make the moment dramatic, and to make it sound like real, honest thoughts.

  4. 15. Why does the author use personal voice throughout the extract?

    Show answer
    To make the story real and believable, to build a close link with the reader, to let us share his feelings, to show his personality (honest, a little lazy, proud), to add humour, and to make the final lesson more convincing.

  5. 16. Why does the author begin with the narrator's love of staying at home?

    Show answer
    To create a contrast with the hard trip, to show how much he changes, to make the ending more surprising, and to make the message about leisure clearer.

رابعًا: اكتب بنفسك (Write your own)

  1. 17. Write a simile to describe how you feel after a long exam.

    Show answer
    مثال: My brain felt like an empty battery. — تشبيه التعب الذهني ببطارية فاضية.

  2. 18. Write a simile about a crowded place.

    Show answer
    مثال: The market was as busy as a beehive.

  3. 19. Write two sentences that show contrast between your screen time before and after a change.

    Show answer
    مثال: At first, I spent six hours a day on my phone. Now, I spend that time reading and playing football.

  4. 20. Rewrite in a personal voice: "The walk was long and the walker was tired."

    Show answer
    مثال: Honestly, I thought that walk would never end. My feet were begging me to stop!

  5. 21. Write a short diary opening (2-3 sentences) using all three techniques.

    Show answer
    مثال: Dear Diary, I thought today would be easy. Instead, the hike was like climbing a wall. I'll be honest: I nearly cried!
