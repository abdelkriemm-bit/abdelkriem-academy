تانية بكالوريا · Listening · Units 2, 4, 5

Listening for Main Ideas and Specific Information

إزاي تفرّق بين الفكرة الأساسية للتسجيل كله والمعلومة المحددة (رقم، اسم، سبب)، وتجاوب على الاتنين صح.

تانية بكالوريا English

الدرس ده عن إيه؟

When you listen, you need two different skills: listening for the main idea (the big message of the whole recording) and listening for specific information (small exact details such as numbers, names, dates and reasons).

الدرس ده عن مهارتين مختلفين خالص: الفكرة الأساسية (main idea) يعني التسجيل كله بيتكلم عن إيه وعايز يوصل لإيه، والمعلومة المحددة (specific information) يعني رقم أو اسم أو سبب واحد بالظبط.

في الكتاب المهارة دي بتيجي في Unit 2 (الـ research report عن الـ screen time والتسجيل عن الرياضة)، وUnit 4 (الكتاب بيقول: «extract specific information from an audio broadcast» زي The Food Waste Minute، والراديو عن الاستهلاك أونلاين)، وUnit 5 (البودكاست عن الطاقة: «extract key information, including main ideas, specific details»).

الفرق ببساطة: الصورة الكبيرة والصورة الصغيرة

Main idea = What is the whole recording about, and what is the speaker's message? Specific information = one exact fact that answers *how many, when, who, where, which, why*.

تخيل إنك بتتفرج على ماتش من فوق خالص في الاستاد: هتعرف مين بيكسب ومين مسيطر. ده main idea. لكن لو حد سألك «الجون اتجاب في الدقيقة كام؟» لازم تكون مركز في لحظة معينة. ده specific information.

أولًا: إزاي ألاقي الـ Main idea؟

  1. اسمع المقدمة كويس: المتكلم غالبًا بيقول الموضوع صريح: «Today we're looking at…».
  2. خد بالك من الكلام اللي بيتكرر: لو كلمة زي balance اتقالت 4 مرات، يبقى هي قلب الموضوع.
  3. اسمع الخاتمة: «So, the key message is… / In the end… / The first step is…».
  4. اختبر الاختيار: اسأل نفسك «الاختيار ده بيغطي كل التسجيل؟ ولا جزء صغير بس؟». الصح هو اللي بيغطي الكل.
  5. ابعد عن الكلام المتطرف: اختيارات فيها completely / always / only / banned غالبًا غلط، لأن تسجيلات الكتاب غالبًا متوازنة.
  • The goal is not to stop using screens but to find a healthy balance.دي جملة main idea: بتلخص الرسالة كلها (التوازن مش المنع).
  • Teenagers spend an average of seven hours a day on screens outside school.دي specific information: رقم واحد (seven hours).
  • In short, enjoying sport matters more than winning.main idea: In short قبل الرسالة الأساسية.
  • The club meets every Thursday at five o'clock.specific information: يوم وساعة.
  • Small changes at home can cut food waste by a third.فيها الاتنين: الفكرة (تغييرات صغيرة بتفرق) + رقم محدد (a third = الثلث).

ثانيًا: إزاي ألاقي الـ Specific information؟

  1. اقرا السؤال قبل ما تسمع وحدد نوع الإجابة: رقم؟ اسم؟ مكان؟ سبب؟ لو فراغ: اسم ولا فعل ولا صفة؟
  2. حط خط تحت الكلمة المفتاحية (keyword) في السؤال، واستنى تسمعها أو كلمة بنفس معناها.
  3. اتوقع المرادفات: السؤال ممكن يقول reduce والتسجيل يقول cut، السؤال children والتسجيل young people.
  4. خلي بالك من التصحيح: المتكلم ساعات يقول رقم وبعدين يصححه: «about thirty… actually, closer to fifty». الإجابة هي الأخير.
  5. اكتب بسرعة واختصر: 40% أو 7h، وبعد التسجيل اكتبها كاملة وصح.
الكلمة في السؤالممكن تسمعها كده في التسجيلمعناها
reducecut / lower / bring downيقلل
increaserise / go up / growيزيد
half50 per cent / one in twoالنص
a quarter25 per cent / one in fourالربع
teenagersyoung people / students aged 14 to 17المراهقين
advantagesbenefits / the good sideالمميزات
problemschallenges / obstacles / risksالمشاكل
cheapercosts less / more affordableأرخص
أشهر الأخطاء في الأرقام
  • ✗ fifteen (15) ← ✓ fifty (50): في fifteen الضغط على teen في الآخر، وفي fifty الضغط في الأول.
  • ✗ تكتب 30% وهو قال «thirty per cent of the boys» والسؤال عن girls ← ✓ اسمع الرقم بتاع مين.
  • ✗ تكتب أول رقم سمعته ← ✓ لو صحح الرقم بعدها (actually, in fact) خد الأخير.
  • ✗ تكتب الكلمة غلط في الإملاء ← ✓ راجع الإملاء بعد التسجيل، والجمع (s) لو الجملة محتاجاه.

تطبيق 1: Research report – Unit 2

نص التسجيل 1: Weekends Off-Screen: A Research Report

Good morning. In this report, we look at what happens when teenagers spend their weekends away from screens. Our aim is not to blame technology, but to understand how young people can find a healthier balance.

The study took place in three secondary schools in Mansoura and lasted eight weeks. Two hundred and forty students aged fifteen to seventeen took part. Before the study, they spent an average of six and a half hours a day on their phones at weekends.

During the study, half of the students agreed to a simple rule: no phones between ten in the morning and six in the evening on Fridays. Instead, they chose offline activities, such as football, drawing, helping in family businesses or visiting grandparents.

What did the researchers find? First, the off-screen group reported better sleep. About sixty per cent said they fell asleep more quickly on Friday nights. Second, they described their mood as calmer. Several students said they no longer felt the need to check what their friends were doing every few minutes. Third, and this surprised the researchers, the students did not do worse in their schoolwork. In fact, their homework marks improved slightly.

However, not everything was easy. In the first two weeks, about one third of the students broke the rule at least once. Many said the hardest part was feeling left out of group chats.

So, what is the key message? Screens are part of modern life, and they can be useful. But a regular break, even one day a week, can give teenagers more time to rest, move and connect with the people around them.

ملحوظة: نصوص التسجيل في الدرس ده مكتوبة مخصوص للتدريب، والأسماء والأرقام اللي فيها للتمرين بس. اقرا النص بصوت عالي أو خلي حد يقراه لك وانت مقفّل الكتاب.

إزاي بييجي في الأسئلة؟ (أمثلة محلولة)

نوع 1: Main purpose (MCQ)

What is the speaker's main purpose?

a. To prove that phones should be banned for teenagers.

b. To report the results of a study on screen-free weekends and suggest a balance.

c. To advertise offline hobbies such as drawing.

d. To explain how group chats work.

الإجابة: b. ليه؟ المقدمة قالت «Our aim is not to blame technology, but to understand… a healthier balance»، والخاتمة رجعت لنفس الفكرة. a فيها banned (متطرفة وعكس كلامه)، وc وd تفاصيل صغيرة اتذكرت مرة واحدة.

نوع 2: Specific information (Identify)

How many students took part in the study?

الإجابة: 240 students (two hundred and forty). ليه؟ السؤال How many يبقى مستنيين رقم، والكلمة المفتاحية took part اتقالت بالظبط. خلي بالك متخلطش مع 8 weeks أو 3 schools.

نوع 3: Listen again and fill in the blanks

About sixty per cent said they fell asleep more ________ on Friday nights.

الإجابة: quickly. ليه؟ بعد more وبعد فعل (fell asleep) محتاجين ظرف (adverb)، ودي الكلمة اللي اتقالت.

تطبيق 2: Radio broadcast – Unit 4

نص التسجيل 2: Smart Shopper – Radio Programme

Welcome back to Smart Shopper, the programme about the choices we make with our money. Today: online flash sales. You've seen them. A red timer on the screen says 'Only 2 hours left!' and suddenly you feel you must buy now.

Why does this work so well? Psychologists call it urgency. When we think time is running out, we stop comparing prices carefully. A study of online shoppers found that people spent around thirty per cent more when a countdown clock was shown. Another trick is social proof: messages like '120 people bought this today'. They make us believe that, if everyone is buying it, it must be good.

We asked listeners to send us their stories. Salma, a student from Tanta, told us she bought three pairs of trainers during a midnight sale. 'I only needed one pair,' she said. 'The timer made me panic.'

So how can you protect yourself? Our money expert, Mr Hany Saad, has a simple rule: wait twenty-four hours. If you still want the item tomorrow, and it's still within your budget, then buy it. He also suggests turning off shopping app notifications, especially at night.

The main thing to remember is that a sale is only a saving if you really needed the item. After the news, we'll talk about second-hand clothes markets. Stay with us.

لاحظ: الـ main idea اتقالت في الأول (online flash sales) واتلخصت في الآخر («a sale is only a saving if you really needed the item»). والـ specific information متوزعة في النص: 30%، 120 people، Tanta، three pairs، 24 hours.

تدريب

A. Main idea: choose the correct answer from a, b, c or d.

  1. 1. Transcript 1 is mainly about a. the dangers of group chats. b. the effects of spending weekend time away from phones. c. how to improve homework marks. d. football clubs in Mansoura.

    Show answer
    b — ده اللي بيغطي التسجيل كله من أوله لآخره. الباقي تفاصيل.

  2. 2. Which is the best title for Transcript 2? a. Why Timers and Crowds Make Us Spend b. The History of Trainers c. A Student's Life in Tanta d. How to Open an Online Shop

    Show answer
    a — التسجيل كله عن urgency (التايمر) وsocial proof (الناس اللي اشترت). Tanta وtrainers تفاصيل جوه المثال.

  3. 3. What is the key message of Transcript 2? a. Never shop online. b. Sales are always a trick. c. Only buy in a sale if you really need the item. d. Buy quickly before the timer ends.

    Show answer
    c — آخر جملة: «a sale is only a saving if you really needed the item». a وb متطرفين (never, always).

  4. 4. What is the speaker's attitude to technology in Transcript 1? a. completely negative b. balanced c. completely positive d. not interested

    Show answer
    b. balanced — قال «Screens are part of modern life, and they can be useful. But a regular break…».

  3. 7. On which day was the 'no phones' rule?

    Show answer
    Friday. — «on Fridays».

  4. 8. What surprised the researchers?

  5. 9. What was the hardest part for students who broke the rule?

    Show answer
    Feeling left out of group chats. — «the hardest part was feeling left out».

  6. 10. How much more did shoppers spend when a countdown clock was shown?

    Show answer
    Around thirty per cent (30%) more.

  7. 11. How many pairs of trainers did Salma buy?

    Show answer
    Three. — وقالت إنها محتاجة واحد بس. السؤال عن اللي اشترته.

  8. 12. What will the programme discuss after the news?

    Show answer
    Second-hand clothes markets. — آخر جملتين في التسجيل.

  2. 14. Instead of phones, students chose ________ activities such as football and drawing.

    Show answer

  3. 15. In the first two weeks, about one ________ of the students broke the rule.

    proof — social proof: إن الناس كلها بتعمل كده فتصدّق إنه كويس.

E. Answer in full sentences.

  1. 23. Analyse: Why does the speaker in Transcript 2 include Salma's story?

    Show answer
    مثال: The speaker includes Salma's story to give a real example of urgency. It shows that the timer can make people buy more than they need. — القصة دليل على الفكرة الأساسية.

  2. 24. Evaluate (JRE): Do you think a one-day screen break is enough to improve well-being? Use evidence from Transcript 1.

ملخص الدرس في سطرين
  • Main idea = التسجيل كله عن إيه + الرسالة. دوّر عليها في الأول والآخر وفي الكلام اللي بيتكرر.
  • Specific information = رقم، اسم، وقت، سبب. اقرا السؤال الأول وحدد نوع الإجابة والـ keyword.
  • التسجيل غالبًا بيقول المعلومة بكلام تاني (synonyms): cut = reduce، one in four = a quarter.
  • ابعد عن الاختيارات المتطرفة (always, never, completely, banned) في أسئلة الـ main idea.
  • في الأرقام: fifteen ≠ fifty، ولو المتكلم صحح الرقم خد الأخير.
