تانية بكالوريا · Listening · Units 1, 5

The Speaker's Perspective

إزاي تعرف رأي المتكلم وموقفه من كلامه: الصفات والكلمات القوية والحذرة والأسئلة البلاغية والخاتمة.

تانية بكالوريا English

الدرس ده عن إيه؟

A speaker's perspective is their point of view: what they think and feel about the topic. To find it, listen to how they say things, not only what they say: their word choice, their tone, the evidence they choose and their final message.

الدرس ده عن سؤال «المتكلم رأيه إيه؟». مش بس المعلومات اللي قالها، لكن موقفه: مؤيد؟ معارض؟ متوازن؟ حذر؟ متفائل؟ وعايز يوصلنا لإيه؟

في الكتاب ده في Unit 1 (broadcast عن السوشيال ميديا وصورة المراهق عن نفسه: الكتاب بيدربك على «media bias, intensifiers, and rhetorical devices»، وبيسألك «What message is the speaker trying to convey?»)، وفي Unit 5 (الكتاب بيقول: «the speaker's perspective on renewable energy»، وبيسألك «Why is Dr. Hussain optimistic…?»).

يعني إيه Perspective؟

Perspective = attitude (positive / negative / balanced) + purpose (to inform / persuade / warn / advise) + certainty (sure / cautious).

تخيل اتنين بيتكلموا عن نفس الماتش: واحد مشجع للفريق التاني بيقول «الحكم ظالمنا»، والتاني محايد بيقول «الماتش كان متكافئ». نفس الحدث، بس زاوية الرؤية مختلفة. ده بالظبط الـ perspective.

الكلمة (English)معناهاالمتكلم بيقول حاجات زي…
positive / supportiveمؤيد، شايف الموضوع كويسThis is a huge opportunity. / I strongly believe…
negative / criticalمعارض، بينتقدThis is a serious problem. / Sadly, …
balancedمتوازن، بيعرض الناحيتينOn the one hand… on the other hand… / It has benefits, but…
optimisticمتفائل بالمستقبلI'm confident that… / We're getting closer every year.
cautiousحذر، مش متأكد 100%This may / might / could… / The evidence suggests…
worried / concernedقلقانWhat worries me is… / I'm concerned that…
biasedمنحاز، بيعرض ناحية واحدة بس بكلام قويEveryone knows… / It is completely obvious that…

إزاي أعرف رأي المتكلم؟ 6 علامات

  1. الكلمات اللي بتوصف (evaluative words): amazing, worrying, unfair, impressive, dangerous. الصفة بتكشف الرأي.
  2. الكلمات اللي بتقوّي (intensifiers): extremely, really, absolutely, deeply, completely. كل ما زادت، كل ما الرأي بقى أقوى (وساعات منحاز).
  3. الكلمات اللي بتخفف (hedging): may, might, could, perhaps, it seems, suggests. المتكلم هنا حذر وبيحترم الدليل.
  4. الكلام المطلق (absolute language): always, never, everyone, nobody. ده غالبًا علامة bias.
  5. الأسئلة البلاغية (rhetorical questions): «Do we really want our children to…?» مش مستني إجابة، هو بيقنعك برأيه.
  6. كلمات العكس والخاتمة: بعد However / But / Although بييجي غالبًا رأي المتكلم الحقيقي. وآخر جملة = الرسالة.
  • Social media is absolutely destroying young people's confidence.intensifier + كلام قوي ← رأي سلبي جدًا وممكن يكون biased.
  • Heavy use may be linked to lower self-esteem, although more research is needed.hedging (may) + although ← رأي حذر ومتوازن.
  • What worries me most is how quickly teenagers compare themselves to others.«اللي مقلقني» ← المتكلم concerned.
  • Solar power has become remarkably cheap, and I'm confident the trend will continue.remarkably + I'm confident ← optimistic.
  • Do we really believe a perfect photo shows a perfect life?سؤال بلاغي ← المتكلم عايزك تقول «لأ»، يعني بينتقد الصور المثالية.
  • Renewables are growing fast. However, the grid isn't ready yet.بعد However رأيه: فيه تحدي حقيقي ← متوازن مع شوية حذر.
  • Everyone knows that gaming is a waste of time.«الكل عارف» ← كلام مطلق ← bias (مفيش دليل).
  • The evidence suggests that short breaks improve focus.suggests ← بيقدّم دليل بحذر ← objective / cautious.

الرأي vs الحقيقة vs رأي حد تاني

Do not mix the speaker's opinion with facts or with other people's opinions that the speaker reports.

الجملةالنوعليه؟
«Sixty per cent of users are under twenty-five.»fact (حقيقة)رقم ممكن نتأكد منه
«I think this figure is alarming.»speaker's opinionI think + alarming
«Some parents argue that phones should be banned.»reported opinionرأي الأهالي مش رأي المتكلم
«Critics claim solar farms take too much land.»reported opinionCritics claim = بيحكي رأيهم
«Personally, I disagree with them.»speaker's opinionPersonally + I disagree
أشهر الأخطاء
  • ✗ تفتكر إن رأي الناقدين هو رأي المتكلم ← ✓ اسمع «Critics say / Some people believe» وبعدين اسمع هو بيرد إزاي.
  • ✗ تحكم على رأيه من جملة واحدة في النص ← ✓ اسمع الخاتمة: فيها الموقف النهائي.
  • ✗ تقول إن الحذر (may, might) يعني «مش عارف» ← ✓ ده معناه إنه علمي وأمين مع الدليل.
  • ✗ تختار «completely negative» لمجرد إنه ذكر مشكلة ← ✓ لو ذكر كمان مميزات يبقى balanced.

تطبيق 1: Broadcast فيه رأيين – Unit 1

نص التسجيل 1: Behind the Filter – Youth Radio Debate

PRESENTER: Welcome to Behind the Filter. Today we're asking: do beauty and lifestyle influencers harm teenagers' self-image? I have two guests: Dr. Samir Ezzat, a school psychologist, and Hassan Rady, a fitness content creator with half a million followers.

DR. EZZAT: Thank you. I'd like to start carefully. Social media is not the enemy, and many young people use it in healthy ways. However, what concerns me is the constant comparison. In my clinic, I meet students who feel ugly or lazy after scrolling for an hour. The research suggests that teenagers who spend more than three hours a day on image-based apps may be more likely to report low self-esteem. 'May' is important here. It is a link, not proof that one causes the other.

HASSAN: With respect, I think people blame influencers far too much. My videos show real workouts and healthy meals. Thousands of young men have messaged me to say they've become fitter and more confident. Isn't that a good thing? Of course, I use good lighting, like any professional. But I never pretend my life is perfect.

DR. EZZAT: I believe Hassan wants to help, and his intentions are good. But even honest creators show the best ten seconds of a long day. A teenager watching at midnight doesn't see the bad days. So I'd ask parents and schools to teach media literacy: the habit of asking 'What am I not seeing?'

PRESENTER: Hassan, a final word?

HASSAN: Just this: switch off when you feel bad, and follow people who make you feel stronger, not smaller.

PRESENTER: Two perspectives, one important message: it's not only what we watch, it's how we watch it.

ملحوظة: نصوص التسجيل في الدرس ده مكتوبة مخصوص للتدريب، والأسماء والأرقام اللي فيها للتمرين بس. اقرا النص بصوت عالي أو خلي حد يقراه لك وانت مقفّل الكتاب.

إزاي بييجي في الأسئلة؟ (أمثلة محلولة)

نوع 1: Choose the correct answer (attitude)

Which best describes Dr. Ezzat's perspective?

a. Social media should be banned for teenagers.

b. Social media has no effect on self-image.

c. Social media can be healthy, but constant comparison may harm self-esteem.

d. Influencers are deliberately lying to young people.

الإجابة: c. ليه؟ بدأ بـ «Social media is not the enemy» (يعني مش ضدها خالص)، وبعد However قال اللي مقلقه: «the constant comparison». وd غلط لأنه قال «his intentions are good».

نوع 2: Which words show… ? (Analyse)

Which words show that Dr. Ezzat is cautious about the research?

الإجابة: 'The research suggests', 'teenagers… may be more likely', and 'It is a link, not proof'. — دي كلمات hedging، بتوري إنه مش بيبالغ في النتيجة.

نوع 3: What message is the speaker trying to convey? (Evaluate)

إجابة نموذجية: The presenter's final message is that the problem is not only the content we watch, but the way we watch it. Both guests also give advice: Dr. Ezzat suggests media literacy, and Hassan tells young people to switch off when they feel bad and follow people who make them feel stronger. — الرسالة في آخر جملة.

تطبيق 2: Expert podcast – Unit 5

نص التسجيل 2: Is Egypt Ready for Wind? – The Energy Hour Podcast

HOST: Welcome to The Energy Hour. With me are two guests with very different views: Eng. Mostafa Hegazy, who designs wind farms in the Gulf of Suez, and Dr. Ibrahim Sobhy, an economist.

ENG. HEGAZY: Honestly, I'm extremely excited. The Gulf of Suez has some of the strongest and most regular winds in the world. Every year, the turbines become bigger and more efficient. I truly believe wind could become one of the main sources of Egypt's electricity within our lifetime. This is a golden opportunity, and we must not miss it.

DR. SOBHY: I share the goal, but I'm a little more careful. Building wind farms is expensive at the start, and the money often has to be borrowed. We also need new power lines to carry the electricity from the desert to the cities. If we move too quickly without planning, costs could rise for ordinary families. So yes to wind, but step by step.

ENG. HEGAZY: Of course planning matters. But think of the alternative. How long can we keep burning fuel that pollutes our air and gets more expensive every year?

DR. SOBHY: That's a fair point. Perhaps the real question isn't 'if', but 'how fast'.

HOST: A good place to stop. In our next episode, we'll ask young engineers what skills this new industry will need.

تدريب

A. Choose the correct answer from a, b, c or d.

  1. 1. Hassan's attitude to influencers is mainly a. critical b. defensive and positive c. worried d. neutral

    Show answer
    b — قال «people blame influencers far too much» ودافع عن الفيديوهات بتاعته (defensive = بيدافع).

  2. 2. Why does Hassan ask, 'Isn't that a good thing?' a. He wants information. b. He is not sure. c. It is a rhetorical question to persuade listeners. d. He is asking Dr. Ezzat a medical question.

    Show answer
    c — سؤال بلاغي: مش مستني إجابة. Possible effects: to make listeners think, to make them agree that his videos are good, to defend his work and to persuade the audience.

  3. 3. How does Dr. Ezzat feel about Hassan? a. He thinks Hassan is dishonest. b. He thinks Hassan means well, but still disagrees with part of his view. c. He agrees with everything Hassan says. d. He does not mention Hassan.

    Show answer
    b — «his intentions are good. But even honest creators show the best ten seconds…».

  4. 4. Eng. Hegazy's perspective on wind energy is a. optimistic b. cautious c. negative d. uncertain

    Show answer
    a. optimistic — «extremely excited», «I truly believe», «a golden opportunity».

  5. 5. Dr. Sobhy's main concern is a. that wind is not strong enough b. that costs could rise if Egypt moves too quickly c. that turbines are ugly d. that engineers lack skills

    Show answer
    b — «If we move too quickly without planning, costs could rise for ordinary families».

  6. 6. By the end, Dr. Sobhy a. completely changes his mind. b. moves slightly closer to Eng. Hegazy's view. c. refuses to discuss the topic. d. becomes more negative.

    Show answer
    b — قال «That's a fair point. Perhaps the real question isn't 'if', but 'how fast'». يعني قرب شوية، بس لسه حذر (Perhaps).

B. Which words or phrases show the speaker's attitude? Find them in the transcripts.

  1. 7. Find TWO intensifiers that Eng. Hegazy uses.

    Show answer
    extremely (excited) وtruly (believe). وكمان «most regular».

  2. 8. Find a hedging word in Dr. Sobhy's first turn.

    Show answer
    could — «costs could rise». وكمان «a little more careful».

  3. 9. Find a rhetorical question in Transcript 2.

    Show answer
    «How long can we keep burning fuel that pollutes our air…?» — Eng. Hegazy مش مستني رقم، بيقنعنا إن الوقود مش حل.

  4. 10. Which phrase shows what worries Dr. Ezzat?

    Show answer
    «what concerns me is the constant comparison».

  5. 11. Which phrase tells us that Dr. Ezzat is not completely against social media?

    Show answer
    «Social media is not the enemy, and many young people use it in healthy ways».

C. Fact, speaker's opinion, or reported opinion? Write F, SO or RO.

  1. 12. «Hassan has half a million followers.»

    Show answer
    F — رقم (حقيقة)، والمذيع قاله وهو بيعرّفه.

  2. 13. «I think people blame influencers far too much.» (Hassan)

    Show answer
    SO — I think: رأي المتكلم نفسه.

  3. 14. «I'd ask parents and schools to teach media literacy.» (Dr. Ezzat)

    Show answer
    SO — ده اقتراح ونصيحة من Dr. Ezzat نفسه (I'd ask)، يعني رأيه هو.

  4. 15. «The research suggests that teenagers… may be more likely to report low self-esteem.»

    Show answer
    RO — بيحكي نتيجة البحث (رأي الباحثين) بحذر، مش رأيه الشخصي.

  5. 16. «This is a golden opportunity.» (Eng. Hegazy)

    Show answer
    SO — golden opportunity: وصف فيه رأي قوي.

D. True (T) or False (F)? Justify.

  1. 17. Both guests in Transcript 1 give young people advice about how to use social media.

    Show answer
    T — Dr. Ezzat نصح بـ «media literacy: the habit of asking 'What am I not seeing?'»، وHassan قال «switch off when you feel bad, and follow people who make you feel stronger».

  2. 18. Dr. Sobhy is against wind energy.

    Show answer
    F — قال «I share the goal» و«yes to wind, but step by step». هو حذر مش معارض.

  3. 19. The presenter in Transcript 1 supports only Hassan's view.

    Show answer
    F — قال «Two perspectives, one important message»، يعني متوازن وجمع الرأيين.

E. Answer in full sentences (Analyse / Evaluate / JRE).

  1. 20. Why does Dr. Ezzat say ''May' is important here'? What does this show about him?

    Show answer
    مثال (possible reasons): to show that the research found a link, not proof of cause / to show caution / to avoid exaggerating / to sound fair and reliable. This shows he is careful and honest about evidence. — hedging = علامة على objective perspective.

  2. 21. Compare the perspectives of the two guests in Transcript 2. How are they similar and different?

    Show answer
    مثال: Both guests support wind energy for Egypt. However, Eng. Hegazy is optimistic and wants fast action, while Dr. Sobhy is cautious because of costs and planning. — مقارنة: نقطة اتفاق + نقطة اختلاف + كلمة whereas/while.

  3. 22. Whose argument in Transcript 1 do you find more convincing? Use JRE.

    Show answer
    مثال: I find Dr. Ezzat's argument more convincing (J) because he uses research and explains its limits instead of relying on personal messages (R). For example, he says the link 'is a link, not proof' and gives a practical solution: media literacy (E). — Justify + Reasoning + Evidence من التسجيل.

  4. 23. Why does the host say 'A good place to stop' after Dr. Sobhy's words 'not if, but how fast'?

    Show answer
    مثال: Because Dr. Sobhy's words show that both guests now agree on the main goal, and the disagreement is only about speed. Stopping there leaves listeners with a balanced final idea. — الخاتمة بتكشف الرسالة.

ملخص الدرس في سطرين
  • Perspective = رأي المتكلم وموقفه: positive / negative / balanced / optimistic / cautious / biased.
  • العلامات: صفات (impressive, worrying)، intensifiers (extremely)، hedging (may, suggests)، كلام مطلق (everyone, never)، أسئلة بلاغية.
  • بعد However / But وفي الخاتمة بيبان الرأي الحقيقي.
  • فرّق بين fact ورأي المتكلم ورأي حد تاني بيحكيه (Critics say…).
  • في الإجابة: قول الموقف + الكلمات من التسجيل اللي بتثبته.
