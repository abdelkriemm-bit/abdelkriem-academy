English Course · Level 4 (B1+) · Weeks 5–8

Progress Check 2 · Weeks 5–8

20 marks · no help · 20 minutes

My score: ______ / 20 18–20 Excellent · 16–17 Very good · under 16: repeat the sentences of these weeks for 3 days, then try again.

A. Complete the sentences (10 marks)

Complete the sentences

  1. 1) My car ___ (repair) at the moment.
  2. 2) Our order ___ (not deliver) yet.
  3. 3) I had my hair ___ (cut) yesterday.
  4. 4) This time tomorrow, I ___ (fly) to Luxor.
  5. 5) By 2030, they ___ (build) a new airport.
  6. 6) He denied ___ (take) the money.
  7. 7) My doctor advised me ___ (cut) down on sugar.
  8. 8) She suggested ___ (rent) a flat in Maadi.
  9. 9) ___ health is more important than money. (a / the / –)
  10. 10) My brother is ___ engineer.
Show the answers
1) is being repaired 2) hasn't been delivered 3) cut 4) will be flying 5) will have built 6) taking 7) to cut 8) renting 9) – (no article) 10) an

B. Translate into English (5 marks)

Translate into English

  1. 1) أُلغيت رحلتنا.
  2. 2) بحلول الوقت الذي تصل فيه، سأكون قد أنهيت العمل.
  3. 3) وعد المالك بإصلاح الباب.
  4. 4) أصلحت هاتفي عند فني.
  5. 5) لا تتصل بي في التاسعة؛ سأكون أشاهد المباراة.
Show the answers
1) Our flight was (has been) cancelled. 2) By the time you arrive, I'll have finished (the) work. / By the time you get here, I will have finished the work. 3) The landlord promised to fix the door. 4) I had (got) my phone repaired. / I had my phone fixed. 5) Don't call me at nine — I'll be watching the match.

C. Write the Arabic meaning (5 marks)

Write the Arabic meaning

  1. 1) compensation = __________
  2. 2) cutting-edge = __________
  3. 3) a landlord = __________
  4. 4) a painkiller = __________
  5. 5) cramped = __________
Show the answers
1) تعويض 2) حديث جدًا / من أحدث ما وصلت إليه التكنولوجيا 3) المالك / صاحب الشقة 4) مسكّن 5) ضيق ومزدحم

D. Speaking check — tick what you can do

  1. ☐ I can make a complaint politely and firmly, in writing and on the phone.
  2. ☐ I can talk about future plans and predictions.
  3. ☐ I can report what other people said, promised, suggested or denied.
  4. ☐ I can talk about health problems, treatment and healthy habits.
