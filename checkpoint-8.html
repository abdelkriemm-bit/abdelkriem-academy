Checkpoint 8 English (Cambridge Lower Secondary 0861)

The complete free course for Year 8 Cambridge Lower Secondary English: Paper 1 non-fiction and Paper 2 fiction, every reading topic (grammar, punctuation, language and comprehension) and a writing pack for every genre with strong 350+ word samples.

Start the course →

Start here: the exam

Both papers, the marks, and exactly how your writing is marked.

  • The Exam: OverviewEverything about the Year 8 English exam (Progression Test Stage 8 and Checkpoint 0861): both papers, timings, marks, what the reading questions and writing tasks ask, results, and a 12-week study plan.
  • How Your Writing Is MarkedThe five writing categories (25 marks) explained in plain English, what top marks need in each, the rules that cap marks, and the G.A.A.P. and G.C.S.P.P. planning techniques with a 35-minute plan and checking routine.

Reading: grammar & punctuation

Every grammar and punctuation point the reading questions test, with exam-style practice.

  • Parts of SpeechAll nine word classes in depth — nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, determiners and interjections — how a word changes class, how Checkpoint asks about them, and practice with answers.
  • Phrases and ClausesNoun, verb, prepositional and adverbial phrases; main and subordinate clauses; defining and non-defining relative clauses; adverbial and embedded clauses — and how writers use them for effect, with exam practice.
  • Sentence Types, Kinds and Their EffectsSimple, compound, complex and compound-complex sentences; the four kinds of sentence; minor sentences; how sentence length creates effects; the mistakes to avoid; and exactly how Checkpoint asks you to identify a sentence and explain its effect.
  • Connectives, Conjunctions and Linking ParagraphsConnectives by function, coordinating and subordinating conjunctions, how writers link paragraphs, a reliable method for the 'how does this sentence link the paragraphs?' question, and how to match paragraphs to main ideas.
  • Punctuation: Rules and 'Why Has the Writer Used...?'Every punctuation mark you need for Checkpoint 8: the rules for using it, all the answers you can give when you are asked why the writer used it, and a large set of practice questions with answers.
  • Grammar in Use: Tenses, Voice, Speech and AccuracyThe 12 tenses, if-conditionals, active and passive voice, reported speech, subject-verb agreement, pronoun reference, standard and formal English, modifiers, parallelism and proofreading, with the reading questions Checkpoint asks and practice with answers.

Reading: understanding texts

Text types, purpose, inference, language effects, vocabulary and how to tackle each reading paper.

  • Text Types and Their FeaturesFiction and non-fiction, every genre you can meet in the reading papers with its purpose and features, and how to answer “name the text type” and “give two features” questions.
  • Purpose, Fact and Opinion, and ViewpointThe author’s purpose and its clues, fact versus opinion, first, second and third person, the writer’s attitude, and the PEE method for “give your opinion with two quotations” questions.
  • Inference, Main Ideas, Themes and SummaryExplicit and implicit information, a step-by-step inference method with STEAL, main ideas and themes, the 40-50-word summary with a model answer, and table-completion questions.
  • Language, Devices and Their EffectsEvery figure of speech and literary device with all the effects you can write, tone and mood word banks, connotation, and a step-by-step method for “explain the effect” questions with model answers.
  • Vocabulary Skills: Context Clues, Word Parts, Idioms and SpellingWork out unfamiliar words from context and word parts, answer 'find a word' and word-effect questions, and master phrasal verbs, British idioms, connotation and the spelling rules marked in your writing.
  • Poetry and Drama: Elements, Types and How They're AskedHow poems are built (stanza, rhyme scheme, rhythm, enjambment, caesura, speaker), sound devices, every type of poem, and how a play script works, with original examples and exam-style practice.
  • Reading Exam Technique: Paper 1 and Paper 2How to tackle the 25-mark reading section of both papers in about 35 minutes: reading the insert, marks as points, every question type with a model method, tick boxes, quoting and timing, plus two full original practice papers with answers.
