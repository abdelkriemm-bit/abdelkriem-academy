Grammar: Relative clauses · who / which / that / where

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

Relative clauses give more information about a noun.

who / that = people · which / that = things · where = places

The woman who works with me … · The phone which / that I bought … · The café where we met …

في العربية نكرر الضمير بعد «الذي»: «الكتاب الذي قرأتُه» (الهاء)، فيقول المتعلم The book which I read it ✗. في الإنجليزية لا نكرر الضمير: The book which I read. وأيضًا: لا تستخدم which مع الأشخاص (the man which ✗)، واستخدم where للأماكن عندما يأتي بعدها فاعل وفعل: the city where I was born.

WordForExample
whopeopleShe's the doctor who helped my father.
whichthings / animalsIt's a phone which takes great photos.
thatpeople or thingsThe man that I met … / The film that we saw …
whereplacesThat's the school where I studied.

When who / which / that is the object, you can leave it out: The film (that) we saw was great. But not when it is the subject: The man who lives here … (NOT The man lives here …).

Don't repeat the pronoun or the place: The village where I grew up (NOT where I grew up there).

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Complete with who, which or where.

  1. 1) A chef is a person ___ cooks in a restaurant.
  2. 2) This is the camera ___ I bought in Dubai.
  3. 3) Luxor is the city ___ you can see Karnak Temple.
  4. 4) I lost the ring ___ my mother gave me.
  5. 5) Do you know the man ___ is talking to Omar?
  6. 6) The hotel ___ we stayed was near the beach.
Show the answers
1) who (or that) 2) which (or that) 3) where 4) which (or that) 5) who (or that) 6) where

Exercise 2 · Join the sentences with who, which or where.

  1. 1) I have a friend. She speaks four languages.
  2. 2) This is the house. I grew up there.
  3. 3) We visited a palace. It has four hundred rooms.
  4. 4) That's the man. He fixed my car.
  5. 5) Dahab is a town. You can go diving there.
Show the answers
1) I have a friend who (that) speaks four languages. 2) This is the house where I grew up. (or: … the house (that) I grew up in.) 3) We visited a palace which (that) has four hundred rooms. 4) That's the man who (that) fixed my car. 5) Dahab is a town where you can go diving.

Exercise 3 · Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) The man which lives upstairs is very polite.
  2. 2) This is the phone that I bought it yesterday.
  3. 3) Alexandria is the city which I was born.
  4. 4) I know a woman who she is a pilot.
  5. 5) The village where my grandparents live there is small.
Show the answers
1) The man who lives upstairs is very polite. 2) This is the phone that I bought yesterday. 3) Alexandria is the city where I was born. 4) I know a woman who is a pilot. 5) The village where my grandparents live is small.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) أحمد هو الشخص الذي ساعدني.
  2. 2) هذا هو المقهى الذي نلتقي فيه كل يوم جمعة.
  3. 3) هل شاهدت الفيلم الذي أخبرتك عنه؟
  4. 4) الأقصر مدينة مشهورة بمعابدها القديمة.
Show the answers
1) Ahmed is the person who helped me. 2) This is the café where we meet every Friday. 3) Have you seen the film (that) I told you about? 4) Luxor is a city which (that) is famous for its ancient temples. (or: Luxor is a city famous for …)

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — “A person and a place I love”: write 80–100 words about a special person in your life and a place that is special to you. Use who, which, that and where at least four times. Then describe the person and the place to a friend without reading.

Example: The person who means the most to me is my grandmother, Fatma. She's a generous, patient woman who always has time for her grandchildren. She lives in a small village near Tanta, where she has a garden full of fruit trees. When I was a child, I spent every summer in that village. There is an old house which belongs to our family, and a little mosque where my grandfather prayed every day. The village isn't famous, but for me it is the most beautiful place in Egypt. It's the place where I feel really happy.
