Grammar: Question tags · indirect questions (Could you tell me where…? Do you know if…?)

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

Question tags: positive sentence → negative tag · negative sentence → positive tag. Use the same helping verb (be, do, have, can…).

Indirect questions are more polite. After Could you tell me… / Do you know… use statement word order (no do / does / did). Use if for yes / no questions.

«أليس كذلك؟» في العربية ثابتة لا تتغير، فيقول المتعلمون isn't it? مع كل جملة: ✗ You live in Cairo, isn't it? لكن في الإنجليزية نكرّر الفعل المساعد نفسه مع الفاعل: ✓ You live in Cairo, don't you? وفي السؤال المهذّب بعد Could you tell me لا نقلب الترتيب: ✗ Could you tell me where is the station? ✓ Could you tell me where the station is?

✗ Could you tell me where is the bank? ✓ Could you tell me where the bank is?

✗ She lives in Giza, isn't it? ✓ She lives in Giza, doesn't she?

I'm … → aren't I? · Let's … → shall we?

Your voice goes down on the tag when you expect “yes”, and up when it is a real question.

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Add question tags.

  1. 1) You're from Mansoura, ___?
  2. 2) The train leaves at nine, ___?
  3. 3) They didn't lose their passports, ___?
  4. 4) She can drive, ___?
  5. 5) We missed the bus, ___?
  6. 6) It isn't expensive, ___?
  7. 7) You've been to Siwa, ___?
  8. 8) Let's take a taxi, ___?
Show the answers
1) aren't you 2) doesn't it 3) did they 4) can't she 5) didn't we 6) is it 7) haven't you 8) shall we

Exercise 2 · Make the questions polite. Begin with the words in brackets.

  1. 1) Where is the platform? (Could you tell me…)
  2. 2) What time does the museum open? (Do you know…)
  3. 3) Is breakfast included? (Could you tell me if…)
  4. 4) How much does a return ticket cost? (Do you know…)
  5. 5) Where can I find a pharmacy? (Could you tell me…)
  6. 6) Did my luggage arrive? (Do you know if…)
Show the answers
1) Could you tell me where the platform is? 2) Do you know what time the museum opens? 3) Could you tell me if breakfast is included? 4) Do you know how much a return ticket costs? 5) Could you tell me where I can find a pharmacy? 6) Do you know if my luggage arrived? / Do you know if my luggage has arrived?

Exercise 3 · Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) Could you tell me where is the hotel?
  2. 2) You live in Cairo, isn't it?
  3. 3) Do you know what time does the flight land?
  4. 4) He didn't call, didn't he?
  5. 5) Can you tell me is there a bus to Giza?
Show the answers
1) Could you tell me where the hotel is? 2) You live in Cairo, don't you? 3) Do you know what time the flight lands? 4) He didn't call, did he? 5) Can you tell me if / whether there is a bus to Giza?

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) هل يمكنك أن تخبرني أين المحطة؟
  2. 2) أنت مصري، أليس كذلك؟
  3. 3) هل تعرف متى يغادر القطار؟
  4. 4) للأسف التكييف معطّل.
Show the answers
1) Could you tell me where the station is? / Can you tell me where the station is? 2) You're Egyptian, aren't you? 3) Do you know when the train leaves? / Do you know what time the train leaves? 4) I'm afraid the air conditioner is out of order. / Unfortunately, the air conditioning isn't working.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — Write 120–150 words: an email to a hotel or an airline to complain about a travel problem. Explain what happened and ask at least three polite indirect questions. Then role-play the phone call with a friend and use at least three question tags.

Example: Dear Sir or Madam, I am writing about my stay at your hotel in Hurghada from 3rd to 7th August. Unfortunately, there were several problems. When we arrived, our room was not ready, so we waited at reception for two hours. I had booked a room with a sea view, but we were given a noisy room above the kitchen. On the second day, the air conditioner was out of order, and nobody came to fix it until the next evening. The receptionist was polite, but she said that the hotel was full and that she couldn't move us. Could you tell me why my reservation was not checked before we arrived? I would also like to know if I can get a partial refund. Finally, I was wondering if you could offer us a discount on a future stay. I look forward to hearing from you. Yours faithfully, Omar Hassan
