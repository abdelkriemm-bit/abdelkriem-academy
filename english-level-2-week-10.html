Grammar: Verb patterns · verb + -ing / verb + to + verb

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

verb + -ing: enjoy, like, love, hate, mind, finish, keep, practise, avoid, can't stand, spend time

verb + to + verb: want, would like, decide, hope, plan, agree, offer, manage, refuse, afford, learn

after a preposition, use -ing: interested in learning, good at drawing, before going

في العربية نقول «أحب أن أسبح» و«أستمتع بالسباحة»، ولا توجد قاعدة ثابتة تساعدنا، لذلك يقول المتعلم I enjoy to swim ✗ أو I want going ✗. الحل: احفظ كل فعل مع النمط الذي يليه كأنهما كلمة واحدة: enjoy + ing و want + to. وبعد حروف الجر (in, at, about, of, before, after) يأتي الفعل دائمًا بـ ing: I'm interested in joining …

verb + -ingverb + to + verb
I enjoy reading.I want to read.
She finished cooking.She decided to cook.
Do you mind waiting?Would you like to wait?
He keeps forgetting.He managed to remember.
They avoid driving at night.They refused to drive at night.

like / love / hate / prefer can take both: I like swimming. / I like to swim. But would like always takes to: I'd like to swim.

Negative: I decided not to go. · I enjoy not working on Fridays.

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Choose the correct form.

  1. 1) I enjoy (cooking / to cook).
  2. 2) We decided (to stay / staying) at home.
  3. 3) Do you mind (to open / opening) the window?
  4. 4) He refused (paying / to pay) the bill.
  5. 5) She keeps (to make / making) the same mistake.
  6. 6) I'd like (to join / joining) the club.
Show the answers
1) cooking 2) to stay 3) opening 4) to pay 5) making 6) to join

Exercise 2 · Complete with the -ing form or to + verb.

  1. 1) Mona is interested in ___ (learn) photography.
  2. 2) I can't afford ___ (buy) a new laptop.
  3. 3) My son practises ___ (play) the oud every day.
  4. 4) They agreed ___ (meet) on Friday.
  5. 5) Ahmed spends hours ___ (fish) on the Nile.
  6. 6) Did you manage ___ (find) a good gym?
Show the answers
1) learning 2) to buy 3) playing 4) to meet 5) fishing 6) to find

Exercise 3 · Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) I want learning to swim.
  2. 2) She avoids to eat sugar.
  3. 3) He's good at draw.
  4. 4) We hope seeing you soon.
  5. 5) Thank you for help me.
Show the answers
1) I want to learn to swim. 2) She avoids eating sugar. 3) He's good at drawing. 4) We hope to see you soon. 5) Thank you for helping me.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) أستمتع بقراءة القصص قبل النوم.
  2. 2) قررت أن أنضم إلى نادٍ رياضي.
  3. 3) هل تمانع أن تساعدني؟
  4. 4) أريد أن أبدأ هواية جديدة.
Show the answers
1) I enjoy reading stories before bed (before I go to sleep). 2) I decided to join a sports club. 3) Do you mind helping me? 4) I want to take up (start) a new hobby.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — “My hobby”: write 80–100 words about a hobby you have or want to take up. Say when you started, why you enjoy it and what you'd like to do in the future. Use at least four verbs from the grammar table. Then talk about your hobby for one minute.

Example: My favourite hobby is photography. I started taking photos five years ago with my old phone. Now I have a small camera, but I can't afford to buy a professional one yet. I enjoy walking around old Cairo and taking pictures of people and buildings. I usually avoid going out at midday, because the light is too strong. Last year I decided to join a photography club in Zamalek, and I managed to win a small competition! In the future, I'd like to take photos of the White Desert, and I hope to have my own exhibition one day.
