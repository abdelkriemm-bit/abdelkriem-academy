Grammar: Mixed conditionals · unless / provided that / as long as

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

Mixed conditional 1 (past → present): If + past perfect, would + verb. If I had saved money, I would be rich now.

Mixed conditional 2 (present → past): If + past simple, would have + p.p. If I were braver, I would have said no.

unless = if … not · provided that / as long as = only if.

الشرط المختلط يربط زمنين: سبب في الماضي ونتيجة في الحاضر، أو صفة دائمة في الحاضر ونتيجة في الماضي. انظر إلى كلمات الزمن: now و today تعني would + الفعل، و yesterday و last year تعني would have + التصريف الثالث. أما unless فمعناها «إلا إذا / ما لم» وهي منفية بنفسها، فالخطأ الشائع أن نقول unless you don't come، والصحيح unless you come. وبعد unless و as long as و provided that نستخدم المضارع للمستقبل وليس will.

TypeFormExample
Past → presentIf + had + p.p., would + verbIf I had taken that job, I would live in Dubai now.
Present → pastIf + past simple, would have + p.p.If I spoke English well, I would have got the job last month.
unless= if … notUnless you hurry, you'll be late. (= If you don't hurry …)
provided that / as long as= only ifI'll come as long as I finish work early.

Wrong: Unless you don't study, you'll fail. ✔ Unless you study, you'll fail.

Wrong: as long as you will help me ✔ as long as you help me.

in case = to be ready for something that may happen: Take water in case you get thirsty. (It is not the same as if.)

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Choose the correct form.

  1. 1) If I had learnt to swim as a child, I (would be / would have been) more confident in the sea now.
  2. 2) If she weren't so shy, she (would speak / would have spoken) at the meeting yesterday.
  3. 3) If Omar were more organised, he (wouldn't forget / wouldn't have forgotten) his passport last week.
  4. 4) If we had bought that flat, we (would live / would have lived) in Alexandria now.
  5. 5) If he didn't love his job, he (would leave / would have left) years ago.
  6. 6) If I hadn't eaten so much, I (wouldn't feel / wouldn't have felt) sick now.
Show the answers
1) would be 2) would have spoken 3) wouldn't have forgotten 4) would live 5) would have left 6) wouldn't feel

Exercise 2 · Complete with unless, as long as, provided that, in case or even if.

  1. 1) You'll miss the bus ___ you leave now.
  2. 2) I'll lend you the money ___ you pay me back next month.
  3. 3) Write down my number ___ you need help.
  4. 4) I'll go to the wedding ___ it rains — nothing will stop me.
  5. 5) ___ you apologise, she won't talk to you.
  6. 6) You can stay out late ___ you call me first.
Show the answers
1) unless 2) as long as / provided that 3) in case 4) even if 5) Unless 6) as long as / provided that

Exercise 3 · Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) Unless you don't hurry, we'll be late.
  2. 2) I'll help you as long as you will be honest.
  3. 3) If I had saved money, I would had a car now.
  4. 4) If I didn't hate crowds, I would go to the concert with you yesterday.
  5. 5) Provided that you will finish early, you can leave.
  6. 6) If he studied medicine ten years ago, he would be a doctor now.
Show the answers
1) Unless you hurry, we'll be late. 2) …as long as you are honest. 3) …I would have a car now. 4) …I would have gone to the concert with you yesterday. 5) Provided that you finish early, you can leave. 6) If he had studied medicine ten years ago, he would be a doctor now.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) لو كنت قبلت ذلك العرض، لكنت أعيش في لندن الآن.
  2. 2) لن تتحسن لغتك الإنجليزية ما لم تمارسها كل يوم.
  3. 3) يمكنك الخروج طالما ترجع قبل منتصف الليل.
  4. 4) تعلمت من التجربة أن الصبر مهم.
  5. 5) حتى لو كان الأمر صعبًا، لا تستسلم.
Show the answers
1) If I had accepted that offer, I would live (would be living) in London now. / If I'd accepted that offer, I'd be living in London now. 2) Your English won't improve unless you practise it every day. / Your English won't get better unless you practise (it) every day. 3) You can go out as long as you come back before midnight. / You can go out provided that you come back before midnight. 4) I learnt from experience that patience is important. 5) Even if it's difficult, don't give up. / Even if it is hard, don't give up.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — “The best lesson life has taught me”: write 150–200 words. Describe what happened, what you learnt and give some advice. Use at least one mixed conditional and unless or as long as. Then record yourself telling the story.

Example: The best lesson life has taught me is that honesty always pays. When I was at university in Assiut, I worked part-time in a mobile phone shop. One evening, a customer paid me two hundred pounds too much. Nobody saw it, and I needed the money, but I ran after him and gave it back. He was so surprised that he thanked me again and again. A month later, the same man came back to the shop. He owned a big company, and he offered me a job because, as he said, he needed people he could trust. I still work there today. If I had kept that money, I wouldn't have this job now, and I would probably feel ashamed of myself. Life has taught me other lessons too, often the hard way, but this one was the easiest and the best. My advice is simple: be true to yourself and keep your word. People will trust you as long as you are honest with them, and unless you respect others, you can't expect them to respect you.
