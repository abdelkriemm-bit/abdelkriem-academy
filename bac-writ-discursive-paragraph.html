تانية بكالوريا · Writing · Unit 3

Discursive Paragraph

إزاي تكتب فقرة مناقشة متوازنة (150-180 كلمة): الرأيين بالعدل باستخدام concession clauses، وبعدين رأيك والخلاصة.

تانية بكالوريا English

الدرس ده عن إيه؟

A discursive paragraph discusses different ideas or opinions about a topic in a balanced way. It presents one side, then the opposite side using concession clauses, and ends with your own view or a balanced conclusion.

الـ Discursive Paragraph يعني فقرة «مناقشة». مش بتقول رأيك من أول سطر وتدافع عنه بس، لأ: بتعرض الرأي ده والرأي العكسي بشكل عادل ومتوازن، وفي الآخر تقول رأيك أو حل وسط.

في الكتاب: Unit 3: Echoes of the Past، صفحة Writing in Action: Concession Clauses و Writing Task: Discursive Paragraph (صفحة 86-88)، والـ Summative Assessment صفحة 99، والمطلوب about (150-180) words. وموضوعات الوحدة: الأماكن التاريخية (sites of memory)، المتاحف زي المتحف المصري الكبير (GEM)، والسوشيال ميديا والتاريخ. (والكتاب بيرجع للنوع ده في Unit 10 وUnit 7 كمان.)

الفكرة ببساطة: القاضي العادل

In an opinion paragraph you are a lawyer for one side. In a discursive paragraph you are a fair judge: you listen to both sides, then decide.

في الـ opinion paragraph إنت محامي بيدافع عن جهة واحدة. في الـ discursive إنت قاضي: بتسمع الطرف الأول، وبعدين الطرف التاني، وبعدين تحكم. القاضي الكويس بيعترف بالنقطة القوية عند الطرف التاني حتى لو مش موافقها، وده بالظبط شغل الـ concession clauses.

رأيك فين؟من أول جملةفي الآخر (بعد عرض الرأيين)
الجانب التانيجملة رد صغيرة (اختياري)نص الفقرة تقريبًا

الهيكل زي ما الكتاب بيشرحه (5 أجزاء)

الجزءالكتاب بيقوليعني إيه بالعربيعبارات الكتاب
1. Topic SentenceIntroduce the issue or topic clearly.قدّم القضية من غير ما تاخد جنبMany people have different opinions about…
2. First Point / First PerspectivePresent one side of the argument with explanation or example.الرأي الأول + شرح أو مثالOn the one hand, … / Some people believe that … / One argument in favour of … is that …
3. Contrasting Point / Opposite PerspectivePresent the opposite viewpoint using concession clauses.الرأي العكسي، بـ concession clausesOn the other hand, … / However, …
4. Personal Opinion / Balanced ViewState your opinion or show a balanced conclusion.رأيك أو حل وسطIn my opinion, … / From my perspective, … / It seems that … / A balanced approach may be the best solution.
5. Concluding SentenceEnd with a final idea that summarises the discussion.جملة تلخص المناقشةIn conclusion, … / To sum up, … / Overall, …
خلي بالك

الكتاب بيطلب في القواعد: concession clauses (at least 3) وprepositional phrases. يعني تلات جمل على الأقل فيها Although / Despite / While it is true that / Admittedly / Even though / It may be argued that.

الـ Concession Clauses: قلب الفقرة

A concession clause introduces the opposite idea politely before you give your own idea. It shows that you are aware of other viewpoints.

الـ concession يعني «تنازل» أو «اعتراف»: إنك تقول «ماشي، معاك حق في النقطة دي… بس…». ده بيخلي كلامك محترم ومقنع أكتر، لأن القارئ بيحس إنك فاهم الرأي التاني.

الأداةبعدها إيه؟معناهامثال من موضوع الوحدة
Although + clauseفاعل + فعلبالرغم من إنAlthough Karnak is crowded in winter, it is calm early in the morning.
Even though + clauseفاعل + فعل (أقوى شوية)حتى لو / مع إنEven though the museum is far from my home, it is worth the trip.
Despite + noun phrase / -ingاسم أو -ing (مش جملة)بالرغم منDespite the long queue, we enjoyed every minute inside the museum.
While it is true that + clauseفاعل + فعلصحيح إن… (بس)While it is true that online photos are free, they cannot show the real size of a statue.
Admittedly, …; however, …جملة كاملةلازم نعترف إن…Admittedly, restoring old monuments is expensive; however, it protects our heritage for future generations.
It may be argued that …; nevertheless, …جملة كاملةممكن حد يقول إن…It may be argued that history books are enough; nevertheless, seeing real objects makes the past feel alive.
  • Although many young people prefer social media, museums still attract school trips.Although + جملة كاملة (many young people prefer).
  • Despite the high ticket price, the museum was full.Despite + اسم (the high ticket price).
  • Despite being thousands of years old, Karnak Temple still stands proudly in Luxor.Despite + -ing.
  • In spite of the heat, we visited the temple at noon.In spite of = Despite بالظبط.
  • While it is true that online videos are popular, they cannot replace real objects.اعتراف + رد في نفس الجملة.
  • Admittedly, some historical posts online are accurate; however, many spread myths.الـ semicolon هنا بيربط جملتين كاملتين مرتبطين ببعض.
أهم غلطتين في الـ concession
  • ✗ Although it was hot, but we visited the temple. ✓ Although it was hot, we visited the temple. (Although و but مايجوش مع بعض).
  • ✗ Despite it was hot, … ✓ Despite the heat, … / Although it was hot, … (Despite بعدها اسم مش جملة).

الـ Prepositional Phrases of Place and Time

Unit 3 also asks you to use prepositional phrases of place and time (at, in, on, from… to…, during, for, across, along) to give precise details.

الحرفبنستخدمه معمثال
atمكان محددat the entrance of the museum
inمكان مقفول / مدينة / سنةin Luxor / in the Hypostyle Hall / in 1858
onسطح / تاريخ يومon the walls / on 4 November 2025
from … to …من… لـ… (مكان أو وقت)from the age of the pharaohs to the present day
duringخلال فترةduring the reign of Ramesses II
forمدةfor more than two thousand years
across / alongعلى مساحة واسعة / على طول خطacross the Nile Valley / along the avenue of sphinxes

إزاي تكتب خطوة بخطوة

  1. اقرا المهمة كويس: الكتاب ساعات بيدي Role – Audience – Purpose (مثلًا: إنت كاتب في بلوج المدرسة، وبتكتب لزميل شايف إن «المتاحف للسياح بس»). خلي ده قدامك وإنت بتكتب.
  2. اكتب جدول صغير في الهامش: عمود «with» وعمود «against»، وفي كل عمود نقطتين ومثال.
  3. قرر رأيك في الآخر: هتنحاز لجهة؟ ولا حل وسط (balanced approach)؟
  4. Topic sentence: قدّم القضية من غير رأي.
  5. First perspective: On the one hand / Some people believe… + مثال.
  6. Opposite perspective: On the other hand + concession clauses (Although / Despite / While it is true that) + مثال.
  7. Your view: From my perspective… أو A balanced approach…
  8. Conclusion: Overall / To sum up + فكرة أخيرة.
  9. راجع الـ self-check بتاع الكتاب: الرأيين موجودين؟ 3 concession clauses؟ مثال لكل جهة؟ prepositional phrases؟ 150-180 كلمة؟

موديل كامل

ده موديل على Writing Task اللي في الكتاب صفحة 88 (Role – Audience – Purpose).

Model discursive paragraph177 words
Task: As a student contributor to your school's heritage blog, write for a classmate who thinks "museums are just for tourists". Discuss the extent to which museums play an important role in preserving history and identity. Write a discursive paragraph of about (150-180) words.

Many people have different opinions about whether museums are only for tourists or whether they play a deeper role in preserving history and identity. On the one hand, some people believe that museums mainly serve visitors from abroad. Admittedly, crowds of tourists queue at the entrance of the Grand Egyptian Museum daily, and their tickets support the economy. On the other hand, museums protect objects which belong to every Egyptian. Although many of these artefacts are thousands of years old, they still teach us who we are. Despite being a brand-new building, the GEM, which opened to the public on 4 November 2025, is filled with treasures that our ancestors made, and school trips let students stand in front of statues from their textbooks. While it is true that one visit cannot cover every hall, even a short tour shows students thousands of years of their own history. From my perspective, museums are for Egyptians first and tourists second. Overall, a museum is not just a building for visitors; it is a memory that we all share.

ليه النموذج ده قوي؟
  • Topic Sentence: «Many people have different opinions about whether…» نفس عبارة الكتاب، وبتقدّم القضية من غير رأي.
  • First Perspective: «On the one hand, some people believe that museums mainly serve visitors from abroad.» ده رأي الزميل.
  • Concession 1: «Admittedly, crowds of tourists queue… and their tickets support the economy» الكاتب بيعترف بنقطة قوية عند الطرف التاني + مثال.
  • Opposite Perspective: «On the other hand, museums protect objects which belong to every Egyptian.» + relative clause.
  • Concession 2: «Although many of these artefacts are thousands of years old, they still teach us who we are.»
  • Concession 3: «Despite being a brand-new building, the GEM… is filled with treasures that our ancestors made» (Despite + -ing) + معلومة من الكتاب (opened to the public on 4 November 2025) جوه non-defining relative clause بين فاصلتين (معلومة زيادة).
  • Concession 4: «While it is true that one visit cannot cover every hall, even a short tour shows…». يعني الموديل فيه 4 concession clauses والكتاب طالب 3 على الأقل.
  • Prepositional phrases: from abroad, at the entrance of, on 4 November 2025, in front of, for visitors.
  • Personal Opinion: «From my perspective, museums are for Egyptians first and tourists second.» رأي واضح بعد عرض الرأيين.
  • Concluding Sentence: «Overall, a museum is not just a building…; it is a memory that we all share.» الـ semicolon بيربط جملتين كاملتين مرتبطين، والفكرة بترد على الزميل مباشرة.
  • عدد الكلمات: 177 كلمة (المطلوب about 150-180).
أشهر الأخطاء (غلط ← صح)
  • ✗ تقول رأيك بقوة من أول جملة (Museums are definitely not for tourists!). ✓ ابدأ محايد: Many people have different opinions about…
  • ✗ تعرض جهة واحدة بس. ✓ لازم الرأيين ومثال لكل واحد.
  • ✗ Although … , but … ✓ Although … , … (من غير but).
  • ✗ Despite + جملة (Despite it is modern). ✓ Despite its modern design / Despite being modern.
  • ✗ On the one hand من غير On the other hand. ✓ الاتنين بييجوا مع بعض.
  • ✗ تقفل من غير رأي ولا خلاصة. ✓ From my perspective… + Overall…
  • ✗ Admittedly, …, however, … (فاصلة قبل however بين جملتين). ✓ Admittedly, …; however, … أو جملتين منفصلتين بنقطة.
إزاي تجاوب في الامتحان
  • استخدم On the one hand / On the other hand كأعمدة الفقرة: المصحح هيشوف التوازن على طول.
  • اكتب الـ 3 concession clauses بتلات أدوات مختلفة (Although / Despite / While it is true that).
  • حط معلومة حقيقية من الوحدة (Karnak، الـ GEM، المتحف المصري 1858…) مع prepositional phrase.
  • لو مش عارف تنحاز، قول: A balanced approach may be the best solution. وده مقبول في الكتاب.

إزاي بييجي في الأسئلة؟

الأسئلة في الكتاب بتيجي بالأشكال دي:

1) Writing Task بـ Role – Audience – Purpose زي الموديل. ومعاها Support (بداية جملة: While some people use museums as…, they actually… because…) وStretch (تكبرها لـ mini essay من فقرتين: فقرة لكل رأي).

مثال تكملة الـ sentence starter بتاع الكتاب:

While some people use museums as places to take photos for social media, they actually connect us with the people who built our civilisation because every object tells a real story from our past.

2) Summative Assessment: سؤال «To what extent do you agree?» أو «Discuss.» أو «Evaluate this claim.» مع Writing Frame. مثال على تكملة الفريم:

  • The issue of digital platforms and history is widely discussed today, particularly in the context of Egyptian heritage.تقديم القضية.
  • This essay examines multiple perspectives using evidence from the unit's texts.هتعمل إيه.
  • Although online videos reach millions of people, it is also true that many of them contain mistakes. This suggests that we should check the source.Although + الرأيين + شرح.
  • Despite their convenience, only by visiting real sites can we feel the size of Karnak's columns.Despite + اسم، و only by … can we (قلب ترتيب).
  • In contrast, it has been argued that digital tours help people who cannot travel. However, they cannot replace real objects.الرأي التاني ورد.
  • Overall, it can be argued that physical sites are more valuable because they keep the real evidence of our past.الخلاصة.

تدريب

A. Which part of the discursive paragraph is each sentence? (Topic sentence / First perspective / Opposite perspective / Personal opinion / Concluding sentence)

  1. 1. To sum up, both real and digital museums have a place in protecting our heritage.

    Show answer
    Concluding sentence — To sum up.

  2. 2. Some people believe that social media helps young people learn about history.

    Show answer
    First perspective — Some people believe that.

  3. 3. Many people have different opinions about the role of social media in sharing history.

    Show answer
    Topic sentence — بيقدّم القضية من غير رأي.

  4. 4. On the other hand, while it is true that posts are quick to read, many of them spread myths.

    Show answer
    Opposite perspective — On the other hand + concession.

  5. 5. From my perspective, a balanced approach may be the best solution.

    Show answer
    Personal opinion / balanced view.

B. Join each pair of sentences using the word in brackets.

  1. 6. The temple is very old. It still attracts thousands of visitors. (Although)

    Show answer
    Although the temple is very old, it still attracts thousands of visitors.

  2. 7. The weather was hot. We walked along the avenue of sphinxes. (Despite)

    Show answer
    Despite the hot weather, we walked along the avenue of sphinxes. — Despite + اسم.

  3. 8. Online maps are useful. Walking through the old streets of Islamic Cairo teaches you more. (It may be argued that … nevertheless)

    Show answer
    It may be argued that online maps are useful; nevertheless, walking through the old streets of Islamic Cairo teaches you more. — الـ semicolon بيربط جملتين كاملتين.

  4. 9. The museum cannot show every object. It offers an excellent selection. (While it is true that)

    Show answer
    While it is true that the museum cannot show every object, it offers an excellent selection.

  5. 10. Repairing the old mosque was expensive. It saved an important part of our history. (Admittedly … however)

    Show answer
    Admittedly, repairing the old mosque was expensive; however, it saved an important part of our history.

  6. 11. I live far from Luxor. I visit Karnak every summer. (Even though)

    Show answer
    Even though I live far from Luxor, I visit Karnak every summer.

C. Choose the correct answer.

  1. 12. ______ its huge size, the GEM is easy to explore. a. Although b. Despite c. Even though d. However

    Show answer
    b. Despite — بعدها اسم (its huge size).

  2. 13. ______ many posts look convincing, they are not always accurate. a. Despite b. In spite of c. Although d. On the other hand

    Show answer
    c. Although — بعدها جملة (many posts look).

  3. 14. The Egyptian Museum was founded ______ 1858. a. on b. at c. in d. during

    Show answer
    c. in — مع السنة.

  4. 15. The GEM opened to the public ______ 4 November 2025. a. in b. on c. at d. for

    Show answer
    b. on — مع التاريخ بالـ يوم.

  5. 16. Which sentence is the best topic sentence for a discursive paragraph? a. Social media is terrible for history. b. Many people have different opinions about the role of social media in preserving history. c. I love museums. d. Overall, museums are important.

    Show answer
    b — بتقدّم القضية بشكل محايد.

D. Find the mistake and correct it.

  1. 17. Although the ticket was expensive, but we enjoyed the visit.

    Show answer
    شيل but: Although the ticket was expensive, we enjoyed the visit.

  2. 18. Despite it was crowded, the museum was calm inside.

    Show answer
    Despite the crowds, … / Although it was crowded, …

  3. 19. On the one hand, museums protect history. In the other hand, they are expensive to build.

    Show answer
    In ← On the other hand.

  4. 20. The temple has stood there since more than two thousand years.

    Show answer
    since ← for more than two thousand years (مدة).

E. Writing tasks: write a discursive paragraph of about (150-180) words.

  1. 21. Physical sites of memory such as monuments and museums are more valuable for preserving history than digital platforms. To what extent do you agree?

    Show answer
    خطة مقترحة: Topic sentence: the issue of real vs digital heritage. On the one hand: physical sites keep real evidence (Karnak's columns, the GEM's collection). On the other hand: digital platforms reach people who cannot travel. Concessions: Although…, Despite…, While it is true that… View: A balanced approach: digital tours should lead people to real sites. Overall: … prepositional phrases: in Luxor, for thousands of years, on social media.

  2. 22. Social media has done more harm than good to our understanding of Egyptian history. Discuss.

    Show answer
    خطة مقترحة: Topic sentence. First perspective: viral posts spread myths (e.g. claims with absolute language and no source). Opposite: some accounts share accurate news of discoveries quickly. Concessions: Admittedly…; however… / It may be argued that…; nevertheless… / Even though… View: From my perspective, it depends on checking sources. Conclusion: To sum up, …

Checklist قبل ما تسلّم

دي الـ Self check بتاعة الكتاب صفحة 88 + حاجات مهمة:

  • الـ topic sentence بتقدّم القضية بوضوح.
  • عرضت الرأيين (On the one hand / On the other hand).
  • استخدمت concession clauses (3 على الأقل) صح.
  • كل جهة معاها مثال.
  • قلت رأيي أو balanced view وختمت بجملة خلاصة.
  • استخدمت prepositional phrases of place and time.
  • مفيش Although مع but، ومفيش Despite + جملة.
  • عدد الكلمات about 150-180.
ملخص الدرس في سطرين
  • Discursive paragraph = الرأيين بعدل، وبعدين حكمك: إنت قاضي مش محامي.
  • الهيكل: Topic sentence – First perspective – Opposite perspective – Personal/balanced view – Concluding sentence.
  • 3 concession clauses على الأقل: Although / Even though + جملة، Despite + اسم، While it is true that، Admittedly…; however…
  • Although مايجيش معاها but، و Despite مايجيش بعدها جملة.
  • زوّد prepositional phrases ومعلومة حقيقية من الوحدة، والطول 150-180.
