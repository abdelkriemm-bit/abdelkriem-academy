Grammar: Modals of deduction (present) · must, might, could, can't

must = I'm sure it's true · can't = I'm sure it's NOT true · might / could / may = it's possibly true.

modal + be: He must be tired. · modal + be + -ing (now): She might be driving.

عندما تستنتج شيئًا من الدليل الذي أمامك: «أكيد / لا بد» = must، و«مستحيل يكون» = can't، و«ربما» = might / could / may. الخطأ الشائع أن يقول المتعلم ✗ He mustn't be at home للمعنى «مستحيل يكون في البيت»؛ لكن mustn't معناها «ممنوع». الصحيح: ✓ He can't be at home. ولا تضع to بعد must: ✗ It must to be expensive.

How sure?ModalExample
100% yesmustThe lights are on. They must be at home.
about 50%might / could / mayHe isn't answering. He might be asleep.
100% nocan'tThat can't be Ali. He's in Aswan this week.

✗ He mustn't be at home — the lights are off. ✓ He can't be at home.

✗ It must to be new. ✓ It must be new.

For actions happening now use be + -ing: Listen! She must be practising the piano.

Exercises

Exercise 1 · Complete with must, might or can't.

  1. 1) I'm not sure where Nour is. She ___ be at the gym.
  2. 2) He's been working for twelve hours. He ___ be exhausted.
  3. 3) You've just eaten a big lunch. You ___ be hungry again!
  4. 4) That ___ be Omar — he's in Aswan this week.
  5. 5) Take an umbrella. It ___ rain later.
  6. 6) This phone costs 60,000 pounds! It ___ be the newest model.
Show the answers
1) might 2) must 3) can't 4) can't 5) might 6) must

Exercise 2 · Rewrite the sentences with must, might or can't.

  1. 1) I'm sure he is at home.
  2. 2) It's possible that the shop is closed.
  3. 3) I'm sure this isn't your bag.
  4. 4) Maybe she is working late.
  5. 5) I'm sure they are watching the match.
Show the answers
1) He must be at home. 2) The shop might / could / may be closed. 3) This can't be your bag. 4) She might / could / may be working late. 5) They must be watching the match.

Exercise 3 · Match 1–5 with a–e. a) It can't be new. b) He might be asleep. c) The battery must be dead. d) It might be out of paper. e) The internet must be down.

  1. 1) My phone won't turn on.
  2. 2) No websites will open.
  3. 3) Ali isn't answering his phone.
  4. 4) This laptop is only 3,000 pounds.
  5. 5) The printer isn't working.
Show the answers
1) c 2) e 3) b 4) a 5) d

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) لا بد أنها في العمل.
  2. 2) لا يمكن أن يكون هذا صحيحًا.
  3. 3) ربما يكون الواي فاي بطيئًا اليوم.
  4. 4) نسيت كلمة المرور الخاصة بي.
Show the answers
1) She must be at work. 2) That can't be true. 3) The Wi-Fi might / could / may be slow today. 4) I've forgotten my password. / I forgot my password.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak

Write & Speak — “Technology in my life”: write 120–150 words. Which devices do you use, what problems have you had, and are you spending too much time online? Then look at three photos on your phone and say aloud what the people must, might or can't be doing.

Example: I use technology all day. I check my phone as soon as I wake up, and I work on a laptop from nine to five. I also use my tablet to watch videos and to have video calls with my sister, who lives in Dubai. Technology makes my life easier, but it also causes problems. Last month my laptop crashed and I lost some important files because I hadn't backed them up. Now I back up everything every Friday. Sometimes the Wi-Fi at home is very slow in the evening. It might be because everyone in the building is online at the same time. My screen time is about six hours a day, and my eyes are always tired, so I must be spending too much time in front of screens. I'm going to switch off my phone an hour before bed.
