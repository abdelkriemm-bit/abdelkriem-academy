# American Diploma tests — verified facts (checked 25 Oct 2026). State ONLY these exam facts.

## Digital SAT (College Board, Assessment Framework v4, Sept 2026; satsuite.collegeboard.org)
- Two sections: Reading and Writing (RW) first, 10-minute break, then Math. Whole test 134 minutes, 98 questions.
- RW: two modules, 32 minutes each (64 min total). Each module 27 questions = 25 scored + 2 unscored pretest. Section = 54 questions (50 scored). About 1 minute 11 seconds per question.
- Adaptive by module (multistage): Module 1 is a broad mix of easy, medium and hard questions; performance on Module 1 decides whether Module 2 is harder or easier. You cannot go back to Module 1 once Module 2 starts. Within a module you can move back and forth and mark questions for review.
- Every RW question: one short passage (or a pair of short passages), 25–150 words, one four-option multiple-choice question. Passages: literature, history/social studies, humanities, science. Some include a table or graph.
- No penalty for wrong answers (answer every question).
- Scores: total 400–1600; each section 200–800; 10-point steps.
- Taken in the Bluebook app on a laptop or tablet. (Desmos calculator only matters for Math.)
- RW content domains (scored questions, both modules):
  - Information and Ideas ≈26% (12–14 q): Central Ideas and Details; Command of Evidence — Textual; Command of Evidence — Quantitative; Inferences
  - Craft and Structure ≈28% (13–15 q): Words in Context; Text Structure and Purpose; Cross-Text Connections
  - Expression of Ideas ≈20% (8–12 q): Rhetorical Synthesis (student notes → sentence that achieves a stated goal); Transitions
  - Standard English Conventions ≈26% (11–15 q): Boundaries (end-of-sentence and within-sentence punctuation: linking clauses, list items, supplements such as appositives/parentheticals, and when NO punctuation is needed — e.g., never between a subject and its verb); Form, Structure, and Sense (subject–verb agreement, pronoun–antecedent agreement, verb finiteness — gerunds, participles, infinitives vs finite verbs —, verb tense and aspect, subject–modifier placement / dangling modifiers, plural and possessive nouns, its/it's, their/they're/there, your/you're)
- Question order inside a module (typical): Craft and Structure → Information and Ideas → Standard English Conventions → Expression of Ideas (rhetorical synthesis last). Say "usually" if you mention it.

## Enhanced ACT (act.org; ACT Design Framework R2519, Feb 2026)
- Sections: English 50 questions (40 scored) / 35 min; Math 45 (41 scored) / 50 min; Reading 36 (27 scored) / 40 min; Science (optional) 40 / 40 min; Writing (optional) 1 essay / 40 min.
- Composite 1–36 = average of English, Math and Reading only (Science and Writing reported separately). Core test time 125 minutes.
- Since September 2025 the enhanced ACT applies to all tests, online and paper, including international. Not adaptive.
- English: reporting categories — Production of Writing 38–43%; Knowledge of Language 18–23%; Conventions of Standard English 38–43%. Scored passages: 5 essays (3 of about 340 words with 10 questions each, 2 of about 185 words with 5 questions each); essay types informational, argumentative, narrative. Questions refer to underlined portions; a "NO CHANGE" option is common.
- Reading: categories — Key Ideas and Details 44–52%; Craft and Structure 26–33%; Integration of Knowledge and Ideas 19–26%. Scored: 3 passages (two of about 750 words, one of about 650 words), 9 questions each; 1 literary narrative (fiction or memoir) + 2 informational (humanities, social science, natural science); each form has either a paired-passage set or a passage with charts/graphs.
- No penalty for wrong answers.

## EST (Egyptian Scholastic Test) — Ministry of Education; official site est.moe.gov.eg
- Computer-based; an alternative/equivalent to SAT/ACT for American Diploma students in Egypt; launched 2020 with Pearson; results certified by the Ministry.
- EST I is published BY SCHOOLS (not an official spec page) as: Literacy Test 1 (Writing and Language) 44 MCQ / 35 min; Literacy Test 2 (Reading) 52 MCQ / 65 min; Math 45 MCQ + 13 short answer / 90 min; optional essay 50 min. EST II = subject tests (Math 1, Math 2, Biology, Chemistry, Physics), 60 min each.
- Always add: "These figures are as published by schools; check the latest official EST candidate guide on est.moe.gov.eg before your test." Do NOT state official score scales or university minimums.
