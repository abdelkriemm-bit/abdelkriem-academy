STEM: Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

## Practice 1 | Foundation
### Form, Structure, and Sense (modifiers)
P: After studying the ancient map for hours, ______
A) the location of the lost temple became clear to the archaeologist. || The location did not study the map; the opening phrase must be followed by the person who studied.
*B) the archaeologist realised where the lost temple was. || Correct: the archaeologist did the studying.
C) the lost temple's location was finally understood. || The location did not study the map.
D) there was a clear answer for the archaeologist. || "there" cannot do the studying.

P: Built in the fourteenth century, ______
*A) the mosque has a minaret that can be seen from across the city. || Correct: the mosque was built in the fourteenth century.
B) visitors can see the mosque's minaret from across the city. || The visitors were not built in the fourteenth century.
C) people describe the mosque's minaret as beautiful. || People were not built in the fourteenth century.
D) from across the city one can see the minaret of the mosque. || "one" was not built in the fourteenth century.

P: Running across the field, ______
A) the ball was kicked by Omar into the net. || The ball was not running.
B) the net was reached by Omar's ball. || The net was not running.
*C) Omar kicked the ball into the net. || Correct.
D) Omar's ball went into the net. || The ball was not running; "Omar's" is only a possessive.

P: Known for its sweet dates, ______
A) farmers in Siwa grow more than twenty kinds of palm trees. || Farmers are not known for their sweet dates here; the phrase describes the oasis.
*B) the oasis of Siwa has more than twenty kinds of palm trees. || Correct: Siwa is known for its dates.
C) more than twenty kinds of palm trees grow in the oasis of Siwa. || The palm trees are the subject, but "its" refers to a single place.
D) people come to Siwa to see more than twenty kinds of palm trees. || People are not known for sweet dates.

P: Excited about the trip, ______
A) the bags were packed by the children the night before. || The bags were not excited.
*B) the children packed their bags the night before. || Correct.
C) the night before was when the children packed their bags. || "the night before" was not excited.
D) packing the bags was done the night before by the children. || "packing" was not excited.

P: To pass the exam, ______
A) hard work is needed by students. || "hard work" does not pass the exam.
B) the textbook must be read carefully. || The textbook does not pass the exam.
*C) students need to read the textbook carefully. || Correct: students pass the exam.
D) careful reading of the textbook is required. || "reading" does not pass the exam.

P: Covered in snow, ______
*A) the mountain looked completely white. || Correct: the mountain was covered in snow.
B) we thought the mountain looked completely white. || We were not covered in snow.
C) the climbers saw a completely white mountain. || The climbers were not covered in snow (the mountain was).
D) it was hard for the climbers to see the path on the mountain. || "it" is vague and was not covered in snow.

P: While walking home from school, ______
A) a strange noise came from the old house and frightened Hana. || The noise was not walking home.
*B) Hana heard a strange noise coming from the old house. || Correct.
C) the old house made a strange noise that Hana heard. || The house was not walking home.
D) there was a strange noise in the old house. || "there" was not walking.

P: Tired after the long flight, ______
A) the hotel bed looked very welcoming to the travellers. || The bed was not tired.
B) sleep came quickly to the travellers. || Sleep was not tired.
C) the travellers' room was booked for two nights. || The room was not tired.
*D) the travellers went straight to sleep. || Correct.

P: Written in the ninth century, ______
A) scholars still study the book on algebra by al-Khwarizmi. || Scholars were not written in the ninth century.
*B) al-Khwarizmi's book on algebra is still studied by scholars. || Correct: the book was written then.
C) al-Khwarizmi wrote a book on algebra that scholars still study. || Al-Khwarizmi was not "written".
D) students can still read the book on algebra. || Students were not written.

*A) the ancient scrolls have helped historians understand the region's past. || Correct: the scrolls were discovered.

C) the region's past has been explained by the ancient scrolls. || The past was not discovered in a cave.
D) a shepherd's discovery of ancient scrolls changed history. || A discovery is not itself discovered in a cave; the scrolls were.

P: Using data from more than a thousand weather stations, ______

D) the penguin's ability to swim is unusual. || "ability" is compared with birds.

P: Famous for its colourful markets, ______
