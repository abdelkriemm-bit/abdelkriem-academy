STEM: Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

## Practice 1 | Foundation
### Form, Structure, and Sense (parallel structure)
P: On weekends, Yasmin enjoys swimming, reading mystery novels, and ______
A) to bake bread with her grandmother. || Not parallel: "to bake" does not match "swimming" and "reading".
*B) baking bread with her grandmother. || Correct: three "-ing" forms.
C) she bakes bread with her grandmother. || A full clause does not match the "-ing" items.
D) bread baking with her grandmother. || Changes the form and is awkward.

P: The new park will give children a place to play, to learn, and ______
*A) to make friends. || Correct: three infinitives.
B) making friends. || Not parallel with "to play, to learn".
C) they can make friends. || A clause does not match the infinitives.
D) friends are made. || Not parallel.

P: The guide told us to stay together, to drink plenty of water, and ______
A) that we should not touch the paintings. || Not parallel with the infinitives.
B) not touching the paintings. || Not parallel.
*C) not to touch the paintings. || Correct.
D) the paintings must not be touched. || Not parallel.

P: The recipe is simple, cheap, and ______
A) it is quick to make. || A clause does not match the adjectives.
*B) quick. || Correct: three adjectives.
C) quickly. || An adverb does not match the adjectives.
D) has speed. || Not parallel.

P: Ancient Egyptians used the Nile for travel, for trade, and ______
A) to farm. || Not parallel with "for travel, for trade".
B) farming was done there. || Not parallel.
*C) for farming. || Correct.
D) they farmed. || Not parallel.

P: The teacher asked us to read the chapter, answer the questions, and ______
*A) write a short summary. || Correct: "read … answer … write".
B) writing a short summary. || Not parallel.
C) a short summary should be written. || Not parallel.
D) to be writing a short summary. || Not parallel.

P: The museum is famous not only for its gold masks but also ______
A) because its statues are huge. || Not parallel with "for its gold masks".
B) its huge statues. || Missing "for"; the two halves do not match.
*C) for its huge statues. || Correct: "not only for … but also for …".
D) the statues are huge. || Not parallel.

P: Karim likes to run in the morning and ______ in the evening.
A) swimming || Not parallel with "to run".
*B) to swim || Correct.
C) he swims || Not parallel.
D) swam || Wrong tense and not parallel.

P: The project was difficult, expensive, and ______
A) it took a long time. || A clause does not match the adjectives.
B) taking a long time. || Not parallel.
C) lengthily. || An adverb.
*D) slow. || Correct.

P: The students could either take the bus to the museum or ______
A) walking there. || Not parallel with "take".
*B) walk there. || Correct: "either take … or walk".
C) they could walk there. || Not parallel.
D) to walk there. || Not parallel.

*B) how far they migrated each year. || Correct: three question-word clauses.
C) migrating far each year. || Not parallel.
D) their yearly migration distance. || Not parallel.

P: The novel is less about solving a crime than ______
*A) about understanding a family's secrets. || Correct: "less about … than about …".
B) understanding a family's secrets. || Missing "about".
C) it is about understanding a family's secrets. || Not parallel.

P: The new law requires companies to reduce their waste, to recycle their packaging, and ______

D) emissions reported every year. || Not parallel.

P: For the ancient Egyptians, the Nile was both a source of food and ______
A) they travelled on it. || Not parallel.
B) for travelling. || Not parallel with "a source of food".
*C) a highway for travel. || Correct: "both a source … and a highway …".
D) travelling. || Not parallel.

P: The documentary explains why the reefs are dying, what scientists are doing to save them, and ______
A) the help that ordinary people can give. || Not parallel.
*B) how ordinary people can help. || Correct.
C) ordinary people helping. || Not parallel.
D) to help ordinary people. || Not parallel and changes meaning.

P: The program aims not only to train new teachers but also ______

P: The writer's essays are witty, clear, and ______
A) persuade readers easily. || Not parallel.
*B) persuasive. || Correct.
C) they persuade. || Not parallel.

P: Mona had to decide whether to accept the scholarship abroad or ______
A) staying in Cairo with her family. || Not parallel with "to accept".

C) stayed in Cairo with her family. || Not parallel.
*D) to stay in Cairo with her family. || Correct.

P: The study found that students who slept well were more focused, more patient, and ______
*A) more likely to remember new information. || Correct.
