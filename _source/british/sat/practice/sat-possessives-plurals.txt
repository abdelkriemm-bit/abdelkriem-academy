STEM: Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

## Practice 1 | Foundation
### Form, Structure, and Sense (plurals and possessives)
P: The ______ new building has solar panels on every roof.
A) schools || Plural, but the sentence needs a possessive (the building belongs to the school).
*B) school's || Correct: singular possessive.
C) schools' || Plural possessive; only one school is meant ("The … new building").
D) school || No possession shown.

P: All of the ______ uniforms were washed before the final match.
A) player's || Singular possessive; "All of the" shows there are many players.
B) players || Plural without possession.
*C) players' || Correct: plural possessive.
D) players's || Not a correct form.

P: The company changed ______ logo last year.
*A) its || Correct: possessive "its".
B) it's || Means "it is".
C) its' || Not a word.
D) their || Plural.

P: The ______ in the museum are more than three thousand years old.
A) statue's || Possessive; nothing belongs to the statues here.
*B) statues || Correct: simple plural.
C) statues' || Plural possessive; nothing follows to be possessed.
D) statue || Singular, but "are" needs a plural subject.

P: ______ going to visit the pyramids tomorrow morning.
A) Their || Possessive.
B) There || Place word.
*C) They're || Correct: "They are going…".
D) Theyre || Missing apostrophe.

P: The ______ wings were damaged in the storm.
A) bird || No possession.
B) birds || Plural without possession.
C) birds's || Not standard.
*D) bird's || Correct: one bird's wings.

P: ______ too hot to play football outside this afternoon.
A) Its || Possessive.
*B) It's || Correct: "It is too hot…".
C) Its' || Not a word.
D) It || "It too hot" has no verb.

P: The ______ wrote their names on the board.
*A) children || Correct: plural subject.
B) children's || Possessive; the children are the subject.
C) childrens' || Not a word.
D) childs || Not a word.

P: The students put ______ bags under the desks.
A) there || Place word.
B) they're || Means "they are".
*C) their || Correct.
D) theirs || Pronoun standing alone; it cannot come before a noun.

P: The three ______ cages are cleaned every morning by the zookeepers.
A) lion's || Singular, but "three" shows several lions.
*B) lions' || Correct: plural possessive.
C) lions || No possession.
D) lions's || Not standard.
