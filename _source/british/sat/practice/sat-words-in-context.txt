STEM: Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word or phrase?

## Practice 1 | Foundation
### Words in Context
P: The old map was so ______ that the hikers could not tell where one path ended and the next began; many of its lines had faded completely.
A) colourful || Bright colours would make the map easier, not harder, to read.
*B) unclear || Correct: faded lines make the map hard to read.
C) recent || A recent map would not have faded lines.
D) accurate || An accurate map would help the hikers.

P: Unlike her talkative older brother, Salma is ______; she often sits quietly through whole family dinners without saying a word.
*A) reserved || Correct: quiet and not talkative, contrasting with her brother.
B) cheerful || Cheerful does not mean quiet.
C) generous || Unrelated to talking.
D) curious || A curious person usually asks many questions.

P: The new medicine had a ______ effect: patients felt better within minutes of taking it.
A) gradual || "within minutes" means fast, not gradual.
B) harmful || Patients felt better, so the effect was not harmful.
*C) rapid || Correct.
D) temporary || The text says nothing about the effect ending.

P: Scientists were ______ by the results, which went against everything they had expected.
A) bored || Unexpected results would not bore scientists.
*B) puzzled || Correct: results that contradict expectations are confusing.
C) delighted || The text does not suggest the results were welcome.
D) persuaded || Nothing says the results convinced them of something.

P: The village relies on the river for almost everything: it provides water for drinking, for farming, and for transport. Without it, life there would be nearly ______.
A) pleasant || The point is that life would be very hard without the river.
B) peaceful || Not the logical result of losing the river.
C) famous || Unrelated.
*D) impossible || Correct.

P: Because the bridge had become ______, engineers closed it until it could be repaired.
*A) unsafe || Correct: closing until repair suggests danger.
B) popular || Popularity would not lead to closing it.
C) beautiful || Beauty would not lead to closing it.
D) expensive || Cost does not explain closing the bridge for repairs.

P: The author's description of the market is so ______ that readers can almost smell the spices and hear the traders calling out.
A) brief || A brief description would not create such strong images.
B) confusing || Confusing writing would not help readers imagine the scene.
*C) vivid || Correct: full of strong sensory detail.
D) formal || Formal style does not explain the sensory effect.

P: Although the team practised every day, its performance in the first match was ______: it lost by five goals.
A) impressive || Losing by five goals is not impressive.
*B) disappointing || Correct: the result was bad despite the practice.
C) predictable || The "Although" clause suggests a result that was not expected.
D) improving || One match cannot show improvement.

P: The desert plant can ______ months without rain by storing water in its thick leaves.
A) prevent || A plant cannot prevent months without rain.
B) remember || Unrelated.
*C) survive || Correct.
D) predict || Storing water does not predict anything.

P: Many visitors find the museum's layout ______; signs are missing, and several rooms have more than one exit.
*A) confusing || Correct: missing signs and many exits make it hard to navigate.
B) logical || The details describe problems.
C) modern || Unrelated to the problems listed.
D) spacious || Size is not discussed.

## Practice 2 | Foundation
### Words in Context
P: The coach praised the players for their ______: even when they were losing badly, they kept running until the final whistle.
A) speed || Not about speed specifically.
*B) determination || Correct: they kept trying even while losing.
C) height || Unrelated.
D) humour || Unrelated.

P: The ancient temple was ______ for centuries under the sand until archaeologists uncovered it in 1909.
A) built || It was not being built under the sand.
B) visited || It could not be visited while buried.
*C) hidden || Correct.
D) repaired || Nothing suggests repairs.

P: The scientist chose her words carefully, wanting her report to be ______ so that no reader could misunderstand it.
*A) precise || Correct: exact language prevents misunderstanding.
B) lengthy || Length does not prevent misunderstanding.
C) humorous || Unrelated.
D) mysterious || Mysterious writing invites misunderstanding.

P: Prices in the market ______ sharply during the holiday season, when demand for food and gifts is highest.
A) disappear || Prices do not disappear.
B) remain || "sharply" suggests a change.
*C) rise || Correct: high demand pushes prices up.
D) begin || Unclear and imprecise.

P: Karim is known for being ______ with his time: he never arrives late and always finishes his homework before it is due.
A) careless || The details show the opposite.
*B) disciplined || Correct.
C) generous || Unrelated to time management.
D) anxious || Nothing suggests worry.

P: The rainforest is home to a remarkable ______ of life, from tiny insects to huge trees and jaguars.
*A) variety || Correct: many different kinds of living things.
B) shortage || The text describes abundance.
C) memory || Unrelated.
D) silence || Unrelated.

P: The documentary offers a ______ look at the lives of fishermen, showing both the beauty and the danger of their work.
A) careless || A careless look would not show both sides.
*B) balanced || Correct: it shows both the beauty and the danger.
C) brief || Nothing suggests length.
D) humorous || Unrelated to the content described.

P: After hours of heavy rain, the dry riverbed became a ______ stream that swept away small trees.
A) gentle || Sweeping away trees is not gentle.
B) frozen || Unrelated.
C) shallow || Unlikely to sweep away trees; and the text stresses power.
*D) powerful || Correct.

P: The new law is meant to ______ air pollution by limiting the number of cars allowed in the city centre each day.
*A) reduce || Correct.
B) celebrate || Unrelated.
C) measure || Limiting cars does not measure pollution.
D) increase || Opposite of the goal.

P: The old letters gave historians a rare ______ into the daily life of a merchant family in the eighteenth century.
A) prize || Not used with "into".
B) error || Unrelated.
*C) glimpse || Correct: a brief view into something.
D) warning || Unrelated.

## Practice 3 | Exam level
### Words in Context
P: For decades, many biologists dismissed the idea that plants could "communicate." Recent studies showing that some plants release chemicals that warn their neighbours of insect attacks have led some researchers to ______ that view.
A) confirm || The new studies challenge, not confirm, the old dismissive view.
*B) reconsider || Correct: new evidence leads researchers to think again about the old view.
C) ignore || Nothing suggests they ignore it; they respond to it.
D) celebrate || Unrelated and illogical.

P: The novelist's style is notably ______: she uses short sentences, few adjectives, and almost no description of her characters' feelings.
A) elaborate || Elaborate means detailed and complex, the opposite.
*B) spare || Correct: plain and stripped down.
C) emotional || The text says she rarely describes feelings.
D) playful || Nothing suggests playfulness.

P: Although the committee's report was praised for its research, its recommendations were criticised as ______, since several of them would cost far more than the city could afford.
A) innovative || Being new is not a reason for criticism here.
B) cautious || Expensive plans are not cautious.
*C) impractical || Correct: they cannot realistically be carried out.
D) modest || Very expensive plans are not modest.

P: The archaeologist warned that the team's conclusions were ______: the findings came from a single site, and more excavations would be needed before anything could be said with certainty.
*A) tentative || Correct: not final, open to change.
B) definitive || The opposite: she says certainty is not yet possible.
C) irrelevant || She does not dismiss them.
D) obvious || Nothing suggests they are obvious.

P: In the early 1900s, Cairo's population grew so quickly that the city's water system, built for a much smaller population, could not ______ the demand.
A) predict || Not the issue; the issue is capacity.
B) reduce || The system does not reduce demand.
*C) meet || Correct: satisfy the demand.
D) describe || Unrelated.

P: Many critics found the film's ending ______; after two hours of careful storytelling, the main conflict is resolved in a single, rushed scene.
A) inevitable || The criticism is about the rush, not predictability.
B) faithful || Unrelated.
*C) abrupt || Correct: sudden and rushed.
D) lengthy || A single rushed scene is not lengthy.

P: The researchers' method was ______: they repeated each test three times and asked an independent lab to check every result.
A) hasty || Repeating tests is not hasty.
*B) rigorous || Correct: careful and thorough.
C) novel || Nothing says the method was new.
D) informal || Independent checks are not informal.

P: The poet's early work shows the clear ______ of the classical Arabic poets she read as a girl; her rhythms and images often echo theirs.
A) rejection || Echoing them is the opposite of rejecting them.
*B) influence || Correct.
C) criticism || Nothing suggests criticism.
D) absence || The text shows their presence.

P: The professor was known for her ______ explanations, which turned complicated theories into ideas that first-year students could easily follow.
A) obscure || Obscure explanations are hard to follow.
B) lengthy || Length is not the point.
*C) lucid || Correct: clear and easy to understand.
D) ambitious || Unrelated.

P: Some economists argue that the new tax will ______ small businesses, which already struggle with high costs, while doing little harm to large companies.
A) benefit || The text suggests harm to small businesses.
*B) burden || Correct: put extra weight on them.
C) ignore || A tax does not ignore businesses.
D) imitate || Unrelated.

## Practice 4 | Exam level
### Words in Context
P: The historian's account of the 1919 revolution is ______: she examines newspapers, letters, police reports, and diaries, leaving almost no available source unexamined.
*A) exhaustive || Correct: very thorough, covering nearly everything.
B) biased || Nothing suggests one-sidedness.
C) brief || A study of so many sources is not brief.
D) speculative || It is based on many sources, not guesses.

P: The engineer's design was ______: it used only cheap, locally available materials but was strong enough to survive major storms.
A) fragile || The text says it was strong.
B) extravagant || Cheap materials are not extravagant.
*C) ingenious || Correct: clever, combining low cost with strength.
D) outdated || Nothing suggests it was old-fashioned.

P: Although the two species of bird look almost ______, a trained observer can tell them apart by the small white mark on one species' wings.
A) different || The contrast with "a trained observer can tell them apart" requires that they look alike.
*B) identical || Correct.
C) dangerous || Unrelated.
D) rare || Unrelated to telling them apart.

P: The playwright ______ the old folk tale for modern audiences, keeping its main characters but moving the story to a present-day apartment building in Giza.
A) rejected || She kept the main characters.
*B) adapted || Correct: changed it to suit a new setting.
C) memorised || Unrelated.
D) translated || Moving the setting is not translating.

P: The town's decline was ______ rather than sudden: over fifty years, factories closed one by one and young people slowly moved away.
A) dramatic || The text contrasts it with a sudden decline.
*B) gradual || Correct.
C) temporary || Fifty years of decline is not temporary, and the contrast is with "sudden".
D) mysterious || The causes are given.

P: The museum curator was careful not to ______ the age of the statue until laboratory tests had been completed.
A) admire || Admiring it is unrelated to waiting for tests.
B) protect || Unrelated.
*C) announce || Correct: she would not state the age publicly before the tests.
D) increase || One cannot increase a statue's age.

P: The scientist's theory was initially met with ______, but within a decade, new evidence had convinced most of her colleagues.
*A) scepticism || Correct: doubt that later turned into agreement.
B) enthusiasm || The text suggests colleagues had to be convinced.
C) confusion || Doubt, not confusion, fits being "convinced" later.
D) silence || Not clearly supported.

P: The journalist's questions were ______, forcing the minister to explain exactly how the new budget would be spent.
A) vague || Vague questions would not force exact answers.
*B) pointed || Correct: sharp and direct.
C) friendly || Nothing suggests friendliness; the questions put pressure on him.
D) irrelevant || They led to important answers.

P: The new building's glass walls ______ with the old stone houses around it, and some residents complain that it does not belong in the neighbourhood.
A) harmonise || Residents complain that it does not fit.
B) compete || Imprecise; the complaint is about not fitting in.
*C) clash || Correct: they look out of place next to each other.
D) agree || Buildings do not "agree"; and the meaning is the opposite.

P: Rather than state her opinion directly, the essayist ______ it through a series of questions that lead readers to her conclusion.
*A) implies || Correct: suggests without stating directly.
B) abandons || She still communicates it.
C) exaggerates || Nothing suggests exaggeration.
D) denies || She does not deny it.

## Practice 5 | Challenge
### Words in Context
P: Early accounts of the expedition described it as a heroic success. More recent historians, drawing on the explorers' private letters, offer a more ______ picture, revealing serious disagreements, mistakes, and near-disasters.
A) flattering || The new picture is less flattering.
*B) nuanced || Correct: more complex, showing both good and bad.
C) simplistic || Adding disagreements and mistakes makes it more complex, not simpler.
D) cheerful || Mistakes and near-disasters are not cheerful.

P: The economist's predictions have proven remarkably ______: in each of the past five years, her forecasts for inflation have been within half a percentage point of the actual figure.
A) controversial || Accuracy does not make them controversial.
*B) reliable || Correct: consistently accurate.
C) pessimistic || Nothing indicates gloomy forecasts.
D) unpopular || Unrelated.

P: The novelist's characters rarely say what they feel; instead, their emotions are ______ in small gestures, such as a cup of tea left untouched or a door closed a little too firmly.
A) concealed || The emotions are shown through gestures, not hidden completely.
*B) conveyed || Correct: communicated through gestures.
C) exaggerated || Small gestures are the opposite of exaggeration.
D) dismissed || The emotions are not dismissed.

P: The council's decision to demolish the historic market was widely criticised, and a year later it was ______: the market was restored instead.
A) reinforced || The decision was reversed, not strengthened.
B) celebrated || It was criticised.
*C) rescinded || Correct: cancelled.
D) explained || Explaining it would not lead to restoration.

P: Ibn Khaldun's ideas about how societies rise and fall were largely ______ in Europe until the nineteenth century, when his work was finally translated and widely discussed.
A) controversial || They could not be controversial if they were unknown.
*B) overlooked || Correct: not noticed until later.
C) celebrated || The opposite of the situation described.
D) disproved || Nothing suggests they were proved wrong.

P: The scientist cautioned that the link between screen time and poor sleep, while ______, did not prove that screens cause sleep problems.
*A) consistent || Correct: the link appears regularly, but correlation is not proof.
B) imaginary || If the link were imaginary, there would be nothing to caution about.
C) causal || The sentence says it does NOT prove cause.
D) irrelevant || She takes it seriously.

P: The translator faced a difficult task: the poem's humour depends on Arabic wordplay that has no ______ in English.
A) audience || Unrelated.
B) history || Unrelated.
*C) equivalent || Correct: nothing in English means and sounds the same.
D) value || Imprecise.

P: Though the documentary claims to present both sides of the debate, critics note that it gives far more time to supporters of the dam, making its supposed ______ difficult to believe.
A) popularity || Not what is being questioned.
*B) impartiality || Correct: fairness to both sides.
C) accuracy || The criticism is about balance, not facts.
D) length || Unrelated.

P: The artist's later paintings are ______ her early ones: the bright colours and busy street scenes of her youth give way to pale, empty landscapes.
A) indistinguishable from || The text describes clear differences.
B) inspired by || The text stresses contrast, not influence.
*C) a departure from || Correct: a clear move away from the earlier style.
D) copies of || They are different.

P: Though she had little formal training, the self-taught engineer's understanding of hydraulics was so ______ that university professors often asked for her advice.
A) superficial || Professors would not seek advice from shallow understanding.
*B) profound || Correct: deep.
C) limited || Opposite.
D) recent || Unrelated to why experts seek her advice.
