## Practice 1 | Foundation
### Form, Structure, and Sense (verb tense)
P: In 1869, the Suez Canal ______ to ships for the first time, cutting the sea journey from Europe to Asia by thousands of kilometres.
A) opens || Present tense; the date 1869 places the event in the past.
*B) opened || Correct: simple past for a finished event at a stated past time.
C) has opened || The present perfect is not used with a finished past time like "In 1869".
D) will open || Future tense.

P: Every year, millions of birds ______ over Egypt on their way between Europe and Africa.
*A) fly || Correct: simple present for a regular, repeated event ("Every year").
B) flew || Past; "Every year" describes a habit that is still true.
C) had flown || Past perfect; there is no earlier past event.
D) will have flown || Future perfect; nothing points to a deadline in the future.

P: Since 2015, the number of electric cars in the city ______ steadily.
A) grows || "Since 2015" needs the present perfect.
B) grew || Simple past cannot be used with "since" for a situation continuing to now.
*C) has grown || Correct: present perfect for something that started in the past and continues.
D) will grow || Future tense.

P: By the time the firefighters arrived, the fire ______ most of the warehouse.
A) destroys || Present tense in a past narrative.
B) has destroyed || Present perfect does not fit a past reference point ("By the time … arrived").
*C) had destroyed || Correct: past perfect for an action finished before another past action.
D) will destroy || Future tense.

P: Next summer, the museum ______ a special exhibition on the history of Egyptian cinema.
A) hosted || Past tense; "Next summer" is in the future.
B) had hosted || Past perfect.
C) has hosted || Present perfect.
*D) will host || Correct.

P: Ibn Battuta began his journey in 1325 and ______ through more than forty modern-day countries over the next thirty years.
A) travels || Present tense; the passage describes the past.
*B) travelled || Correct: past tense, parallel with "began".
C) has travelled || Present perfect does not fit a finished past period.
D) will travel || Future.

P: At this moment, the astronauts on the space station ______ above the Pacific Ocean.
A) flew || Past; "At this moment" is the present.
B) had flown || Past perfect.
*C) are flying || Correct: present continuous for an action happening now.
D) have flown || Present perfect does not describe an action in progress "at this moment".

P: Scientists ______ the surface of Mars with robots for more than fifty years.
A) explore || Simple present does not express a duration up to now ("for more than fifty years").
B) explored || Simple past suggests the exploration has ended.
*C) have explored || Correct: present perfect for an action continuing up to now.
D) will explore || Future.

P: When the bell rang, the students ______ their books and left the classroom.
A) close || Present tense in a past narrative.
*B) closed || Correct: past tense, matching "rang" and "left".
C) have closed || Present perfect.
D) are closing || Present continuous.

P: Water ______ at 100 degrees Celsius at sea level.
A) boiled || Scientific facts are stated in the present tense.
B) had boiled || Past perfect.
*C) boils || Correct.
D) has been boiling || Present perfect continuous; a general fact needs the simple present.
