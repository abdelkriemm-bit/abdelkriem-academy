## Practice 1 | Foundation
### Form, Structure, and Sense (pronoun–antecedent agreement)
P: The new museum in Giza will open ______ doors to the public next spring.
*A) its || Correct: "museum" is singular, so the pronoun is "its".
B) their || Plural pronoun for a singular noun.
C) it's || "it's" means "it is", which makes no sense before "doors".
D) they're || "they're" means "they are".

P: The students handed in ______ projects on Thursday.
A) its || Singular pronoun for the plural "students".
*B) their || Correct: plural antecedent "students".
C) his or her || Singular, but the antecedent is plural.
D) there || "there" is a place word, not a possessive.

P: Each country sent ______ best athletes to the games.
A) their || Plural, but "Each country" is singular.
B) they're || Means "they are".
*C) its || Correct: singular antecedent "country".
D) it's || Means "it is".

P: The company announced that ______ would open a new factory in Sadat City.
A) they || Plural, but "company" is singular.
*B) it || Correct.
C) them || Plural and in the object form.
D) one || "one" does not refer back to the company.

P: When the bees returned to the hive, ______ performed a dance to show where the flowers were.
*A) they || Correct: plural antecedent "bees".
B) it || Singular; it would seem to refer to "the hive".
C) one || Does not refer to the bees.
D) she || Singular.

P: The committee published ______ final report after six months of work.
A) their || The committee acts as one group here, so the singular "its" is used.
B) it's || Means "it is".
C) they're || Means "they are".
*D) its || Correct.

P: The old trees in the park are beautiful, but ______ roots have damaged the paths.
A) its || Singular, but "trees" is plural.
*B) their || Correct.
C) it's || Means "it is".
D) there || Not a possessive.

P: The football club has hired a new coach for ______ youth team.
A) their || Plural; "club" is singular.
*B) its || Correct.
C) it's || Means "it is".
D) they're || Means "they are".

P: The Nile Delta is famous for ______ rich soil.
*A) its || Correct.
B) their || Plural.
C) it's || Means "it is".
D) its' || "its'" is not a word.

P: The two teams shook hands after ______ match ended in a draw.
A) its || Singular; "teams" is plural.
B) it's || Means "it is".
*C) their || Correct.
D) there || Not a possessive.
