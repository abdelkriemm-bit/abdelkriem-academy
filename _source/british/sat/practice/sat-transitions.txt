STEM: Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?

## Practice 1 | Foundation
### Transitions
P: Camels can go for days without drinking water. ______ they can close their nostrils to keep out blowing sand.
A) However, || "However" signals a contrast, but the second sentence adds another way camels are suited to the desert rather than contradicting the first.
*B) Moreover, || Correct: the second sentence adds a second, related fact about how camels survive in the desert.
C) As a result, || Closing their nostrils is not a result of going days without water.
D) For example, || Closing their nostrils is not an example of going without water.

P: The old bridge was too narrow for modern trucks. ______ the city decided to build a wider bridge next to it.
*A) Therefore, || Correct: building a wider bridge is the result of the old one being too narrow.
B) However, || There is no contrast.
C) Similarly, || The second sentence is not a similar case.
D) For instance, || Not an example.

P: Many people believe that bats are blind. ______ most bats can see quite well, and some see better than humans in low light.
A) Similarly, || The second sentence corrects the belief; it is not similar to it.
B) As a result, || Not a result.
*C) In fact, || Correct: "In fact" introduces information that corrects a mistaken belief.
D) Furthermore, || "Furthermore" adds support; this sentence contradicts the belief.

P: Some desert plants store water in their thick stems. ______ the cactus can store enough water to survive for months without rain.
A) However, || No contrast.
B) In contrast, || No contrast.
C) Therefore, || The cactus's ability is an example, not a result.
*D) For example, || Correct: the cactus is an example of a desert plant that stores water.

P: Nour studied for the exam every day for a month. ______ she earned the highest score in her class.
A) Nevertheless, || "Nevertheless" suggests an unexpected result, but a high score after studying is expected.
*B) As a result, || Correct.
C) For example, || Not an example.
D) Instead, || Nothing is being replaced.

P: The first part of the novel takes place in a busy Cairo neighbourhood. ______ the second part is set in a quiet village in the Delta.
*A) By contrast, || Correct: busy city vs quiet village.
B) Similarly, || The settings are different, not similar.
C) Consequently, || The village setting is not caused by the city setting.
D) For instance, || Not an example.

P: The library opens at 8 a.m. on weekdays. ______ on Fridays, it does not open until noon.
A) Therefore, || Not a result.
B) For example, || Not an example of opening at 8 a.m.
C) Likewise, || The Friday time is different, not alike.
*D) However, || Correct: Friday is an exception to the weekday time.

P: First, mix the flour and the water. ______ add the salt and knead the dough for ten minutes.
A) In contrast, || Adding the salt does not contrast with mixing the flour and water; it is the next step.
*B) Next, || Correct: the next step in a sequence.
C) However, || No contrast.
D) For example, || Not an example.

P: Solar power is clean and renewable. ______ it is becoming cheaper every year.
A) Instead, || Nothing is replaced.
B) In contrast, || No contrast.
*C) Additionally, || Correct: a further advantage is added.
D) Nevertheless, || No unexpected contrast.

P: The team lost its first three matches of the season. ______ it went on to win the championship.
A) Therefore, || Winning is not a result of losing.
B) Similarly, || Not similar.
*C) Nevertheless, || Correct: winning is surprising after a bad start.
D) For example, || Not an example.

## Practice 3 | Exam level
### Transitions
P: In 1922, Howard Carter found the tomb of Tutankhamun almost untouched. Most royal tombs in the Valley of the Kings, ______ had been robbed in ancient times.
A) for example, || The other tombs are not an example of an untouched tomb.
B) as a result, || The robbing of other tombs is not a result of Carter's discovery.
*C) by contrast, || Correct: untouched tomb vs robbed tombs.
D) in addition, || The second sentence contrasts with the first; it does not add a similar point.

P: Researchers once thought that octopuses were solitary animals that avoided one another. ______ recent studies have recorded groups of octopuses sharing dens.
*A) However, || Correct: the new evidence contradicts the old belief.
B) Similarly, || Not similar.
C) Therefore, || The studies are not a result of the belief.
D) For instance, || Not an example of the belief.

P: The poet rarely used rhyme in her early work. ______ her final collection is written almost entirely in rhyming couplets.
A) Likewise, || The final collection is different, not alike.
B) Consequently, || Not a result.
C) For example, || Not an example.
*D) In contrast, || Correct.

P: The medieval city of Fez had more than a hundred public fountains. ______ residents rarely had to walk far for clean water.
A) Nevertheless, || Not a surprising contrast; it is a logical result.
*B) Thus, || Correct: easy access to water follows from having many fountains.
C) Similarly, || Not a similar case.
D) By contrast, || No contrast.

P: Elephants have excellent memories. ______ they can remember the locations of water holes they visited decades earlier.
*A) Specifically, || Correct: the second sentence gives a specific case of the general claim.
B) However, || No contrast.
C) Instead, || Nothing is replaced.
D) Regardless, || Nothing is being ignored or set aside.

P: The engineers considered using steel for the new bridge. ______ they chose reinforced concrete, which resists salty sea air better.
A) For example, || Not an example.
B) Similarly, || Not similar.
*C) Ultimately, || Correct: it introduces the final decision after considering steel.
D) Furthermore, || The choice of concrete is not an extra point added to the steel idea; it replaces it.

P: Most mammals, including cattle, cannot see the colour red well. ______ bulls charge at a matador's red cape because of its movement, not its colour.
A) In contrast, || The second sentence follows from the first; it does not contrast with it.
B) Similarly, || Not a similar case.
*C) Thus, || Correct: since bulls cannot see red well, the movement must be what they react to.
D) Meanwhile, || Not about simultaneous events.

P: The first telephones were connected by long wires. ______ today's mobile phones send signals through the air.
*A) In contrast, || Correct.
B) Similarly, || Not similar.
C) As a result, || Mobile phones are not a result of wired phones in this text.
D) For instance, || Not an example.

P: The town's population doubled between 1990 and 2010. ______ the number of schools stayed the same, and classrooms became crowded.
A) Therefore, || The number of schools staying the same is not caused by the population growth.
B) For example, || Not an example.
*C) However, || Correct: schools did not grow while the population did.
D) Likewise, || Not similar.

P: The writer spent ten years researching the history of the Suez Canal. ______ her book contains details that no other account includes.
A) Nevertheless, || Not surprising; it follows from the research.
B) In contrast, || No contrast.
*C) Consequently, || Correct.
D) For instance, || Not an example.
