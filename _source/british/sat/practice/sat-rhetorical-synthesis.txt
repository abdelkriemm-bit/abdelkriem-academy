## Practice 1 | Foundation
### Rhetorical Synthesis
P: While researching a topic, a student has taken the following notes:
• The Aswan High Dam is on the Nile River in southern Egypt.
• It was completed in 1970.
• It controls the yearly flooding of the Nile.
• It produces electricity for millions of homes.
• It created Lake Nasser, one of the largest artificial lakes in the world.
Q: The student wants to state when the dam was completed. Which choice most effectively uses relevant information from the notes to accomplish this goal?
*A) The Aswan High Dam on the Nile River was completed in 1970. || Correct: it gives the completion date, which is the goal.
B) The Aswan High Dam controls the Nile's yearly floods. || True, but it does not say when the dam was completed.
C) Lake Nasser is one of the largest artificial lakes in the world. || Does not mention the completion date.
D) The dam produces electricity for millions of homes in Egypt. || Does not mention the completion date.

P: @
Q: The student wants to emphasise two benefits of the dam. Which choice most effectively uses relevant information from the notes to accomplish this goal?
A) The Aswan High Dam, completed in 1970, is in southern Egypt. || Gives a date and a place, not benefits.
B) The dam created Lake Nasser in 1970. || One fact, not two benefits.
*C) The Aswan High Dam both controls the Nile's yearly flooding and produces electricity for millions of homes. || Correct: two benefits.
D) Lake Nasser, created by the dam, is one of the world's largest artificial lakes. || Describes the lake, not benefits.
