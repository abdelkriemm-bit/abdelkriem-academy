## Practice 1 | Foundation
### Form, Structure, and Sense (pronoun case)
P: The teacher asked Karim and ______ to carry the science equipment to the lab.
A) I || "I" is a subject pronoun, but here the pronoun is an object of "asked". Test it alone: "asked I" is wrong.
*B) me || Correct: object form. Test it alone: "The teacher asked me…".
C) myself || "myself" is used only when the subject is also "I".
D) mine || Possessive, not an object.

P: My brother and ______ visited the Egyptian Museum last weekend.
*A) I || Correct: subject form. Test it alone: "I visited…".
B) me || Object form in a subject position.
C) myself || Reflexive; no "I" subject for it to refer back to.
D) mine || Possessive.

P: The award was given to the student ______ had written the best essay.
A) whom || Object form; the pronoun is the subject of "had written".
*B) who || Correct: subject of "had written".
C) which || Used for things, not people.
D) whose || Possessive; it would need a noun after it.

P: Between you and ______, I think the test was easier than last year's.
A) I || "between" is a preposition, so it takes the object form.
*B) me || Correct.
C) myself || Reflexive; there is no "I" subject in that phrase.
D) mine || Possessive.

P: The coach told ______ players that practice would start at seven o'clock.
*A) us || Correct: object form. Test it: "told us that practice…".
B) we || Subject form in an object position.
C) our || Possessive; "told our players" changes the meaning and is not what the sentence means.
D) ourselves || Reflexive; no "we" subject.

P: Hoda is taller than ______, but I am faster.
A) me || In formal writing, the comparison is completed with the subject form: "taller than I [am]".
*B) I || Correct.
C) myself || Reflexive.
D) mine || Possessive.

P: The scientist ______ discovered the new comet was only twenty-three years old.
A) whom || Object form; the pronoun is the subject of "discovered".
B) which || For things.
*C) who || Correct.
D) whose || Possessive.

P: The prize will be shared between Laila and ______.
A) he || Subject form after a preposition.
B) himself || Reflexive; no "he" subject.
*C) him || Correct: object of the preposition "between".
D) his || Possessive.

P: ______ and her cousins built a small boat out of recycled bottles.
A) Her || Object form in a subject position: "Her built…" is wrong.
*B) She || Correct.
C) Herself || Reflexive.
D) Hers || Possessive.

P: The guide showed the tourists and ______ the hidden room behind the altar.
A) we || Subject form in an object position.
B) ourselves || Reflexive; no "we" subject.
C) our || Possessive.
*D) us || Correct.

## Practice 3 | Exam level

A) who || The pronoun is the object of "chose" (the committee chose her), so "whom" is needed.
*B) whom || Correct.
C) whose || Possessive.
D) which || For things.

D) theirs || Possessive.

P: Neither my sister nor ______ had ever seen snow before the trip.
A) me || Subject position: "I had ever seen snow".

A) I || Object of the preposition "among" needs "me".

P: The project was a success thanks to the volunteers and ______.
A) she || After "to", the object form is needed.
B) herself || Reflexive.
