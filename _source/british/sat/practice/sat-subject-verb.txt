STEM: Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

## Practice 1 | Foundation
### Form, Structure, and Sense (subject–verb agreement)
P: The collection of ancient coins in the museum's basement ______ more than four thousand pieces, some of them over two thousand years old.
*A) includes || Correct: the subject is "collection" (singular); "of ancient coins" is only a describing phrase.
B) include || Plural verb, but the subject "collection" is singular.
C) are including || Plural, and the continuous form does not fit a permanent fact.
D) have included || Plural verb for a singular subject.

P: Every morning, the students in Ms. Rania's class ______ a short poem before the lesson begins.
A) reads || Singular verb, but the subject "students" is plural.
*B) read || Correct: plural subject "students" + plural verb "read".
C) has read || Singular verb.
D) is reading || Singular verb.

P: Neither the coach nor the players ______ expected the storm to cancel the final match.
A) was || "Neither … nor": the verb agrees with the nearer subject, "players" (plural).
B) has || Singular, but the nearer subject "players" is plural.
*C) had || Correct: "had expected" works with the plural "players" (and the past context).
D) is || Singular and present.

P: The list of books that students must read before the exam ______ on the school's website.
A) are || The subject is "list", not "books".
*B) is || Correct: singular subject "list".
C) were || Plural verb.
D) have been || Plural verb.

P: There ______ three reasons why the old bridge was closed last year.
*A) were || Correct: in a "there" sentence, the verb agrees with what follows: "three reasons" (plural), and the context is past.
B) was || Singular verb for "three reasons".
C) is || Singular, and the context ("last year") is past.
D) has been || Singular verb.

P: Bread, cheese, and olives ______ the traditional breakfast in many villages of the Delta.
A) makes || Singular, but the compound subject joined by "and" is plural.
B) has made || Singular.
*C) make || Correct: three items joined by "and" form a plural subject.
D) is making || Singular.

P: Each of the five paintings in the exhibition ______ a different season of the year.
A) show || Plural, but the subject "Each" is singular.
B) are showing || Plural.
C) have shown || Plural.
*D) shows || Correct: "Each" is singular; "of the five paintings" does not change that.

P: The number of students who walk to school ______ fallen sharply since the new bus service started.
*A) has || Correct: "The number of …" is singular.
B) have || "The number" (singular) needs "has".
C) are || Plural and wrong tense form.
D) were || Plural.

P: The flowers on the balcony ______ every morning by Omar's grandmother.
A) is watered || Singular verb for the plural subject "flowers".
*B) are watered || Correct: plural subject "flowers" + plural verb.
C) was watered || Singular.
D) has been watered || Singular.

P: A team of engineers from three universities ______ designing a robot that can climb walls.
A) are || The subject is "team" (singular), not "engineers" or "universities".
B) were || Plural.
*C) is || Correct: singular subject "team".
D) have been || Plural.

P: The painter's use of bright colours and simple shapes ______ her work easy to recognise.

P: Behind the school ______ a small garden and a pond full of fish.
