STEM: Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

## Practice 1 | Foundation
### Form, Structure, and Sense (comparisons)
P: The population of Cairo is much larger than ______
A) Alexandria. || Compares a population with a city.
*B) that of Alexandria. || Correct: "that of Alexandria" = the population of Alexandria.
C) those of Alexandria. || "those" is plural; "population" is singular.
D) Alexandria does. || Compares a population with a city.

P: Of the two bridges, the new one is ______
*A) stronger. || Correct: use the comparative "-er" form for two things.
B) strongest. || The superlative is for three or more.
C) more stronger. || Double comparative.
D) most strong. || Superlative and incorrect form.

P: Nile crocodiles are larger than ______ found in most other African rivers.
A) the crocodile || Singular; it does not match "crocodiles".
B) that || Singular; it does not match "crocodiles".
*C) those || Correct: plural, matching "crocodiles".
D) it || Singular pronoun.

P: This is the ______ book I have ever read.
A) more interesting || Comparative; "ever read" compares with all books (three or more).
*B) most interesting || Correct.
C) interestingest || Not a word.
D) most interestingly || Adverb.

P: There are ______ students in the evening class than in the morning class.
A) less || "less" is used with things you cannot count.
*B) fewer || Correct: "students" can be counted.
C) lesser || "lesser" means "of lower importance".
D) littler || Not standard.

P: The weather in Aswan is hotter than ______ in Alexandria.
*A) the weather || Correct: weather is compared with weather.
B) Alexandria || Compares weather with a city.
C) Alexandria's || The possessive "Alexandria's in Alexandria" makes no sense.
D) the city || Compares weather with a city.

P: Hoda runs faster ______ anyone else on her team.
A) then || "then" refers to time; comparisons use "than".
*B) than || Correct.
C) as || "faster as" is not standard.
D) from || "faster from" is not standard.

P: The second test was not as difficult ______ the first.
A) than || "as … as" is the correct pair.
B) like || "as difficult like" is not standard.
*C) as || Correct.
D) then || Not a comparison word.

P: Between Karim and Omar, Omar is the ______ player.
*A) better || Correct: two players → comparative.
B) best || Superlative is for three or more.
C) more better || Double comparative.
D) most good || Not standard.

P: The prices at the new market are lower than ______ at the old one.
A) the old market || Compares prices with a market.
B) that || Singular; it does not match "prices".
C) it || Singular.
*D) those || Correct: plural, matching "prices".

## Practice 2 | Foundation
### Form, Structure, and Sense (comparisons)
P: Like ______ the walls of the new library are made of local stone.
A) the old library, || Compares walls with a library.
*B) those of the old library, || Correct: walls are compared with walls.
C) that of the old library, || Singular; it does not match "walls".
D) the old library's wall, || Singular; it does not match "walls".

P: This year's harvest was ______ than last year's.
A) more large || Not standard; "larger" is the correct form.
*B) larger || Correct.
C) largest || Superlative.
D) more larger || Double comparative.

P: The Nile is the ______ river in Africa.
A) longer || Comparative; Africa has many rivers.
B) more long || Not standard.
*C) longest || Correct.
D) most longest || Double superlative.

P: Mona's essay was better organised than ______
*A) Salma's. || Correct: "Salma's" = Salma's essay.
B) Salma. || Compares an essay with a person.
C) that of Salma's. || "that of" + possessive doubles the possession.
D) Salma was. || Compares an essay with a person.

P: There was ______ rain this winter than last winter.
A) fewer || "fewer" is for things you can count.
*B) less || Correct: "rain" cannot be counted.
C) lesser || Means "lower in importance".
D) little || Not a comparative.

P: The bus is cheaper ______ the train, but it is much slower.
A) then || Time word, not comparison.
B) as || "cheaper as" is not standard.
*C) than || Correct.
D) to || "cheaper to" is not standard.

P: The pyramids at Giza are older than ______ in Mexico.
A) the pyramid || Singular.
*B) the pyramids || Correct: pyramids compared with pyramids.
C) that || Singular.
D) Mexico || Compares pyramids with a country.

P: Of all the planets, Jupiter is the ______
A) bigger. || Comparative; there are more than two planets.
B) most big. || Not standard.
C) biggest one of them all planets. || Ungrammatical.
*D) biggest. || Correct.

P: The students' results this year were as good as ______
*A) last year's. || Correct: results compared with results.
B) last year. || Compares results with a year.
C) last year was. || Compares results with a year.
D) the year before. || Compares results with a year.

P: The red car is ______ of the two cars in the showroom.
A) the most expensive || Superlative; there are only two cars.
*B) the more expensive || Correct.
C) the expensivest || Not a word.
D) more expensiver || Double comparative.

## Practice 3 | Exam level
### Form, Structure, and Sense (comparisons)
P: According to the survey, students' interest in science is higher today than ______ twenty years ago.
A) students || Compares interest with students.
*B) it was || Correct: "it" = students' interest; interest is compared with interest.
C) they were || "they" refers to students, not interest.
D) those || Plural; "interest" is singular.

P: Unlike ______ the novelist's latest book is set entirely in the countryside.
A) her other novels, which || "which" turns the comparison into a fragment.
*B) her earlier novels, || Correct: book compared with novels.
C) her earlier time, || Compares a book with a time.
D) when she wrote earlier, || Compares a book with an event.

P: The salaries of teachers in the city are higher than ______ in rural areas.
A) that of teachers || Singular "that" does not match plural "salaries".
*B) those of teachers || Correct.
C) teachers || Compares salaries with teachers.
D) teachers do || Compares salaries with teachers.

P: Of the three candidates, Ahmed had the ______ experience.
A) more || Comparative; there are three candidates.
B) mostest || Not a word.
*C) most || Correct.
D) much || Not a comparison form.

P: The coral reefs of the Red Sea are more colourful than ______
*A) those of the Mediterranean. || Correct: reefs compared with reefs.
B) the Mediterranean. || Compares reefs with a sea.
C) that of the Mediterranean. || Singular "that" does not match "reefs".
D) the Mediterranean's reef. || Singular.

P: Like ______ Ibn Sina studied both medicine and philosophy.
A) the work of al-Razi, || Compares a person (Ibn Sina) with work.
*B) al-Razi, || Correct: person compared with person.
C) al-Razi's studies, || Compares a person with studies.
D) that of al-Razi, || Compares a person with "that".

P: The new phone's battery lasts twice as long as ______
A) the old phone. || Compares a battery with a phone.
B) the old phone's battery does it. || "does it" is ungrammatical here.
*C) the old phone's. || Correct: "the old phone's" = the old phone's battery.
D) that of the old phone's. || Doubled possession.

P: There are ______ trees in the city centre than there were ten years ago.
A) less || "trees" can be counted.
B) lesser || Means "lower in importance".
*C) fewer || Correct.
D) littler || Not standard.

P: The climate of Siwa is drier than ______
A) the Delta. || Compares climate with a region.
B) those of the Delta. || Plural; "climate" is singular.
*C) that of the Delta. || Correct.
D) the Delta is. || Compares climate with a region.

P: The second experiment was ______ than the first because the samples were kept colder.
*A) more successful || Correct.
B) most successful || Superlative.
C) successfuller || Not a word.
D) more successfuller || Double comparative.

## Practice 4 | Exam level
### Form, Structure, and Sense (comparisons)
P: The paintings in the east gallery are larger than ______ in the west gallery, but the west gallery's paintings are older.
A) that || Singular.
*B) those || Correct.
C) it || Singular.
D) the gallery || Compares paintings with a gallery.

P: Compared with ______ modern bricks are lighter but less able to keep homes cool.
A) the old days, || Compares bricks with a time.
B) mud, || Compares bricks with a material, not with bricks.
*C) traditional mud bricks, || Correct: bricks compared with bricks.
D) traditional builders, || Compares bricks with people.

P: The cost of living in Cairo has risen faster than ______ in most other cities in the region.
A) other cities || Compares cost with cities.
*B) it has || Correct: "it" = the cost of living; cost is compared with cost.
C) they have || "they" would refer to cities.
D) those || Plural.

P: Of the two proposals, the committee chose the ______ one.
A) cheapest || Superlative; only two proposals.
*B) cheaper || Correct.
C) more cheap || Not standard.
D) most cheaper || Double form.

P: The writing of Naguib Mahfouz is often compared to ______
*A) that of Charles Dickens. || Correct: writing compared with writing.
B) Charles Dickens. || Compares writing with a person.
C) those of Charles Dickens. || Plural.
D) Charles Dickens's writer. || Makes no sense.

P: The temperatures recorded this summer were higher than ______ recorded in any summer since 1950.
A) that || Singular.
B) it || Singular.
*C) those || Correct.
D) any || "any recorded" leaves the comparison unclear.

P: Desert foxes have larger ears than ______
A) the ears of colder regions. || Regions do not have ears; the comparison is illogical.
*B) foxes that live in colder regions. || Correct: foxes compared with foxes.
C) colder regions. || Compares foxes with regions.
D) those of colder regions. || "those of colder regions" refers to regions' ears.

P: Mariam is ______ of the two sisters.
A) the tallest || Superlative; only two sisters.
*B) the taller || Correct.
C) more tall || Not standard.
D) tallest || Superlative.

P: The museum received ______ visitors in 2020 than in any other year.
*A) fewer || Correct: visitors can be counted.
B) less || For things that cannot be counted.
C) lesser || Means "lower in importance".
D) the fewest || Superlative does not fit with "than".

P: The streets of the old city are narrower than ______
A) the new city. || Compares streets with a city.
B) that of the new city. || Singular.
*C) those of the new city. || Correct.
D) the new city's street. || Singular.

## Practice 5 | Challenge
### Form, Structure, and Sense (comparisons)
P: The economist argues that the benefits of the new trade agreement for small farmers will be greater than ______ for large companies.
A) that || Singular; "benefits" is plural.
*B) those || Correct.
C) it || Singular.
D) the large companies || Compares benefits with companies.

P: Like many birds that migrate long distances, ______
A) the stork's journey across Egypt depends on warm air currents. || Compares birds with a journey.
*B) the white stork depends on warm air currents to cross Egypt. || Correct: bird compared with birds.
C) warm air currents help the white stork cross Egypt. || Compares birds with air currents.
D) crossing Egypt depends on warm air currents for the stork. || Compares birds with an action.

P: The researchers found that the memory of students who slept eight hours was stronger than ______
A) students who slept five hours. || Compares memory with students.
*B) that of students who slept five hours. || Correct.
C) those of students who slept five hours. || Plural; "memory" is singular.
D) five hours of sleep. || Compares memory with sleep.

P: The influence of the Nile on Egyptian culture has been greater than ______ any other natural feature.
*A) that of || Correct: influence compared with influence.
B) those of || Plural.
C) the one || "greater than the one any other natural feature" is ungrammatical.
D) of || Missing "that"; it compares influence with a feature.

P: Compared with ______ the novel's ending is surprisingly hopeful.
A) the author's life, || Compares an ending with a life — illogical here.
B) the author, || Compares an ending with a person.
*C) the endings of the author's earlier novels, || Correct: ending compared with endings.
D) when the author was young, || Compares an ending with a time.

P: The water of the Dead Sea is far saltier than ______
A) the ocean. || Compares water with an ocean as a place.
*B) that of the ocean. || Correct: "that of the ocean" = the ocean's water.
C) those of the ocean. || Plural; "water" is singular here.
D) the ocean is. || Compares water with the ocean.

P: The new engine uses ______ fuel than the old one and produces fewer emissions.
*A) less || Correct: "fuel" is not counted as separate items.
B) fewer || For countable nouns.
C) lesser || Means "lower in importance".
D) least || Superlative; it does not fit with "than".

P: Compared with the novels of her youth, which were full of adventure, ______
A) the writer became quiet and reflective in later life. || Compares novels with a person.
B) the writer's later life was quiet and reflective. || Compares novels with a life.
*C) the writer's later novels are quiet and reflective. || Correct: novels compared with novels.
D) her later years produced quiet novels. || Compares novels with years.

P: Among the four finalists, the team from Tanta was the ______ prepared.
A) better || Comparative; there are four finalists.
B) more better || Double comparative.
C) most best || Double superlative.
*D) best || Correct.

P: The traditions of the Nubian villages are as rich as ______ found anywhere in Egypt.
*A) those || Correct: plural, matching "traditions".
B) that || Singular.
C) it || Singular.
D) the villages || Compares traditions with villages.
