## Practice 1 | Foundation
### Words in Context (key vocabulary)
P: The guide gave a __brief__ talk about the history of the mosque, and then the visitors were free to explore on their own.
*A) short || Correct: the talk was short, and then visitors explored.
B) serious || Not suggested.
C) loud || Unrelated.
D) famous || Unrelated.

P: The scientists hope to __preserve__ the ancient paintings so that future generations can see them.
A) copy || Copying does not keep the originals safe.
*B) protect || Correct: keep them safe from damage.
C) explain || Unrelated.
D) sell || Opposite of keeping them for future generations.

P: The results of the experiment were __consistent__: every time the students repeated it, they got the same answer.
A) surprising || The same answer every time is not surprising.
B) incorrect || Nothing suggests error.
*C) unchanging || Correct: the same each time.
D) complicated || Unrelated.

P: The author's argument is __compelling__; after reading it, many readers changed their minds about the issue.
*A) convincing || Correct: it made readers change their minds.
B) confusing || Confusing arguments do not change minds.
C) long || Unrelated.
D) unusual || Imprecise.

P: Because water was __scarce__ in the desert village, families collected rainwater in large clay jars.
A) dirty || The jars suggest a lack of water, not dirty water.
*B) rare || Correct: in short supply.
C) expensive || Not stated.
D) cold || Unrelated.

P: The teacher asked the students to __revise__ their essays, fixing errors and improving their arguments.
A) read aloud || Fixing errors means changing the text.
B) shorten || Revising may not mean shortening.
*C) improve by changing || Correct.
D) memorise || Unrelated.

P: The bridge is a __vital__ link between the two villages; without it, the trip would take three hours.
A) beautiful || Unrelated to the point.
B) old || Unrelated.
*C) essential || Correct: very important.
D) narrow || Unrelated.

P: The novel __depicts__ life in a small fishing town on the Red Sea coast.
*A) shows || Correct: portrays.
B) changes || A novel does not change life in a town.
C) criticises || Not suggested.
D) ignores || Opposite.

P: The two scientists __collaborated__ on the project, sharing their data and writing the report together.
A) argued || They worked together.
*B) worked together || Correct.
C) competed || Opposite.
D) waited || Unrelated.

P: The old law was __abolished__ in 1990, and a new one took its place.
A) written || It already existed.
B) praised || Unrelated.
C) explained || Unrelated.
*D) ended || Correct: officially done away with.
