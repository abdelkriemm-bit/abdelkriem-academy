10 sentences to memorise · جمل تحفظها

احفظ جملتين كل يوم، وقول كل جملة بصوت عالي 5 مرات لحد ما تطلع منك لوحدها.

  1. 1Have you ever been to Aswan?هل سبق لك أن ذهبت إلى أسوان؟
  2. 2Yes, I have. I've been there twice.نعم، سبق لي ذلك. ذهبت إلى هناك مرتين.
  3. 3I've never flown in a plane.لم يسبق لي أن سافرت بالطائرة.
  4. 4Have you ever tried sushi? — No, never.هل سبق لك أن جربت السوشي؟ — لا، أبدًا.

Grammar: Present perfect for experiences · ever / never · been / gone

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

Present perfect = have / has + past participle (been, seen, eaten, tried …)

Use it for life experiences when we don't say when: I've visited Luxor. · Have you ever ridden a camel? · She's never tried sushi.

When you say WHEN, use the past simple: I visited Luxor in 2023.

لا يوجد في العربية زمن مثل present perfect؛ فنقول «زرت الأقصر» سواء ذكرنا الوقت أم لم نذكره. في الإنجليزية: التجربة في حياتك بدون وقت محدد = have / has + التصريف الثالث، ومع ذكر الوقت (last year, in 2020) = الماضي البسيط. أخطاء شائعة: Did you ever …? بدل Have you ever …?، و I have went بدل I have gone، و I have been to Luxor last year ✗.

PositiveNegativeQuestionShort answer
I / You / We / They have ('ve) seen it.I haven't seen it. / I've never seen it.Have you ever seen it?Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
He / She has ('s) seen it.He hasn't seen it. / He's never seen it.Has she ever seen it?Yes, she has. / No, she hasn't.
beengone
He has been to Luxor. = He went there and came back.He has gone to Luxor. = He is there now.

Regular verbs: the past participle = the past simple (visit → visited → visited). Irregular verbs: learn the third form (see → saw → seen).

ever goes before the participle in questions (Have you ever eaten …?). never goes before the participle (I've never eaten …) — don't use not with never.

  1. 1) ___ you ever ___ (eat) feteer meshaltet?
  2. 2) I ___ never ___ (ride) a horse.
  3. 3) My sister ___ (visit) Paris twice.
  4. 4) ___ Omar ever ___ (win) a prize?

Show the answers

Exercise 3 · Present perfect or past simple? Choose.

  1. 1) I (have been / went) to Hurghada last summer.
  2. 2) (Did you ever try / Have you ever tried) diving?
  3. 3) She (lost / has lost) her phone two days ago.
  4. 4) I (have never flown / never flew) in a hot-air balloon.
  5. 5) We (have met / met) a famous actor at the airport in 2019.
  6. 6) My brother (has broken / broke) his arm three times in his life.
Show the answers
1) went 2) Have you ever tried 3) lost 4) have never flown 5) met 6) has broken

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — “Things I've done”: write 80–100 words about your life experiences: three things you have done and two things you have never done but want to do. For one experience, say when it happened and how it was (past simple). Then ask a friend five “Have you ever …?” questions.

Example: I have done some exciting things in my life. I've been to Aswan twice and I've ridden a camel near the Pyramids. I've also climbed Mount Sinai. I did it in 2021 with my friends. We started at midnight and watched the sunrise from the top. It was hard, but it was unforgettable. However, I've never flown in a plane and I've never been abroad. I'd love to visit Turkey one day. I've also never tried diving, but I want to try it in Dahab next summer.
