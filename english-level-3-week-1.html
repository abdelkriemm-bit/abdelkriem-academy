Grammar: Present perfect continuous · for / since · How long…?

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

have / has + been + verb-ing = an action that started in the past and is still going on now (or has just stopped).

for + a period of time (for three years) · since + a starting point (since 2019) · How long have you been…?

في العربية نقول «أنا أعمل هنا منذ خمس سنوات» بالمضارع، فيقول كثير من المتعلمين: ✗ I am working here since five years. لكن الإنجليزية تستخدم هنا المضارع التام المستمر: ✓ I have been working here for five years. واستخدم for مع المدة (لمدة / بقاله…) و since مع نقطة البداية (منذ يناير، منذ ٢٠٢٠). والسؤال «بقالك قد إيه…؟» = How long have you been…?

PositiveNegativeQuestion
I / You / We / They have been working. He / She has been working.I haven't been sleeping well. She hasn't been feeling well.Have you been waiting long? How long has she been working here?
for + a periodsince + a starting point
for ten minutes · for two years · for a long timesince 9 o'clock · since 2019 · since I was a child

✗ I am working here since 2020. ✓ I have been working here since 2020.

State verbs (know, like, want, have = own) use the present perfect simple: I've known Mona for ten years (not I've been knowing).

Use it for an action that has just stopped when you can see the result: Your eyes are red! — I've been working on the computer all day.

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Complete with for or since.

  1. 1) She has been waiting ___ twenty minutes.
  2. 2) I've been living in Giza ___ 2015.
  3. 3) They've been working here ___ March.
  4. 4) It has been raining ___ this morning.
  5. 5) We've been learning English ___ three months.
  6. 6) Mona has been teaching ___ a long time.
  7. 7) He has been driving ___ he was eighteen.
  8. 8) I've been reading this book ___ a week.
Show the answers
1) for 2) since 3) since 4) since 5) for 6) for 7) since 8) for

Exercise 2 · Complete with the present perfect continuous.

  1. 1) I ___ (work) on this report all morning.
  2. 2) How long ___ you ___ (study) English?
  3. 3) She ___ (not feel) well lately.
  4. 4) They ___ (look) for a new flat since June.
  5. 5) Ahmed ___ (run) — that's why he's so hot.
  6. 6) What ___ you ___ (do) since you left university?
Show the answers
1) have been working 2) have … been studying 3) hasn't been feeling 4) have been looking 5) has been running 6) have … been doing

Exercise 3 · Correct the mistake.

  1. 1) I am working here since 2019.
  2. 2) How long are you waiting?
  3. 3) She has been knowing him for years.
  4. 4) We have been living in Cairo since five years.
  5. 5) He have been working overtime.
Show the answers
1) I have been working here since 2019. / I have worked here since 2019. 2) How long have you been waiting? 3) She has known him for years. 4) We have been living in Cairo for five years. / We have lived in Cairo for five years. 5) He has been working overtime.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) منذ متى وأنت تعمل في هذه الشركة؟
  2. 2) أبحث عن وظيفة منذ ثلاثة أشهر.
  3. 3) رفض أخي العرض لأن الراتب كان منخفضًا.
  4. 4) أنا متعب لأنني أعمل طوال اليوم.
Show the answers
1) How long have you been working for this company? / How long have you been working at / in this company? / How long have you worked for this company? 2) I've been looking for a job for three months. / I have been looking for work for three months. 3) My brother turned down the offer because the salary was low. / My brother refused the offer because the salary was low. 4) I'm tired because I've been working all day. / I am tired because I have been working all day long.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — “My working life”: write 120–150 words about your job (or the job you want). Say how long you have been doing it, what you have been doing lately, and what you like and don't like about it. Then record yourself reading it and check every for and since.

Example: My name is Mona and I'm an accountant. I have been working for a small software company in New Cairo for four years. Before that, I worked part-time in a bank while I was studying at university. My job is quite interesting, but the workload is heavy. Every month we have to meet tight deadlines, so I sometimes work overtime. Lately I have been learning a new accounting program, and I have been taking an online English course in the evenings, because our company has started working with clients in Dubai and London. I get on well with my colleagues, and my manager is very fair. Last year I got a pay rise, and I hope to get promoted soon. One day, I would like to set up my own business and take on two or three young accountants.
