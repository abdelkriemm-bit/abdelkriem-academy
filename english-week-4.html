10 sentences to memorise

  1. 1I wake up at seven o'clock.أستيقظ الساعة السابعة.
  2. 2I have breakfast at home.أتناول الإفطار في البيت.
  3. 3I go to work by bus.أذهب إلى العمل بالحافلة.
  4. 4I start work at nine and finish at five.أبدأ العمل في التاسعة وأنتهي في الخامسة.
  5. 5What time is it? — It's half past three.كم الساعة؟ — الثالثة والنصف.
  6. 6What time do you go to bed?متى تذهب إلى السرير؟
  7. 7I don't work on Friday.لا أعمل يوم الجمعة.

Grammar: Present simple (I / you / we / they) · at, on, in · telling the time

Use it for habits and routines. at + time (at 7) · on + day (on Monday) · in the morning / afternoon / evening · at night

لا تقل I am work every day. الفعل في المضارع البسيط يأتي وحده بدون am: I work every day.

PositiveNegativeQuestionShort answer
I work.I don't work.Do you work?Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

Exercises

Exercise 1 — Complete with at, on or in.

  1. 1. I get up ___ six o'clock.
  2. 2. I don't work ___ Friday.
  3. 3. I read ___ the evening.
  4. 4. We pray ___ night.
  5. 5. We visit my parents ___ Saturday.
  6. 6. I have lunch ___ two o'clock.
Show the answers
1) at · 2) on · 3) in · 4) at · 5) on · 6) at

Exercise 2 — Make the sentence negative (–) or a question (?).

  1. 1. I watch TV in the morning. (–)
  2. 2. They cook every day. (?)
  3. 3. We go to bed late. (–)
  4. 4. You work on Saturday. (?)
Show the answers
1) I don't watch TV in the morning. · 2) Do they cook every day? · 3) We don't go to bed late. · 4) Do you work on Saturday?

Exercise 3 — Write the time in words.

  1. 1. 7:00
  2. 2. 3:30
  3. 3. 9:15
  4. 4. 5:45
Show the answers
1) seven o'clock · 2) half past three · 3) quarter past nine · 4) quarter to six

Exercise 4 — Translate into English.

  1. 1. أستيقظ مبكرًا كل يوم.
  2. 2. لا أشاهد التلفاز.
  3. 3. متى تبدأ العمل؟
  4. 4. نعود إلى البيت الساعة السادسة.
Show the answers
1) I wake up early every day. · 2) I don't watch TV. · 3) What time do you start work? · 4) We come back home at six o'clock.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak

Write & Speak — “My day”: write 8 sentences about your normal day, with times. Say it aloud without looking. Example: I wake up at six thirty. I take a shower and have breakfast. I leave home at half past seven. I start work at eight. I have lunch at two. I come back home at five. In the evening I relax and watch TV. I go to bed at eleven.
