Grammar: much / many / a lot of / a few / a little · too / enough

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

much + uncountable (much salt) · many + countable (many eggs) — mostly in negatives and questions

a lot of / lots of + both, especially in positive sentences

a few + countable (a few times) · a little + uncountable (a little oil)

too + adjective (too hot) · too much / too many + noun · adjective + enough (fit enough) · enough + noun (enough sleep)

يترجم العربي «جدًا» دائمًا إلى too، فيقول The food is too delicious ✗. الصحيح very delicious؛ لأن too معناها «أكثر من اللازم» وفيها مشكلة. وانتبه لمكان enough: يأتي بعد الصفة (old enough) وقبل الاسم (enough money)، فلا تقل enough old ✗. ولا تقل many water أو much eggs: much مع غير المعدود، و many مع المعدود.

Countable (eggs, people)Uncountable (water, salt)
a big quantitymany / a lot ofmuch / a lot of
a small quantitya fewa little
more than is goodtoo manytoo much
less than you neednot enough eggsnot enough water
PatternExample
too + adjectiveThe soup is too salty.
adjective + enoughHe's old enough to drive.
enough + nounWe don't have enough time.
too + adjective + to + verbIt's too hot to go jogging.

very = a lot (good or bad): The food is very good. too = more than is good: The food is too spicy for me.

a few / a little = some (positive). few / little without a = almost none (negative): I have few friends here.

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Complete with much or many.

  1. 1) How ___ glasses of water do you drink a day?
  2. 2) I don't eat ___ bread.
  3. 3) There aren't ___ gyms in my village.
  4. 4) How ___ time do you spend on your phone?
  5. 5) She doesn't have ___ free time.
  6. 6) Are there ___ people in the pool?
Show the answers
1) many 2) much 3) many 4) much 5) much 6) many

Exercise 2 · Complete with a few or a little.

  1. 1) I go to the gym ___ times a week.
  2. 2) Add ___ salt to the rice.
  3. 3) Can I have ___ milk in my coffee?
  4. 4) There are ___ apples in the fridge.
  5. 5) We stayed in Dahab for ___ days.
  6. 6) I need ___ help with this exercise.
Show the answers
1) a few 2) a little 3) a little 4) a few 5) a few 6) a little

Exercise 3 · Rewrite with too or enough.

  1. 1) The coffee is very hot. I can't drink it. (too … to)
  2. 2) Omar isn't tall. He can't reach the shelf. (enough … to)
  3. 3) I can't pay for a gym membership. (not enough money)
  4. 4) There are a lot of cars in the city. The air is polluted. (too many)
  5. 5) You eat a lot of sugar. It's bad for you. (too much)
Show the answers
1) The coffee is too hot to drink. (or: … too hot for me to drink.) 2) Omar isn't tall enough to reach the shelf. 3) I don't have enough money for a gym membership (to pay for a gym membership). 4) There are too many cars in the city, so the air is polluted. 5) You eat too much sugar. It's bad for you.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) لا أمارس الرياضة بما فيه الكفاية.
  2. 2) يوجد سكر أكثر من اللازم في هذا العصير.
  3. 3) أشرب بضعة أكواب من الشاي كل يوم.
  4. 4) الجو حارّ أكثر من اللازم للجري.
Show the answers
1) I don't exercise enough. (or: I don't get enough exercise.) 2) There's too much sugar in this juice. 3) I drink a few cups of tea every day. 4) It's too hot to go jogging (to run).

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — “Is my lifestyle healthy?”: write 80–100 words about your habits. Use much, many, a lot of, a few, a little, too and enough, and say what you want to change. Then give a friend three pieces of advice about healthy living (use should).

Example: I think my lifestyle is quite healthy, but it isn't perfect. I eat a lot of vegetables and a little fruit every day, and I drink plenty of water. However, I drink too many cups of tea, and I put too much sugar in them. I don't get enough exercise, because I work long hours, and I only walk a few times a week. I also spend too much time on my phone at night, so I don't get enough sleep. This month I'm going to cut down on tea and go jogging three times a week.
