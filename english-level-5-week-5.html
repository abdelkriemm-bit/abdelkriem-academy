Grammar: Participle clauses · Walking home, I… · Built in 1902, the dam… · Having finished, we…

اقرا الشرح بالعربي الأول، وبعدين بص على الجدول والأمثلة. لو مش فاهم، اسمع درس الجرامر اللي في خطة الأسبوع.

Participle clauses make writing shorter and more formal. -ing (present participle) = active meaning, at the same time or giving a reason (Walking home, I saw…) · V3 (past participle) = passive meaning (Built in 1902, the dam…) · Having + V3 = an action completed before the main action (Having finished, we left).

أشهر خطأ هو «الفاعل المعلّق»: Walking to school, the rain started ✗ — كأن المطر هو الذي كان يمشي! يجب أن يكون فاعل جملة الـ participle هو نفسه فاعل الجملة الرئيسية: Walking to school, I got caught in the rain ✓. وفرّق بين المعنى المبني للمعلوم (ing) والمبني للمجهول (V3): Using solar power, the village… (القرية تستخدم) / Powered by the sun, the village… (القرية تُشغَّل). واستخدم Having + V3 عندما يحدث الفعل الأول وينتهي قبل الثاني.

Long sentenceParticiple clauseMeaning
While I was walking along the Nile, I saw a heron.Walking along the Nile, I saw a heron.same time (active)
Because she didn't have a bag, she bought one.Not having a bag, she bought one.reason (negative = Not + -ing)
The report, which was written by experts, is alarming.Written by experts, the report is alarming.passive
After they had collected the rubbish, they recycled it.Having collected the rubbish, they recycled it.earlier action
The people who live near the factory are worried.The people living near the factory are worried.shorter relative clause

The subject of the participle clause must be the same as the subject of the main clause.

Passive and earlier: Having been warned about the storm, the fishermen stayed at home.

Participle clauses are common in reports, articles and stories; in conversation we usually use the full clause.

Exercises · تمارين

حل كل تمرين في يومه بالقلم الرصاص، وبعدين دوس «Show the answers» وصحّح لنفسك. اكتب أخطاءك في الكشكول وراجعها في اليوم السادس.

Exercise 1 · Combine the sentences with a participle clause.

  1. 1) I opened the window. I noticed the smell of smoke.
  2. 2) The village is surrounded by desert. It has very little water.
  3. 3) She had read the report. She decided to stop using plastic bags.
  4. 4) He didn't know the answer. He kept quiet.
  5. 5) The cans were collected by students. They were sent to a recycling centre.
  6. 6) We had planted the trees. We watered them every day.
Show the answers
1) Opening the window, I noticed the smell of smoke. 2) Surrounded by desert, the village has very little water. 3) Having read the report, she decided to stop using plastic bags. 4) Not knowing the answer, he kept quiet. 5) Collected by students, the cans were sent to a recycling centre. 6) Having planted the trees, we watered them every day.

Exercise 2 · Choose the correct participle.

  1. 1) (Making / Made) from recycled paper, this notebook is very eco-friendly.
  2. 2) (Feeling / Felt) tired, Mona went to bed early.
  3. 3) The children (playing / played) in the park are from our school.
  4. 4) (Finishing / Having finished) his degree in 2020, Ahmed joined an environmental charity.
  5. 5) Most of the fish (catching / caught) in the lake are small.
  6. 6) (Not wanting / Not wanted) to waste food, she gave the leftovers to her neighbours.
Show the answers
1) Made 2) Feeling 3) playing 4) Having finished 5) caught 6) Not wanting

Exercise 3 · Correct the dangling participle: rewrite the sentence so that the subject is correct.

  1. 1) Walking to work, the rain started.
  2. 2) Having finished the project, the report was sent to the manager.
  3. 3) Built in 1902, I visited the old dam in Aswan.
  4. 4) Looking out of the window, the sea was beautiful.
Show the answers
1) Walking to work, I got caught in the rain. / While I was walking to work, it started to rain. 2) Having finished the project, we sent the report to the manager. 3) Built in 1902, the old dam in Aswan is still impressive. / I visited the old dam in Aswan, which was built in 1902. 4) Looking out of the window, I saw how beautiful the sea was.

Exercise 4 · Translate into English.

  1. 1) بعد أن قرأت المقال، قرّرت أن أقلّل استخدامي للبلاستيك.
  2. 2) بينما كنا نسير على الشاطئ، وجدنا سلحفاة صغيرة.
  3. 3) هذه الحقيبة، المصنوعة من القماش، قابلة لإعادة الاستخدام.
  4. 4) الناس الذين يعيشون بالقرب من المصنع يعانون من التلوّث.
Show the answers
1) Having read the article, I decided to reduce my use of plastic. 2) Walking along the beach, we found a small turtle. / While we were walking along the beach, we found a small turtle. 3) Made of cloth, this bag is reusable. / This bag, made of cloth, is reusable. 4) The people living / who live near the factory suffer from pollution.

Do each exercise in pencil first, then open the answers to check.

Write & Speak · اكتب واتكلم

اكتب الموضوع في الكشكول بنفسك، وبص على المثال لو احتجت، وبعدين اقراه بصوت عالي أو سجّل نفسك.

Write & Speak — Write a report (200–250 words) for your local council about an environmental problem in your area. Use headings (Introduction · The problem · Recommendations · Conclusion) and at least four participle clauses. Then present it in two minutes as if you were at a meeting.

Example: Report: Plastic waste on the beaches of Alexandria Introduction The aim of this report is to describe the problem of plastic waste on the public beaches of Alexandria and to suggest practical solutions. Having visited five beaches in August and spoken to local residents, I have gathered the following information. The problem Walking along the beaches early in the morning, visitors can see piles of plastic bottles, bags and food containers. Most of this rubbish is left by holidaymakers, but some of it is washed up by the sea. Because it is not collected regularly, the waste is blown into the water, where it harms fish, turtles and other wildlife. Several fishermen interviewed for this report said that their catches had fallen noticeably in recent years. Recommendations 1. Recycling bins should be placed every hundred metres, clearly labelled in Arabic and English. 2. Cafés and kiosks on the beach should be encouraged to phase out single-use plastic, offering paper straws and reusable cups instead. 3. A volunteer clean-up day could be organised every month, supported by local schools and businesses. 4. Fines should be introduced for people caught leaving rubbish on the sand. Conclusion Known for its beautiful coastline, Alexandria cannot afford to lose its beaches to pollution. Taken together, these measures would protect the marine ecosystem, attract more tourists and make the city a cleaner place to live. I strongly recommend that the council should act before next summer.
